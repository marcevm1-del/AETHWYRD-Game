# 09a — Gear Systems, Weapons, Armor & Sets (v0.2)

## 1. Slots
Head · Chest · Hands · Legs · Feet · Cloak/Back · Main Hand · Off Hand · Ring ×2 · Amulet · Charm · Belt (potion slots) · **Cosmetic layer for every visible slot** (transmog, dyes).

| Slot | Notes |
|---|---|
| Main / Off Hand | Off hand holds a shield, a second weapon (dual-wield classes), a focus (casters) or a tool (lantern, instrument) |
| Charm | One trinket with a unique effect (luck charm, Mochi's ribbon, a companion's keepsake). Many charms are quest rewards |
| Belt | **2 quick-item slots at F, +1 at D, C, A and S** (up to 6). Holds potions, bombs and food |
| Cloak/Back | Cosmetic-heavy; some cloaks give climate comfort (Big Upgrade §48) |
| Rings ×2 | Rings of the same set don't stack; rare rings come from Tyrants |
| Relic | A **separate slot** that opens with your first Relic, for non-weapon relics (crowns, masks, orbs). Weapon and armor relics (e.g. *Fang of the First Wolf*) use their normal slots (see Relics & Chase Items) |
- **Durability:** gear wears down, never breaks completely (it drops to 50% stats at 0). Repairs at smiths or with a field repair kit.
- **Growth weapons** (Big Upgrade §25) can sit in any weapon slot and keep their history when reforged.

|---|---|---|

| **Reforging** | Blacksmith | Reroll bonus stats |
| **Awakening** | Shrine forges | Mythic+ unlocks a hidden skill using monster cores |

| **Dyes & Transmog** | Tailor | Full cosmetic freedom |

## Relics & Chase Items
The "overpowered" fantasy now comes from **grinding for legendary-tier items**. They're hard to get, worth it, and every one has a story.

| Tier | How you get it | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Relic** (Legendary+) | Multi-step chain: rare drop → quest → forge | *Fang of the First Wolf*: 0.5% drop from Alpha wolves → hunt the Silver Fenrir → Torvald forges it |
| **Grand Relic** (Mythic) | 3–5 chains across regions + a boss at Worthy+ rating | *Starfall Edge*: collect 7 meteor shards that fall during Twin Eclipses |
| **Apex Relic** (Mythic+, 50+ hrs each) | Account-long chase: crafting, taming, Labyrinth floors, a world event | *Crown of the Beast God* |
| **Divine Artifact** | Story + endgame | *Solgrace* |

- **Relic Codex** (in the Journal) shows silhouettes of undiscovered relics with cryptic hints, so players know the chase exists.
- Relics **level up** with you (Relic Lv 1 → 50) through use and feeding materials.
- **Rank concealment** stays only as one optional relic (*Mask of the Humble*). It's a fun extra, not a theme.
