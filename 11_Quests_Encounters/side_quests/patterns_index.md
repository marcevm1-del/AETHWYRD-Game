# 11e — Pattern Side Quests: Forty Original Quests (draft)

> *"We looked at how other games tell small stories. Then we told our own."*

**Status: draft proposal.** `Canon.md` wins; `Data/` is canonical for numbers. These **forty quests** (eight patterns, five quests each) were written after reading about how Skyrim, Genshin Impact, The Witcher 3, Breath of the Wild, Elden Ring and similar games structure their side content. **Only ideas and mechanics were borrowed** (`CLAUDE.md`'s commercial-game rule); every premise, name, line and place is original. The research, with sources and caveats, is in [[side_quests_inspiration_notes]]. A table of every side quest is in [[all_side_quests]].

## What is different from the region files

- **Pattern, not place.** Each file groups quests by *shape* and spreads them across regions that the ten region files did not use (Sunscar, Goldmere Coast, Shen-Lu, Strife Marches, Whitewater Reach, Haneul, Zarvan, Umbravel, Xochitl Mire, Stormreach, Gearholm, Moraveil, Velmark).
- **Systems built in.** Cairns respect the guidance channels; Riddles have three hint tiers; Hangouts track Trust, Respect, Closeness and Tension with **no fail state**; Contracts use the field-journal deduction idea; Guild Ladders keep the faction's own voice.
- **No Tyrant dies.** No quest here ends a siege.

## What these quests propose (Canon §6)

Small new facts, all marked in the files and all drafts for approval: the Qadira guides' table (Sunscar); the dark lighthouse and the Krakenmother's attraction to light (Goldmere); the hundred-step stair (Shen-Lu); the Wedding Tent treaty (Strife Marches); the Tithe-Hound as the land's creditor (Velmark); the hydra's heads as named, fed ancestors (Xochitl Mire); the first garden's carved treaty (Zarvan); the Examination Hall's seventh lantern (Haneul); the Last Fire-style *offering* customs; and about forty supporting NPCs, including three companion records (Kiri, Sir Aldric Voss, Bram Copperpot).
