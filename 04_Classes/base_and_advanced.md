# 04a — Base & Advanced Classes (v0.2)

## 0. Class Rules
- **Tiers:** Base (F–E) → Advanced (D–C) → Expert (B–A) → Master (S–SS) → Legendary (SSS). Hidden and Monster classes sit outside the ladder.
- **Main Class + Sub-Class** (Sub unlocks at C). The Sub-Class gives 50% passives, 2 active skill slots, and no Ultimate.
- **Class change needs** Rank + Level + Class Quest + a Mastery requirement (e.g. *Sword Mastery 5*).
- **Every class has:** a unique **Class Mechanic** (meter or rule), a **Class Trainer** NPC, a **Class Quest chain**, an **Ultimate** at Lv 30 of that tier, and its own **combat idle and victory pose**.
- **Respec:** at the **Shrine of Ilvaris** for gold (cheap at F, expensive later). Skills learned stay learned.

Stat growth per level is shown as STR/VIT/AGI/DEX/INT/WIS/LUK/CHA (the class bonus on top of +5 free points).
