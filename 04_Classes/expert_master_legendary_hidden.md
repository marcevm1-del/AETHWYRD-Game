## 4. Hidden Classes — 20 secret paths
Can be taken as Main or Sub-Class. Requirements are hinted only through rumors, books and odd NPC comments.

| Hidden Class | Secret unlock | Why it's secretly broken |
|---|---|---|
| **Porter** | Carry 150%+ weight for 30 hrs total | Throw inventory items as projectiles (damage = item weight); infinite item box later |
| **Appraiser** | Appraise 3,000 things | See enemy HP, skills, weaknesses and *future drops*; reveal hidden quests |
| **Farmer** | Harvest 1,000 crops | Hoe arts deal true Earth damage; plant monster-plant turrets |
| **Cook** | Cook 300 unique dishes | Meals grant permanent stat points; "cook" a monster to learn its passive |
| **Gambler** | Win 50 high-stakes games | Dice skills: random multiplier up to ×20 |
| **Villager** | Reach Lv 30 without class change | Even growth; learn ANY skill at half speed |
| **Bard** | Perform in 30 taverns | Songs as party-wide buffs; music duels; charm bosses |
| **Painter** | Paint 50 landscapes at viewpoints | Paint creatures into existence (summons) |
| **Fisherman** | Catch the 7 legendary fish | Rod is a whip-spear; reel enemies; summon sea-beasts |
| **Librarian** | Read 200 books | Recall any skill read about in a book (limited uses) |
| **Gardener of Graves** | Tend 100 graves | Commune with the dead; spirit army (non-necromantic, Holy) |
| **Maid / Butler** | Serve a noble household for 30 days | Perfect evasion, combat cleaning, "never seen" stealth |
| **Mapmaker** | Discover 95% of a region | Fast travel anywhere seen; reveal enemy routes |
| **Blacksmith of Ruin** | Break 500 weapons | Weapons shatter into shrapnel AoE |
| **Mimic Hunter** | Be eaten by 10 mimics and escape | Become a mimic-disguise master |
| **Chicken Knight** | Defend Granny Mabel's "chickens" (cockatrices) 20 times | Cockatrice cavalry; petrifying charge |
| **Healer of Monsters** | Heal 100 wounded monsters | Monsters avoid harming you; recruit Named monsters peacefully |
| **Moonlight Dancer** | Dance under full Lumen at 12 shrines | Dance-based combat, crit and evasion boosts at night |
| **Unemployed** | Refuse every class change offer 3 times | No class, but all class caps removed. The joke class that is actually the best |
| **The Otherworlder** | Recall 10 "old world" memories (Otherworld Knowledge) | Modern-tech crafting: guns, trains, radio, photography |
