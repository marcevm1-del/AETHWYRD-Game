# Weekly Board

The board turns the seven-day calendar into recurring local story texture. Specials frame a day; dailies provide varied work; rank, time and region-state filters keep the offer plausible. On a siege attack day, the defense contract takes priority at the top of the board.

## Player-facing rules

A Day Special should appear at most once in the same in-game week. Three dailies are selected from the weekday’s pool plus the Any pool, filtered by availability and time. A missed board offer is not a settlement disaster; only a posted defense follows the siege auto-resolution rules.
