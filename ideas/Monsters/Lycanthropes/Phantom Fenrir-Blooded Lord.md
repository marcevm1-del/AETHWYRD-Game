# Phantom Fenrir-Blooded Lord

> The Phantom Fenrir-Blooded Lord is a large creature with a broad-shouldered humanoid build, about 3.6 m tall. Its fur is patchy and subtly wrong, as if assembled from memory, colored in warm gold and pearl white. Its most striking feature is a crown of frost spikes growing from its mane. It has eyes set slightly too far apart. An elegant crest and glowing veins of mana mark it as a champion of its kind. It glides without moving its legs, surrounded by soft radiance and floating motes of light. Its body is translucent blue, half in the spirit world; weapons pass through it unless they are blessed or enchanted.
