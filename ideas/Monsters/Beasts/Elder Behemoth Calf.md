# Elder Behemoth Calf

> The Elder Behemoth Calf is a huge creature with a powerful four-legged hunter's body, about 6.4 m long. Its coat is weathered and natural, colored in ochre, clay and mossy stone. Its most striking feature is a stone-plated back and stump-like legs. It has dark, wary eyes. An elegant crest and glowing veins of mana mark it as a champion of its kind. It stalks low to the ground, surrounded by pebbles that tremble and float near it. It is far larger than its kin, with a battle-scarred silhouette and a commanding presence; others of its kind follow it.
