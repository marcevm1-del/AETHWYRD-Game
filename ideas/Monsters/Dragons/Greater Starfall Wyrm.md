# Greater Starfall Wyrm

> The Greater Starfall Wyrm is a colossal creature with an ancient, serpentine dragon body, about 96 m long. Its scales are rough, scarred and spiked, colored in warm gold and pearl white. Look for scales flecked with fallen starmetal that glitter like a night sky. It has narrow glowing eyes. Its presence is world-scale; the sky changes color when it appears. It charges without hesitation, surrounded by soft radiance and floating motes of light. It is far larger than its kin, with a battle-scarred silhouette and a commanding presence; others of its kind follow it.
