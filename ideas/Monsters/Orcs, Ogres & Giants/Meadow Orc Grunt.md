# Meadow Orc Grunt

> The Meadow Orc Grunt is a person-sized creature with a lean humanoid frame, about 2 m tall. Its hide is rough, scarred and spiked, colored in pale jade and cloud white. Survivors describe tusks, war paint and a dented iron axe. It has eyes that catch torchlight like coins. Small scars and rough edges mark it as a survivor. It charges without hesitation, surrounded by swirling leaves and a constant breeze. Adapted to plains lands, it has golden, grass-toned coloring.
