# Idea Inbox

Capture concepts that arrive in conversation, research, reference boards or playtests. A rough sentence is enough to preserve an idea; use [[Templates/Idea|the Idea template]] when it is ready to connect to the world.

## How ideas become design

1. **Capture — raw:** preserve the original spark and where it came from. Keep the emotional reason it sounded exciting.
2. **Shape — draft:** describe the player moment, choices, world connection and production cost. Link every affected node.
3. **Check:** compare it with [[../Design Bible/Canon|Canon]], region and character data, rating, accessibility goals and scope. Mark conflicts explicitly.
4. **Integrate — accepted:** place the finished design in its topic document or structured data, update cross-links and changelog, then rebuild the vault.

**Authority:** this inbox is not canon. [[Design Bible/Canon|Canon.md]] settles story facts and decisions; `Data/` settles gameplay numbers. Visual references are inspiration, not permission to reproduce a specific artist’s work.

**Useful capture details:** desired feeling, player action, availability, possible changes, companion reactions, costs and rewards, plus the smallest playable version.

## Shaping checklist

- Does it strengthen exploration, combat, investigation, social play or preparation?
- Can the player understand the choice and its consequence?
- Does it create a memorable Aethwyrd-specific moment?
- Does it respect canon and avoid accidental spoilers?
- Can a small version be prototyped before expanding it across the world?
- Which notes should link to the idea or its eventual design?
