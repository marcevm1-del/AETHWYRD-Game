## Overview

Ashira Vale is the kindest valley in the world. Its fields are soft, its doors are always open and its single town welcomes every traveller with bread, honey and a bed. Visitors who come for a night stay a week, and those who stay a week stop writing home, though their last letters always say they are happy. The vale has no Tyrant that anyone in it would recognise as one. The Gentle Warden makes sure that no one who arrives ever wants to leave.

## Geography

The vale is a sheltered bowl of green fields, orchards and beech woods ringed by round hills that keep out the wind. A clear, slow river runs through the middle, and the town of Ashira stands on both its banks, joined by a dozen little bridges. The weather is always mild: rain falls at night, the sun shines by day and the seasons change so gently that no one notices. Only one road leads in over the hills, and the milestones along it stop counting halfway.

## Ecology

Deer graze at the edge of the orchards and do not run from people. Rabbits, songbirds and bees fill the fields, and the flowers bloom all year. There are no wolves, no foxes, no hawks and, the locals say proudly, no flies. Travellers who know the wild find this unsettling: a valley with only prey animals in it should be overrun, and Ashira is not. Something keeps the balance, and it does not do it by hunting.

## Peoples

The people of Ashira are farmers, bakers, weavers and hosts, and most of them came from somewhere else. A retired soldier keeps bees; a former pirate grows roses; a famous adventurer from two generations ago runs the bakery and cannot remember her own legend. They are warm, cheerful and incurious about the outside world. They are happy to talk about the weather, the harvest and each other, and they change the subject, gently and completely, when asked where they came from or what they have lost.

## Power & trade

Ashira has a council of elders that meets on the first Solenday of each month and has nothing to decide. There is no crime, no debt and no quarrel worth bringing to it. The vale trades nothing with the outside world and needs nothing from it; its fields feed everyone with something left over. Merchants who come to sell find that they no longer want to, and they stay to help with the harvest.

## Faith & customs

The people honour the day-gods with flowers and songs and speak of the Gentle Warden as the vale's protector, the reason Ashira is safe. They leave honey at a garden in the middle of town where the Warden is said to walk. There is no graveyard in Ashira, no funeral customs and no word in the local speech for mourning.

## The Tyrant

The Gentle Warden, Keeper of Ashira, is an aberration that feeds on contentment. It is not cruel; it genuinely wants everyone to be happy. It soothes away grief, ambition, regret and memory, one by one, until a person is perfectly at peace and perfectly empty, and then it takes what is left. It is everywhere in the vale and nowhere in particular, and the only sign of it is how good everything feels. Fighting it means persuading people to want their pain back.

## Senses

Warm bread and honey; birdsong all day; laughter from every doorway; a sweetness that turns cloying if you stay too long.

## History

- **Now:** Travellers are disappearing on the road into the vale, and their families are asking the guild where they went. The guild's own investigator went in a month ago and sent back a letter saying she had never been happier.
- **Parents:** Within living memory a famous party of adventurers came to the vale to rest after a great campaign. They are still there, and they do not remember the campaign.
- **Grandparents:** In their grandparents' time Ashira was an ordinary farming valley with ordinary troubles, and the old maps show a graveyard on the hill behind the mill.
- **Ancient:** Ashira has no graveyard. No one remembers when anyone last asked why.

## Settlements

- **Ashira:** A warm, welcoming town of bridges and gardens on the river, where everyone is happy.

## Landmarks

- **The Warden's Garden:** A walled garden at the centre of town, always in bloom, where honey is left for the Warden.
- **The Hill Behind the Mill:** Where the old maps show a graveyard, and where now there is only a meadow that hums.
- **The Hill Road:** The only road in, whose milestones stop counting halfway.

## Story hooks

- A woman from Aldmere is looking for her husband, who came to Ashira a year ago and wrote that he was happy, and then stopped writing.
- The baker, once a famous adventurer, has begun to dream of a battle she cannot remember, and she wants to know whether to let herself remember it.
- A child in Ashira asks why there are no old people, and nobody can answer her.
