## Overview

Haneul is a mountain kingdom built around a single pass, the only road through the great mountains to the east. Its gate-towns climb the pass in terraces, and its capital stands at the top, behind a wall that has never fallen, though it has come close. The kingdom is famous for its scholars, its courtesy and its endless, demanding examinations. A red gate has opened in the clouds above the pass, and a demon herald has come through it.

## Geography

The kingdom occupies a long, narrow valley climbing to a high pass between snowy peaks. The gate-towns are built on terraces up the valley's sides, and the capital sits in the saddle of the pass. Pine forests and rhododendrons cover the slopes, and the river below runs fast and cold. The red gate hangs in the clouds above the pass, visible from everywhere.

## Ecology

Magpies, tigers, deer and wild boar live in the forests, and mountain spirits live higher up. The people farm the terraces and gather mushrooms and herbs from the forest. Demons have begun to come through the red gate, and they hunt the mountain spirits first.

## Peoples

The people of Haneul are farmers, merchants, soldiers and scholars, and scholarship is the path to everything. Every family dreams of a child who passes the great examinations and joins the court. The people are courteous, proud and fiercely loyal to their king, and they take off their shoes at every door.

## Power & trade

Haneul is ruled by a king advised by a council of scholar-officials chosen by examination. The wall and the gate-towns are garrisoned by a standing army that drills daily. The kingdom grows rich on the tolls of the pass, and on ginseng, paper, porcelain and scholarship.

## Faith & customs

The people honour the day-gods, their ancestors and the mountain spirits, whose shrines stand at every bend of the pass. The ancestral rites are performed on Solenday, and every household keeps the names of its ancestors on tablets.

## The Tyrant

Varos, Herald of the Red Gate, is a demon officer who came through the gate in the sky to prepare the way for something larger. He is precise, polite and utterly without mercy, and he treats the siege like an examination that the kingdom is failing. He wants the pass, because whoever holds it holds the road between east and west.

## Senses

Pine and ink; the clack of examination brushes; drums on the wall; the red light that never fades from the clouds.

## History

- **Now:** The red gate opened in the sky at midsummer, and Varos came through it with a host of demons. They have taken the upper gate-towns and are pressing on the capital's wall.
- **Parents:** Within living memory a scholar-official discovered that the wall was built for the wrong enemy, and was exiled for saying so.
- **Grandparents:** In their grandparents' time the wall held against an army from the north for a whole winter, and the kingdom still celebrates its anniversary.
- **Ancient:** Haneul built its wall for an enemy from the north. The red gate opened in the sky instead.

## Settlements

- **Haneul gate-towns:** Terraced towns climbing the pass, the capital at the top with its Cloud Gate Guesthouse.

## Landmarks

- **The Red Gate:** A gate of red light hanging in the clouds above the pass.
- **The Examination Hall:** Where every scholar of the kingdom sits the great examinations.
- **The Unfallen Wall:** The great wall across the pass, scarred but unbroken.

## Story hooks

- The exiled scholar who said the wall was built for the wrong enemy wants to come home and help.
- A mountain spirit is hiding in a farmhouse from the demons, and the farmer wants it gone.
- The examinations are being held despite the siege, and a candidate needs an escort to the hall.

## Clans

- **The Scholar Owls** (Owl Clan, led by Examiner Hwang Nightquill): The examination-masters of Haneul, who see in the dark and remember everything.
- **The Tigerguard** (Tiger Clan, led by Captain Baek Horang): Haneul's elite wall-guard.

## Fauna

- **Tingra** (Predator): A lean, striped hunter of the mountain passes.
