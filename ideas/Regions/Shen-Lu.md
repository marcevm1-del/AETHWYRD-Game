## Overview

Shen-Lu is a land of steep mountains, misty forests and cultivation sects, where martial artists train on stairs that climb into the clouds and seek to master the qi that flows through all things. Tournaments are held at every crossroads, secret manuals change hands for fortunes and a sect's reputation can rise or fall in a single duel. A colossus has come to the mountains, and it is devouring their qi.

## Geography

The mountains are sheer limestone pinnacles wrapped in mist and pine, cut by deep gorges and roaring rivers. Sect monasteries cling to the cliffs, reached by stairways of hundreds or thousands of steps. The Jade Crossroads, a great market town, lies in the valley where the main roads meet.

## Ecology

Cranes, monkeys, pandas, tigers and golden pheasants live in the forests, and spirit beasts live higher up: qilin, foxes and dragons. Spirit herbs grow on the high peaks and are prized by cultivators. The mountains' qi feeds everything, and the Hunger Colossus is draining it.

## Peoples

The people of Shen-Lu are farmers, merchants, scholars and martial artists, and the sects are their pride. Each sect has its own style, philosophy and rivals. Sect disciples are respected and feared, and the ordinary people are expected to defer to them. Some sects protect the villages; some exploit them.

## Power & trade

The sects hold the real power, and the mountain towns answer to whichever sect controls them. An emperor rules from a distant capital in name only. Tea, silk, spirit herbs, medicines and martial manuals are traded across the world, and a good manual can buy a town.

## Faith & customs

The people honour the day-gods and the mountains themselves, which they believe are alive. Cultivators seek to become one with the mountain's qi and ascend. Every sect keeps a shrine to its founder, and the founders of the two greatest sects are buried facing away from each other.

## The Tyrant

Wuxiang, the Hunger Colossus, is a giant made of mountain stone and stolen qi, created by a sect that sought to steal the power of the mountains. It escaped its creators and now walks the peaks, devouring their qi to grow larger. It cannot be fought with qi, because it eats it. The sect that made it is keeping that fact very quiet.

## Senses

Pine and mist; tea and incense; the clash of training swords at dawn; a silence on the grey peaks like held breath.

## History

- **Now:** Wuxiang, the Hunger Colossus, is walking through the mountains, draining their qi. Peaks have gone grey, and cultivators are falling ill.
- **Parents:** Within living memory the great tournament of the Jade Crossroads ended in a massacre, and the sects have not held one since.
- **Grandparents:** In their grandparents' time the sects were united in a single school, until the Hundred-Stair Schism split it.
- **Ancient:** The sects were one school before the Hundred-Stair Schism. Their founders' graves face away from each other.

## Settlements

- **Sect foothill towns:** Towns at the feet of the sect mountains, where disciples buy supplies and villagers sell them, near the Jade Crossroads and the Wandering Dragon Inn.

## Landmarks

- **The Hundred Stair:** A stairway of a thousand steps leading to the old united school.
- **The Jade Crossroads:** A market town where the roads and the sects meet, home of the Wandering Dragon Inn.
- **The Grey Peaks:** Mountains drained of qi, bare and silent.

## Story hooks

- A sect wants an outsider to find out which rival made the Colossus, before anyone finds out it was them.
- A cultivator has fallen ill from the drain, and her master wants a spirit herb from a grey peak.
- A secret manual has been stolen, and three sects are blaming one another.

## Clans

- [[Clans/The Vanari|The Vanari]] (Ape Clan, led by Grandmaster Hou Shan, the Peach Thief's Heir): A sect of monkey-kin who claim their founder stole a peach of immortality from the mountain gods and was sealed under a peak for it. They are mischievous, loyal and the best climbers on the Hundred Stair.
- [[Clans/The Hundred-Stair Monks|The Hundred-Stair Monks]] (Monk Clan, led by Abbot Wen Jinghai of the Still Flame): The martial monks of Shen-Lu's sects, split into orders devoted to fire, stillness and the staff, who settle doctrine by duel.
- [[Clans/The Brew-Bears|The Brew-Bears]] (Panda Clan, led by Master Brewer Bao Tuan): Shen-Lu's brewers and wandering fighters, beloved at every crossroads.
