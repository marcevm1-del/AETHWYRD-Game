## Overview

Mossdeep is a forest where everything is too big except the people. Ferns grow as tall as houses, mushrooms as wide as wagons and beetles as big as ponies, and the Thimblefolk live among them at the height of a traveller's knee. Visitors drink a shrinking draught at the door of the town's inn and see the world from below. It is charming, cosy and full of small heroism, and it is under attack by something with eight legs.

## Geography

Mossdeep lies in a sheltered valley where warm springs keep the air damp all year. The ground is a thick carpet of moss over rich black soil, and the canopy is mostly giant fern and toadstool rather than trees. Streams that a person could step over are rivers to the Thimblefolk, crossed by bridges of woven grass. A single enormous oak, the Old Shade, stands at the valley's head and blocks the cold north wind.

## Ecology

Mossdeep's giants are mostly gentle. Beetles graze the mushrooms, snails tend the moss and bees the size of sparrows make honey in hollow stems. The Thimblefolk ride beetles, keep aphid herds and trade honey for everything else. The predators are spiders, centipedes and the occasional wandering cat, which the Thimblefolk consider a natural disaster. The giant insects are the reason Mossdeep exists: the warm springs feed a mana-rich soil, and everything that eats from it grows.

## Peoples

The Thimblefolk are a small, hardy, sociable people, ingenious with tools and fond of complicated etiquette. They call everyone else the big folk and are polite to them in the way one is polite to a friendly bear. Beetle riders are their knights and heroes, and every child dreams of riding one. There is a lively debate in the town between those who think the big folk should be asked for help and those who think they would only step on things.

## Power & trade

Thimblefolk Mushroom Town is governed by a council of guild-heads: the honey-keepers, the beetle-riders, the moss-weavers and the brewers. The council meets in a toadstool hall and votes by dropping seeds in cups. Mossdeep's honey, silks and tiny, perfect tools are traded to the big folk at the valley mouth, and Mossdeep honey sells for a fortune in Aldmere.

## Faith & customs

The Thimblefolk keep a festival for each day-god and several for gods of their own, including the Giant who once shared their bread. On the first warm day of spring, they put out a thimble of milk at the foot of the Old Shade. Every house has a pressed-flower calendar, and a family's luck depends on keeping it accurate.

## The Tyrant

Mother Webcrawler, Terror of the Small, is a spider grown huge on the valley's mana-rich soil. She webs the high ferns into a canopy of silk and drops her children on anything that moves beneath it. She is not cruel in any human sense; she is a mother with a very large family, and the Thimblefolk are the right size to feed them. Her nest is in the hollow trunk of a fallen giant fern near the springs.

## Senses

Warm wet moss; honey in the air; the drone of bees as loud as a mill; the rustle of something large in the ferns.

## History

- **Now:** Mother Webcrawler's brood has spread through the ferns, and the beetle riders have lost half their mounts. The town has built thorn walls for the first time in its history.
- **Parents:** Within living memory a big-folk adventurer spent a summer in Mossdeep and taught the Thimblefolk to forge. They still talk about her as if she might come back.
- **Grandparents:** In their grandparents' time the spiders were small and the beetle-riders hunted them for silk.
- **Ancient:** Thimblefolk tell of a giant who once shared their bread and kept the spiders off. They still set out a thimble of milk for him.

## Settlements

- **Thimblefolk Mushroom Town:** A town of hollowed toadstools and grass bridges, home of the Thimble & Thistle inn.

## Landmarks

- **The Old Shade:** A great oak at the valley head; its roots hold the town's honey stores.
- **The Silk Canopy:** The ferns that Mother Webcrawler has webbed over, dim and quiet.
- **The Warm Springs:** Steaming pools where the beetles bathe and the Thimblefolk do their laundry.

## Story hooks

- A beetle rider's mount has been taken alive into the Silk Canopy, and she is going after it with or without help.
- The council wants a big-folk volunteer to carry a beehive to safety, very carefully.
- A Thimblefolk inventor is building a war machine from a snail shell and needs parts.

## Clans

- **The Carapace** (Insecta Clan, led by Queen-Mantis Zirrikh): Insect-folk who live in Mossdeep's ferns and are uneasy allies of the Thimblefolk; Mother Webcrawler hunts them too.
- **The Capfolk** (Mushroom clan, led by Old Puffball Morel): Mushroom-folk who live alongside the Thimblefolk and the beetles; Mycelia is their nightmare cousin.

## Fauna

- **Velvet Bee** (Insect): A sparrow-sized bee with a furry body, the source of Mossdeep honey.
