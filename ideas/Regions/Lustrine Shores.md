## Overview

The Lustrine Shores are beaches of glittering moon-glass, where every tide leaves light behind it. Their people are glass-gatherers, lamp-makers and fishers, and their villages glow at night like scattered stars. The glass began washing in after the red moon rose, and nobody knows where it comes from. Ilyanthe, the Moon Harvester, gathers that light for the red moon.

## Geography

The shores are long beaches of fine sand mixed with fragments of glowing glass, backed by dunes, salt marshes and pine woods. The tides here are higher than anywhere else, pulled by both moons. At low tide, great flats of glass shine for miles. The villages stand on the dunes above the tideline.

## Ecology

Crabs, shorebirds, seals and fish live along the shores, and many of them have begun to glow. Glass-shrimp feed on the light in the moon-glass, and glass-gulls feed on the shrimp. Moon-moths flutter over the flats at night.

## Peoples

The people of the shores are glass-gatherers, lamp-makers, fishers and salt-panners. They are dreamy, superstitious and gentle, and their songs are slow and sad. Lustrine lamps, made of moon-glass, are sold across the world and never need oil.

## Power & trade

The villages are governed by their elders, and the glass-gatherers' guild controls the beaches. Moon-glass, lamps, salt and fish are traded abroad.

## Faith & customs

The people honour the day-gods and the moons, both of them. They believe the glass is the moon's tears, and they leave the largest pieces on the beach as offerings. They do not look at the red moon directly.

## The Tyrant

Ilyanthe, the Moon Harvester, is a vesperite, a creature from the red moon, who descends on the high tides to harvest the light from the moon-glass. She gathers it into a great lantern and carries it back up. She takes the light from people too, and she does not seem to know the difference.

## Senses

Salt and pine; the glitter of glass underfoot; slow sad songs; a red glow on the horizon.

## History

- **Now:** Ilyanthe has begun harvesting the light from the glass, and the beaches are going dark. Gatherers who are on the flats when she comes are found the next morning, pale and empty-eyed.
- **Parents:** Within living memory the first moon-glass washed ashore, and the shores went from poverty to prosperity in a generation.
- **Grandparents:** In their grandparents' time the shores were poor fishing villages, and there was only one moon.
- **Ancient:** The glass began washing in after the red moon rose. The fishers' songs changed key that same year.

## Settlements

- **Lustrine glass beaches:** Villages on the dunes above the glowing beaches.

## Landmarks

- **The Glass Flats:** Miles of glowing glass revealed at low tide.
- **The Lamp Market:** Where Lustrine lamps are made and sold.
- **The Tide Stair:** A stair cut into the dunes where Ilyanthe descends.

## Story hooks

- A glass-gatherer's daughter has lost her light, and her mother wants it back.
- A lamp-maker has made a lamp that glows red, and it is calling to something.
- Scholars want a sample of the light Ilyanthe carries.

## Clans

- **The Lanternwings** (Moth Clan, led by Gatherer Mothwyn Silverdust): They gather the moon-glass by night and are the first to lose their light to Ilyanthe.

## Fauna

- **Lantern Moth-Hawk** (Flyers): A moth-winged raptor that hunts by moon-glass light.
