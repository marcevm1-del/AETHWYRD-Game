> **The Salvage Shore.** Port Goldmere runs on salvage, and the pirate stilt-towns run on what Goldmere misses. The Krakenmother Ys rules the wreck-tide, which is where all the salvage comes from.
>
> *Half the port is built from the timber of one great fleet that sank in a single night.*

## Overview

Goldmere Coast is a warm, wild shore of reefs, wrecks and pirate stilt-towns on the southern sea. Port Goldmere runs on salvage, and the pirate towns run on whatever Goldmere misses. Half the buildings in the port are made from ships, and half the people in it are wanted somewhere else. The wreck-tide that brings the coast its wealth is ruled by the Krakenmother, and she has stopped waiting for ships to wreck on their own.

## Geography

The coast is a maze of coral reefs, sandbars and the skeletons of ships, with a single safe channel into Port Goldmere that changes a little with every storm. A great current, the wreck-tide, sweeps across the southern sea and drops whatever it carries onto Goldmere's reefs: timber, cargo, cannon and, now and then, whole ships with their crews still aboard. The pirate stilt-towns stand on the outer reefs, joined by rope bridges that sway in the trade winds. Inland, low jungle hills hide caves full of things that were not declared at customs.

## Ecology

The reefs are some of the richest waters in the world, full of parrotfish, turtles, rays, sharks and octopus. The wrecks become reefs in their turn, and divers say you can date a wreck by the coral on it. Giant crabs scavenge the shallows, merrows and sea-hags haunt the wrecks, and sea serpents follow the wreck-tide looking for the drowned. Beyond the reef the water drops away to black, and that is where the Krakenmother's brood lives.

## Peoples

Goldmerers are salvagers, divers, shipwrights, sailors, smugglers and gamblers from every coast in the world, and many of them came to Goldmere because the wreck-tide brought them. They are cheerful, quarrelsome, superstitious and generous with anything they did not have to pay for. The pirate towns are surprisingly democratic: captains are elected by their crews and removed by them, sometimes over the side. Sea shanties are sung every night in every tavern, and card sharks work every table.

## Power & trade

Port Goldmere is governed by a council of salvage captains, each of whom holds a claim to a stretch of reef. Salvage law is the only law that matters: whoever first lays a hand on a wreck owns it, and whoever takes it from them had better be quick. The pirate towns are independent, and their relationship with the port is part rivalry, part business partnership. Salvage, rum, fish, pearls and stolen goods flow through the port, and its markets sell things you cannot buy anywhere else.

## Faith & customs

Goldmerers honour all the day-gods but pray to Vex most of all, because every dive is a gamble. Divers make an offering of a coin to the sea before every descent and keep the first thing they find on every wreck as a charm. The dead are buried at sea with their boots on, so they can walk home along the bottom.

## The Tyrant

Krakenmother Ys, Queen of the Wreck-Tide, is a kraken large enough to wrap a galleon. For centuries she let the wreck-tide carry ships to the reef and fed on what the salvagers left. Now she has grown tired of sharing, and she drags ships under before they wreck, keeping the cargo in a great hoard in the deep. Her brood of lesser krakens and sea-hags raids the stilt-towns. She is clever enough to know which ships carry gold.

## Senses

Salt, tar and rum; shanties over clinking glasses; creaking rope bridges; warm water and the smell of rotting timber.

## History

- **Now:** The Krakenmother has begun dragging ships down before they reach the reefs, and taking the salvage with her. The port's income has halved, and the captains are starting to fight among themselves.
- **Parents:** Within living memory Goldmere fought a long, bitter trade war with Lirian, and a generation of sailors grew up hating the canal city.
- **Grandparents:** In their grandparents' time a great fleet sank in a single night on the outer reef, and the timber built half the town.
- **Ancient:** The salvagers say the wreck-tide was a gift from the sea to the first Goldmerers, who saved a drowning sea-queen. The Krakenmother may disagree.

## Settlements

- **Port Goldmere:** The salvage port, a town of ship-timber houses around a deep harbour, home of the Drowned Mermaid inn.
- **Pirate stilt-towns:** Towns on stilts on the outer reefs, joined by rope bridges and governed by elected captains.

## Landmarks

- **The Wreck-Tide:** The great current that brings the coast its salvage.
- **The Sunken Fleet:** The wrecks of the great fleet, now a reef of coral and cannon.
- **The Krakenmother's Hoard:** A trench in the deep water where she keeps what she has taken.

## Story hooks

- A salvager has found a ship drifting on the wreck-tide with no crew and a hot meal on the table.
- A pirate captain wants a truce with the port to fight the Krakenmother together, and both sides want someone neutral to negotiate.
- A map to the sunken fleet's flagship is being auctioned at the Drowned Mermaid, and three bidders are already dead.

## Clans

- **The Shellguard** (Crustacean Clan, led by Champion Kanigawa the Twice-Moulted): Salvage samurai of the outer reefs, sworn to whichever captain feeds them. They moult every few years and are very vulnerable when they do.
- **The Free Captains** (Pirate Clan, led by Captain Anne Saltgrave): Goldmere's elected captains and their crews.
- **The Reef-Sharks** (Shark Clan, led by Commodore Mako Grinwater): Hunters and enforcers of the reefs, hired by both port and pirates.
- **The Drowned Crews** (Undead skeleton pirate Clan, led by Captain Morrin Bleakwater): Crews the wreck-tide brought home dead.

## Fauna

- **Reef Dragon** (Water Base): A long, spiny sea-dragon that nests in the wrecks.
