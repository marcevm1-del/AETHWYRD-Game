## Overview

Velmark is a cluster of wealthy duchies east of Aldmere, a land of white stone towns, tidy fields and well-paid taxes. It is orderly, prosperous and slightly smug, and its people believe hard work and good accounts solve every problem. Something under its foundations disagrees. The great stones on which the duchies were built have woken up, and they are hungry.

## Geography

Velmark is a fertile plain with a bedrock of white limestone, quarried for every town and road in the region. The towns are built of the same stone, and their foundations go deep. Old quarries pit the landscape, many flooded and turned into lakes. In the middle of the duchies stands the Foundation Stone, a single enormous block of stone that the first duke laid as the cornerstone of his realm.

## Ecology

The plains are farmed intensively and the wild has been pushed back to the quarries and hedgerows. Stone golems, earth elementals and burrowing beasts have begun to emerge from the old quarries, and the farmers have taken to sleeping with their tools.

## Peoples

Velmarkers are farmers, stonemasons, clerks, bankers and lawyers, proud of their order and their ledgers. They value thrift, punctuality and good manners, and they look down on the chaotic marches and the extravagant Lirians. A growing underclass of quarry-workers and labourers lives in the shadow of the white towns.

## Power & trade

The duchies are ruled by dukes who inherit their titles, advised by councils of guildmasters and bankers. The dukes answer in theory to a grand duke, who is elected by the others. The economy runs on wheat, stone, banking and law; Velmark lawyers are hired across the world.

## Faith & customs

Velmarkers keep the day-gods with sober piety and dislike displays of feeling. Every house has a tithe-box by its door, and the tithe is collected every Kaelsday. The first duke's tithe to the land, a stone offering laid at the Foundation Stone every year, was stopped generations ago to save money.

## The Tyrant

The Tithe Colossus, Hunger of the Foundation Stones, is a giant of white stone that has risen from beneath the duchies. It was made from the land's own bedrock, and it is collecting what it was promised: stone for stone, a tithe for every block the duchies took. It does not hate the people of Velmark; it only wants what it is owed, and it does not understand that the people are not made of stone.

## Senses

Dust of cut stone; ledger ink; church bells on the hour; the deep grinding of something moving underground.

## History

- **Now:** The Tithe Colossus has risen from the foundations of the oldest duchy and is walking toward the Foundation Stone. Towns crack where it passes.
- **Parents:** Within living memory a duke reformed the tax system and abolished all the old customary tithes, including the stone offering.
- **Grandparents:** In their grandparents' time the duchies quarried so much stone that one whole town collapsed into its own cellars.
- **Ancient:** The first duke paid the land a tithe for its stones. His heirs stopped paying.

## Settlements

- **Velmark ducal towns:** White stone towns of markets, banks and courts, each ruled by a duke.

## Landmarks

- **The Foundation Stone:** The great cornerstone of the realm, where the tithe was once laid.
- **The Drowned Quarries:** Old quarries filled with water, from which stone things have begun to climb.
- **The Grand Ducal Court:** A court of law whose rulings are respected across the world.

## Story hooks

- A clerk has found the original tithe contract, and it has a clause no one remembers.
- A quarry-worker's family has been taken into the earth, and the dukes say it is not their problem.
- The grand duke wants the Colossus stopped quietly, without admitting that the duchies owe anything.
