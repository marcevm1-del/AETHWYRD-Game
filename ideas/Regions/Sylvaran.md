## Overview

Sylvaran is the forest realm of the elves, a land of trees so tall that whole cities hang in their branches. Canopy City Lothaniel is its crown, a city of bridges, halls and gardens high above a forest floor that most elves rarely visit. The kingdom is beautiful, ancient, proud and very slow to admit it needs help. It needs help now: a blight is spreading through the roots, softening bark and minds alike, and it has a name.

## Geography

Sylvaran is a vast temperate rainforest of giant trees, some of them older than any kingdom on the map. The forest floor is dim, damp and spongy, crossed by clear streams and dotted with moonwells, pools of water that glow faintly at night. The canopy cities sit two hundred feet up, joined by living bridges grown over centuries. At the centre of the realm stands the World Tree, whose crown can be seen from three regions away.

## Ecology

The forest is a layered world. The canopy is home to birds, tree-lynx, gliding squirrels and the elves; the middle storey to dryads and owlbears; the floor to fungi, giant beetles and the mushroom-folk. Fungus breaks down the forest's fallen wood and feeds everything else, which is what makes the blight so dangerous: the Spore Lord has turned the fungus itself against the trees.

## Peoples

The elves of Sylvaran are not one people. Court elves of Lothaniel are formal, long-lived and status-conscious; the moonwell hamlets below are home to wood elves who farm, hunt and find the court absurd. Humans, half-elves, gnomes and beastfolk live in the trade quarters, and the court charges them twice as much for everything until they earn its respect. Young elves are increasingly impatient with the old ways.

## Power & trade

The realm is ruled by a hereditary queen and a council of elder houses whose members can be centuries old. Court politics are slow and ruthless, conducted through favours, marriages and very long memories. The economy runs on living wood, moonwell water, herbs and the finest bows in the world, and the court controls all four.

## Faith & customs

Elves honour the day-gods but revere the World Tree above them, and Sylvasday is the realm's holy day. Moonwells are sacred and may not be drunk from without permission. Every elven child plants a seed when they are named, and the tree that grows from it is their twin for life.

## The Tyrant

Mycelia, the Spore Lord, is a vast fungal intelligence that has spread through the root network beneath Sylvaran. It does not hate the elves; it wants to grow, and it has learned that minds are fertile ground. Its spores soften the bark of the great trees and soften the will of those who breathe them, until they wander down to the forest floor to tend it. Its body is everywhere; its heart is in a cave of mushrooms beneath the World Tree's roots.

## Senses

Green light through a hundred feet of leaves; the creak of living bridges; damp earth and sweet resin; a faint, sweet smell of rot.

## History

- **Now:** The Spore Lord's blight is spreading from the forest floor upward, and three moonwell hamlets have been abandoned. The court debates whether to cut the infected trees, and the trees it would cut are people's twins.
- **Parents:** Within living memory the court closed its borders to humans for twenty years after a dispute over a stolen seed. The trade quarters still bear the scars of that time.
- **Grandparents:** In their grandparents' time the elves fought the dwarves of Iron Hold over a river of timber, and the peace treaty is still read aloud each year.
- **Ancient:** The elves remember planting these trees. The trees remember something older that grew there first.

## Settlements

- **Canopy City Lothaniel:** The court city high in the canopy, a maze of halls and bridges grown from living wood, home of Leafhall Rest.
- **Moonwell hamlets:** Wood-elf villages on the forest floor, built around glowing pools, now the first in the blight's path.

## Landmarks

- **The World Tree:** A tree so large it has its own weather; its roots reach places the elves will not discuss.
- **The Twin Groves:** Groves of trees planted by elven children, each one bound to a living elf.
- **The Grey Hollow:** The heart of the blight, a cave of luminous fungus beneath the forest floor.

## Story hooks

- A young elf's twin tree is infected, and he has started sleepwalking toward the forest floor.
- The court wants a human envoy to negotiate with the dwarves for fire, and none of them wants to be seen asking.
- A moonwell has turned from silver to grey, and someone has to find out why before anyone drinks.
