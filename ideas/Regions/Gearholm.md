> **The Clockwork Mountain.** A clockwork city stacked on a mountain, and an Undercity where the steam and the poor collect. Cogmaw the Gremlin Engine King is taking it apart from the bottom up.
>
> *Gearholm's founders built the city's engines and lost the instructions within a generation.*

## Overview

Gearholm is a city of clockwork and steam that climbs the side of a mountain in tiers, where gnome and human inventors have built machines for everything: lifts, rail-carts, looms, pumps, automaton porters and a clock that tells the time in seven cities at once. The upper city is bright, clean and proud of itself. The Undercity, where the steam, the smoke and the poor collect, is dark, loud and forgotten. Something down there has started eating the city from the bottom up.

## Geography

Gearholm is built on the southern face of a mountain, its tiers linked by lifts, funicular railways and spiral stairs. The upper tiers are terraces of workshops, academies and mansions with gardens and glass roofs. The middle tiers are factories and markets. The lower tiers vanish into permanent steam, and below them the Undercity runs deep into the mountain: a maze of tunnels, pipes, boilers and slums built around the Great Engines that power the whole city. No one living has seen all of them.

## Ecology

Pigeons, rats and steam-moths are the city's wildlife, along with the clockwork birds that inventors release for fun and never recover. Gremlins, little mechanical vermin that eat metal and breed inside machines, have always infested the Undercity, and the city has always employed gremlin-catchers to keep them down. Lately the gremlins have stopped behaving like vermin. They are organised, and they are building.

## Peoples

The people of Gearholm are gnomes and humans, with a scattering of dwarves who came for the work and stayed for the arguments. Upper-city folk are inventors, engineers, academics and merchants, brilliant, competitive and a little arrogant. Undercity folk are labourers, boiler-tenders, scavengers and gremlin-catchers, tough, resourceful and bitterly aware of who keeps the city running. Ideas move up the mountain; credit rarely moves down it.

## Power & trade

Gearholm is ruled by a council of guild-masters, one for each great industry, elected by their guilds. The council meets in the Clock Tower and votes by pulling levers. The Undercity has no seat on it. The economy rests on machines, clocks, engines, tools and patents, and Gearholm's patents are worth more than many kingdoms' treasuries.

## Faith & customs

The people honour the day-gods, especially Kael, whom inventors claim as their patron. Undercity folk keep a quieter faith in the Great Engines themselves: they leave oil and bolts at the engine shrines and speak to the machines as if they could hear. A machine is never broken up without a word of thanks.

## The Tyrant

Cogmaw, the Gremlin Engine King, began as an ordinary gremlin that crawled inside one of the Great Engines and never came out. It ate, grew and merged with the engine until it became a single thing: a vast, living machine that thinks. It commands the gremlins as its body commands its gears, and it wants the whole mountain as its body. It is very clever and very hungry, and it has found the founders' lost instructions inside its own engine.

## Senses

Steam, coal and machine oil; the clank and whir of a thousand gears; whistles and bells on every hour; something small and metal scurrying in the dark.

## History

- **Now:** Cogmaw's gremlins have taken the lowest levels of the Undercity and are moving upward, stripping pipes, rails and lifts as they go. The council wants the Undercity sealed off, with its people inside.
- **Parents:** Within living memory a Great Engine exploded and killed hundreds in the Undercity. The council blamed the boiler-tenders, and the boiler-tenders have never forgiven it.
- **Grandparents:** In their grandparents' time the founders' instructions for the Great Engines were lost in a fire, and the city has been maintaining machines it does not fully understand ever since.
- **Ancient:** Gearholm's founders built the city's engines and lost the instructions within a generation.

## Settlements

- **Gearholm Undercity:** The tunnels, boilers and slums beneath the mountain, home of the city's labourers and of the Great Engines.

## Landmarks

- **The Great Engines:** The enormous machines at the mountain's heart that power the whole city.
- **The Clock Tower:** A tower whose clock tells the time in seven cities, where the council meets.
- **The Long Funicular:** A railway that climbs from the lowest tier to the highest, and is the only safe way between them.

## Story hooks

- An upper-city inventor needs a part that exists only in the gremlin-held Undercity, and she will pay a fortune not to go herself.
- A boiler-tender's union wants proof that the council knew about Cogmaw and did nothing.
- A child in the Undercity has befriended a gremlin that says it does not want to be part of the king.

## Clans

- **The Clockborn** (AI Clan, led by Archivist Seven-of-Brass): Gearholm's first automaton porters woke up one winter and asked for names. They live in the middle tiers as a quiet guild of repairers and archivists, still arguing with the city over whether they are citizens or property.
- **The Cog-Touched** (AI Infected Clan, led by Maren Vey, the Half-Wound): People and Clockborn alike whom Cogmaw has begun to absorb. They still remember their names for a while; the Undercity hides the early cases.
