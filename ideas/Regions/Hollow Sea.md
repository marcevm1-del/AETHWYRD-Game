## Overview

The Hollow Sea is a lightless ocean beneath the surface, reached by a great waterfall that pours down from the world above. Boat-towns sail it, steering by the glow of fish and the songs of their navigators. It is a dark, vast, eerie place, and its people love it. The Lumen-Eater swims below with its lure, and every boat-town remembers a town that followed it.

## Geography

The Hollow Sea fills a cavern so vast that its ceiling cannot be seen. The water is cold, still and black, lit only by bioluminescent fish, algae and fungi. Islands of rock and fungus rise from it here and there. The great waterfall that fills it can be heard from miles away.

## Ecology

Glowing fish, jellyfish, eels and squid live in the water, and pale crabs scuttle on the islands. Fungus forests grow on the rocks. The Lumen-Eater hunts in the depths, luring prey with a light that looks like home.

## Peoples

The people of the Hollow Sea are sailors, fishers and navigators, descended from people who came down the waterfall by accident. They are pale, quiet and tough, and they have never seen the sun. They love the dark and pity surface-dwellers.

## Power & trade

Each boat-town is governed by its captain and navigators. Fish, glowing algae, fungi and pearls are traded with the Underworld and, rarely, with the surface.

## Faith & customs

The people honour the day-gods, though they have never seen a day, and the light of the fish, which they believe is the light of their ancestors. They never follow a light they do not know.

## The Tyrant

The Lumen-Eater, the Abyss Angler, is a vast angler fish whose lure glows with a light that looks like whatever its prey most wants to see. Most often it looks like the surface.

## Senses

Cold, still air; the distant roar of the waterfall; the glow of fish under the hull; songs sung in the dark to keep the boats together.

## History

- **Now:** The Lumen-Eater has taken two boat-towns this year. Both followed a light that looked like the surface.
- **Parents:** Within living memory a boat-town tried to sail back up the waterfall and was never seen again.
- **Grandparents:** In their grandparents' time the boat-towns numbered in the dozens.
- **Ancient:** The first boats came down the great waterfall by accident. Their children made a nation of it.

## Settlements

- **Hollow Sea boat-towns:** Floating towns of boats lashed together, sailing the lightless sea.

## Landmarks

- **The Great Waterfall:** The waterfall that feeds the Hollow Sea from the world above.
- **The Glow Gardens:** Fields of glowing algae where the boat-towns fish.
- **The False Light:** A light in the deep that every navigator knows not to follow.

## Story hooks

- A boat-town's navigator has started seeing the sun in her dreams, and the captain wants to know why.
- A survivor from a lost boat-town has been found clinging to a rock.
- A surface-born adventurer wants a guide across the Hollow Sea, and no one will take her.
