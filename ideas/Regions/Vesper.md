## Overview

Vesper is the red moon that hangs over Aethwyrd, and after the war is over it opens to those strong enough to reach it. Its surface is a broken landscape of ruins, crimson dust and floating stone, and its monsters are unlike anything on the world below. Nobody on the surface remembers the moon being red before; everybody remembers it red now. What lies at its heart is the last mystery of the world.

## Geography

From the surface, Vesper is a red disc with darker marks like the outlines of streets. Up close, those marks are exactly that: the ruins of a city, broken and scattered across the moon's surface, some of it drifting in the sky above. The ground is red dust and shattered stone, and the sky is black with stars. The gravity is light, and the silence is complete.

## Ecology

Vesperites, creatures born from the red moon's light, roam the ruins: some delicate, some monstrous, some almost human. The undead, shadows, aberrations, constructs and demons wander among them. Nothing grows here, but the ruins sometimes bloom with flowers of red light.

## Peoples

No one is known to live on Vesper. Those who reach it report that they feel watched, and that the ruins seem to remember them.

## Power & trade

No power is known to hold Vesper.

## Faith & customs

Every faith in Aethwyrd fears the red moon. The Church calls it an omen; the elves, a wound in the sky; the fishers of the Lustrine Shores, a thief of light.

## The Tyrant

Vesper has no Tyrant and no siege; it is the post-game region. What waits there belongs to the end of the story.

## Senses

Silence and red dust; starlight without air; flowers of light opening in the ruins; the feeling that the city remembers you.

## History

- **Now:** After the war, Vesper opens to those who can reach it, and the truth of the red moon waits at its heart.
- **Parents:** Within living memory the red moon's light has drawn vesperites down to the Lustrine Shores.
- **Grandparents:** In their grandparents' time the scholars began to argue about the marks on the red moon's face, and why they looked like streets.
- **Ancient:** Nobody on the surface remembers the moon being red before. Everyone remembers it red now.

## Landmarks

- **The Broken Streets:** Ruins of a city scattered across the moon's surface.
- **The Floating Quarter:** Fragments of the city drifting in the black sky above.
- **The Red Heart:** The centre of the moon, where the light is strongest.

## Story hooks

- A scholar's map of the marks on the red moon matches a city on no map of the world.
- A vesperite has followed an adventurer back to the surface and is trying to find someone.
- The red flowers that bloom in the ruins sing a song that an old woman in Aldmere recognises.
