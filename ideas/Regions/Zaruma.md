## Overview

Zaruma is a kingdom of golden grassland and desert edge strung along the gold road, the great trade route that carries gold north from the mines of the south. Its towns are rich, its markets loud and its caravans heavier than their camels. Its griots keep its history in songs, and its kings are remembered for their generosity. Mansacor, the gold-eating manticore, eats the gold first and then the people carrying it.

## Geography

The northern part of Zaruma is savanna: golden grass, flat-topped acacias and great baobab trees. Further south the grass thins into scrub and then desert, and beyond the desert lie the gold mines in the hills. The gold road runs north-south through the whole kingdom, following a line of wells. Towns stand at the wells, and the largest of them grow into cities of mud-brick towers and great temples of the day-gods.

## Ecology

The savanna supports herds of antelope, zebra, giraffe and elephant, and the predators that follow them: lions, leopards, hyenas and wild dogs. Vultures circle the caravans. The manticore is not native here; it followed the gold road north, and it has learned that the caravans carry the one thing it craves.

## Peoples

The people of Zaruma are traders, caravaneers, miners, farmers, smiths and griots. Griots are the kingdom's historians, genealogists and musicians, and every noble family has one. The people are proud, hospitable and fond of argument. The miners of the south are a separate people with their own language and customs, and they guard the secrets of the mines jealously.

## Power & trade

Zaruma is ruled by a king, chosen from the royal family by a council of elders, who controls the gold road and taxes everything on it. The kings are expected to be generous, and a king who hoards is soon replaced. Gold, salt, ivory, cloth, books and kola nuts are traded on the road. Zaruma's scholars have built great libraries in the cities.

## Faith & customs

The people honour the day-gods and their ancestors, whose stories and names are kept by the griots. Generosity is a sacred duty. The kings make pilgrimages to the holy places of the day-gods and give away gold along the way.

## The Tyrant

Mansacor, the Gold-Eating Manticore, is a monster with a lion's body, a scorpion's tail and a human face twisted into a permanent grin. It has a hunger for gold that it cannot satisfy, and it eats the gold first, then the caravaneers. Its hide has become flecked with gold, and its spines are tipped with it.

## Senses

Red dust and spice; drums and kora under the baobab; the jingle of harness bells; a laugh that sounds almost human.

## History

- **Now:** Mansacor attacks the caravans every week, and merchants are refusing to travel the gold road. The northern cities are running short of gold, and the southern mines of everything else.
- **Parents:** Within living memory a king made a pilgrimage with so much gold that he crashed its price across the world, and the griots still sing about it.
- **Grandparents:** In their grandparents' time the golden mile was found by the manticore and eaten, and the manticore has followed the road ever since.
- **Ancient:** Zaruma's first queen paved a mile of road in gold to shame a rival. The manticore found the mile.

## Settlements

- **Zaruma gold-road towns:** Rich towns of mud-brick towers, libraries and markets at the wells along the gold road.

## Landmarks

- **The Golden Mile:** The stretch of road once paved in gold, now bare and scarred.
- **The Griots' Baobab:** A vast baobab where the griots gather to sing the history of the kingdom.
- **The Southern Mines:** The gold mines in the hills, guarded by the miners' people.

## Story hooks

- A caravan master wants to bait the manticore with gilded lead.
- A griot has forgotten a verse of the kingdom's founding song, and it is the verse that names the manticore's weakness.
- A miner has found a seam of gold that the manticore will not touch.

## Clans

- [[Clans/The Tuskers|The Tuskers]] (Elephant Clan, led by Grand Memory Bhoomrah): Keepers of memory along the gold road, partners to the griots. A Tusker never forgets a debt or a kindness.
- [[Clans/The River-Lords|The River-Lords]] (Hippo Clan, led by Admiral Hippolyte Fordwarden): They control the river fords on the gold road and charge tolls in gold, which makes them Mansacor's next target.
- [[Clans/The Pridelands|The Pridelands]] (Lion Clan, led by Prince Kofi Goldmane): The royal lineage of Zaruma, who rule by generosity and are shamed by the gold road's closing.
