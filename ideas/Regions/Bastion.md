## Overview

Bastion is a city of walls on an open steppe, built in rings, one inside the other, each one raised after the horde broke the last. It is the last stop before the horde lands and the shield of everything behind it. Its people are soldiers, builders and farmers who till the fields between the rings. Thrakka, Sabretooth Khan of the Steppe Horde, rides against the outer ring.

## Geography

The steppe around Bastion is flat, open and windswept, with nothing to stop a cavalry charge. The city sits on a low rise, its rings of walls spreading outward like ripples in a pond. Fields, pastures and villages fill the spaces between the rings, and the oldest, innermost ring encloses the citadel. Beyond the outermost ring, the steppe stretches north to the horde lands.

## Ecology

The steppe supports grass, wildflowers, horses, antelope and wolves, and sabretooth cats, the horde's mounts, hunt there. Within the rings the land is farmed intensively, and every scrap of food is precious during a siege.

## Peoples

The people of Bastion are soldiers, masons, farmers and smiths. They are stoic, practical and fiercely loyal to the city. Every child learns to lay stone and to hold a spear. Their humour is dark, and their songs are about walls.

## Power & trade

Bastion is ruled by a Warden chosen by the captains of the rings. Each ring has its own captain, and the outermost ring's captain is the most honoured and the most likely to die. The city trades grain, horses and stone with the south and depends on it for iron and supplies.

## Faith & customs

The people honour the day-gods and the walls, which they believe hold the spirits of the builders. A stone from the last broken ring is built into each new one.

## The Tyrant

Thrakka, Sabretooth Khan of the Steppe Horde, is a beastkin warlord who rides a giant sabretooth cat at the head of a horde of riders and beasts. He believes the steppe belongs to the horde and that Bastion is a wound in it. He has sworn to break every ring.

## Senses

Stone dust and woodsmoke; the ring of masons' hammers; war drums on the steppe; the low growl of great cats.

## History

- **Now:** Thrakka's horde is besieging the outermost ring, and the masons are already laying the foundations of the next.
- **Parents:** Within living memory the horde broke two rings in a single summer, and the city nearly fell.
- **Grandparents:** In their grandparents' time Bastion had half as many rings, and the city was a fort.
- **Ancient:** Bastion builds a new ring every time the horde breaks the outermost one. There are many rings.

## Settlements

- **Bastion outer ring:** The outermost ring of walls, its villages and fields, the front line of the siege.

## Landmarks

- **The Citadel:** The innermost ring, the oldest part of the city.
- **The Broken Rings:** The ruins of rings the horde has broken, now the foundations of new walls.
- **The Stone of Founding:** A stone from the first wall, built into every new ring.

## Story hooks

- The outermost ring's captain needs scouts to find out how the horde breaks the walls so quickly.
- A mason has found a flaw in the new ring's foundations, and someone put it there.
- A horde rider has deserted and wants to tell the Warden something.
