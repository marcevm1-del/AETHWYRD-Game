## Overview

Lumbaya is a jungle of temple villages under a hot green canopy, where ruined stone stairs climb into the leaves and vanish. The people farm, fish and tend the temples their ancestors built, and they are warm, cheerful and fond of music. At dusk the Nightmother's shapeshifters stop pretending to be people, and every village has started counting its children.

## Geography

Lumbaya is a coastal jungle of tall trees, rivers and mangroves. The ruins of an ancient temple civilisation are everywhere: stepped pyramids, carved terraces and roads paved in stone, half-swallowed by roots. The temple villages are built around the ruins and use the old stones in their walls.

## Ecology

The jungle is home to monkeys, birds, snakes, frogs, big cats and great clouds of insects. Fruit, nuts and medicinal plants grow everywhere, and the rivers are full of fish. The Nightmother's nightshifters live among the people, taking the form of someone familiar.

## Peoples

The people of Lumbaya are farmers, fishers, healers, musicians and temple-keepers. They live in close extended families and share everything. Music is part of every task, and every village has its own songs.

## Power & trade

Each village is led by a council of elders and its temple-keeper. The villages trade fruit, spices, medicines, carvings and rare woods with the coast.

## Faith & customs

The people honour the day-gods and the sky, which the old temples were built to watch. Temple-keepers maintain the old astronomical alignments, though they no longer understand them fully. The people never speak the names of the dead after dark.

## The Tyrant

The Nightmother, Queen of the Nightshifters, is a shapeshifting horror who leads a brood of creatures that take the forms of people. She can wear anyone's face and voice, and she sends her children into villages to replace people one by one. She wants the temples, though nobody knows why.

## Senses

Heat and wet leaves; drums and flutes; overripe fruit; footsteps that stop when you do.

## History

- **Now:** The nightshifters have infiltrated three villages, and people have been found dead with someone they loved standing over them, smiling.
- **Parents:** Within living memory the last temple-keepers who understood the old star alignments died.
- **Grandparents:** In their grandparents' time the temples still watched the sky every night, and the nightshifters never came.
- **Ancient:** Lumbaya's temples were built to watch the sky. Their roofs have all been torn off from the inside.

## Settlements

- **Lumbaya temple villages:** Villages built among the ruins of the old temples, deep in the jungle.

## Landmarks

- **The Sky Temples:** Stepped pyramids built to watch the stars, their roofs torn off from inside.
- **The Root Stair:** A stone stair swallowed by the roots of a giant fig tree.
- **The Night Market:** A market that appears only at night, where the nightshifters trade.

## Story hooks

- A woman believes her husband has been replaced, and the only way to be sure is to ask him something only he would know.
- A temple-keeper wants to restore the old star alignments, believing they kept the nightshifters away.
- A child says she has seen the Nightmother, and that the Nightmother looked like her mother.

## Clans

- **The Mangrove Jaws** (Crocodile Clan, led by Old Mother Sumbaru): They guard the river-mouths and mangroves of Lumbaya and remember the temple-builders as friends. They can smell a nightshifter, which makes them very unpopular with the Nightmother.
- **The Silverbacks** (Gorilla Clan, led by Engineer Obaku Stonepalm): The jungle's engineers and guardians, who rebuilt the temple roads. They can smell fear, and they fear the Nightmother.
- **The Slow Sages** (Sloth Clan, led by Sage Bradyon Who-Waits): Lumbaya's canopy-dwelling sages, who keep the old star-records.
