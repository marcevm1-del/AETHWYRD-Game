> **The Drowned Gold Kingdom.** A drowned underground kingdom of gold, now an explorers' camp at its edge. The Mad Magician who sank it is still performing.
>
> *Aurumdeep was the richest kingdom on any map, until a single show-night sank it.*

## Overview

Aurumdeep was the richest kingdom on any map: a realm of golden halls built into a great cavern beneath the earth, lit by enchanted lamps and fed by underground rivers. Then, on the night of the most famous magic show in history, the whole kingdom sank deeper into the earth, and the rivers poured in after it. Its golden streets lie flooded and dark, full of treasure and of the dead who never left their seats. An explorers' camp clings to the cavern's edge, and every season more treasure-hunters come down the Long Stair. The Mad Magician who sank the kingdom is still performing.

## Geography

The cavern of Aurumdeep is so large that clouds of mist form under its roof. The sunken city fills its floor: palaces, towers, bridges and avenues of gilded stone, flooded to different depths so that some streets are dry, some knee-deep and some lost under fathoms of clear, cold water. Enchanted lamps still burn in many of the halls, so the drowned city glows gold from beneath the surface. The explorers' camp sits on a broad ledge high on the cavern wall, at the foot of the Long Stair that winds up to the surface.

## Ecology

Blind white fish, cave eels and golden-shelled crabs have colonised the flooded streets, feeding on the algae that grows around the lamps. Bats roost under the cavern roof. The dead of Aurumdeep still walk the dry halls in their finest clothes, polite and confused, and the Magician's illusions wander everywhere: painted doves, silk dragons, smiling assistants that dissolve when touched. Nothing here is quite what it looks like, including the gold.

## Peoples

The camp is home to treasure-hunters, divers, scholars, fences, cooks and a handful of descendants of Aurumdeep's survivors, who were outside the kingdom on the night it sank and who consider the treasure-hunters grave-robbers. The explorers are brave, greedy and superstitious, and they trade stories as freely as gold. A diver's reputation rests on how deep she has gone and what she brought back.

## Power & trade

An explorers' council governs the camp and sells diving rights to each district of the sunken city. Claim disputes are settled by the council, or in the water. Gold, gems, relics, books and enchanted lamps flow up the Long Stair, and food, rope, lamps and diving gear flow down it. The survivors' descendants petition every kingdom on the surface to declare Aurumdeep a tomb and close it; none has agreed.

## Faith & customs

Explorers pray to Vex before every dive and leave a coin on the first step of the water for the dead. No one in the camp applauds, ever, for anything. The survivors' descendants hold a silent vigil on the anniversary of the sinking and leave a single empty seat at every table.

## The Tyrant

The Mad Magician of Aurumdeep, Sinker of Kingdoms, was the court magician of the golden kingdom. On the night of his greatest show he promised the king a trick that would make Aurumdeep the richest realm forever, and he performed it, and the kingdom sank. He has been performing ever since to an audience of the dead, waiting for applause that would end the trick. His illusions fill the city, and his greatest trick turns those who watch it into gold. He may be mad, cursed or simply unable to stop.

## Senses

Cold clear water and the glint of gold; the echo of applause that never comes; dripping stone; the smell of old greasepaint and lamp oil.

## History

- **Now:** The Mad Magician's show has started again in the Golden Theatre, and explorers who stay to watch it are found the next day turned to gold, smiling in their seats.
- **Parents:** Within living memory the first explorers found the Long Stair, and the camp grew from a single tent to a town in a decade.
- **Grandparents:** In their grandparents' time the survivors' families still spoke of Aurumdeep as home and refused to say what the Magician had promised the king that night.
- **Ancient:** Aurumdeep was the richest kingdom on any map, until a single show-night sank it.

## Settlements

- **Aurumdeep explorers' camp:** A rough town of tents, huts and cranes on a ledge above the sunken city, at the foot of the Long Stair.

## Landmarks

- **The Golden Theatre:** The theatre where the show is still performed to an audience of the dead.
- **The Sunken Palace:** The king's palace, flooded to its upper floors, where the crown is said to still sit on the throne.
- **The Long Stair:** The winding stair from the surface, with a memorial shrine at every hundredth step.

## Story hooks

- An explorer has been turned to gold in his seat at the theatre, and his partner wants him back before the fences carve him up.
- A survivor's descendant wants to find out what trick the Magician promised, and believes the answer is in the theatre's prompt-book.
- A diver claims to have seen the king alive in the Sunken Palace, sitting on his throne, waiting for the show to end.

## Clans

- **The Gilded** (Golden Clan, led by Last Steward Aurelion Vane-Gold): Descendants of Aurumdeep's survivors, sworn to guard their drowned kingdom from treasure-hunters.
