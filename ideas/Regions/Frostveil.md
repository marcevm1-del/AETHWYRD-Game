## Overview

Frostveil is the far north, a land of fjords, glaciers and longhouses where the sun barely clears the peaks in winter and barely sets in summer. Its people are sailors, hunters and skalds, as hard as the land and as warm as the fires they never let die. A frost giant king has claimed every stead his breath can reach, and his breath reaches farther each year.

## Geography

Deep fjords cut into black mountains capped with glaciers, and the coast is a maze of islands and skerries. Pine forests cling to the lower slopes, and tundra stretches inland. The great hold of Skjaldheim stands at the head of the longest fjord, around a hall whose fire has burned for six hundred years. In the far north the sky sometimes seems to crack, and the people call it the Shattered Sky.

## Ecology

Reindeer, wolves, bears, seals and walrus live here, and the fjords are rich with fish and whales. Frost trolls, ice wolves and frost giants live in the mountains, and in hard winters they come down to the coast. The people survive by hunting, fishing and storing everything they can for the dark months.

## Peoples

The people of Frostveil are humans, half-giants and a few dwarves, organised into clans led by jarls. They value courage, loyalty, hospitality and a good story above everything else. Skalds are honoured as historians, lawgivers and entertainers, and a good saga can make or break a reputation.

## Power & trade

The jarls meet at the Thing, an annual assembly at Skjaldheim, where laws are made and disputes settled. A king is elected only in times of war, and only for its duration. Fish, furs, ivory, amber and whale-oil are traded south, and the longships of Frostveil are the best in the world.

## Faith & customs

The people honour the day-gods and the fire, which they believe holds the souls of their ancestors. Letting a hearth fire die is a crime, and the fire at Skjaldheim has burned for six hundred years. The dead are sent to sea in burning ships.

## The Tyrant

Ymgrir, the Frost Sovereign, is a frost giant king from the high glaciers who has claimed Frostveil as his own. His breath freezes the sea, and wherever his breath reaches the winter never ends. He believes the long winter is the true state of the world, and that the summer the skalds sing of was a mistake. He wants to bring the winter back to everywhere.

## Senses

Woodsmoke and salted fish; the crack of ice; skalds singing in the long dark; a cold so sharp it smells of iron.

## History

- **Now:** Ymgrir has frozen the outer fjords, and the fishing fleets are trapped. The jarls have called the Thing early and are debating whether to elect a war-king.
- **Parents:** Within living memory a fleet sailed north to find the source of the Shattered Sky and never came back.
- **Grandparents:** In their grandparents' time Frostveil fought a long war against the frost giants and won, at the cost of half its jarls.
- **Ancient:** The skalds of Skjaldheim sing of a summer that lasted a hundred years. No one living has seen one.

## Settlements

- **Skjaldheim:** The great hold at the head of the longest fjord, home of the Last Fire inn.
- **Longhouse steads:** Clan farms of long turf-roofed houses scattered along the fjords.

## Landmarks

- **The Last Fire:** The hearth at Skjaldheim that has burned for six hundred years.
- **The Shattered Sky:** A place in the far north where the sky seems to crack.
- **The Frozen Fleet:** The fishing fleet trapped in the ice of the outer fjords.

## Story hooks

- The keeper of the Last Fire has run out of the sacred wood, and the nearest grove is in Ymgrir's territory.
- A survivor of the fleet that sailed north has come back, older than he should be.
- A skald needs a new saga before the Thing, and she wants to write it about someone who is still alive.
