> **The Lantern Canals.** A city of canals, lanterns and bridges where songs float up from the water at dusk. Those songs belong to the Drowned Choir, and the people who follow them into the canals are never seen again.
>
> *Lirian was built over a flooded older Lirian. On quiet nights you can see its rooftops under the boats.*

## Overview

Lirian is a coastal republic of canal cities, built on islands in a lagoon and joined by bridges, gondolas and lantern-lit waterways. It is rich, artistic and cheerfully corrupt, the place where the world's merchants come to make deals and the world's artists come to spend the proceeds. In the evening songs float up from the water. Lately some of the songs have been coming from beneath it, and those who follow them do not come back.

## Geography

The lagoon is shallow, brackish and protected from the sea by a long sandbar with three gaps that the city controls. The cities stand on thousands of oak piles driven into the mud, and the canals between them rise and fall with the tide. Beneath the modern city lies an older Lirian, drowned when the sea rose long ago, and on clear calm nights its rooftops can be seen from the boats.

## Ecology

The lagoon teems with fish, shellfish and seabirds, and the canals with rats, cats and gulls. Merrows, crabs and sea-eels hunt the outer channels. The drowned city has become a reef, colonised by coral, octopus and stranger things, and it is from there that the Drowned Choir sings.

## Peoples

Lirians are merchants, gondoliers, glassblowers, bankers, painters and spies, and many are several of those at once. They love masks, music and gossip, and they judge one another by wit rather than birth. A large population of sea-elves and merfolk lives in the outer lagoon and trades with the city through water-gates.

## Power & trade

Lirian is a republic ruled by a doge elected for life by a council of merchant houses. Elections are expensive and occasionally fatal. The city's wealth comes from trade, glass, banking and the tolls on the sandbar gaps; its fleet is small but very fast.

## Faith & customs

Lirians keep the day-gods with gusto and the sea with caution. Every gondola carries a lantern, and on Maelsday the whole city lights its lanterns at once. A coin is thrown into the canal for every deal made, which keeps the city's divers rich.

## The Tyrant

The Drowned Choir is a chorus of sirens who nest in the drowned city's bell towers. Their song is a lure and a hunger: those who hear it long enough want to go down to the old city and join it. The sirens claim the drowned city as theirs and the living city as their larder. They are led by a voice that no one has seen, and the lantern-galleys of the old war were built to fight them.

## Senses

Salt and lamp oil; bells over water; fried fish and sugared almonds; a song you cannot quite hear.

## History

- **Now:** The Drowned Choir's song is heard every week now, and people walk into the canals smiling. The city has banned singing after dusk, which has not helped.
- **Parents:** Within living memory a doge tried to drain part of the lagoon to build a new district, and the sea took it back in a single storm.
- **Grandparents:** In their grandparents' time Lirian fought a long trade war with Goldmere's pirates, and its old lantern-galleys still hang in the arsenal.
- **Ancient:** Lirian was built over a flooded older Lirian. On quiet nights you can see its rooftops under the boats.

## Settlements

- **Lirian canal cities:** A cluster of island cities joined by bridges and canals, with the doge's palace at their centre.

## Landmarks

- **The Doge's Palace:** A palace of pink marble on the main canal, half government and half theatre.
- **The Drowned Belltowers:** The towers of old Lirian, rising almost to the surface at low tide.
- **The Glass Isle:** An island of furnaces where the city's famous glass is blown.

## Story hooks

- A famous singer has lost her voice, and she thinks the Drowned Choir took it.
- The doge wants an outsider to escort a sealed chest past the sandbar at night.
- A gondolier has started sleepwalking to the edge of the canal, humming.

## Clans

- **The Lagoon Folk** (Merfolk Clan, led by Queen Corallin of the Water-Gates): The sea-elves and merfolk of Lirian's outer lagoon, trading through water-gates.
- **The Canal Otters** (Otter Clan, led by Gondolier Pip Slipwater): Lirian's gondoliers and divers, cheerful and nosy.
