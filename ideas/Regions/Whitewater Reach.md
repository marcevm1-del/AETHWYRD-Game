## Overview

Whitewater Reach is a frozen coast of white water, white ice and fishing villages built on both. Its people fish through holes in the ice in winter and from boats in the brief summer, and they marry the sea before they marry each other. A leviathan with jaws of ice has begun breaking the floes from below.

## Geography

The coast is a maze of ice floes, frozen fjords and rocky islands, battered by storms. In winter the sea freezes for miles out, and the villages move onto the ice. In summer the ice breaks up and the villages move back to the rocks. Great icebergs drift past, some carrying the bones of ancient creatures.

## Ecology

Seals, walrus, whales, polar bears and enormous fish live in these waters, and the people hunt and fish all of them. Ice wyrms and frost serpents hunt in the deep, and the leviathan hunts everything.

## Peoples

The people of the Reach are fishers, hunters and boat-builders, small, tough and stubborn. They are deeply superstitious about the sea and deeply practical about everything else. Every child learns to swim in icy water and to read the ice before they can read words.

## Power & trade

Each village is governed by its elders and its best fisher. Disputes are settled by contests of skill. Fish, oil, ivory and furs are traded south for grain, iron and wood.

## Faith & customs

The people honour the day-gods and the sea, which they consider a jealous spouse. Every fisher is married to the sea in a ceremony at sixteen, before any human marriage. Offerings of fish and blood are made before every voyage.

## The Tyrant

Ysgrath, the Ice-Jaw Leviathan, is a vast sea serpent with jaws of living ice. It breaks the floes from below and swallows whatever falls in: seals, boats, villages. It is drawn to the vibrations of people walking on the ice, and it has learned that villages mean food.

## Senses

Salt and fish oil; the boom of ice cracking; wind like knives; the smell of seal blubber burning.

## History

- **Now:** Ysgrath has broken the winter ice under three villages, and the people are afraid to go out on the floes.
- **Parents:** Within living memory a great storm froze a whole fishing fleet in place, and the crews survived by eating their boats.
- **Grandparents:** In their grandparents' time the leviathan was a story to frighten children.
- **Ancient:** Fishers here marry the sea first and their spouses second. It is in the vows.

## Settlements

- **Ice-fishing villages:** Villages that move between the rocks and the ice with the seasons.

## Landmarks

- **The Bone Bergs:** Icebergs carrying the frozen bones of ancient creatures.
- **The Marriage Rock:** A rock where every fisher is married to the sea.
- **The Broken Floe:** A field of shattered ice where the leviathan surfaced.

## Story hooks

- A fisher wants to divorce the sea, and the elders say it has never been done.
- A Bone Berg is drifting toward a village, and something inside it is moving.
- The villages need a way to cross the ice without waking the leviathan.
