## Overview

Dragon Spine is a long ridge of volcanic peaks that runs along what the monks say is the backbone of a sleeping dragon. Great white bones break through the black rock along its length, and the monasteries of the dragon-monks are built into them: halls in ribs, libraries in vertebrae, a temple in a skull. Pilgrims come from every land to pray there. The drakes of the peaks have a lord now, and he wants the bones back.

## Geography

The range is a ridge of black basalt peaks, some still active, with rivers of lava creeping down their flanks and steaming vents along the valleys. The bones rise from the rock in a line that runs the length of the range, each one as big as a tower, and the soil around them is strangely fertile. The Bone Road, a pilgrim path cut along the crest, links the monasteries from end to end. At the northern end stands the Skull, the greatest monastery of all.

## Ecology

Drakes, wingless cousins of the true dragons, nest on the hottest peaks and hunt goats, boars and anything slow. Wyverns roost in the cliffs, and fire salamanders swim in the lava. Hardy pines, fire-lilies and lichens grow in the volcanic soil around the bones, which give off a faint warmth even in winter. The drakes are territorial and grow larger the closer they nest to the bones, which the monks find worrying.

## Peoples

The dragon-monks are scholars, healers and keepers of the bones, sworn to study dragons and keep the sleeper asleep. They come from every land and give up their old names when they take their vows. Pilgrims, miners and the families who serve the monasteries make up the rest of the population. Some miners have been caught chipping relics from the bones, which fetch a fortune in the lowland markets.

## Power & trade

The monasteries govern the range jointly through an abbots' council that meets in the Skull. Pilgrims bring gold and offerings, and the monasteries trade medicines, books and fire-lily oil. The black market in dragon-bone relics is the monks' greatest shame and the miners' greatest temptation.

## Faith & customs

The monks honour the day-gods and the sleeping dragon, whom they believe is neither dead nor a god but simply resting. Their daily prayers are lullabies. Any sound loud enough to echo off the bones is forbidden within the monastery walls, and visitors are asked to remove their boots.

## The Tyrant

Vyrmax, the Drake Lord, is the largest drake ever recorded, bigger than many true dragons. He believes the bones are those of his greatest ancestor and that the monks are grave-robbers who have defiled them for a thousand years. He wants the bones returned to the drakes and the monasteries torn down. He is proud and furious but not stupid, and he might be reasoned with by anyone who can prove what the bones really are.

## Senses

Sulphur and incense; lullabies sung in deep voices; the warmth of the rock underfoot; the feeling of something breathing far below.

## History

- **Now:** Vyrmax the Drake Lord has attacked three monasteries and demands that the monks give up the bones. The abbots' council is split between those who would fight and those who would bargain.
- **Parents:** Within living memory a monk tried to wake the dragon with a ritual he found in an old book, and was burned alive by a fire that came out of the rock.
- **Grandparents:** In their grandparents' time the drakes were few and small and feared the monks, and pilgrims walked the Bone Road without guards.
- **Ancient:** The monks say the mountain was a dragon who lay down to rest. They pray that it stays asleep.

## Settlements

- **Dragon-bone monasteries:** Monasteries built into the great bones along the Bone Road, with the Skull at their head.

## Landmarks

- **The Skull:** The greatest monastery, built inside a dragon skull large enough to hold a town.
- **The Burning Peaks:** Active volcanoes at the southern end of the range, where Vyrmax nests.
- **The Bone Road:** The pilgrim path along the crest, linking every monastery.

## Story hooks

- A young monk believes the bones are not dragon bones at all, and she wants proof before the council decides to fight.
- Vyrmax has offered peace in exchange for a single bone, and the abbots want someone to find out which one he means and why.
- A pilgrim has stolen a relic from the Skull, and the bone has started to grow warm in her pack.

## Clans

- **The Scaleborn** (Dragonkin Clan, led by Brother Pyrrin Emberscale): Lay brothers and sisters of the dragon-monasteries who believe they hatched from the sleeper's dreams. Vyrmax calls them traitors to the drakes.
- **The Wyvern Oath** (Dragon/wyvern Rider Clan, led by Rider-Abbess Calyx Thornwing): An order who raise wyverns from eggs in the monastery hatcheries. (Canon: the player cannot ride flying mounts, so riders are NPCs and allies, never mounts the player controls.)
