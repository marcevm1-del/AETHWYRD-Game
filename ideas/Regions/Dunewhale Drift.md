## Overview

Dunewhale Drift is a sea of sand, and on it swims Thalassa, a sand-whale as big as a hill, with a whole town lashed to her back. The town moves wherever she swims, and it has been moving for longer than anyone can remember. The inn sways, the streets roll and first-time visitors get seasick. Glassmaw, the worm of the endless dune, hunts them both.

## Geography

The Drift is a deep desert of fine, soft sand that flows almost like water, and the creatures that live there swim through it rather than walk on it. The dunes rise and fall like waves, and storms raise sand-spray higher than towers. The town on Thalassa's back is built of wood, rope and canvas, its houses lashed to her hide by cables hooked into her barnacles. Glassmaw leaves trails of glass behind it, the only fixed landmarks in the Drift.

## Ecology

The Drift has its own sea life: sand-whales, sand-sharks, sand-rays, burrowing crabs and swarms of glittering sand-fish. The whales filter the sand-fish through baleen like their ocean cousins. Glassmaw hunts the whales. There were once many sand-whales; now Thalassa is the last anyone knows of.

## Peoples

The people of the Drift are born on Thalassa's back and live their whole lives there. They are sailors, traders, whale-singers and sand-fishers. They are proud, close and suspicious of land-dwellers, whom they find boring. The whale-singers can speak to Thalassa and guide her, and they are the most respected people in the town.

## Power & trade

The town is governed by a captain elected by its people and advised by the whale-singers. When Thalassa surfaces near an oasis or a city, the town trades sand-fish, whale-oil, glass and news for water, food and timber.

## Faith & customs

The people honour the day-gods and Thalassa, whom they think of as their mother. Every child's birth is announced to her by the whale-singers, and every death is sung into her ear before the body is given to the sand.

## The Tyrant

Glassmaw, the Worm of the Endless Dune, is a sandworm of incredible size with a mouth full of teeth like broken glass. Wherever it passes, it leaves a trail of glass behind it. It hunts the sand-whales, and it has killed every one of them except Thalassa.

## Senses

The slow sway of the streets; whale-song through the floorboards; sand-spray on the wind; the hiss of something burrowing far below.

## History

- **Now:** Glassmaw has been following Thalassa for a month, and she is swimming faster and singing less. The whale-singers think she is afraid.
- **Parents:** Within living memory Thalassa swam all the way to the sea and back, and the town saw the ocean for the first time.
- **Grandparents:** In their grandparents' time there were three sand-whale towns, and they met every year for a great festival. The other two whales were lost to Glassmaw.
- **Ancient:** Thalassa's riders keep a calendar of her songs. She has been singing less.

## Settlements

- **The town on Thalassa's back:** A town of rope, timber and canvas lashed to the hide of a sand-whale, home of the Whale's Belly inn.

## Landmarks

- **Thalassa's Crown:** The highest point of her back, where the whale-singers sing to her.
- **The Glass Trails:** The trails Glassmaw leaves behind, the only fixed landmarks in the Drift.
- **The Whale Graveyard:** Where the bones of the other sand-whales lie.

## Story hooks

- Thalassa is sick, and the whale-singers need a herb from an oasis she refuses to approach.
- Sand pirates have been shadowing the town, waiting for Glassmaw to weaken it.
- Glassmaw has been seen near the whale graveyard, and the whale-singers think it is waiting for Thalassa.

## Clans

- **The Plateback** (Pangolin Clan, led by Hide-Warden Pango Scalehold): Riders of Thalassa who patch her hide and polish her barnacles.
- **The Whale-Singers** (Whale Clan, led by First Singer Oruna-of-the-Deep-Note): The singers who speak to Thalassa.

## Fauna

- **Plated Crawler** (Insect): An armoured centipede that burrows in Thalassa's hide.
