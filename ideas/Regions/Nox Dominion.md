## Overview

Nox Dominion is the volcanic heartland of the demon realms, a land of black rock, red rivers and ruined fortresses under a sky that is always dark. It is the seat of the Demon Overlord's power and the end of every hero's road. It is also, to the surprise of everyone who reaches it, a place where ordinary demons live ordinary lives: farmers, merchants, innkeepers and children, who fear the war as much as anyone. No Tyrant holds it, because the Dominion answers to someone far worse.

## Geography

The Dominion is a land of active volcanoes, lava rivers, ash plains and obsidian cliffs, under clouds of smoke that block the sun. The ruins of ancient fortresses, many built by armies that came to conquer it, stand on every ridge. At its centre stands the Overlord's citadel, a fortress of black stone on a mountain of fire. Border towns cling to the edges where the land is gentler.

## Ecology

Fire-adapted plants and fungi grow in the ash, and demon livestock graze on them. Hellhounds, salamanders, fire drakes and stranger creatures live in the wilds. The demons farm, hunt and trade like any other people.

## Peoples

The demons of Nox Dominion are as varied as any people: soldiers, farmers, merchants, artisans and scholars, cruel and kind, loyal and rebellious. Many fear the Overlord and his Generals, and some quietly oppose them. Human prisoners, deserters and traders also live in the border towns.

## Power & trade

The Dominion is ruled by the Demon Overlord and his five Generals, each commanding a province and an army. Below them, demon nobles govern the towns. The economy rests on obsidian, sulphur, fire-crystals and war.

## Faith & customs

The demons have their own gods and their own stories, and many of them honour the Overlord as a saviour. Some still pray to the day-gods in secret.

## The Tyrant

Nox Dominion has no Tyrant and no siege; it is a story region. Its masters are the Demon Overlord and his Generals, and their story is told elsewhere.

## Senses

Sulphur and ash; the glow of lava rivers; the clang of forges; a sky that never brightens.

## History

- **Now:** The war is reaching its climax, and the Dominion's armies are on the march. The border towns are full of refugees from both sides.
- **Parents:** Within living memory the Lantern Seal held the Overlord, and the Dominion knew a generation of uneasy peace.
- **Grandparents:** In their grandparents' time the heroes of past ages marched here, and the ruins still bear their marks.
- **Ancient:** The heroes of past ages marched here. The ruins remember them, and so do the statues.

## Landmarks

- **The Overlord's Citadel:** A fortress of black stone on a mountain of fire.
- **The Hero's Road:** A path of ruined fortresses left by past heroes' campaigns.
- **The Border Towns:** Towns at the Dominion's edge where demons and humans trade, home of the Crimson Rest.
