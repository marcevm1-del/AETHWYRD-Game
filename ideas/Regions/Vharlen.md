## Overview

Vharlen is the heart of an empire that has spent its whole history facing the demon frontier. Its capital is grand, cold and full of spies, its plains are drilled with legions and its every citizen knows the sound of the alarm horns. It is the most powerful state in the world and the most anxious. Morvane, Vanguard of the Overlord, has come to test its walls, and the legions are not sure they will hold.

## Geography

Vharlen occupies the high plains north-west of the continent's heart, bordered by mountains to the north and the demon lands beyond them. The plains are cut by military roads and dotted with forts, watchtowers and garrison towns. The capital stands on a terrace above a great river, a city of grey stone towers, broad avenues and imperial palaces. The frontier forts stand in the passes of the northern mountains.

## Ecology

The plains are farmed intensively to feed the legions, and the wild has been pushed back to the mountains. Wolves, bears and mountain cats live there, and demons come through the passes. Warhorses are bred on the plains and are the finest in the world.

## Peoples

The people of Vharlen are soldiers, farmers, bureaucrats and spies. Every citizen serves in the legions for a period, and military rank shapes social standing. They are disciplined, proud and suspicious, with a dry, dark humour. The capital is cosmopolitan and full of diplomats, merchants and informants from every nation.

## Power & trade

Vharlen is ruled by an emperor, advised by a senate of generals and nobles, and administered by a vast bureaucracy. Succession is by appointment: each emperor names an heir from among the generals. The economy rests on grain, horses, iron and the business of war. The imperial spy service is the most feared in the world.

## Faith & customs

The people honour the day-gods with military discipline, and the Church is powerful in the capital. Every legion carries a shrine. A pledge sworn by the founders of the empire is carved under the barracks of the capital, and every new recruit swears it.

## The Tyrant

Morvane, Vanguard of the Overlord, is a demon general who leads the advance guard of the demon armies. He is disciplined, tactical and relentless, and he respects Vharlen as a worthy enemy. He wants to break the empire's legions and open the road to the south.

## Senses

Polished steel and horse sweat; marching drums; ink and wax in the ministries; the alarm horn's long note.

## History

- **Now:** Morvane's legions have struck the frontier forts, and two have fallen. The emperor has called up the reserves, and the capital is full of soldiers and rumours.
- **Parents:** Within living memory Vharlen fought a long war with the demons and won, at a terrible cost, and the frontier forts were rebuilt.
- **Grandparents:** In their grandparents' time Vharlen held the line against the Demon Overlord's armies, and the pledge carved under the barracks was the price.
- **Ancient:** Vharlen held the line the last time. The pledge it took to do so is carved under the barracks.

## Settlements

## Landmarks

- **The Imperial Capital:** A city of grey towers, palaces and spies on a terrace above the river.
- **The Frontier Forts:** Forts in the northern passes, facing the demon lands.
- **The Barracks Pledge:** The founders' oath carved under the capital's barracks.
