## Overview

The True-Name Isles are a scattering of small islands far out in the eastern sea, where every child sails out on a Naming Voyage and comes home with a true name given by the sea. A true name is a person's deepest self, and those who know it can call them across any distance. The islanders are calm, thoughtful and deeply private. The Nameless Tide is a serpent no one has named, and it has begun taking names for itself.

## Geography

The isles are low, rocky islands of white sand and wind-bent trees, spread across a wide stretch of ocean. The waters between them are clear and calm, except at the Naming Rocks, a ring of stone pillars in the open sea where the currents meet. The harbour of the Naming Voyage stands on the largest island.

## Ecology

Seabirds, turtles, dolphins and whales live around the isles, and the reefs are full of colourful fish. Sea serpents, merfolk and sea spirits live in the deeper waters. The sea itself seems to have a will here, and the islanders say it listens.

## Peoples

The islanders are fishers, sailors, navigators and namers. They are quiet, patient and careful with words. Everyone has a common name and a true name, and they share their true name only with those they love most.

## Power & trade

The isles are governed by a council of namers, who guide the Naming Voyages and interpret the sea. Fish, pearls, navigation charts and naming-services are the islands' trade; people from other lands pay to have their children named.

## Faith & customs

The islanders honour the day-gods and the sea, which gives the names. A child who returns from the Naming Voyage without a name is considered unfinished and may try again the next year.

## The Tyrant

The Nameless Tide is a serpent without a name, born in the deep where the sea gives names. It has no self, and it wants one. It takes the true names of those on their Naming Voyages, trying each one on, but none of them fit.

## Senses

Calm water and white sand; wind in the trees; whispers that sound like names; a silence at the Naming Rocks.

## History

- **Now:** The Nameless Tide has begun taking the true names of children on their Naming Voyages, and they return empty, unable to remember who they are. The council has suspended the voyages.
- **Parents:** Within living memory a child returned from the Naming Voyage with a name that no one could pronounce, and became the greatest navigator of her age.
- **Grandparents:** In their grandparents' time the sea gave every name it was asked for.
- **Ancient:** Names here are given by the sea. The sea has started keeping some of them.

## Settlements

- **Naming Voyage harbor:** The harbour on the largest island, where the Naming Voyages begin.

## Landmarks

- **The Naming Rocks:** A ring of stone pillars in the open sea where names are given.
- **The Hall of Names:** Where the council keeps the records of every name given.
- **The Silent Reef:** A reef where nameless children are said to wander.

## Story hooks

- A child has returned from her Naming Voyage empty, and her parents want her name back.
- The council wants someone to give the Nameless Tide a name, and they have a theory about how.
- A navigator claims to have found the place where the sea keeps the names it has taken.

## Clans

- **The Starbodied** (Ethereal clan, led by Little Ellowen-of-the-Long-Name): Children whose Naming Voyage returned them with a name too large for one body. They are calm, kind and slightly far away.
- **The Raywings** (Manta clan, led by Current-Mother Maraelle): Sea-people who carry the Naming Voyagers through the currents to the Naming Rocks.

## Fauna

- **Turtle Drake** (Water Base): A shelled sea-drake that carries islands of coral on its back.
