## Overview

Cinderhole is a city built inside a smoking crater, in caves that are never dark and never clean. It was a mine until the coal seams caught fire and never stopped burning, and the people who stayed learned to live in the heat, the smoke and the endless glow. It is a city of smugglers, alchemists, refugees and fortune-seekers, where anything can be bought and everyone is hiding something. Cinderlord Vasque rules the smoke, and everything sold through it.

## Geography

The crater is a vast pit in a range of barren hills, wreathed in smoke that rises day and night. Fires burn in the coal seams beneath it, and vents in the rock leak heat and fumes. The city is built into the crater's walls and the caves behind them, terraces and tunnels lit by the glow of the fires. Great chimneys carved into the rock carry the worst of the smoke away.

## Ecology

Little lives in the crater except fire beetles, ash moths, smoke bats and the people. Fire elementals and smoke spirits drift up from the burning seams. Food, water and nearly everything else must be brought in by caravan.

## Peoples

The people of Cinderhole are miners, alchemists, smugglers, mercenaries and refugees from every land who have nowhere else to go. They are tough, wary and fiercely independent. Smoke-masks are worn by everyone, and a person's mask says more about them than their face.

## Power & trade

Cinderhole is ruled by the Cinderlord, who controls the smoke vents and the chimneys and therefore who can breathe. Under him, gangs and guilds compete for territory. The economy rests on coal, alchemy, smuggling and the black market, and Cinderhole is the place to buy anything that cannot be bought elsewhere.

## Faith & customs

The people honour the day-gods quietly and the fire openly. They believe the burning seams are a punishment for the old miners' greed, and that the fire will go out only when the debt is paid.

## The Tyrant

Cinderlord Vasque, the Smoke Mage, is a human mage who has mastered the smoke and fire of the crater. He controls the vents and the chimneys with his magic, and he can choke a district or burn it at will. He sees himself as a ruler who keeps order in a city that would otherwise tear itself apart. He is not wrong about the city, and he is utterly wrong about himself.

## Senses

Smoke that stings the eyes; the roar of the fires below; sweat and ash; the hiss of breath through a mask.

## History

- **Now:** Vasque has begun closing chimneys to punish districts that resist him, and people are choking in their homes.
- **Parents:** Within living memory a great fire-storm swept the crater and killed thousands, and Vasque rose to power promising it would never happen again.
- **Grandparents:** In their grandparents' time Cinderhole was a mine, and the miners dug too deep.
- **Ancient:** Cinderhole was a mine until it caught fire. It never stopped burning, so people moved in.

## Settlements

- **Cinderhole:** The city in the smoking crater, built into its walls and the caves behind them.

## Landmarks

- **The Great Chimneys:** Chimneys carved into the crater wall to carry the smoke away.
- **The Burning Seams:** The coal seams that have burned for generations.
- **The Mask Market:** A market where smoke-masks are sold, traded and stolen.

## Story hooks

- A district's chimney has been closed, and its people need someone to open it.
- An alchemist claims to have a way to put out the burning seams, and every gang in the city wants her dead.
- A refugee family wants to get out of the crater, and Vasque has forbidden it.

## Clans

- **The Undercity Whiskers** (Mouse/Rat Clan, led by Mask-Maker Skritch Velvetnose): Rat-folk of Cinderhole's lowest tunnels, smugglers and mask-makers.
- **The Velvet Knives** (Panthar Clan, led by The Velvet Mother, Noirelle): Cinderhole's quietest guild, who sell secrets to Vasque and his enemies alike.
