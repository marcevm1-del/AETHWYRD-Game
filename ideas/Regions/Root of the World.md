> *Legends call it the place the gods knelt down to weave.*

## Overview

The Root of the World is the deepest place there is, far below the Underworld's other layers. Here vast roots of light and stone rise out of the darkness and hold up everything above: the Underworld, the surface, the seas and the sky. The air hums, the stone glows and the monsters are among the oldest and strongest in existence. No one lives here. Very few have ever come here and returned. Legends call it the place the gods knelt down to weave.

## Geography

The Root is a cavern without visible walls or floor, crossed by immense pillars that seem to be both roots and stone, threaded with veins of light. Paths of rock wind between them, bridges of crystal span gulfs of nothing and islands of earth float in the dark. The light in the roots pulses slowly, like a heartbeat. Some of the roots are cracked, and light leaks from them like sap.

## Ecology

Only elementals, aberrations, living plants, constructs, dragons and slimes survive here, the creatures that need no sun and can feed on raw mana. They cluster around the roots and drink the light that leaks from them. Some are so old that they seem to have forgotten what they were.

## Peoples

No one lives at the Root. A few of the world's greatest adventurers have reached it, and they speak of it rarely and always with reverence.

## Power & trade

No power holds the Root. It belongs to whatever made the world.

## Faith & customs

Every faith in Aetheria has a story about the Root. The Church calls it the foundation stone of creation; the elves, the root of the World Tree; the dwarves, the first anvil. All of them agree that it should not be damaged, and that the cracks are a bad sign.

## The Tyrant

The Root of the World has no Tyrant and no siege; it is the Underworld's deepest layer and holds no settlements. Its monsters are some of the oldest beings in creation.

## Senses

A deep hum felt in the bones; the slow pulse of light; air like the inside of a bell; absolute, ancient stillness.

## History

- **Now:** Adventurers who have reached the Root report that the cracks in the roots are widening and that the light leaking from them is growing brighter.
- **Parents:** Within living memory a party reached the Root for the first time in a century and brought back a shard of root-light that still glows in the Church's treasury.
- **Grandparents:** In their grandparents' time the Root was thought to be a metaphor.
- **Ancient:** Legend says the gods knelt here to weave the world, and that their kneeling marks are still pressed into the stone.

## Landmarks

- **The Kneeling Stones:** Two great hollows in the rock, said to be where the gods knelt.
- **The Cracked Root:** A root split from top to bottom, leaking light.
- **The Crystal Bridges:** Bridges of crystal spanning bottomless gulfs.

## Story hooks

- The Church wants to know whether the shard in its treasury is growing.
- A slime that has lived at the Root for an age has learned to speak, and it wants to tell someone what it saw.
- An adventurer swears she heard someone weeping inside the Cracked Root.
