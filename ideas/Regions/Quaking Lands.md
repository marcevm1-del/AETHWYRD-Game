## Overview

The Quaking Lands are a region of mountains and plains where the ground never stops moving. Hills shift, rivers change course overnight and new valleys open with a sound like thunder. The people who live here, the Stillers, have built their towns around the few springs where the ground is still. Quakeback, the Earthshaker Behemoth, is the reason, and it is turning in its sleep.

## Geography

The land is a broken country of ridges, cracks, landslides and new-made hills, between a range of jagged mountains and a broad, rippling plain. Rivers appear and disappear, and lakes drain overnight. The still springs are scattered across the region, each one a calm pool where the ground does not move, and every town is built around one. The Stillers' oldest maps show mountains that are no longer there.

## Ecology

Animals here are hardy and adaptable: burrowing creatures, mountain goats, wolves and birds that nest on the ground and can fly at the first tremor. Earth elementals and stone beasts thrive in the quakes. Plants grow fast and shallow, and the Stillers farm in quick seasons between tremors.

## Peoples

The Stillers are a resilient, patient people who read tremors the way sailors read weather. They build in wood and rope rather than stone, and every house can be taken down and moved in a day. They are calm in a crisis and nervous in calm weather.

## Power & trade

Each spring-town is ruled by its tremor-readers, who decide when to farm, when to move and when to run. The towns trade tremor-charts, wood, herbs and the strange crystals the quakes bring up from below.

## Faith & customs

The Stillers honour the day-gods and the still springs, which they consider sacred. They believe the earth is a sleeping beast and that the springs are where it has promised not to move.

## The Tyrant

Quakeback, the Earthshaker Behemoth, is a creature so vast that the region's mountains are its back. It has slept beneath the Quaking Lands for ages, and its dreams make the ground move. Now it is waking, and every turn it makes reshapes the land. It does not know the Stillers exist.

## Senses

Dust and pine; the creak of rope houses; a constant faint vibration underfoot; the deep groan of the earth.

## History

- **Now:** Quakeback has begun to stir, and the tremors are stronger and more frequent. Two still springs have cracked, and their towns have had to flee.
- **Parents:** Within living memory a great quake split the plain and opened a valley that the Stillers still call the New Gorge.
- **Grandparents:** In their grandparents' time the tremors were gentle and the springs were many.
- **Ancient:** The Stillers learned to read tremors the way sailors read weather. Their oldest maps are all wrong now.

## Settlements

- **Stiller spring-towns:** Towns of wood and rope built around the still springs.

## Landmarks

- **The Still Springs:** Calm pools where the ground does not move.
- **The New Gorge:** A valley opened by a great quake within living memory.
- **Quakeback's Ridge:** A mountain ridge that breathes.

## Story hooks

- A tremor-reader has predicted a great quake, and no one believes her.
- A spring-town needs to move, and the route crosses Quakeback's Ridge.
- A Stiller child has learned to calm the tremors by singing to the ground.

## Clans

- **The Stillhorns** (Oxen Clan, led by Elder Tauron Deephoof): Herders and tremor-readers of the Quaking Lands, who feel quakes through their hooves.

## Fauna

- **Stone-Horned Ox** (Herbivore): A ram-horned grazer that senses tremors; the Stillhorns' herds.
