## Overview

Stormreach is a coast of cliffs and mountains where the sea never calms and the sky is never still. Its towns are bolted to the rock above the waves, and lightning rods crown every roof. The people are fishers, rope-makers and storm-callers, as wild as the weather and as tough as the cliffs. Raikaru, the Storm Roc, nests in the thunderheads, and the storms have grown worse since it arrived.

## Geography

Sheer cliffs of dark rock rise straight from the sea, battered by waves that throw spray hundreds of feet into the air. Behind them, jagged mountains catch the storm clouds that roll in from the ocean. The cliff-towns are built on ledges and terraces, joined by stairs, ladders and rope bridges, and every building is anchored to the rock. Lightning strikes the mountains so often that whole peaks are scorched black.

## Ecology

Seabirds nest on the cliffs in vast numbers, and their eggs and guano are harvested by the people. Seals, sea lions and fish fill the waters below. Thunderbirds, storm elementals and giant eagles live in the mountains. The roc hunts everything, including the people.

## Peoples

The people of Stormreach are fishers, egg-gatherers, rope-makers and storm-callers. They are loud, cheerful, fearless and slightly mad, by the standards of everyone else. Storm-callers can read the weather and, it is said, call lightning down where they want it. Children learn to climb before they can walk.

## Power & trade

Each cliff-town is governed by its storm-caller and a council of elders. Fish, eggs, rope, guano and lightning-glass are traded south. Stormreach rope is the strongest in the world.

## Faith & customs

The people honour the day-gods and the storm, which they believe is a living thing. Lightning that strikes a person is considered a blessing, if they survive. The dead are left on high peaks for the lightning to take.

## The Tyrant

Raikaru, the Storm Roc, is a giant bird of prey whose wings create thunder and whose feathers crackle with lightning. It has made its nest in the thunderheads above Stormreach, and it feeds on the storms themselves, growing larger and the storms fiercer with every passing month.

## Senses

Salt spray and ozone; thunder that never stops; wet rope and seabird cries; the hair on your arms standing up.

## History

- **Now:** Raikaru has made its nest in the thunderheads above the cliffs, and the storms have become relentless. Lightning has struck two towns, and the fishing fleets cannot go out.
- **Parents:** Within living memory a storm-caller called down lightning on a pirate fleet and sank it in a single afternoon.
- **Grandparents:** In their grandparents' time the first lightning rods were bolted to the cliffs, and the towns stopped burning.
- **Ancient:** The bolts in the cliff are lightning rods. The towns were built after the first ones worked.

## Settlements

- **Stormreach cliff-towns:** Towns bolted to the cliffs above the sea, crowned with lightning rods.

## Landmarks

- **The Black Peaks:** Mountains scorched black by lightning.
- **The Rod Forest:** A field of lightning rods on the highest cliff, where the storm-callers train.
- **The Roc's Nest:** A nest in the thunderheads, visible only during lightning flashes.

## Story hooks

- A storm-caller has lost her power, and she thinks the roc took it.
- A fishing fleet is trapped in a cove by the storm, and the town needs rope carried to it along the cliffs.
- An egg-gatherer has found a roc egg.
