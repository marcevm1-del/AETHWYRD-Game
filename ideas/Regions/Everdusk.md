> *The courts made the dusk to settle an argument about whether it was day or night. They are still arguing.*

## Overview

Everdusk is a forest of endless twilight, where the sun never fully rises or sets and the fey courts hold revels in the glades. Politeness is law here, thanks are debts and every bargain binds. The Summer Court and the Winter Court have argued for as long as the forest has existed. The Briar King of the Winter Court wants the Summer Court's last glade.

## Geography

The forest is a vast, ancient woodland of oak, ash and thorn, lit by a perpetual dusk. Glades open among the trees, each one the seat of a fey lord. Rivers run silver, and paths change depending on who walks them. The Summer Court's glades are warm and golden; the Winter Court's are cold and blue.

## Ecology

Deer, foxes, owls and nightingales live in the forest, along with pixies, sprites, dryads and fey beasts of every kind. Plants grow strangely, flowering and fruiting at once. Time flows differently in the glades.

## Peoples

The fey of Everdusk are beautiful, capricious and bound by rules no mortal fully understands. Mortals who live in or near the forest are woodcutters, hunters and changelings, and they know to be very careful with their words.

## Power & trade

The forest is divided between the Summer and Winter Courts, each ruled by a monarch. The courts are bound by ancient agreements and constantly test them. Mortal trade with the fey is dangerous but lucrative: fey goods, enchantments and secrets for mortal names, memories and years.

## Faith & customs

Mortals honour the day-gods and leave offerings for the fey at the forest's edge. Never say thank you to a fey, and never give your name.

## The Tyrant

The Briar King, Lord of the Winter Court, is a fey lord of thorns, ice and cold beauty. He believes the Summer Court is weak and the forest should be his. His thorns grow wherever he walks, and his bargains are cruel.

## Senses

Honey and frost; music that never stops; the rustle of thorns; light that is always just about to change.

## History

- **Now:** The Briar King's thorns are swallowing the Summer Court's glades, and only one remains. The Summer Court has begun to beg mortals for help, which is unprecedented.
- **Parents:** Within living memory a mortal child was stolen from the forest's edge and returned as an adult, with no memory of where she had been.
- **Grandparents:** In their grandparents' time the courts held a great treaty that divided the forest in two.
- **Ancient:** The courts made the dusk to settle an argument about whether it was day or night. They are still arguing.

## Settlements

- **Summer Court glades:** The last glades of the Summer Court, warm and golden, home of the Hour That Isn't.

## Landmarks

- **The Last Glade:** The Summer Court's final glade, ringed by encroaching thorns.
- **The Thorn Throne:** The Briar King's seat.
- **The Edge Stones:** Stones at the forest's edge where offerings are left.

## Story hooks

- The Summer Court wants a mortal champion, and its terms are generous, perhaps too generous.
- A woodcutter's daughter has been invited to a fey revel, and her father wants her back before dawn.
- A mortal who made a bargain with the Briar King wants to break it.

## Clans

- **The Antlered Court** (Elk-kin/Antelopion Clan, led by Herald Cervaine of the Summer Glades): Mortal-blooded courtiers who serve the Summer Court as heralds and duellists. Their antlers shed each spring and are given as tokens of oath.
- **The Wingborn** (Fairy Clan, led by Captain Sylphine Thistledart): The Summer Court's soldiery and messengers, losing ground to the Briar King's thorns.
- **The Pipers** (Satyr/Faun Clan, led by Fennick Reedsong): Musicians and messengers of both courts, who are allowed to cross between them.
