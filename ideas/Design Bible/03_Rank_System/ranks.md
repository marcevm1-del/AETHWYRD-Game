> *"Your rank is what the world believes you can survive. Prove it wrong, upward."*
> — Engraved over the doors of every Guild Hall

### GP rules

| Same | 100% |

### Trial grading
- The Examiner scores **Speed, Damage Taken, Style, Allies/Civilians Saved, Creativity** (unusual solutions count).
- Grade **C/B/A/S**. An S grade gives a unique title, gear, and an examiner quote that NPCs repeat in gossip.
- Fail → retry next in-game day. Mina the receptionist gives a pep talk (new lines each time).

## 3. Rank-Up Ceremony
1. The Guild Master calls your name; adventurers in the hall react based on your reputation.
2. Your old card goes into a **ley-fountain**, melts, and re-forms as the new metal (a short unique cutscene per rank).
3. System Voice: *"Adventurer Rank updated: [C]. World recognition has increased."*
4. Rank Gift chest + new title.
5. At **S and up**, the promotion is announced kingdom-wide, and bards write songs about you (a real in-game song list that grows).

### The Nine Ceremonies
| Rank | Where | The moment | Gift |
|---|---|---|---|
| **F** (registration) | Brindlewick guild counter | Mina burns your name into an oak card with a hot stamp. Someone in the hall laughs at your Status | Rookie's Pouch |
| **E** | Brindlewick guild hall | The oak card **burns away into copper**. Lyra and the regulars cheer; Brutus grudgingly claps | Copper Compass upgrade, title *Novice* |
| **D** | Regional guild branch | Your first ceremony in front of strangers. The examiner reads your exam score aloud (Big Upgrade §29) | Iron-bound journal, map seal |
| **C** | The capital's Grand Guild | An audience of adventurers; the rival party you've been racing is there too | Silver card chain, a trade license |
| **B** | Grand Guild + town square | The Adventurer Times prints your portrait; townsfolk you've helped attend | First Relic hint, mentee unlock (Big Upgrade §13) |
| **A** | Royal court (Aldmere) | Knighted-style ceremony (no crown, Canon); nobles start courting your favor | Platinum crest, kingdom-wide waystone pass |
| **S** | Cathedral of Ilvaris | Kingdom-wide announcement, the **System Voice speaks to the whole crowd**. The first time the System glitches in public | Mithril card, your first bard song |
| **SS** | Before the 7 living SS-ranks | One of the seven challenges you to a duel right there | Adamantite card, a seat at the Calamity Council |
| **SSS** | The ley-fountain beneath the Grand Guild, alone | The fountain doesn't re-forge your card. **The sky does.** Constellations draw themselves onto it. The harvest begins to notice you | Starcard, Transcendence T1 |
- Every ceremony is affected by **Renown and Karma**: who attends, who boos, which companions stand beside you, and what the System says.
- **Exceptional Promotion** (skipping a rank) uses a shortened ceremony and then the Guild Master's trial.
- Each ceremony is saved to the **Memory Reel** automatically, and a photo of the new card goes to the Trophy Hall.

## 6. Rank Concealment & "The Reveal" (minor feature; the main power fantasy is Relics, see 09_Equipment)
- **[Concealment]** skill, the *Mask of the Humble*, or a guild favor makes your card show a lower rank.
- NPCs react to your *displayed* rank: tavern bullies, snobbish nobles, merchants overcharging rookies.
- **Reveal Events (20+ scripted):** when your real rank is exposed, there's a cinematic beat. The bully drops his mug, the hall goes silent, Mina faints.
  - *The Tavern Brawl*: thugs mock your "F" card; one punch later the System announces your real rank to the room.
  - *The Noble's Duel*: a rich D-Rank brat challenges you over someone's honor.
  - *The Examiner's Shock*: you retake an old trial in disguise and S-grade it in 4 seconds.

## Exceptional Promotion
- Completing a quest or boss **2+ ranks above your rank** (rated Perilous or higher) triggers **"Exceptional Feat"**.
- The Guild Master summons you to a **Trial of Merit**: a duel against the Guild Master or an SS-ranked examiner. Win (any grade) → **skip one rank**.
- Maximum **one skip per rank band**. It can't be used to reach SS or SSS (those require their full trials).

## Pacing to ~1,000 Hours
| Rank | Levels | Target cumulative hours |
|---|---|---|
| F | 1–15 | ~10 |
| E | 16–35 | ~30 |
| D | 36–60 | ~70 |
| C | 61–90 | ~140 |
| B | 91–125 | ~240 |
| A | 126–165 | ~380 |
| S | 166–210 | ~560 |
| SS | 211–260 | ~780 |
| SSS | 261–300 + T1–T10 | ~1,000+ |
These are **explorer-pace** hours (doing most side content). A **focused** player who mostly follows the story gains GP and XP faster and finishes the main story (Act VI, around S rank) in ~200–250 hrs. The road to SSS is the long-haul adventurer's journey: Labyrinth to floor 120, Relics, Elder Dragons, the taming codex, and the Calamities.

## Parallel Track: Cultivation
Research: xianxia realm lists vary by work. Common stages are Body Refinement, Qi Condensation, Foundation Establishment, Core Formation, Nascent Soul, Soul/Spirit Formation and Void Refinement, each with early/middle/late/peak sub-stages, and breakthroughs that can require a heavenly lightning tribulation.

| Realm | Sub-stages | Unlocks | Breakthrough trial |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 · **Body Tempering** | Early/Mid/Late/Peak | Body passives | Waterfall training |
| 2 · **Qi Condensation** | ×4 | Qi meter, Qi skills | Meditation minigame |
| 3 · **Foundation Establishment** | ×4 | Flying sword *techniques* (combat only, not travel) | **Lightning Tribulation** boss fight |
| 4 · **Core Formation** | ×4 | Golden Core: +regen, pill alchemy | Inner-demon duel |
| 5 · **Nascent Soul** | ×4 | Soul projection (scout as a spirit) | Tribulation II |
| 6 · **Spirit Severing** | ×4 | Domains | Sever an attachment (story choice) |
| 7 · **Void Refinement** | ×4 | Space techniques | Tribulation III |
| 8 · **Immortal Ascension** | — | Sect Ancestor title | The Heavens' Trial (ties into the System's truth: the System *fears* cultivators) |
- **Lore:** cultivation was taught by the Veilborn Chen Wei. It grows power **without the System's harvest channel**, so the Church and Ilvaris quietly fear it. That gives it a big role in the *Unwoven Sky* ending.
- **Balance:** cultivation realms add their own power layer. Guild rank stays the world's main "language," but sects respect only realms.
