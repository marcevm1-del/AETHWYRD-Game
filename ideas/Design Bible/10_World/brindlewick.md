# 10b — Brindlewick, the Frontier Town (Ring 1 hub)

> *"Every legend in Aldmere starts the same way: a wooden card, a bowl of stew at the Sleeping Griffon, and Mina saying 'Welcome to Brindlewick!' a little too loudly."*

**Region:** `reg_aldmere` (town) bordering `reg_whisperwood` (north). **Rank band of surroundings:** F–E. **Population:** ~600. **Prosperity at start:** Stage 2 · Stable (see Systems → Settlement Prosperity). **NPCs:** `Data/NPCs/npcs.json` (filter `settlement: "Brindlewick"`). **Quests:** `Data/Quests/quests.json` (Ring 1).

## 1. History in Brief
- **~300 SC:** a palisade fort built on a ley-line crossing to guard the road north into Whisperwood after the Gorrumvar war.
- **~520 SC:** the fort becomes a market town; the mill on the Brindle river makes it rich in flour, hence the name ("Brindle-wick," the river village).
- **~880 SC:** the Adventurers' Guild opens its frontier branch here. Brindlewick becomes *the* town where rookies register; the Guild calls it "the Cradle."
- **1019–1024 SC:** goblin raids grow organized, Vesper swells early, and more Veilborn wander out of Whisperwood than ever before.

## 6. Secrets in Brindlewick
1. **Whiskers** follows you if you pet him 30 times (*The Cat Who Was a God*).
2. **The Old Watchtower cellar**: Old Weave-tongue carvings of nine thrones.
3. **Hollis's buried sword** under the third training post (after losing to his broom 10 times).
4. **The orphanage songs** are in Old Weave-tongue; once you can read it, they're hymns to the red moon.
5. **The first copper** you nailed to the guild wall is still there at the end of the game, with a note from Mina.
