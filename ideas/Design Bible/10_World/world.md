# 10 — The World of Aethwyrd

> *"Every road in Aethwyrd leads somewhere wonderful. Most of them also lead somewhere that wants to eat you."*

One seamless map: **40 surface regions + 5 Underworld layers + the Great Labyrinth + Vesper (post-game)**. Rank bands and build rings per region live in `Data/World/regions.json` (canonical). Each region lists the anime and literature that inspired it; we combine and twist, never copy.

# Part 1 — Region Catalog
One continuous map. Regions blend into each other through **border zones** (mixed architecture, bilingual signs, trade posts). Each region lists its **inspirations** from anime and fantasy literature: we combine and twist them rather than copy them.

**Region types:** 🏰 Human · 🌳 Elf · ⚒️ Dwarf · 🐾 Beastfolk · 👹 Monster-dominated · 🌀 Wild/Magical · 🔥 Demon · ⛏️ Underworld

## THE CENTRAL CONTINENT

### 1. 🏰 Kingdom of Aldmere (F–C)
- **Inspired by:** the classic isekai starter kingdom (*Konosuba*'s Axel, *Goblin Slayer*'s frontier town), plus *Spice and Wolf*'s grounded medieval farm life.
- **Culture:** feudal, faithful, bread-and-ale warm. Founded by Hero #1's knight. A weak king and a strong Church.
- **Unique tradition:** **First Card Day**: every new adventurer's first copper is nailed to the guild wall.

### 2. 🏰 Imperium of Vharlen (B–A)
- **Inspired by:** Rome's legions, *Fullmetal Alchemist*'s military state alchemy, *Black Clover*'s class divide by magic power, the Nilfgaard-style empire of *The Witcher*.
- **Culture:** a **Status Caste** society. Your rank on Appraisal Day sets your citizenship tier for life. Gleaming marble cities, magitech legions, state-licensed mages.
- **Hook:** a Veilborn engineer (Lukas Brandt) built its war machines; Lady Nocturne controls its court.
- **Unique tradition:** **The Ranking Parade**: citizens march in order of Status.

### 3. 🏰 The Strife Marches (C–B)
- **Inspired by:** *Mushoku Tensei*'s war-torn, poor north of the Central Continent; *The Witcher*'s monster-contract culture; *Berserk*'s mercenary bands (tone only, no grimdark excess).
- **Culture:** a patchwork of tiny feuding baronies, mercenary companies, monster-hunter guilds with mutation rites, refugees.
- **Hook:** Dorran "Red Ledger" Hask's company; Diego Alvarez's slaver empire.
- **Unique tradition:** **Coin Oaths**: contracts sealed by biting a coin in half.

### 4. 🏰 The Free Cities of Lirian (D–B)
- **Inspired by:** *Spice and Wolf*'s merchant towns and harvest-god pagan faith, Venice's canals, the Hanseatic League.
- **Culture:** canal cities ruled by merchant councils; banks, guild halls, masked carnivals; the old harvest deity **Ysolde the Golden Hare**, half-forgotten.
- **Hook:** the Consortium's heart; trade-route gameplay; the Masked Carnival.
- **Unique tradition:** **Ledger Weddings**: marriages are recorded in the bank, not the temple.

### 5. 🏰 Bastion, the Walled City (B–S)
- **Inspired by:** *Attack on Titan*'s ringed walls, *Claymore*'s besieged towns, Minas Tirith.
- **Culture:** a city of three giant rings built against the **Calamity Steppe** beyond it. Everyone serves on the wall. Grim, proud, tight-knit.
- **Hook:** the main defense set-piece against the Hive Mother Calamity.
- **Unique tradition:** **Wall Names**: every defender who dies is carved into the stone.

### 6. 🏰 Gearholm, the Upper and Under City (B–A)
- **Inspired by:** *Arcane*'s Piltover/Zaun divide, *Castle in the Sky* steampunk, *Last Exile* sky-tech.
- **Culture:** a gleaming magitech city on a cliff; beneath it the **Undercity** of core-smog, pit fights, black markets and inventors.
- **Hook:** Otherworlder engineering, illegal Abyssal Pit Fights, an airship-port (vehicles, not mounts).
- **Unique tradition:** **Gear-Gifting**: lovers exchange hand-made clockwork.

### 7. 🐾 Beastkin Plains / Fanghold Federation (D–B)
- **Inspired by:** the Mongol steppe and Maasai savannah, *Beastars*' society, *Mushoku Tensei*'s Great Forest beastfolk honor codes.
- **Culture:** a federation of clans (wolf, cat, fox, bear, rabbit, bird), arena honor, nomad yurt cities, oral law.
- **Hook:** they revere the "Healer-Hero" Adrian, so they resist the war against Azrael.
- **Unique tradition:** **The First Hunt**: a coming-of-age solo hunt under the moon.

### 8. 🌳 Sylvaran Forest (D–B)
- **Inspired by:** *Frieren*'s long-lived elven melancholy, Tolkien's Lothlórien, *Princess Mononoke*'s forest gods.
- **Culture:** an arrogant High Court in the World Tree; humble wood elves below; forest spirits that must be asked permission.
- **Unique tradition:** **Century Songs**: elves sing one verse per century of their lives.

### 9. ⚒️ Iron Hold Peaks (C–A)
- **Inspired by:** Tolkien's dwarves, *Dungeon Meshi*'s non-monolithic dwarf culture (the outcast who doesn't fit dwarven norms), Norse forge myths.
- **Culture:** clan halls, ancestor worship, rune law, rival clans with different philosophies (miners, smiths, brewers, an outcast "surface clan").
- **Unique tradition:** **The Oath-Anvil**: promises hammered into iron.

### 10. 🌀 Greymoor & the Witchwood (D–C)
- **Inspired by:** Slavic folklore (Baba Yaga's walking hut), *Witch Hat Atelier*'s secret witch society, *The Witcher*'s bogs.
- **Culture:** a fog-bound marsh with a **hidden witch society** whose magic is drawn, not chanted. A walking hut-market moves each night.
- **Unique tradition:** **Mirror Midnights**: you ask your reflection one question a year.

## THE EAST

### 11. 🏰 Eastern Isles: Shogunate of Hoshizora (C–A)
- **Inspired by:** Japanese myth and *Mushishi*'s spirit ecology, *Natsume's Book of Friends* (gentle yokai-like spirits), *Demon Slayer*'s breathing-style sword schools (as a concept).
- **Culture:** shrines, sword schools, spirit festivals, a spirit-world overlay at night, fox shrines.
- **Unique tradition:** **Lantern Release**: write a regret, float it out to sea.

### 12. 🏰 Jade Dynasty of Shen-Lu (B–S)
- **Inspired by:** Chinese **cultivation (xianxia)** fiction and donghua (*Mo Dao Zu Shi*, *Battle Through the Heavens*), Wuxia sects, imperial bureaucracy.
- **Culture:** mountain **sects** that rank disciples by their own cultivation stages (Qi Condensation → Foundation → Core → Nascent Soul…), running in parallel with the Guild's F–SSS. Sect rivalries, alchemy pills, cloud-sea peaks.
- **Hook:** the player can join a sect as an alternative progression track (cultivation stages convert to stat bonuses).
- **Unique tradition:** **The Ascension Stairs**: 9,999 steps climbed as a sect entry test.

### 13. 🏰 Haneul Highlands (B–A)
- **Inspired by:** Korean webtoon/manhwa (*Solo Leveling*'s Gates and Hunter Association, *Tower of God*), Joseon-era palaces and mountain temples.
- **Culture:** **Gates** (dimensional rifts) open randomly in towns; the **Hunter Bureau** licenses gate-hunters with its own grading; a sudden "gate break" sends monsters into the streets.
- **Hook:** the best region for procedural rift-dungeon gameplay.
- **Unique tradition:** **Gate Rites**: hunters bow to a gate before entering.

### 14. 🌀 Fox Spirit Archipelago (C–A)
- **Inspired by:** kitsune myth, *Spirited Away*'s bathhouse, *Inuyasha*'s demon-human border.
- **Culture:** small islands where spirits run inns and markets; humans are guests; contracts are binding magic.
- **Hook:** Suzune's home; spirit contracts; nine-tail lore.

## THE NORTH

### 15. 🏰 Frostveil North (B–A)
- **Inspired by:** Norse sagas, *Vinland Saga*'s farmland vs warrior culture, *Frieren*'s harsh northern lands.
- **Culture:** longhouses, saga-singers, a **Frost Queen** (a Veilborn legend), guardians of Garmrok's chain.
- **Unique tradition:** **Saga Debts**: you owe a song to whoever saves your life.

### 16. 🌀 Heavenreach Plateau (A–S)
- **Inspired by:** *Mushoku Tensei*'s Heaven Continent (a plateau thousands of meters above sea level) and *Frieren*'s northern plateau, which requires special permission to enter.
- **Culture:** a sky-high, thin-air plateau of the **Heavenfolk** (winged people). **Entry legally restricted to A-Rank+**; monsters here are absurdly strong.
- **Unique tradition:** **Feather Law**: a guest who breaks an oath loses a feather from the host's cloak.

## THE SOUTH & WEST

### 17. 🏰 Sunscar Desert / Oasis Sultanates (B–A)
- **Inspired by:** *Magi: The Labyrinth of Magic* (Arabian Nights dungeons and djinn), *Dune*'s water culture, Egyptian tombs.
- **Culture:** **Water is currency**; oasis sultanates; djinn contracts; tomb-raiding guilds; the ghost of Valdrisia.
- **Unique tradition:** **The Water Oath**: sharing water makes you family.

### 18. 🏰 Zaruma, the Gold-Road Kingdom (B–A)
- **Inspired by:** the Mali Empire and Mansa Musa's gold trade, West African **griot** storytelling, baobab-tree cities.
- **Culture:** a wealthy savannah kingdom of scholars and storytellers. Griots memorize history in song, and they are the only ones who remember Amara Okonkwo (Hero ½). The great library-mosque-style towers are made of earth.
- **Unique tradition:** **Living Libraries**: a griot can "lend" you a memory as a buff.

### 19. 🐾 Mire Kingdoms of Xochitl (C–B)
- **Inspired by:** Mesoamerican cultures (Maya and Aztec step-cities, feathered-serpent myth), *Avatar*'s Foggy Swamp.
- **Culture:** **lizardfolk** and human river-cities; step pyramids on stilts; jaguar warriors; a feathered-serpent dragon god (a minor Elder lineage).
- **Unique tradition:** **The Ball of Stars**: a ritual ball game that decides disputes.

### 20. 🌀 Stormreach Coast (B–S)
- **Inspired by:** *The Stormlight Archive*'s highstorms, which shape a whole ecology; *Earthsea*'s precarious maritime islands.
- **Culture:** a coast hit by a **world-storm every 7 days**. Plants retract, crab-shelled beasts shelter, and storm-chasers harvest **stormlight crystals**. Towns are built into cliffs.
- **Unique tradition:** **Storm Vigils**: families sing through the night as the storm passes.

### 21. 🌀 Archipelago of True Names (B–A)
- **Inspired by:** *Earthsea*'s magic of true names on scattered islands, Polynesian wayfinding.
- **Culture:** islands where mages learn the **true names** of things; everyone has a secret name. Star-navigators sail canoes between islands.
- **Hook:** a unique magic school (*Name Magic*: rename an enemy's element).
- **Unique tradition:** **The Naming Voyage**: at 16, youth sail alone to learn their name.

### 22. 🌀 Abyssal Coast & Port Goldmere (B–S)
- **Inspired by:** *One Piece*'s pirate culture and sea kings, the Caribbean golden age of piracy, the merfolk kingdom myths.
- **Culture:** pirate towns on stilts, the Consortium's port, the coral merfolk kingdom of Ys.
- **Unique tradition:** **Bell Debts**: ring the harbor bell to call in a pirate's favor.

### 23. 🌀 Fey Courts of Everdusk (A–S)
- **Inspired by:** Celtic faerie myth (Seelie/Unseelie), *Ancient Magus' Bride*'s fae and neighbors, *Pan's Labyrinth*.
- **Culture:** two fae courts (Summer and Winter); **time flows differently** (a day inside can be a week outside); deals, names and riddles are dangerous.
- **Unique tradition:** **Never Say Thank You**: it creates a debt.

## THE MONSTER LANDS

### 24. 👹 Free Monster Commonwealth of Moraveil (C–S)
- **Inspired by:** *That Time I Got Reincarnated as a Slime*'s monster nation (goblins, ogres, lizardmen and dire wolves building a city together), *Overlord*'s Nazarick-adjacent monster towns.
- **Culture:** a young nation of goblins, kobolds, orcs, lizardfolk, arachne and slimes trying to be recognized as people. **You can help build it** (Monster Origin or Rhaka recruited), with Settlement Prosperity at nation scale.
- **Unique tradition:** **Naming Day**: monsters who earn a name hold a feast.

### 25. 👹 The Calamity Steppe (S–SS)
- **Inspired by:** *Claymore*'s monster-infested lands, *Made in Abyss*'s lethal beauty, Mongol steppe scale.
- **Culture:** no towns. Endless grass where Calamity-tier beasts roam, a ruined fortress line, nomad "Ghost Riders" who live between hunts.

### 26. 🔥 Nox Dominion (S–SSS)
- **Inspired by:** *Mushoku Tensei*'s poor, monster-heavy Demon Continent; *Frieren*'s eloquent demons (but sympathetic here); *Maoyuu Maou Yuusha*'s demon economy.
- **Culture:** a real civilization (markets, schools, poets), poor and proud, under a red sky. Demons suffer from the war too.

## THE SKY

### 27. 🌀 Sky Archipelago (S–SS)
- **Inspired by:** *Castle in the Sky* (Laputa), *One Piece*'s Skypiea, *Granblue Fantasy*'s sky islands.
- **Culture:** abandoned Titan cities, sky-whale herds, the Wyrmconclave's hall above it. Reached by airship vehicles or flight abilities (no mounts).

### 28. 🌀 Dragon Spine Mountains (A–S)
- **Inspired by:** Himalayan monasteries, *Tower of God* climbing tests.
- **Culture:** dragon-bone monasteries, sky-burial rites, the living mountain Terragon.

## THE UNDERWORLD (5 layers, Q52)
Beneath the whole map. Entrances in many regions, each layer deeper and more dangerous.

| Layer | Name | Rank | Inspired by | Culture & features |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1** | **Umbravel** | C–B | *Dungeons & Dragons*' Underdark, drow cities | Dark-elf matriarchal city of crystal lights; spider-silk markets; exile politics |
| **2** | **The Hollow Sea** | B–A | Journey to the Center of the Earth, *Made in Abyss*'s second layers | A subterranean ocean lit by glowing jellyfish; boat towns; blind fish-folk |
| **3** | **Aurumdeep, the Sunken Gold Kingdom** | A | ***Dungeon Meshi***'s Golden Kingdom (a kingdom sunk underground by a mad magician), with the dungeon as a living ecosystem | A whole kingdom swallowed by a dungeon; undead citizens frozen in time; the dungeon's "immune system" hunts adventurers who over-harvest |
| **4** | **The Maw (Great Descent)** | S–SS | ***Made in Abyss***'s vertical pit and the curse of ascending; *DanMachi*'s floors | A vertical abyss; **Weave Pressure**: going *back up* hurts (debuffs) the deeper you went; camps cling to the walls |
| **5** | **The Root of the World** | SS–EX | Yggdrasil myth, the Titan Atharos's corpse | Where the Great Labyrinth, the Maw and the World Tree's roots meet; the Unclosed Knot's pulse |

Plus **the Great Labyrinth** (120 floors), connected to layers 3–5 by secret passages.

## MORE CULTURES (research pass)
### 31. 🌀 The Dunewhale Drift (B–A): *a town on the back of a whale, sailing a sea of sand*
- **Inspired by:** *Kujira no Kora wa Sajou ni Utau* (*Children of the Whales*): the Mud Whale, a floating island drifting over an endless sea of sand, where an ability shortens its users' lives. Also the *Dune* sandworm.
- **Our version:** **Thalassa**, a colossal sand-leviathan whose back holds a nomad town. It swims through the Sunscar dune-sea on a yearly migration. Its people, the **Driftfolk**, can "sing" to sand (**Sandsong** magic), but every song shortens their lives. They keep an **Archive of the Short-Lived**.
- **Gameplay:** a moving region (it's in a different place each season); find it by tracking its song.

### 32. 🌀 Lustrine Shores (A–S): *the gem people*
- **Inspired by:** *Land of the Lustrous* (*Houseki no Kuni*): a world where gem-like beings live.
- **Our version:** the **Lustrine**, crystalline people (each a different gem with its own hardness and fragility) living on glass beaches. They shatter rather than die and can be **reassembled**. On every **Blood Vesper**, **Vesperite raiders** (Malgareth's moon-servants) descend to harvest them.
- **Gameplay:** a playable **Lustrine race** unlock (high magic, fragile; you can lose and replace limbs with other gems for new powers).

### 33. 🌀 The Mossdeep of the Thimblefolk (D–C): *the tiny world*
- **Inspired by:** *Hakumei to Mikochi* (tiny people living in the forest), *The Borrowers*, *Arrietty*.
- **Our version:** a forest where the **Thimblefolk** (9 cm tall) live in mushrooms and hollow logs, ride beetles and trade with mice. A fairy-ring potion shrinks **you**. Suddenly a horned rabbit is a kaiju.
- **Gameplay:** a cozy and terrifying scale-shift region. F-rank monsters become boss-sized. One of the most memorable places in the game.

### 34. 🏰 The Mana-Tithe Duchies of Velmark (C–B)
- **Inspired by:** *Ascendance of a Bookworm*, where noble rank follows mana capacity rather than wealth and noble society is severe and political; also its feystones and mana-fed weapons.
- **Our version:** small duchies where **nobles must pour their mana into the land's Foundation Stones** every season or the crops die. Mana-rich commoners are "adopted" into nobility, and some are trapped there. Your Veilborn mana is **politically priceless**: three dukes compete for you.
- **Gameplay:** political intrigue, mana-tithe rituals (minigame), and a court mystery arc.

### 35. 🌀 The Serene Vale of Ashira (B): *the perfect village (horror)*
- **Inspired by:** *Shinsekai Yori* (*From the New World*): a seemingly utopian future with psychic powers in small, isolated villages.
- **Our version:** a valley where everyone has gentle telekinesis, everyone smiles, and children are tested at 12. **Children who fail disappear.** The village's "monsters" are the descendants of those who failed.
- **Gameplay:** a slow-burn horror mystery; Menace 4–5; a hard moral choice at the end.

### 36. 🏰 Cinderhole, the Smoke City (A)
- **Inspired by:** *Dorohedoro* (sorcerers who use the non-magical as test subjects), *Arcane*'s Zaun.
- **Our version:** a city below Gearholm where rogue mages test spells on the magic-less; smoke-breathing mages; masked gangs; a lizard-headed vigilante legend (an original character inspired by the genre).
- **Gameplay:** black-market magic, the Abyssal Pit Fights, a revenge storyline.

### 37. 🌀 The Isles of Lumbaya (B–A): *jungle temples and night terrors*
- **Inspired by:** Southeast Asian folklore and settings: a Khmer-inspired sword-and-sorcery setting, and Filipino creatures like the *aswang* (shapeshifting vampire) and *tikbalang* (horse-headed trickster spirit).
- **Our version:** jungle archipelago with **giant stone-faced temple-cities** swallowed by roots, rice terraces, longboat races, shaman-healers (**Babaylans**). Night brings **Nightshifters** (our own shapeshifting vampires who pretend to be villagers) and **Crossroad Steeds** (horse-headed tricksters who make you lose your way).
- **Gameplay:** jungle exploration, temple puzzles, a night-horror village mystery.

### 38. 🌀 The Whitewater Reach (A–S): *the sea-mother's ice*
- **Inspired by:** Arctic folklore themes: a sea spirit who controls marine life and weather, ice giants, transforming shamans.
- **Our version:** frozen coasts where the **Deep Mother**, an original sea spirit, withholds fish and seals when angered. **Spirit-walkers** dive to comb her tangled hair to calm her. **Frost giants** sleep under the ice. Dog-sled travel (ground transport).
- **Gameplay:** survival-lite, a sea-spirit appeasement quest, ice-fishing legends.

### 39. 🌀 The Apu Highlands (A–S): *mountains that are gods*
- **Inspired by:** Andean cosmology themes: a three-tiered cosmos (upper, middle and inner worlds), mountain spirits, a creator who made and destroyed giants before forming humans from stone.
- **Our version:** terraced mountain cities; three-world shrines (Sky, Earth, Deep), with doors to the **Underworld**. **Mountain Lords**, actual living mountains, can be spoken to. **Stone Giants of the First Making** tie into the Titans. Woolly **vicuñal** pack-beasts (pack animals that can die, Q45).
- **Gameplay:** high-altitude exploration, a mountain-spirit council, an Underworld gateway region.

### 40. 🏰 The Peacock Throne of Zarvan (B–A): *the garden-epic empire*
- **Inspired by:** Persian epic tradition (the *Shahnameh*, an influence even on early Slavic fantasy), paradise gardens, the simurgh myth, *Magi*.
- **Our version:** rose-garden cities, fire temples, poets' duels, hero lineages recorded in an endless **Book of Kings** that still writes itself. **Divs** (horned giants) in the mountains. A great healing bird-spirit, **Simurrah**, guards the high peaks.
- **Gameplay:** epic duels, poetry battles (Bard heaven), and the chance to get **written into the Book of Kings** (a legacy system).

### 41. 🌀 The Quaking Lands (S): *the earth that wants to break*
- **Inspired by:** N. K. Jemisin's *Broken Earth* (praised for blending many themes): a world of recurring catastrophes and feared earth-calmers.
- **Our version:** a region where **earthquakes come every hour**. **Stillers**, mages who can calm or cause quakes, are feared, enslaved and drafted. Cities are built on springs and floating platforms.
- **Gameplay:** dynamic terrain (the map literally shifts), a Stiller liberation arc.

### 42. 🏰 Shen-Lu's Martial World (inside the Jade Dynasty)
- **Inspired by:** **murim** manhwa: an orthodox alliance against a demonic cult led by a feared "Heavenly Demon"; demonic sects shown as political organizations with internal struggles; protagonists who are wrongly accused of demonic arts.
- **Our version:** the **Righteous Alliance of Nine Sects** vs the **Crimson Heaven Cult** (no relation to the Red Moon Cult: they hate each other). The Cult is a real political power with factions; its leader, the **Heavenly Sovereign**, may be more honorable than the Alliance.
- **Gameplay:** join either side; a false-accusation questline; martial tournaments.

# Part 2 — Region Deep Dives
The first 12 regions in full detail. The others get deep dives as they come up in the build rings.
### KINGDOM OF ALDMERE — *"The Heartland"* (F–C)
**Mood:** rolling green hills, windmills, stone bridges, cherry orchards, golden wheat at sunset. Classic fantasy, warm and safe-looking (until night).
**Leitmotif:** pastoral flute + strings; becomes brass and choir in the capital.
**Postcard moment:** cresting Hollowpine Hill at dawn. Brindlewick below, Aldmere City's white towers on the horizon, Lumen still visible in the morning sky.

| Sub-zone | Rank | Feature |
|---|---|---|
| **Brindlewick & Meadows** | F | Starter town; horned rabbits, slimes; the training grounds |
| **Whisperwood** | F–E | Old forest; goblins, wolves, wisps; elven ruins; the White Stag |
| **Millbrook Farmlands** | E | Farms, cockatrices, Old Ironhide the beetle; farm plot purchase |
| **Hollowpine Highlands** | E–D | Goblin camps, bandit forts; first dungeon |
| **Greymoor Marsh** | D | Fog, undead, the Lantern Widow; witch's hut |
| **Aldmere City & Royal Valley** | D–C | Capital, Summoning Cathedral, Academy, Labyrinth entrance |
| **Greyhollow Keep** | C | Haunted castle; Sir Aldric's past |

**Settlements:**
- **Brindlewick:** guild branch, the *Sleeping Griffon Inn* (with Whiskers the cat), Hollis's training yard, Granny Mabel's farm, Torvald's forge.
- **Millbrook:** windmills, harvest festival.
- **Ashby:** plague village quest.
- **Aldmere City:** 5 districts (Noble, Market, Temple, Academy, Slums).

**Dungeons:** Hollowpine Goblin Camp (E), Proving Cave (E–D), Silkhollow Cave (D), Greyhollow Keep (C), Cathedral Catacombs (C).
**Secrets:** the 400-year-old cat; a hidden fairy ring that opens only during Festival of Blossoms; Hollis's buried sword; a "lost otherworlder's diary" with notes from a previous summoned person.

### SYLVARAN FOREST — *"The Green Cathedral"* (D–B)
**Mood:** trees as tall as castles, bioluminescent mushrooms, fairy lights, mist, waterfalls, a canopy city.
**Leitmotif:** harp, choir, wooden flutes.
**Postcard moment:** the first sight of the **World Tree Lothaniel**, its crown above the clouds, glowing leaves drifting down like snow.

| Sub-zone | Rank | Feature |
|---|---|---|
| Fairy Glades | D | Pixies, wisps, the fairy court |
| Moonwell Lake | D+ | Undines, a lake library underwater |
| The Weeping Roots | C | Corrupted area: the World Tree's sickness |
| Canopy City Lothaniel | C | Elven capital in the branches |
| Thornwild | B | Treants, man-eater plants, blight |
| Elder Grove | B+ | Ancient Treant, Sylvara's shrine |

**Story:** the World Tree is dying of the **Blight** (Blighted World Root Calamity). Elven politics are split between purists and the Dark Elf exiles' return.
**Secrets:** the drowned library of Moonwell; a Dark Elf entrance to Umbravel; a unicorn glade (Karma 500+).

### BEASTKIN PLAINS — *"The Endless Gold"* (D–B)
**Mood:** savannah, endless grass, herds of thunder-elk, stone monoliths, nomad yurt cities, huge sunsets.
**Leitmotif:** drums, throat singing, wind instruments.
**Postcard moment:** thousands of thunderhorn elk stampeding beneath a lightning storm.

| Sub-zone | Rank | Feature |
|---|---|---|
| Fanghold | D | Federation capital; **Grand Arena** |
| Thunder Steppe | C | Elk herds, Thunderhorn Behemoth field boss |
| Bone Valley | C+ | Ancient beast graveyard |
| Slaver's Road | C | Kiri's arc |
| Monolith Circle | B | Rhaka's domain |

**Systems showcased:** arena ladder (F–SS brackets), mounted combat, beast taming haven.

### IRON HOLD PEAKS — *"The Hammer Under the Mountain"* (C–A)
**Mood:** volcanic mountains, lava rivers, huge dwarven halls carved into mountains, rune-lit doors, mine carts.
**Leitmotif:** anvil percussion, deep male choirs, war horns.
**Postcard moment:** the **Great Forge of Karak Dûn** opening, a lava waterfall feeding a forge the size of a city.

Sub-zones: Karak Dûn (capital), the Ashen Mines, Ember Rift, Ancestor Halls, the Molten Throne (Ignarok's seat).
**Systems:** legendary forging, rune crafting, minecart races, a dwarven brewing contest.

### EASTERN ISLES (Shogunate of Hoshizora) — *"Islands of a Thousand Shrines"* (C–A)
**Mood:** pagodas, torii-style gates (original design), bamboo forests, hot springs, cherry blossoms, mountain shrines, lantern festivals, spirit world overlay.
**Leitmotif:** koto, shakuhachi, taiko.

# Part 3 — Living World
## Traversal & Travel
| Method | Unlock | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| On foot (sprint, climb, swim) | Start | Stamina-based |
| Waystones | Discover | Fast travel network |
| Horse / Raptor mount | D | Mounted combat |
| Beast mounts (Fenrir, Behemoth) | B+ | Special abilities |
| Gliding (race, Wind magic, relic cloaks) | C | Ability-based |
| Ship | B (Abyssal Coast) | Sailing, naval |
| Flight (ability: race, Wind magic, Ki, relics) | A+ | No flying mounts, by design |
| Airship | S | Mobile base |
| Teleport magic / Waystride | SS / SSS | Anywhere |

## Traversal Rules
- **Everyone:** sprint, climb ledges and rough walls (stamina), swim on the surface, ground mounts (horse, raptor, beast mounts).
- **Gliding:** races (Bird-kin, Fairy, Dragonkin), Wind magic (*Featherfall → Sky Glide*), or relic cloaks.
- **Flight:** abilities only. Dragonkin awakening, Wind Grand magic, Ki Sovereign, Seraph Wings relic, Transcendence. **No flying mounts.**
- **Teleport:** Waystones (network), Blink/Teleport spells, Space magic, Waystride (SSS).
- **Underwater:** stretch goal; designed so it can be added later as a region update.

## Dynamic World
- **Day/night**, **weather** (rain, storm, fog, snow, sandstorm, ash), **two moons** and **seasons** all change spawns, NPC schedules and quests.
- **Settlement states:** thriving / strained / threatened / under siege / recovering / rebuilt. Damage is repairable; no siege or missed defense permanently destroys a settlement (Canon §5).
- **Monster migrations** and ecosystems (see Bestiary).

## Living Dungeons: Personalities
Dungeons aren't allies, but each has a **Core Personality** that shapes traps, taunts and loot. All are **reusable** and they reset and remix.
| Personality | Behavior |
|---|---|
| **Obnoxious** | Mocks you with signs; rearranges rooms to annoy you; "fake" treasure |
| **Kind** | Leaves healing springs; warns rookies; is sad when you leave |
| **Dark** | Uses your fears (Menace 4–5 monsters), whispers, mimics |
| **Greedy** | Hoards gold; traps get nastier the more you loot |
| **Lonely** | Builds rooms based on your home; begs you to stay |
| **Theatrical** | Stages "plays" with monster actors; boss rooms are arenas with a cheering monster audience |
| **Scholarly** | Puzzle-dungeon; rewards answering riddles |
| **Hungry** | Grows when it eats adventurers; stampedes if ignored |
| **Ancient** | Silent, majestic, Titan-era; deep lore |
| **Childlike** | Toys, tag games, then sudden tantrums |

## Monster Towns & Intelligence
- Most monsters are animals or instinct-driven. **Intelligent, talking monsters** live mainly in **monster settlements**: goblin villages, lizardfolk towns, orc holds, the Arachne court, and the **Free Monster Commonwealth of Moraveil** (a monster nation you can help build, see World).
- Named monsters can become intelligent through evolution or Name-Giving.
