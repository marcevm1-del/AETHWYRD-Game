# 04c — Monster Evolution (Reborn Monster Origin) (v0.2)

> *"You died. You woke up as something small, wet, and very hungry. The System said: **Congratulations.**"*

## 1. How It Works
- You start as one of **6 starter monsters**, deep inside a dungeon (the **Hollow Nursery**, under Whisperwood).
- **No classes.** You **evolve** when you hit the level cap of your current form (cap 10/20/30/... by stage).
- **Evolution choice** depends on **Evolution Factors**:
  - **Diet:** what you eat (*Predation*). Every devoured monster gives "essence" of its element/family.
  - **Deeds:** kills, stealth, mercy, alliances.
  - **Skills:** which skills you trained highest.
  - **Environment:** where you evolve (volcano = fire branch, moonlit shrine = holy branch).
- **Humanoid Form** unlocks at stage 3 (C-equivalent), so you can enter towns. The guild registers you as F-Rank and you climb the ladder too.
- **Hunted status:** before humanoid form, adventurers attack on sight. NPC reactions change once you have a name and reputation.
- **Monster Rank = Adventurer Rank** for unlocks (evolution stage 1 = F/E, 2 = D/C, 3 = B/A, 4 = S/SS, 5 = SSS).

## 2. Starter Trees

### 🟢 SLIME — *the classic*
```
Slime (F)
 ├─ Big Slime (E) ──▶ Gelatinous Guardian (C) ──▶ Bastion Ooze (A) ──▶ ...
 ├─ Elemental Slime [Fire/Water/Wind/Earth] (D) ──▶ Slime Elemental Lord (B) ──▶ Prismatic Slime (S)
 ├─ Poison Slime (E) ──▶ Acid Devourer (C) ──▶ Plague Ooze Sovereign (A)
 ├─ Metal Slime (D, rare: eat 20 ores) ──▶ Mithril Slime (B) ──▶ Starmetal Slime (S)
 └─ Mimic Slime (D, eat 5 mimics) ──▶ Shapeless One (B, humanoid form!) ──▶ Slime Lord (S)
                                                                                  │
                                                         Primordial Slime (SSS) ◀─┘
```
Signature: **Predator** (absorb skills), **Mimicry** (copy the shape of what you ate).

### 🕷️ SPIDERLING — *the cunning hunter*
```
Spiderling (F) ─▶ Small Spider (E) ─┬─▶ Venom Spider (D) ─▶ Death Weaver (B) ─▶ Fate Weaver (S)
                                   ├─▶ Spirit Spider (D) ─▶ Moon Arachne (B, humanoid) ─▶ Arachne Empress (S)
                                   └─▶ Armored Spider (D) ─▶ Fortress Tarantula (B) ─▶ Titan Weaver (S)
                                                                          ↳ Final: Weaver of Ends (SSS)
```
Signature: **Thread Arts** (webs for traps, swinging, binding, puppetry), **Evil Eye** skills.

### 💀 SKELETON — *the undying knight*
```
Skeleton (F) ─▶ Skeleton Soldier (E) ─┬─▶ Skeleton Knight (D) ─▶ Death Knight (B) ─▶ Grave Lord (S)
                                      ├─▶ Skeleton Mage (D) ─▶ Lich (B, humanoid via illusion) ─▶ Archlich (S)
                                      └─▶ Bone Beast (D) ─▶ Bone Dragon (A) ─▶ Dracolich (S)
                                                                ↳ Final: No-Life Sovereign (SSS)
```
Signature: **Undying** (revive from bones), weak to Holy, immune to poison. A "Holy Skeleton" branch is possible if you pray at Morrowyn's shrines (Paladin skeleton!).

### 👺 GOBLIN — *the underdog king*
```
Goblin (F) ─▶ Hobgoblin (E) ─┬─▶ Goblin Champion (D) ─▶ Ogre (B) ─▶ Oni (A, humanoid) ─▶ Oni Lord (S)
                             ├─▶ Goblin Shaman (D) ─▶ Goblin Sage (B) ─▶ Kijin Sage (S)
                             └─▶ Goblin Rider (D, tame a wolf) ─▶ Goblin Warlord (B) ─▶ Goblin Emperor (S)
                                                                  ↳ Final: Primeval Oni / Demon God (SSS)
```
Signature: **Tribe system**: recruit goblins, build a village that evolves alongside you (monster nation-building).

### 🦎 HATCHLING (Lizard) — *the dragon dream*
```
Hatchling (F) ─▶ Drake (E) ─┬─▶ Fire Drake / Frost Drake / Thunder Drake (D) ─▶ Wyvern (B) ─▶ True Dragon (S)
                            ├─▶ Earth Drake (D) ─▶ Basilisk (B) ─▶ Gorgon Wyrm (S)
                            └─▶ Water Serpent (D) ─▶ Sea Serpent (B) ─▶ Leviathan Spawn (S)
                                                                ↳ Final: Elder Dragon Ascendant (SSS)
```
Signature: **Breath** attacks, flight at Wyvern, humanoid **Dragonkin form**. Slowest leveling, strongest scaling.

### 🌱 MANDRAKE SPROUT — *the secret starter (unlockable)*
```
Mandrake (F) ─▶ Walking Bloom (E) ─┬─▶ Dryad (D, humanoid early!) ─▶ Forest Nymph (B) ─▶ Sylvan Queen (S)
                                   ├─▶ Man-Eater Plant (D) ─▶ Corpse Flower (B) ─▶ Blight Mother (S)
                                   └─▶ Treant Sapling (D) ─▶ Elder Treant (B) ─▶ Ancient Grove (S)
                                                                ↳ Final: World Tree Seedling (SSS)
```
Signature: **Photosynthesis** (regen in sunlight), **Root** (stationary power mode), **Scream** (stun).

## 4. Monster-Origin Exclusive Content
- **The Hollow Nursery** prologue dungeon (survival, hunting, hiding from adventurers).
- **The Monster Council**: an underground parliament of intelligent monsters; a faction only you can join.
- **"Name-Giving"** trope: a strong being (e.g. Nyx the dragon) gives you a name, which triggers a mega-evolution.
- **Rival:** an adventurer party that "keeps almost killing you." They become your first human friends, or your first human meal.
