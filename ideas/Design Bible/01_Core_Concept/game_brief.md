# Game brief (one page)

Title: **AETHWYRD** (chosen by the user; the world is Aethwyrd). This page is a summary. `Canon.md` wins any conflict, `01_Core_Concept/vision.md` has the full pillars, and `Data/` is canonical for numbers. It has no spoilers.

## The game in one paragraph
An open-world isekai / high-fantasy adventurer RPG. You wake in a world with two moons and a card in your pocket that reads **Rank F**: a Status System has granted you a place at the bottom of the adventurers' ladder. You climb from F to SSS and beyond, build a class, tame and fight alongside monsters, and travel a large hand-designed world whose regions, creatures and people react to what you do.

## Pillars (from `vision.md` and `Canon.md`)
- **Slow life, not chores.** Progress without repetition debt; no daily decay, no mandatory upkeep.
- **A world that answers.** Regions, monsters and people react, but settlements are never destroyed and the world does not run an open-ended simulation.
- **Earned power.** Ranks F to SSS, 12 base classes growing to 48 expert, monster evolution, level cap 300.
- **Partners, not tools.** Companions and tamed monsters are characters with their own tracks and limits.
- **Discovery over markers.** The map shows what you have seen, heard of, or visited; puzzles and secrets are never mandatory gates.

## Scope in numbers (see `Canon.md` section 2)
40 surface regions plus 5 Underworld layers, the Great Labyrinth (120 floors) and a post-game area; 680 monster species in 6,754 population variants on 25 shared rigs; about 2,500 named NPCs; 11 main endings plus 1 secret; roughly 200 to 250 hours for the main story.
