# 13b — The Anime Opening, Music Direction & Memory Reel (v0.5)

## 1. The Anime Opening (Q8): "Your character's anime"
**Concept:** each Act begins with a ~90-second anime OP **rendered in-engine with YOUR character**: your face, outfit, class, weapon, and the companions you actually have (and empty spaces for the ones you lost). It's your own anime, introducing you into the world.

### Research
- Openings highlight the main characters, hint at plot arcs and sync to music. ([AV Club: six types of openings](https://www.avclub.com/the-six-types-of-anime-openings))
- An OP should focus on the main characters and their central relationships. ([Anime News Network, Answerman](https://www-d.animenewsnetwork.com/answerman/2016-01-22/.97839))
- OPs often show *more* action than the episodes. ([Action-Hogging Opening trope](https://tropedia.fandom.com/wiki/Action-Hogging_Opening))
- Song structure is usually intro → verse → pre-chorus → chorus (→ bridge). ([Cal Poly piece on OP music](https://grandavehousing.calpoly.edu/news/why-anime-openings-sound-so))

### Shot Template (data-driven, ~90 s, cuts synced to the music)
| Beat | Time | Shots (auto-filled from your save) |
|---|---|---|
| **Cold open** | 0–8 s | Black → your character opens their eyes; the two moons; the System window flickers *"Status granted"* |
| **Intro hook** | 8–20 s | Wide sweep over the **regions you've discovered**; title logo slams in |
| **Verse** | 20–40 s | Each **companion** gets a 2-second pose shot with their name; your **rival** (Kaito) turns away; Mochi bounces |
| **Pre-chorus** | 40–55 s | Villain silhouettes (only the ones you've *met*); the red moon; a hand reaching for yours |
| **Chorus** | 55–80 s | Full action montage: **your class's signature moves**, your Ultimate, an elemental combo, your party charging a boss **you've beaten** |
| **Outro** | 80–90 s | The party walks into a sunrise; **the fallen are shown as transparent silhouettes**; the Act title appears |
- **Changes per Act:** new shots, new villains, darker palette in Act V, the gold of ascension in Act VI.
- Skippable, rewatchable from the Journal ("Openings").

## 2. Music Direction (Q8, Q46)
**Mostly instrumental** for the world; **anime-rock / J-pop-style vocal energy** for bosses and big moments.

### Research
- **Yu-Peng Chen** (*Genshin Impact*): fuses regional traditional instruments with Western orchestral writing; clear melodies, lush neo-Romantic orchestration; Mondstadt used an Impressionist harmonic language with piano, tin whistle and medieval lute. ([Wikipedia: Music of Genshin Impact](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Music_of_Genshin_Impact), [Wikipedia: Yu-Peng Chen](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yu-Peng_Chen), [SCMP](https://www.scmp.com/lifestyle/arts/article/3321168/how-chinese-composer-who-wrote-genshin-impacts-music-breaking-classical-mould))
- **Hiroyuki Sawano** (*Attack on Titan*): a maximalist mix of electronic and orchestral music with high-energy vocal **insert songs** in action scenes. ([TV Tropes](https://www.tvtropes.org/pmwiki/pmwiki.php/Music/HiroyukiSawano))
- **Our formula:** *Chen for the world, Sawano for the fights.*

| Nox Dominion | Pipe organ, dark choir, dissonant strings | Tragic grandeur |
| Vesper (post-game) | Reversed Valdrisian hymns, glass harmonica | Haunted |

### Combat Music Layers
1. **Exploration** (instrumental) → 2. **Encounter** (percussion added) → 3. **Elite/Named** (full orchestra) → 4. **Boss** (orchestral-rock hybrid) → 5. **Ultimate / Awakening** (vocal insert-song chorus kicks in).
Music intensity also scales with the **Style Rank** meter: the higher your combo style, the more layers play.

### Production Path
- Ring 0–1: licensed or royalty-free placeholder tracks.
- Later: commission 2–3 composers (orchestral + rock). The **main theme** motif recurs everywhere (OP, menus, the final boss in a heroic arrangement).

## 3. Memory Reel + Photo Mode (Q7, Q76)
- The game **auto-captures** key moments: your first rank-up, each companion's recruitment, first kills of Named monsters, weddings, the tearjerkers, and every companion's last moment if they die.
- **Your Photo Mode shots** can be **pinned** to the Memory Reel (up to 50).
- **Ending credits:** auto-captures and your photos play as a slideshow-montage to the ending theme, with companion epilogue cards interleaved.
- The Memory Reel is viewable any time from the Journal, as an "album" of your journey.

## Story Presentation
- Big moments: **cinematic cutscenes**. Normal talk: in-world dialogue with choices. Camp: VN-style portrait scenes.
- **Memory Reel:** the game silently captures key moments (first rank-up, companion recruitments, boss kills, weddings, losses) and plays them during the ending credits.
- **Anime Opening per Act:** a 90-second OP with a song, changing visuals (it updates to show the companions *you* have and the ones you lost).
- **Playable flashbacks** for companions and previous Heroes.
- **System Voice is the only narrator.**
