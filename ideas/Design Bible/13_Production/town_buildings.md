# Brindlewick Buildings and Structures

> **Built.** Generated by `Tools/blender/town_buildings.py` (Blender 5.x, no add-ons) and placed by the scene builder (`Game/Assets/_Game/Editor/Ring0SceneBuilder.Town.cs`, plus the forest pieces in `Ring0SceneBuilder.Whisperwood.cs`). Models: `Game/Assets/_Game/Art/Models/Buildings/<id>.fbx`. Source: `Art/Blender/town_buildings.blend`. Preview: `13_Production/screenshots/town_buildings_blender.png`.

## Style

A warm storybook village (the mood of the concept art, none of its assets):
- **Walls and footings:** stone footings with cream plaster walls under dark timber framing (posts, beams, studs, corner braces).
- **Roofs:** stepped shingle or thatch roofs in blue, red, green and slate.
- **Windows and doors:** shuttered windows with timber frames and sills; plank doors with iron rings and stone steps.
- **Detail:** chimneys, hanging signs, banners and lanterns.

Every model is a single mesh with a few flat colours, about 100–2,300 triangles.

**Conventions:**
- Units are metres; ground at z = 0; each model is centred on its origin.
- The front door faces −Y in Blender, which is +Z in Unity at yaw 0.
- FBX exports with Unity axes and transforms applied, the same settings as the Ring 0 monsters.

The plaza where you start stays open for the NPCs and the movement test course; the buildings ring it.

| North wall | The gatehouse at the north gate, with palisade runs out to the edge of the town ward |

| Streets | Lamp posts and crates along the roads |
| Treeline camp | Tents behind Fennick, Eolande and Dax, Lyra's campfire, Pell's wagon; the trees leave the camp clear |

**Names and colliders:**
- **Waystones** keep their object names (`Waystone`, `Waystone_Whisperwood`), so saved attunements still match.
- **Colliders:** buildings and props get mesh colliders; the plaza and roads are flat and walkable with none.
- **Removed:** two of the eight camera-test pillars that stood in the gateway and inside the guild hall.

## Regenerating

```bash
blender -b --factory-startup -P Tools/blender/town_buildings.py
```
(Add `-- --only guild_hall,well` to build just a few.) Then rebuild the scene with *Ascend ▸ Ring 0 ▸ Build Graybox Scene*, or in batch mode with `Ascend.EditorTools.Ring0SceneBuilder.BuildBatch`.

If no model has been exported, the builder falls back to the old gate blockout and primitive stand-ins, so the scene always builds.
