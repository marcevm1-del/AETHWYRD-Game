# Monster Classification & Guild System bible: integration assessment (2026-11-09)

The PDF's own prompt says to inspect the repository first, report conflicts and propose the least disruptive integration. This is that report.

## 4. Conflicts with Canon and project rules (nothing applied)
| # | Conflict | Least disruptive reading |
|---|---|---|
| C1 | **Canon 4d: "no enemy hierarchy (no captains, warchiefs or power struggles)."** Bible section 7 has an Emperor/King/Lord/General hierarchy, section 12 has dynamic faction simulation, and stage 7 to 9 labels are Lord, King, Emperor | Treat stages 7 to 9 as **species names only** (like the Goblin King that already exists in `bestiary_lore.md`). No ranks, captains, warbands or power struggles at runtime. Faction state stays at region level (siege, liberation, flags). The hierarchy table is not imported |
| C2 | **Canon 4d: named monsters grow with region state, never from defeating the player.** Bible evolution conditions include "defeating a powerful opponent" and bounty targets that "gain allies" | Allowed only when the trigger is region state, ecology or story flags. Never from killing or beating the player. The Nemesis-patent lawyer review item still applies |
| C3 | **Canon section 4: player cannot own a guild.** Bible ladder ends in Master and Grandmaster, with voting rights and titles | Fine as **membership** tiers. The player may reach Officer or Master as an honour but never holds guild property or runs one. Grandmaster stays story-only |
| C4 | **Teen rating, no fanservice.** The bible lists Succubus/Incubus (demon branch) and an assassins' network with "covert elimination" contracts | **Succubus/Incubus branch is not imported as a playable path** (the data lists it only because it is in the PDF; note it as skipped when species are assigned). Veiled Hand contracts stay implied, never graphic, and need a Canon OK |
| C5 | **Commercial game: ideas only.** Group names are folklore staples (dragon, hydra, lich, kraken, fenrir) | No third-party names, text or stats were copied. Fine. Keep checking when species are named |
| C6 | **Tamed evolution.** Game: one permanent evolution at Bond 5 | Bible stages describe how a species can look across its life. Tamed partners still take **one** step. The nine stages do not become a tamed-monster grind |
| C7 | **Naming.** The PDF says "Adventurers' Consortium" | Game keeps "Adventurers' Guild" |

## 5. Proposed Canon section 6 rows (need the user's OK; added to Canon.md as M1 to M6)
| ID | Proposal |
|---|---|
