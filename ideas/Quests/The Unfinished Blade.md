# The Unfinished Blade

> One sword blank hangs unfinished above Torvald's forge, and he changes the subject whenever you look at it. He will not say why, but he will let you gather the iron and scrap to bring the old forge back to heat. Do that, then choose: ask about the blank, or leave it be until he speaks. He sings the forge songs either way.
