# The Barrow-Host

> Skeletal knights and kings in rusted crowns.

Ashgrave's host from the Greyhollow barrows.

## At a glance

- **Home:** [[Regions/Aldmere|Aldmere]]
- **Source board:** Undead Skeleton Clan
- **Leader:** Ashgrave, the Oathless Baron
- **Story hook:** A skeleton knight in Ashgrave's host still wears the colours of a living family.

## Origin

The Barrow-Host are the dead of Greyhollow: the knights and soldiers who kept the oath their lord broke and died holding the line. When the barrows opened, they rose and followed the one man who could not follow them in life.

## Society

An army of the dead under Ashgrave, organised as they were in life: knights, wights, foot-soldiers and the ancient crowned dead from older barrows.

## Beliefs

The oath is all that is left. The living forgot them; they will make the living remember.

## Relations

The siege-army of Aldmere; hated and feared by the Hearthbound; some still wear the colours of living families.

## Conflict and defence

Disciplined ranks of the dead, wights who drain the living and knights in rusted plate.

## Trade

None.

## Strengths

Numbers, no fear, no fatigue.

## Vulnerabilities

Silver, holy magic, remembering their names.

## Gameplay role

Aldmere's siege faction; naming the dead weakens them and is a non-violent counter.

## Members and roles

- **Oathless Knights** — Elite: Ashgrave's skeletal knights.
- **Barrow-Wights** — Mage: Undead who drain the living.
- **Bone Soldiers** — Infantry: The marching dead of the barrows.
- **Crowned Dead** — Royal: Ancient kings in rusted crowns.

## Notable figures

- **Ashgrave, the Oathless Baron** (Leader and Tyrant): Leads the dead who kept the oath he broke.
- **Sir Edwyn the Faithful** (Oathless Knight): Still wears the colours of a living family in Ashby.
- **The Barrow-Crown** (Crowned Dead): An ancient king from a barrow older than Aldmere, who serves no one.

## Quest seeds

- Return Sir Edwyn to his family's memory and lay him to rest.
- Learn the Barrow-Crown's story before Ashgrave uses it.
