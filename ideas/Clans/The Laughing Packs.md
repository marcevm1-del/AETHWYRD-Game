# The Laughing Packs

> Spotted hyena-folk raiders and scouts, some in Kade's pay.

Scavenger clans who follow the herds and the armies; half serve Kade, half despise him.

## At a glance

- **Home:** [[Regions/Fanghold|Fanghold]]
- **Source board:** Hyena Clan
- **Leader:** Chief Shenna Redgrin
- **Story hook:** A Laughing Pack chief will betray Kade if someone can promise her people the well.

## Origin

The Laughing Packs are hyena clans who have followed the herds and armies across the steppe for as long as there have been herds and armies. They eat what others leave, and they always find more than others think they leave.

## Society

Matriarchal packs led by the strongest females; males serve as scouts. Packs split and merge constantly.

## Beliefs

Laughter frightens the dark. Only a fool fights a battle someone else can fight for you.

## Relations

Half the packs serve Varrick Kade; the other half despise him. All of them hate the Hoofbound.

## Conflict and defence

Night raids, harrying and feigned retreats.

## Trade

Scavenging, scouting and mercenary work.

## Strengths

Cunning, endurance, night vision.

## Vulnerabilities

Fickle loyalties.

## Gameplay role

Turning the Laughing Packs splits Kade's forces during the Fanghold siege.

## Members and roles

- **Redgrin Raiders** — Raider: Hyena-folk in Kade's pay.
- **Bone-Pickers** — Scavenger: Follow the herds and the armies.
- **Matriarch Packs** — Ruler: Clans led by the strongest females.
- **Night-Laughers** — Scout: Hunters who laugh to confuse their prey.

## Notable figures

- **Chief Shenna Redgrin** (Matriarch): Will betray Kade if someone promises her people the well.
- **Gikk** (Night-Laugher): A scout who laughs at everything, including danger.
- **Old Bone-Picker Maa** (Elder): Knows every battlefield on the steppe.

## Quest seeds

- Negotiate Shenna's betrayal of Kade.
- Find a lost herd with Maa's help.
