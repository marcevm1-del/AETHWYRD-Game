# The Hill-Orcs

> Orcs of every temperament: shamans, warriors, a red-bearded elder.

Wild orc clans of Moraveil's northern hills; some follow Grakkus, some fear him.

## At a glance

- **Home:** [[Regions/Moraveil|Moraveil]]
- **Source board:** Orc Clan
- **Leader:** Shaman Urzha Redbeard
- **Story hook:** An orc shaman wants to warn the truce council, and needs an escort to reach it alive.

## Origin

The Hill-Orcs are the wild orc clans of Moraveil's northern hills, herders and raiders since before the kingdom. Some have followed Grakkus; others fear what he will make of them.

## Society

Clans led by red-bearded shamans in peace and war-chiefs in war; herders, warriors and diplomats each have their place.

## Beliefs

The spirits of the hills speak through fire and smoke. Strength serves the clan, not the self.

## Relations

Divided: Axe-Brothers follow Grakkus, Truce-Seekers want to join Moraveil's council, herders want to be left alone.

## Conflict and defence

Axe-Brothers in heavy furs; shamans with fire-spirits.

## Trade

Goats, wool, furs and hill-herbs.

## Strengths

Strength, endurance, hill-craft.

## Vulnerabilities

Divided loyalties.

## Gameplay role

Turning Hill-Orc clans weakens Grakkus's siege; a possible future member of the truce.

## Members and roles

- **Red-Beard Shamans** — Shaman: Spirit-speakers of the orc clans.
- **Axe-Brothers** — Warrior: Warriors who follow Grakkus.
- **Hill-Herders** — Commoner: Orcs who herd goats and avoid war.
- **Truce-Seekers** — Diplomat: Orcs who want to join the Moraveil truce.

## Notable figures

- **Shaman Urzha Redbeard** (Leader): Wants to warn the truce council about Grakkus.
- **Grosh Axebrother** (War-chief): Follows Grakkus, and has doubts.
- **Little Mog** (Herder): Wants to sell her goats in the Moraveil market.

## Quest seeds

- Escort Urzha to the truce council alive.
- Persuade Grosh to abandon Grakkus.
