# The Brew-Bears

> Panda-folk brewers, monks and martial artists.

Shen-Lu's brewers and wandering fighters, beloved at every crossroads.

## At a glance

- **Home:** [[Regions/Shen-Lu|Shen-Lu]]
- **Source board:** Panda Clan
- **Leader:** Master Brewer Bao Tuan
- **Story hook:** A Brew-Bear's spirit wine might restore drained qi, if someone can fetch the herbs.

## Origin

The Brew-Bears are panda-folk who came to Shen-Lu as wandering brewers, found the mountain water perfect for spirit wine and never left. Every sect owes them a debt, usually unpaid.

## Society

Brewing families who wander between towns, settling for a season wherever the water is good. Master Brewers lead; Drunken Fists protect; Bamboo Wardens tend the groves.

## Beliefs

A good drink makes peace and a good fight makes friends. Never brew in anger; the wine remembers.

## Relations

Friends of every sect, especially the Vanari; welcome at every crossroads inn; enemies of no one except bad tea.

## Conflict and defence

Drunken-fist martial arts that look like stumbling until they land; staff-work with bamboo poles.

## Trade

Spirit wine, tea, medicinal brews and bamboo.

## Strengths

Strength, martial arts, brewing.

## Vulnerabilities

Sleepiness; love of comfort.

## Gameplay role

Spirit-wine brewing restores qi in Shen-Lu; a drunken-fist mentor.

## Members and roles

- **Master Brewers** — Artisan: Brewers of spirit wine.
- **Drunken Fists** — Martial artist: Fight better after a drink.
- **Bamboo Wardens** — Guard: Guardians of the bamboo forests.
- **Wandering Pandas** — Wanderer: Travel the crossroads, seeking good tea.

## Notable figures

- **Master Brewer Bao Tuan** (Leader): Believes his spirit wine might restore drained qi.
- **Yuyu** (Bamboo Warden): Guards the bamboo forests from the Colossus's drain.
- **Old Bamboo** (Wandering Panda): Always looking for good tea; finds trouble instead.

## Quest seeds

- Fetch the herbs for Bao's qi-restoring wine from a grey peak.
- Help Yuyu defend the bamboo forest from the Colossus.
