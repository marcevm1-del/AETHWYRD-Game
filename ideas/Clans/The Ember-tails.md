# The Ember-tails

> Red-panda-folk adventurers, archers and street kids.

Cheerful wanderers of the bamboo villages.

## At a glance

- **Home:** [[Regions/Hoshizora|Hoshizora]]
- **Source board:** Red Panda Clan
- **Leader:** Kip Embertail, Street-Captain
- **Story hook:** An Ember-tail kid has stolen one of Kurogane's masks.

## Origin

The Ember-tails are red-panda-folk of Hoshizora's bamboo villages, cheerful wanderers who arrived generations ago with tea carts and stayed for the festivals.

## Society

Loose bands led by street-captains, often young; elders run tea stalls and tell stories.

## Beliefs

Every festival is a chance to steal a lantern and give it back with a better candle.

## Relations

Beloved by villagers, annoying to samurai, an unexpected thorn in Kurogane's side.

## Conflict and defence

Bamboo bows and quick hands.

## Trade

Tea, carved bamboo and stolen-then-returned lanterns.

## Strengths

Speed, stealth, cheer.

## Vulnerabilities

Small; reckless.

## Gameplay role

Street-urchin informants in Hoshizora; mask-theft questline reveals how Kurogane's masks work.

## Members and roles

- **Street-Captains** — Leader: Leaders of the red-panda street gangs.
- **Bamboo Archers** — Warrior: Red-panda archers of the bamboo villages.
- **Lantern-Thieves** — Thief: Steal lanterns from the spirit festivals.
- **Tea-Sellers** — Merchant: Sell tea from carts.

## Notable figures

- **Kip Embertail** (Street-Captain): Has stolen one of Kurogane's masks.
- **Granny Hoshi** (Tea-Seller): Knows every rumour in the bamboo sea.
- **Rin** (Bamboo Archer): The best shot in the villages.

## Quest seeds

- Protect Kip from Kurogane's hunters and study the stolen mask.
- Help Granny Hoshi set up a festival stall during the siege.
