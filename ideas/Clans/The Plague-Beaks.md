# The Plague-Beaks

> Crow-folk in plague-doctor masks and long coats.

Battlefield healers and grave-keepers who follow every army.

## At a glance

- **Home:** [[Regions/Strife Marches|Strife Marches]]
- **Source board:** Raven/Crow Clan
- **Leader:** Doctor Corvin Ashbeak
- **Story hook:** A Plague-Beak has noticed that the Choir's dead do not rot.

## Origin

The Plague-Beaks are crow-folk who have followed armies since the first war, tending the wounded and burying the dead. They wear beaked masks filled with herbs to keep the sickness of battlefields away.

## Society

A loose order of doctors, grave-keepers, scouts and raven-mages, sworn to treat all sides equally.

## Beliefs

Death is a doorway; a good doctor stands beside it and asks it to wait.

## Relations

Welcome on every battlefield and trusted by no army; now deeply afraid of the Choir of Chains.

## Conflict and defence

They avoid fighting, but raven-mages can command the dead to lie still.

## Trade

Healing, burial, medicine and information on troop movements.

## Strengths

Healing, knowledge of death.

## Vulnerabilities

Neutrality makes enemies on all sides.

## Gameplay role

Healers who revive companions on battlefields; Plague-Beak research reveals the Choir's weakness.

## Members and roles

- **Beak Doctors** — Healer: Plague-doctor crows who heal the wounded.
- **Grave-Keepers** — Priest: Bury the dead of every army.
- **Crow-Scouts** — Scout: Follow armies and report their movements.
- **Raven-Mages** — Mage: Crows who speak with the dead.

## Notable figures

- **Doctor Corvin Ashbeak** (Leader): Has noticed the Choir's dead do not rot.
- **Sister Rookwyn** (Grave-Keeper): Buries the dead of every side with the same care.
- **Caw** (Crow-Scout): Watches every army in the Marches.

## Quest seeds

- Investigate why the Choir's dead do not rot.
- Help Sister Rookwyn bury the dead at the Field of Coins.
