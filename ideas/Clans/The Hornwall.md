# The Hornwall

> Rhino-folk masons and brawlers, heavily armoured.

Bastion's master masons, who build the new rings.

## At a glance

- **Home:** [[Regions/Bastion|Bastion]]
- **Source board:** Rhino Clan
- **Leader:** Master Mason Rhinald Stonebrow
- **Story hook:** A Hornwall mason has found sabotage in the foundations.

## Origin

The Hornwall are rhino-folk masons who came to Bastion when its second ring was raised, hired for a season. They stayed, and they have built every ring since.

## Society

Mason-crews led by Master Masons; haulers carry the stone, rammers clear the breaches and Iron-Horns hold them. A Hornwall's horn is notched for every ring they have helped raise.

## Beliefs

A wall is only as strong as its foundation, and a people only as strong as its builders. A stone from the broken ring goes into every new one.

## Relations

Inseparable from the Shieldwall, honoured by the Warden, and the first target of Thrakka's saboteurs.

## Conflict and defence

Rammers charge through enemy lines; Iron-Horns hold a breach alone with shields of fitted stone.

## Trade

Masonry, quarrying, siegeworks and stone sold to the south.

## Strengths

Strength, masonry, armour.

## Vulnerabilities

Slow; bad tempers.

## Gameplay role

Wall-building and repair during Bastion sieges; breach-defence encounters.

## Members and roles

- **Master Masons** — Artisan: Build Bastion's rings.
- **Rammers** — Warrior: Charge through enemy lines.
- **Stone-Haulers** — Worker: Carry the stone for the walls.
- **Iron-Horns** — Elite: Armoured rhino champions.

## Notable figures

- **Master Mason Rhinald Stonebrow** (Leader): Has found sabotage in the new ring's foundations.
- **Hauler Bertha** (Stone-Hauler): Can carry a stone the size of a cart up a ladder.
- **Iron-Horn Dace** (Elite): Held a breach alone for an hour during the last siege.

## Quest seeds

- Find the saboteur in the masons' crews before the next charge.
- Hold a breach with Dace during the next horde attack.
