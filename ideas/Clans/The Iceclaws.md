# The Iceclaws

> Polar-bear-folk shamans and warriors with lightning in their fists.

Shamans and hunters of the ice, who call storms against the leviathan.

## At a glance

- **Home:** [[Regions/Whitewater Reach|Whitewater Reach]]
- **Source board:** Polar Bear Clan
- **Leader:** Storm-Shaman Ulluq Whitefang
- **Story hook:** An Iceclaw shaman is losing her storms.

## Origin

The Iceclaws are polar-bear shamans and hunters who learned to call storms from the auroras over Whitewater Reach. They have hunted the floes alongside the villages since the first ice-fishing hole was cut.

## Society

Dens led by Ice-Mothers; storm-shamans are chosen by the auroras, which are said to flicker over a cub's den when it is born with the gift.

## Beliefs

Storms are the sky's breath; a shaman borrows them and must give them back.

## Relations

Allies of the Floe-Knights and the Orca Lords; Ysgrath's cold is stealing their storms.

## Conflict and defence

Lightning called down on the ice, claws and rune-carved spears.

## Trade

Furs, ivory, storm-warnings and rune-carved charms.

## Strengths

Strength, storm-magic, cold resistance.

## Vulnerabilities

Heat; the Ice-Jaw's cold drains their power.

## Gameplay role

Storm-calling abilities; aurora events boost lightning magic in Whitewater Reach.

## Members and roles

- **Storm-Shamans** — Shaman: Call lightning across the ice.
- **White Hunters** — Hunter: Hunt seals and walrus on the floes.
- **Rune-Bears** — Mage: Polar bears who carve runes in ice.
- **Ice-Mothers** — Elder: Keepers of the dens.

## Notable figures

- **Storm-Shaman Ulluq Whitefang** (Leader): Her storms are weakening.
- **Ice-Mother Siku** (Elder): Keeps the dens and the old aurora-songs.
- **Nanuq the White Hunter** (Hunter): Tracks seals and leviathans alike.

## Quest seeds

- Find out what is stealing Ulluq's storms.
- Hunt with Nanuq on the floes.
