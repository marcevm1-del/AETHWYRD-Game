# The Snowmanes

> White-furred yetis, huge and shaggy.

Snowline dwellers who carry goods up the Long Stair for the Heavenfolk.

## At a glance

- **Home:** [[Regions/Heavenreach|Heavenreach]]
- **Source board:** Yeti Clan
- **Leader:** Porter-Chief Yuval Frostmane
- **Story hook:** A Snowmane porter has seen Aquilon take a child.

## Origin

The Snowmanes are yetis of Heavenreach's snowline who have carried goods up the Long Stair for the Heavenfolk for generations, in return for honey, cloth and the right to live in the high meadows.

## Society

Porter-families led by a Porter-Chief; Stair-Carriers do the hauling; Avalanche-Callers keep the slopes safe; hermits live alone above the clouds.

## Beliefs

A load carried is a promise kept. The mountain gives way to those who listen to it.

## Relations

Old allies of the Heavenfolk, friendly with the valley folk, frightened of Aquilon's rocs.

## Conflict and defence

Avalanches called down on enemies, great strength and thick fur.

## Trade

Portering up the Long Stair, snow-honey and mountain herbs.

## Strengths

Strength, cold resistance, mountain-craft.

## Vulnerabilities

Heat; lowland air.

## Gameplay role

Snowmane porters carry the player's gear up the Long Stair; avalanche allies in Heavenreach.

## Members and roles

- **Porter-Chiefs** — Leader: Lead the porters of the Long Stair.
- **Stair-Carriers** — Worker: Carry goods up eleven thousand steps.
- **Avalanche-Callers** — Shaman: Can start or stop an avalanche.
- **Snowline Hermits** — Hermit: Live alone above the clouds.

## Notable figures

- **Porter-Chief Yuval Frostmane** (Leader): Has seen Aquilon take a child.
- **Snowdrift** (Avalanche-Caller): Can start or stop an avalanche with a single shout.
- **The Hermit Above** (Snowline Hermit): Lives alone above the clouds and talks to the rocs.

## Quest seeds

- Carry a load up the Long Stair with the Snowmanes.
- Find the Hermit Above and learn what he knows about Aquilon.
