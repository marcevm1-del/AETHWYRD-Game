# The Lagoon Folk

> Merfolk with fins, tridents and coral crowns; some with octopus hair.

The sea-elves and merfolk of Lirian's outer lagoon, trading through water-gates.

## At a glance

- **Home:** [[Regions/Lirian|Lirian]]
- **Source board:** Merfolk Clan
- **Leader:** Queen Corallin of the Water-Gates
- **Story hook:** The Lagoon Folk know where the Drowned Choir sleeps by day.

## Origin

The Lagoon Folk are the merfolk and sea-elves of Lirian's outer lagoon, who were living in the old city before it drowned. When the sea rose, they stayed; the humans built the new city on top.

## Society

A water-court under a queen, with noble houses, trident guards and coral-weaver guilds. They trade with the city through carved water-gates.

## Beliefs

The sea keeps what it is given; the old city is a grave, and graves are not for sirens.

## Relations

Trading partners of the doge, rivals of the Canal Otters for diving rights, enemies of the Drowned Choir.

## Conflict and defence

Trident Guard fight in currents; sea-elf mages call the tide.

## Trade

Pearls, coral jewellery, sea-silk and secrets of the drowned city.

## Strengths

Water combat, tide-magic, knowledge of the drowned city.

## Vulnerabilities

Dry land; city politics.

## Gameplay role

Underwater sections in Lirian; water-gate fast travel.

## Members and roles

- **Water-Gate Nobles** — Ruler: Merfolk aristocracy of the outer lagoon.
- **Sea-Elves** — Mage: Elves who live beneath the water.
- **Trident Guard** — Warrior: Defenders of the lagoon.
- **Coral-Weavers** — Artisan: Grow coral into jewellery and buildings.

## Notable figures

- **Queen Corallin of the Water-Gates** (Leader): Knows where the Drowned Choir sleeps by day.
- **Lord Nerith Saltblood** (Sea-elf noble): Thinks the city should be left to the sirens.
- **Pearl** (Coral-Weaver): A young merfolk artist who sneaks into the canals to sell her work.

## Quest seeds

- Lead a raid on the Drowned Belltowers by day with Corallin's guard.
- Expose Nerith's dealings with the sirens.
