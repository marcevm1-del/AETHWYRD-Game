# The Undercity Whiskers

> Rat-folk in scavenged armour, alchemists with green fire, swashbucklers.

Rat-folk of Cinderhole's lowest tunnels, smugglers and mask-makers.

## At a glance

- **Home:** [[Regions/Cinderhole|Cinderhole]]
- **Source board:** Mouse/Rat Clan
- **Leader:** Mask-Maker Skritch Velvetnose
- **Story hook:** A rat-folk mask-maker can make a mask that filters Vasque's smoke.

## Origin

The Undercity Whiskers are rat-folk who came to Cinderhole with the first miners, living in the walls of the mine. When the seams caught fire and the humans fled, the Whiskers stayed and learned to breathe the smoke.

## Society

Gangs and guilds in the lowest tunnels: mask-makers, green-fire alchemists, tunnel-runners and rapier-swinging swashbucklers. Gang bosses answer to the mask-makers, because without masks no one breathes.

## Beliefs

Air is life and a good mask is a good friend. Everyone in Cinderhole owes a rat a favour, whether they know it or not.

## Relations

Smugglers for every gang, rivals of the Velvet Knives, enemies of Vasque when he closes the chimneys.

## Conflict and defence

Rapiers, alchemical bombs, smoke-bombs and ambushes in tunnels too small for anyone else.

## Trade

Smoke-masks, alchemy, smuggling and tunnel-guiding.

## Strengths

Stealth, alchemy, tunnels, resistance to smoke.

## Vulnerabilities

Small; distrusted by everyone above ground.

## Gameplay role

Smoke-mask crafting is required for Cinderhole's choked districts; rat tunnels provide shortcuts.

## Members and roles

- **Mask-Makers** — Artisan: Make Cinderhole's smoke-masks.
- **Green-Fire Alchemists** — Alchemist: Rat-folk brewers of volatile potions.
- **Tunnel-Runners** — Smuggler: Know every tunnel under the crater.
- **Swashbucklers** — Duelist: Rapier-wielding rat duellists.

## Notable figures

- **Mask-Maker Skritch Velvetnose** (Leader): Can make a mask that filters even Vasque's magic smoke.
- **Brindle Greenfire** (Alchemist): Makes bombs that are mostly safe and potions that are mostly not.
- **Captain Squeak** (Swashbuckler): Fancies himself a hero, and occasionally is one.

## Quest seeds

- Help Skritch make the smoke-mask that could free Cinderhole from Vasque.
- Smuggle a refugee family out through the tunnels.
