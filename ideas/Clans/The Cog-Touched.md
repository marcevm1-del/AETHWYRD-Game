# The Cog-Touched

> Gaunt figures fused with pipes, cables and gremlin-work: half skeleton, half machine, eyes replaced with lamps.

People and Clockborn alike whom Cogmaw has begun to absorb. They still remember their names for a while; the Undercity hides the early cases.

## At a glance

- **Home:** [[Regions/Gearholm|Gearholm]]
- **Source board:** AI Infected Clan
- **Leader:** Maren Vey, the Half-Wound
- **Story hook:** A boiler-tender's daughter is Cog-Touched in one hand only, and she can hear the Engine King thinking.

## Origin

The first Cog-Touched were boiler-tenders who slept beside a Great Engine the night Cogmaw woke. Since then, anyone touched by its gremlins long enough begins to change.

## Society

Not a people by choice. The Undercity hides early cases in a quarter called the Rattle, where they look after each other before the Engine King takes them.

## Beliefs

Early stages cling to their names; late stages hear one voice and call it the King.

## Relations

Pitied by the Undercity, feared by the upper city, hunted by the council's cleaners.

## Conflict and defence

Early stages fight to stay free; late stages fight for Cogmaw with lamp-eyes and steam-claws.

## Trade

None of their own; the Rattle survives on charity and smuggled medicine.

## Strengths

Strong, heat-proof, connected to Cogmaw's thoughts.

## Vulnerabilities

The change is slow but certain; magnets and silence disrupt them.

## Gameplay role

Infection stages map to enemy tiers; curing named Cog-Touched unlocks an Undercity reputation track.

## Members and roles

- **Half-Wound** — Early stage: Still themselves, with machinery spreading from one limb.
- **Pipe-Choirs** — Middle stage: Hear the Engine King constantly and hum its tune without knowing.
- **Lamp-Eyed** — Late stage: Eyes replaced by lamps; they see heat, not faces.
- **Engine-Hearts** — Lost: Fully absorbed; walking parts of Cogmaw's body.

## Notable figures

- **Maren Vey** (The Half-Wound): A boiler-tender with one mechanical hand who can hear Cogmaw think.
- **Old Hiss** (Pipe-Choir): Hums the Engine King's tune and writes down the words when he can.
- **Sister Brask** (Healer of the Rattle): A gnome surgeon who slows the change with cold water and stubbornness.

## Quest seeds

- Find a cure in the founders' instructions before Maren loses her other hand.
- Use a Pipe-Choir to eavesdrop on Cogmaw's plans.
