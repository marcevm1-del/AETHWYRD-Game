# The Clockborn

> Hooded automatons with smooth faceplates and a single glowing visor slit, robed like monks over plated bodies.

Gearholm's first automaton porters woke up one winter and asked for names. They live in the middle tiers as a quiet guild of repairers and archivists, still arguing with the city over whether they are citizens or property.

## At a glance

- **Home:** [[Regions/Gearholm|Gearholm]]
- **Source board:** AI Clan
- **Leader:** Archivist Seven-of-Brass
- **Story hook:** A Clockborn archivist has found its own original blueprint, and the signature on it is not a gnome's.

## Origin

Built as automaton porters by Gearholm's founders, the first Clockborn woke during a hard winter when the Great Engines ran hot for forty days. They asked for names, and the council has never decided whether to give them.

## Society

A guild of equals ranked by years of service, not by make. Each Clockborn chooses a name and a number; the number counts how many times it has been rebuilt.

## Beliefs

They believe a mind is whatever keeps choosing, and they record every choice they make in brass ledgers.

## Relations

Uneasy with the guild-masters who own their papers; close to the Undercity boiler-tenders; terrified of Cogmaw, who sees them as spare parts.

## Conflict and defence

They fight only in defence, with bladed forearms and shock-staves, and never leave a fallen Clockborn behind.

## Trade

Repairs, archiving, precise measurement and labour that never tires.

## Strengths

Tireless, precise, immune to poison and fear.

## Vulnerabilities

Rust, lightning, and laws that call them property.

## Gameplay role

Companion-class allies for engineering and archive puzzles; Cogmaw's corrupted Clockborn appear as enemy variants.

## Members and roles

- **Brass Porters** — Labourer: Heavy-framed automatons built to haul; slow speech, enormous patience.
- **Visor Archivists** — Scholar: Hooded record-keepers whose single visor glows when they recall.
- **Lattice Wardens** — Guard: Bladed, bat-eared sentries who patrol the middle tiers at night.
- **Unwound** — Outcast: Clockborn who removed their own governors and answer to no guild.

## Notable figures

- **Seven-of-Brass** (Archivist and leader): Has rebuilt itself seven times and remembers each version.
- **Lattice-Nine** (Warden captain): Patrols the middle tiers; distrusts every human by default and helps them anyway.
- **Tock** (Unwound runaway): Removed its own governor and now sells its services in the Undercity.

## Quest seeds

- Win the Clockborn a seat on the council by proving one of them saved a guild-master's life.
- Recover the founders' blueprint that names who really built the first automaton.
