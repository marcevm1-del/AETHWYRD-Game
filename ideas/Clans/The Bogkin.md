# The Bogkin

> Frog- and toad-folk in reed cloaks and wide hats, from samurai-straight duelists to round, cheerful herbalists.

Greymoor's frogfolk live in the deeper meres and trade with the stilt-villages by night. They croak in harmonies that carry under the fog, and the Hollow Abbot's silence is killing their songs.

## At a glance

- **Home:** [[Regions/Greymoor|Greymoor]]
- **Source board:** Amphibian Clan
- **Leader:** Choir-Mother Gullagrum
- **Story hook:** A Bogkin choir-master wants to sing against the Choir of Silence, and needs someone to stand with her while she does.

## Origin

Frogfolk lived in the clear lake that became Greymoor. When the abbey sank, they moved to the deeper meres and kept singing.

## Society

Families of a few dozen around a single choir-mother, whose song holds the family together.

## Beliefs

Song is life; silence is death. Every child gets a note of its own at birth.

## Relations

Night-traders with the stilt-villages, friends with the Shellmoot, terrified of the Hollow Abbot.

## Conflict and defence

Reed duelists strike from the water; choirs stun enemies with harmonies.

## Trade

Medicine, frog-silk and reed-craft.

## Strengths

Water, song, poison immunity.

## Vulnerabilities

Dry heat and silence.

## Gameplay role

Bard-style song mechanics; Bogkin choirs can counter silence effects in Greymoor.

## Members and roles

- **Reed Duelists** — Warrior: Long-legged frog swordsmen who strike from the water.
- **Toad Herbalists** — Healer: Round, cheerful brewers of swamp medicine.
- **Choir-Croakers** — Singer: Harmony-singers whose songs carry under the fog.
- **Glass-Skins** — Scout: Translucent tree-frogs nearly invisible in the reeds.

## Notable figures

- **Choir-Mother Gullagrum** (Leader): Ancient and enormous; her voice is fading.
- **Pell Swiftreed** (Reed Duelist): Young and reckless; wants to fight the Abbot alone.
- **Mossy Gip** (Toad Herbalist): Brews the fen's best medicine and worst tea.

## Quest seeds

- Stand with Gullagrum as she sings against the Choir of Silence.
- Recover a choir-mother's note stolen by the silence.
