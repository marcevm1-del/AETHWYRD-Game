# The Warhorse Kin

> Horse-folk warriors and breeders.

Breeders of Vharlen's warhorses.

## At a glance

- **Home:** [[Regions/Vharlen|Vharlen]]
- **Source board:** Horse Clan
- **Leader:** Breeder Equina Strideheart
- **Story hook:** A Warhorse Kin breeder has a foal that can outrun demons.

## Origin

The Warhorse Kin are horse-folk who have bred Vharlen's warhorses since the empire's founding, and they say the first emperor rode one of their ancestors into battle.

## Society

Breeding families led by Grey Mares; Breeders raise the foals; Chargers form the heavy cavalry; Mustangs scout the wild plains.

## Beliefs

A good horse is a good soldier, and a good soldier is a free one.

## Relations

Partners of the Iron Legions, proud of their service and resentful of being treated as stock.

## Conflict and defence

Heavy cavalry charges that break demon lines; Mustangs harass and scout.

## Trade

Warhorses, breeding and cavalry training.

## Strengths

Speed, strength, endurance.

## Vulnerabilities

Rough terrain; being treated as property.

## Gameplay role

Ground warhorse mounts from Vharlen; cavalry charges in sieges.

## Members and roles

- **Breeders** — Artisan: Breed Vharlen's warhorses.
- **Chargers** — Cavalry: Horse-folk heavy cavalry.
- **Mustangs** — Scout: Wild, fast and free.
- **Grey Mares** — Elder: Elders of the horse-clans.

## Notable figures

- **Breeder Equina Strideheart** (Leader): Has a foal that can outrun demons.
- **Charger Brutus Ironhoof** (Cavalry): Leads the heavy horse and has never lost a charge.
- **Wild Sorrel** (Mustang): Free, fast and refuses to serve the legions.

## Quest seeds

- Protect Equina's foal from demon raiders.
- Ride with Brutus's cavalry in the next charge.
