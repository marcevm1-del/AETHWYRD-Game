# The Vanari

> Monkey-folk martial artists with staffs and tails, from wiry red-furred acrobats to grey-maned elders.

A sect of monkey-kin who claim their founder stole a peach of immortality from the mountain gods and was sealed under a peak for it. They are mischievous, loyal and the best climbers on the Hundred Stair.

## At a glance

- **Home:** [[Regions/Shen-Lu|Shen-Lu]]
- **Source board:** Ape Clan
- **Leader:** Grandmaster Hou Shan, the Peach Thief's Heir
- **Story hook:** The Vanari want the outsider to deliver a peach to the sealed peak, and they will not say who eats it.

## Origin

The Vanari say their founder stole a peach of immortality from the mountain gods and was sealed beneath a peak for five hundred years. When he was freed, he founded a sect so no one would have to steal again.

## Society

A martial sect organised by staff-rank; every member owes the sect three years of service and then may leave.

## Beliefs

Mischief is a teacher; pride is the only true sin.

## Relations

Rivals of every other sect in Shen-Lu, friends with the Brew-Bears, guardians of the sealed peak's orchard.

## Conflict and defence

Acrobatic staff fighters who strike from rooftops, ropes and cliff-edges.

## Trade

Peaches, staff training and the occasional stolen manual returned for a fee.

## Strengths

Speed, climbing, unpredictable tactics.

## Vulnerabilities

Easily provoked; pranks have started wars before.

## Gameplay role

Staff and acrobat training mentors; their pranks become a recurring comic-relief questline with a serious ending.

## Members and roles

- **Cloud-Leapers** — Acrobat: Red-furred monkeys who fight from rooftops and ropes.
- **Staff Elders** — Master: Grey-maned teachers of the iron staff style.
- **Peach Wardens** — Guardian: Sworn to the sealed peak and its orchard.
- **Mischief-Makers** — Trickster: Pranksters who test every guest's patience, on purpose.

## Notable figures

- **Grandmaster Hou Shan** (The Peach Thief's Heir): Old, laughing and dangerous; never answers a question directly.
- **Lin Redtail** (Cloud-Leaper): The sect's best acrobat and worst student.
- **Warden Bai** (Peach Warden): Has never left the sealed peak and wants to see the sea.

## Quest seeds

- Carry a peach to the sealed peak and find out who eats it.
- Survive a week of Vanari hazing to learn the iron staff.
