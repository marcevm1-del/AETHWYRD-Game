# The Red Barons

> Vampire nobles and beasts, red-eyed and elegant.

Old blood-lords of the ruined castles who profit from every war.

## At a glance

- **Home:** [[Regions/Strife Marches|Strife Marches]]
- **Source board:** Vampire Clan
- **Leader:** Baron Valcourt Sanguine
- **Story hook:** A Red Baron has offered to fight the Choir of Chains, for a price.

## Origin

The Red Barons are vampire nobles who took the Strife Marches' ruined castles generations ago, when the wars left them empty. Every war since has fed them, and they have quietly made sure the wars never end.

## Society

Blood-lords rule from their castles with thralls, duellists and the night-beasts that some barons become when hunger wins.

## Beliefs

Blood is the only true currency. A wise lord never takes more than the herd can bear.

## Relations

Profit from every baron's war; despise the Choir of Chains, whose dead have no blood; feared by the Plague-Beaks.

## Conflict and defence

Red Duelists with blood-blades; thralls as shields; night-beasts unleashed in desperation.

## Trade

Blood-debts, castle rents and secrets bought at midnight.

## Strengths

Immortality, strength, charm.

## Vulnerabilities

Sunlight, silver, running water, holy ground.

## Gameplay role

A morally grey alliance option in the Strife Marches siege; freeing thralls is a side questline.

## Members and roles

- **Blood-Lords** — Noble: Vampire aristocrats of the castles.
- **Night-Beasts** — Monster: Vampires lost to hunger.
- **Thralls** — Servant: Humans bound to the barons.
- **Red Duelists** — Warrior: Vampire swordmasters.

## Notable figures

- **Baron Valcourt Sanguine** (Leader): Has offered to fight the Choir of Chains, for a price.
- **Lady Ciel Rosethorn** (Red Duelist): The finest swordswoman in the Marches, and bored with eternity.
- **Gaspard** (Thrall): Wants to be free and doesn't know how.

## Quest seeds

- Negotiate (or refuse) Valcourt's price for fighting the Choir.
- Help Gaspard break his thrall-bond.
