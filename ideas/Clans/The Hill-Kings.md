# The Hill-Kings

> Hill giants and half-giants in furs, bigger than longhouses, carrying ship-masts as clubs.

Half-giant families of the fjords who fought Ymgrir's kin in the old war and lost half their jarls.

## At a glance

- **Home:** [[Regions/Frostveil|Frostveil]]
- **Source board:** Giant Clan
- **Leader:** Jarl Hrodgerd Mountainshoulder
- **Story hook:** A half-giant jarl wants the Thing to elect her war-king, and the human jarls are afraid of her.

## Origin

The Hill-Kings are the half-giant families of the fjords, descended from giants who settled among the human clans after the last war with the frost giants. Half the jarls who died in that war were theirs.

## Society

Families led by half-giant jarls who sit at the Thing beside the human jarls. Size brings respect, but cleverness brings the jarlship.

## Beliefs

The Last Fire holds their ancestors too. A giant who breaks hospitality becomes a frost giant in the afterlife.

## Relations

Uneasy kin of the human clans, mortal enemies of Ymgrir's frost giants, protective of the Moss-Trolls.

## Conflict and defence

Mast-Clubbers wade into battle with ship-masts; Stone-Callers raise walls of rock.

## Trade

Heavy haulage, longship-building, stone.

## Strengths

Size, strength, stone-magic.

## Vulnerabilities

Cramped halls; distrust from humans.

## Gameplay role

Thing election questline; half-giant allies break frozen barriers.

## Members and roles

- **Mast-Clubbers** — Warrior: Giants who fight with ship-masts.
- **Half-Giant Jarls** — Ruler: Leaders of the half-giant families.
- **Stone-Callers** — Shaman: Giants who speak to mountains.
- **Fjord-Waders** — Fisher: Giants who fish by wading the fjords.

## Notable figures

- **Jarl Hrodgerd Mountainshoulder** (Leader): Wants the Thing to elect her war-king; the human jarls are afraid.
- **Ulfar Wavebreaker** (Fjord-Wader): Fishes by wading and has seen what swims under the frozen fleet.
- **Grandmother Skaldi** (Stone-Caller): Talks to mountains and gets answers.

## Quest seeds

- Win the human jarls' votes for Hrodgerd at the Thing.
- Follow Ulfar under the ice to see what holds the fleet.
