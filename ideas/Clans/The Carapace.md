# The Carapace

> Mantis-, wasp- and beetle-folk in chitin armour with gauze wings.

Insect-folk who live in Mossdeep's ferns and are uneasy allies of the Thimblefolk; Mother Webcrawler hunts them too.

## At a glance

- **Home:** [[Regions/Mossdeep|Mossdeep]]
- **Source board:** Insecta Clan
- **Leader:** Queen-Mantis Zirrikh
- **Story hook:** The Carapace will help fight Mother Webcrawler if the Thimblefolk stop keeping aphids.

## Origin

The Carapace are insect-folk who live in Mossdeep's ferns: mantises, wasps and beetles grown large on the valley's mana-rich soil. They were here before the Thimblefolk and consider the Thimblefolk recent arrivals.

## Society

A hive-court under a Queen-Mantis, with duelists, riders and guards in castes. Each caste has its own hall in the hollow fern-stems.

## Beliefs

The hive is one body; a body defends itself. Aphid-keeping is slavery, and they will not forgive it.

## Relations

Uneasy neighbours of the Thimblefolk, prey of Mother Webcrawler, allies of the Capfolk.

## Conflict and defence

Mantis Duelists strike from ambush; Wasp Riders attack from the canopy; Beetle Guards hold the halls.

## Trade

Silk, wax and the services of the wasp riders.

## Strengths

Armour, speed, numbers.

## Vulnerabilities

Cold, smoke and spider venom.

## Gameplay role

Alliance choice in Mossdeep: siding with the Carapace changes the Webcrawler fight.

## Members and roles

- **Mantis Duelists** — Warrior: Blade-armed hunters of the ferns.
- **Wasp Riders** — Cavalry: Ride giant wasps through the canopy.
- **Beetle Guards** — Heavy: Armoured defenders of the hive-halls.
- **Queen's Gauze** — Noble: Winged attendants of Queen-Mantis Zirrikh.

## Notable figures

- **Queen-Mantis Zirrikh** (Leader): Will help fight Mother Webcrawler if the Thimblefolk free the aphids.
- **Kzzt** (Wasp Rider): The fastest flier in the valley, and fiercely proud of it.
- **Old Shellback** (Beetle Guard): Has guarded the queen's hall for forty years.

## Quest seeds

- Broker a deal between Zirrikh and the Thimblefolk council over the aphids.
- Ride with Kzzt to scout the Silk Canopy.
