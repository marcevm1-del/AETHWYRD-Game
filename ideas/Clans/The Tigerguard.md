# The Tigerguard

> Tiger-folk warriors and monks, striped and powerful.

Haneul's elite wall-guard.

## At a glance

- **Home:** [[Regions/Haneul|Haneul]]
- **Source board:** Tiger Clan
- **Leader:** Captain Baek Horang
- **Story hook:** A Tigerguard captain has been accused of treason by the Scholar Owls.

## Origin

The Tigerguard are tiger-folk who have guarded Haneul's wall since it was built. The first Tigerguard is said to have walked out of the mountain forest on the day the first stone was laid and asked to stand on it.

## Society

Wall-companies under captains; striped monks train the young; white tigers are rare and revered as lucky.

## Beliefs

The wall is a promise to everyone behind it. Courage without discipline is just noise.

## Relations

Defenders of the king, rivals of the Scholar Owls for influence, and the demons' first target on the wall.

## Conflict and defence

Halberds, claws and martial arts; white tigers lead counter-charges.

## Trade

Military service and martial training.

## Strengths

Strength, courage, martial skill.

## Vulnerabilities

Pride; political rivalries.

## Gameplay role

Martial-arts trainers in Haneul; the treason questline exposes a demon spy.

## Members and roles

- **Wall Captains** — Officer: Commanders of the wall.
- **Striped Monks** — Monk: Tiger-folk martial artists.
- **White Tigers** — Elite: Rare and revered.
- **Mountain Stalkers** — Scout: Scouts of the mountain passes.

## Notable figures

- **Captain Baek Horang** (Leader): Accused of treason by the Scholar Owls.
- **Monk Seol** (Striped Monk): Trains young Tigerguard in the mountain style.
- **Han-Byul** (White Tiger): A rare white tiger, young and very lucky so far.

## Quest seeds

- Clear Baek's name before he is executed.
- Train with Monk Seol in the mountain style.
