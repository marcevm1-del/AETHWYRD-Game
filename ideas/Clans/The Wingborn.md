# The Wingborn

> Insect-winged fey with antennae and leaf armour, spear-wielders and tricksters.

The Summer Court's soldiery and messengers, losing ground to the Briar King's thorns.

## At a glance

- **Home:** [[Regions/Everdusk|Everdusk]]
- **Source board:** Fairy Clan
- **Leader:** Captain Sylphine Thistledart
- **Story hook:** A Wingborn messenger is trapped in a thorn-hedge and owes a favour to whoever frees her.

## Origin

The Wingborn say they were born from the summer flowers at Everdusk's first dawn, before the courts made the dusk to settle their argument. They have fought for the Summer Court ever since, and they are losing.

## Society

Squadrons of a dozen under a captain, sworn to the Summer Queen. Every Wingborn takes a flower-name and loses it if they flee a fight.

## Beliefs

Summer is life and winter is a thief. Thanks are debts, so they never say thank you, and they are scrupulous about repaying favours.

## Relations

Bitter enemies of the Briar King, comrades of the Antlered Court and the Pipers, and wary of mortals, who tend to make bargains they don't understand.

## Conflict and defence

Spears and dartwings in swooping strikes; Moth-Fey scout by night; the last Summer Knights hold the Last Glade.

## Trade

Nectar, fey silk and small enchantments, always for a price that seems fair at the time.

## Strengths

Speed, flight, glamour and illusion.

## Vulnerabilities

Cold iron, frost and their own bargains.

## Gameplay role

Fey allies who grant illusion buffs in Everdusk; every favour they do creates a debt the player must later repay.

## Members and roles

- **Thistledarts** — Spear: Spear-wielding fey soldiers.
- **Dewdrop Messengers** — Messenger: Tiny, fast and gossipy.
- **Moth-Fey** — Scout: Night-flying fey of the dusk.
- **Summer Knights** — Elite: The Summer Court's last champions.

## Notable figures

- **Captain Sylphine Thistledart** (Squadron captain): Trapped in a thorn-hedge, and will owe whoever frees her.
- **Dew** (Dewdrop Messenger): Tiny, fast and unable to keep a secret for longer than a minute.
- **Sir Petalguard** (Summer Knight): The court's last champion, old for a fey and tired of war.

## Quest seeds

- Free Sylphine from the thorns without owing the Briar King anything.
- Stand with Sir Petalguard on the night the thorns reach the Last Glade.
