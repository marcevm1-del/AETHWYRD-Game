# The Tusk Companies

> Heavy boar-folk mercenaries in mismatched plate, tusks capped with iron, carrying halberds and banners.

Sellsword companies who have fought for every baron in the Marches and own a few castles themselves. Contracts are sacred; sides are not.

## At a glance

- **Home:** [[Regions/Strife Marches|Strife Marches]]
- **Source board:** Boar Clan
- **Leader:** Captain Hilde Gorebristle
- **Story hook:** A Tusk captain wants to break her contract before the Choir of Chains reaches her fort, without breaking her word.

## Origin

The Tusk Companies began as a single warband of boar-folk who sold their swords to the first baron who could pay. Three centuries later, they own castles of their own.

## Society

Companies led by matriarch Banner-Sows, with strict contracts and stricter accounts.

## Beliefs

A contract is sacred; a side is not.

## Relations

Hired by every baron, trusted by none; contemptuous of the Choir of Chains.

## Conflict and defence

Heavy plate, halberds and tusk-charges; they break shield-lines.

## Trade

Mercenary contracts, fortifications and truffles.

## Strengths

Discipline, armour, honour.

## Vulnerabilities

Bound by contracts even when they are wrong.

## Gameplay role

Hireable mercenary squads in the Strife Marches.

## Members and roles

- **Ironsnouts** — Heavy infantry: Plate-armoured boars with capped tusks and halberds.
- **Truffle Scouts** — Scout: Sniff out ambushes and buried treasure alike.
- **Banner-Sows** — Officer: Matriarch captains who carry the company colours.
- **Razorbacks** — Shock troops: Lean, bristled chargers who break shield-lines.

## Notable figures

- **Captain Hilde Gorebristle** (Banner-Sow): Wants out of her contract without breaking her word.
- **Snout** (Truffle Scout): Can smell an ambush, a lie and a truffle.
- **Old Grizzlehock** (Retired Razorback): Runs the company tavern and remembers every war.

## Quest seeds

- Find a legal loophole in Hilde's contract.
- Hold a ford with the Ironsnouts against the Choir's dead.
