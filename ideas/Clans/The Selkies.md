# The Selkies

> Seal-folk who can shed their skins.

Fjord-folk who trade with the longhouses and marry into them.

## At a glance

- **Home:** [[Regions/Frostveil|Frostveil]]
- **Source board:** Seal CLAN
- **Leader:** Ronnagh Greyskin
- **Story hook:** A selkie's skin has been stolen by a jarl's son.

## Origin

The Selkies are seal-folk of Frostveil's fjords who can shed their skins and walk on land. They have traded and married with the longhouse clans for generations, and half of Skjaldheim has a selkie grandmother.

## Society

Families led by skin-keepers, who guard the shed skins of those living ashore. A selkie on land without their skin cannot return to the sea.

## Beliefs

A selkie's skin is their soul. To steal it is the worst crime, and to give it freely is the deepest love.

## Relations

Kin to the human clans through marriage; friends of the Ursa and the Hill-Kings; wary of jarls' sons.

## Conflict and defence

Rarely fight; when they do, they drag enemies into the icy water.

## Trade

Fish, seal-oil, songs and the finest fjord-pearls.

## Strengths

Swimming, shape-changing, song.

## Vulnerabilities

Stolen skins; dry land.

## Gameplay role

A selkie skin-cloak allows underwater exploration in Frostveil.

## Members and roles

- **Grey-Skins** — Commoner: Seal-folk of the fjords.
- **Skin-Keepers** — Elder: Guard the shed skins.
- **Song-Selkies** — Bard: Sing to the longhouses.
- **Skinless** — Lost: Selkies whose skins have been stolen.

## Notable figures

- **Ronnagh Greyskin** (Leader): Her daughter's skin has been stolen by a jarl's son.
- **Moira Songselkie** (Bard): Sings to the longhouses on winter nights; every song is a true story.
- **Old Rorie** (Skin-Keeper): Guards his family's skins in a sea-cave no one else can find.

## Quest seeds

- Recover Ronnagh's daughter's skin without starting a feud with the jarl.
- Hear all of Moira's songs and learn the one about the summer that lasted a hundred years.
