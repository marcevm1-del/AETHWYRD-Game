# The Silverbacks

> Massive gorilla-folk in armour and goggles, scholars and bruisers alike.

The jungle's engineers and guardians, who rebuilt the temple roads. They can smell fear, and they fear the Nightmother.

## At a glance

- **Home:** [[Regions/Lumbaya|Lumbaya]]
- **Source board:** Gorilla Clan
- **Leader:** Engineer Obaku Stonepalm
- **Story hook:** A Silverback has been replaced by a nightshifter, and his family is the only one that has noticed.

## Origin

The Silverbacks lived in Lumbaya's highland forests before the temple-builders came, and they helped build the roads. When the temples were abandoned, the Silverbacks kept the roads open alone.

## Society

Troops led by grey elders, with engineers and fighters respected equally. Every adult must repair one stone of the old road each year.

## Beliefs

A road is a promise between places. The temple-builders will come back, and the roads must be ready.

## Relations

Friends of the temple villages and the Mangrove Jaws; the Nightmother's children have infiltrated them.

## Conflict and defence

Iron-Fists in armour; Goggled Tinkers with wrenches and traps.

## Trade

Road-building, stonework, engineering.

## Strengths

Strength, engineering.

## Vulnerabilities

Trusting; nightshifter infiltration.

## Gameplay role

Road-repair minigame opens shortcuts in Lumbaya.

## Members and roles

- **Road-Builders** — Engineer: Rebuilt the temple roads, stone by stone.
- **Goggled Tinkers** — Artisan: Gorillas with brass goggles and tool-belts.
- **Iron-Fists** — Warrior: Armoured guardians of the villages.
- **Grey Elders** — Elder: Silver-furred keepers of the troop's law.

## Notable figures

- **Engineer Obaku Stonepalm** (Leader): Rebuilding the Root Stair.
- **Grey Mother Nyani** (Elder): Has noticed her son is not her son.
- **Tumbo** (Iron-Fist): The strongest gorilla in Lumbaya, and the gentlest.

## Quest seeds

- Unmask the nightshifter in Nyani's family.
- Help Obaku rebuild the Root Stair to reach the Sky Temples.
