# The Pridelands

> Lion-folk kings and bards with great manes, from warriors to guitar-playing poets.

The royal lineage of Zaruma, who rule by generosity and are shamed by the gold road's closing.

## At a glance

- **Home:** [[Regions/Zaruma|Zaruma]]
- **Source board:** Lion Clan
- **Leader:** Prince Kofi Goldmane
- **Story hook:** A Pride prince wants to hunt Mansacor alone to prove himself.

## Origin

The Pridelands are Zaruma's royal lion lineage, descended from the first queen who paved a mile of road in gold. Their claim to rule rests on one thing: generosity.

## Society

A royal pride of golden-maned kings and queens, lionesses who lead the hunts, griot-lions who keep the songs, and young males sent out to prove themselves.

## Beliefs

A king who hoards is no king. Every gift returns, sometimes as a debt and sometimes as a song.

## Relations

Rulers of the gold road, advised by the Tuskers, taxed by the River-Lords' fords, shamed by Mansacor's hunger.

## Conflict and defence

Hunt-Mothers lead coordinated charges; Golden Manes fight in single combat for honour.

## Trade

Gold, salt and the gold road's taxes.

## Strengths

Strength, leadership, prestige.

## Vulnerabilities

Pride; a treasury drained by generosity.

## Gameplay role

Royal favour unlocks Zaruma's gold-road trade; Kofi is a potential hunt companion.

## Members and roles

- **Golden Manes** — Royal: The ruling lion lineage.
- **Griot-Lions** — Bard: Lion poets and musicians.
- **Hunt-Mothers** — Warrior: The lionesses who lead the hunts.
- **Exiled Males** — Wanderer: Young lions sent out to prove themselves.

## Notable figures

- **Prince Kofi Goldmane** (Royal heir): Wants to hunt Mansacor alone to prove himself.
- **Queen Naledi Goldmane** (Ruler): Holds the kingdom together with gifts she can no longer afford.
- **Griot Sundi-Kora** (Griot-Lion): Sings the kingdom's history and knows a verse about the manticore no one else remembers.

## Quest seeds

- Stop Kofi from hunting the manticore alone, or go with him.
- Find the lost verse about the manticore's weakness.
