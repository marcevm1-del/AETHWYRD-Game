# The Greyhowl Clans

> Wolf-folk in armour and cloaks, from wanderers to kings.

The wolfkin clans of the steppe, who trace descent through their grandmothers and follow the herds from pasture to pasture.

## At a glance

- **Home:** [[Regions/Fanghold|Fanghold]]
- **Source board:** Wolf Clan
- **Leader:** Grandmother Ruka Greyhowl
- **Story hook:** A wolfkin grandmother wants her grandchildren back from Kade.

## Origin

The Greyhowl Clans are the wolfkin of the steppe, who say their first grandmother was a wolf who learned to stand at the Wolf Stones. They follow the herds and trace their descent through their grandmothers.

## Society

Clans led by their eldest women, the Grandmother Packs; Wind-Runners scout, Iron-Fangs fight, Moon-Howlers speak with the ancestors in the wind.

## Beliefs

The wind carries the ancestors. The water-peace is holy, and Kade has broken it.

## Relations

Allies of the Hoofbound, enemies of the Laughing Packs who serve Kade, kin to the yurt camps.

## Conflict and defence

Fast raids, coordinated pack tactics and iron-shod fangs.

## Trade

Wool, felt, horses and hunting.

## Strengths

Speed, endurance, pack tactics.

## Vulnerabilities

Walls; separation from the pack.

## Gameplay role

Main Fanghold faction; pack-tactic companions.

## Members and roles

- **Grandmother Packs** — Ruler: Clans led by their eldest women.
- **Wind-Runners** — Scout: Fast wolves of the steppe.
- **Iron-Fangs** — Warrior: Armoured wolf warriors.
- **Moon-Howlers** — Shaman: Wolves who speak with the ancestors in the wind.

## Notable figures

- **Grandmother Ruka Greyhowl** (Leader): Wants her grandchildren back from Kade, and keeps the Howling Hearth in Fanghold.
- **Tavi Windrunner** (Wind-Runner): The fastest scout on the steppe.
- **Moon-Howler Sairan** (Shaman): Hears the ancestors in the wind, and they are angry.

## Quest seeds

- Rescue Ruka's grandchildren from Kade's ledger.
- Help Sairan hear what the ancestors want.
