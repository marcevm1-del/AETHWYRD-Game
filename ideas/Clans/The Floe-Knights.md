# The Floe-Knights

> Penguin-folk knights with round shields and ice spears.

Defenders of the ice villages, formal and fearless.

## At a glance

- **Home:** [[Regions/Whitewater Reach|Whitewater Reach]]
- **Source board:** Penguin Clan
- **Leader:** Sir Pengrove of the White Shield
- **Story hook:** The Floe-Knights have challenged Ysgrath to single combat.

## Origin

The Floe-Knights are penguin-folk who defended the ice villages from the frost serpents generations ago and were knighted by the villagers for it. They have kept the ice ever since.

## Society

An order of knights led by its eldest member, with Emperor Guards, spear-carriers and slide-scouts. Knights are chosen by the villagers, not the order.

## Beliefs

Honour is earned on the ice. A knight who abandons a village is no knight.

## Relations

Defenders of the ice villages, allies of the Orca Lords and the Iceclaws, sworn enemies of Ysgrath.

## Conflict and defence

Round ice shields, ice-spears and belly-slide charges across the floes.

## Trade

Protection, fishing and ice-craft.

## Strengths

Courage, cold resistance, ice-craft.

## Vulnerabilities

Slow on land; outmatched by Ysgrath alone.

## Gameplay role

Ice-combat training; Floe-Knight allies hold the line during the Ysgrath siege.

## Members and roles

- **White Shields** — Knight: Penguin knights with round ice shields.
- **Ice-Spears** — Warrior: Spear-wielding defenders.
- **Emperor Guards** — Elite: The largest and bravest penguins.
- **Slide-Scouts** — Scout: Belly-slide scouts across the ice.

## Notable figures

- **Sir Pengrove of the White Shield** (Leader): Has challenged Ysgrath to single combat.
- **Dame Tuxedra** (Emperor Guard): The largest knight in the order, and the gentlest.
- **Skid** (Slide-Scout): The fastest scout across the ice.

## Quest seeds

- Talk Sir Pengrove out of single combat, or help him prepare.
- Scout the Broken Floe with Skid.
