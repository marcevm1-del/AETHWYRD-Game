# The Hoofbound

> Centaurs from horse-backed steppe archers to antlered forest variants, painted in clan colours.

The horse-clans of the steppe, older than the yurt camps, who keep the Wolf Stones and witness the water-peace. Kade's capture of the Deep Well is an insult they mean to answer.

## At a glance

- **Home:** [[Regions/Fanghold|Fanghold]]
- **Source board:** Centaur Clan
- **Leader:** Khan-Mare Saruhan Windmane
- **Story hook:** The Hoofbound will ride on Fanghold if an outsider can prove Kade's guards would not shoot the hostages.

## Origin

The Hoofbound are the oldest people of the steppe; the clans say they were raising the Wolf Stones when wolves still walked on four legs.

## Society

Herds of related families led by a Khan-Mare; elders keep the stones.

## Beliefs

The wind carries the ancestors; the water-peace is holy.

## Relations

Witnesses of the water-peace; allies of the Greyhowl clans; at war with Kade.

## Conflict and defence

Mounted archers firing at full gallop; Thunder-Hooves carry the camps.

## Trade

Horses, felt and herbs.

## Strengths

Speed, archery, endurance.

## Vulnerabilities

Stairs, walls and cramped spaces.

## Gameplay role

Ground mounts trained by the Hoofbound; archery mentors.

## Members and roles

- **Steppe Archers** — Cavalry: Horse-bodied archers who shoot at full gallop.
- **Antlered Wardens** — Druid: Deer-bodied centaurs from the forest edges.
- **Stone-Keepers** — Elder: Witnesses of the Wolf Stones and the water-peace.
- **Thunder-Hooves** — Heavy: Draught-bodied giants who carry the clan's yurts.

## Notable figures

- **Khan-Mare Saruhan Windmane** (Leader): Will ride on Fanghold if the hostages can be kept safe.
- **Teke Fleetbow** (Steppe Archer): The best shot on the steppe.
- **Grandsire Ulaan** (Stone-Keeper): Remembers the oath of the water-peace word for word.

## Quest seeds

- Prove Kade's guards will not shoot the hostages.
- Escort the Stone-Keeper to the Wolf Stones for the yearly gathering.
