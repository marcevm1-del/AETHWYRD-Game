# The Shellguard

> Crab- and lobster-folk in lacquered armour, claws used as blades; some as big as carts.

Salvage samurai of the outer reefs, sworn to whichever captain feeds them. They moult every few years and are very vulnerable when they do.

## At a glance

- **Home:** [[Regions/Goldmere Coast|Goldmere Coast]]
- **Source board:** Crustacean Clan
- **Leader:** Champion Kanigawa the Twice-Moulted
- **Story hook:** A Shellguard champion is about to moult in the middle of the Krakenmother's war.

## Origin

The Shellguard washed up on Goldmere's reefs with the wreck-tide generations ago and found the salvage life suited them. They swore to the first salvage captain who fed them, and every Shellguard since has served a captain.

## Society

Sworn to captains, not to each other; a Shellguard without a captain is a hermit. They moult every few years, and their kin guard them while they are soft.

## Beliefs

Loyalty is armour; a new shell is a new life.

## Relations

Serve both port and pirate captains; old rivals of the Reef-Sharks; fear the Krakenmother, who eats them during moults.

## Conflict and defence

Crab Samurai duel with claws sharpened into blades; Lobster Knights hold salvage claims with long armoured arms.

## Trade

Salvage guard work, shed shells sold as armour, deep diving.

## Strengths

Armour, grip, underwater endurance.

## Vulnerabilities

Helpless during moults; boiling water.

## Gameplay role

Shellguard armour sets from shed shells; moult-guard escort mission.

## Members and roles

- **Crab Samurai** — Duelist: Lacquered fighters with claws for blades.
- **Lobster Knights** — Heavy: Long-armoured guards of the salvage claims.
- **Hermit Wanderers** — Scout: Live in scavenged shells, change them often.
- **Moulting Sworn** — Vulnerable: Shellguard mid-moult, guarded by their kin.

## Notable figures

- **Champion Kanigawa** (The Twice-Moulted): About to moult in the middle of a war, and too proud to hide.
- **Sister Pinch** (Hermit Wanderer): Changes her shell every season and her opinions every hour.
- **Sir Lobb** (Lobster Knight): Holds the oldest salvage claim on the reef.

## Quest seeds

- Guard Kanigawa through his moult while the Krakenmother hunts.
- Settle a claim dispute between Sir Lobb and a pirate captain.
