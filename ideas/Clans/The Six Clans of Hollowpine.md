# The Six Clans of Hollowpine

> Goblins with goggles, flamethrowers, mechanical arms and cockerel mounts.

Grukk's people are not all raiders. Hollowpine is a tinkering culture of scrap engines, fire-pots and cockerel cavalry, and its best inventors would rather build than burn.

## At a glance

- **Home:** [[Regions/Whisperwood|Whisperwood]]
- **Source board:** Goblin Clan
- **Leader:** Grukk, Goblin Chief of Hollowpine
- **Story hook:** A goblin tinker will build a better mill for Millbrook, if the village promises not to tell Grukk.

## Origin

Hollowpine's six clans came to Whisperwood as refugees from a war in the north, generations ago, and settled in the sinkhole caves. Each kept its own craft, mark and recipe.

## Society

Six clans with six chiefs, now united under Grukk. Grukk shares food fairly and punishes theft, which is why they follow him.

## Beliefs

Full bellies first; clever hands second; a goblin who invents something gets to name it after themselves.

## Relations

Raiders of Brindlewick and Millbrook; secretly envious of the Moraveil goblins' truce; wary of the Sett-folk.

## Conflict and defence

Firepots, flamethrowers, cockerel cavalry and scrap engines; coordinated raids, never two days running.

## Trade

Mushrooms, pelts, scrap machines and gems.

## Strengths

Ingenuity, numbers, coordination.

## Vulnerabilities

Fragile; clan rivalries.

## Gameplay role

Ring 1 Tyrant faction; Grukk the Younger becomes a redeemable villain later (Canon).

## Members and roles

- **Firepot Clan** — Tinkerer: Makers of fire-pots and flamethrowers.
- **Cockerel Riders** — Cavalry: Goblins on giant fighting roosters.
- **Scrap-Engine Clan** — Engineer: Builders of goggled war-engines.
- **Mushroom Clan** — Farmer: Growers in the sinkhole caves.
- **Wolfskin Clan** — Hunter: Trappers who wear wolf pelts.
- **Gem-Hoard Clan** — Merchant: Collectors and traders of shiny things.

## Notable figures

- **Grukk** (Goblin Chief of Hollowpine): Clever, fair to his own and the region's Tyrant.
- **Fizzwick** (Scrap-Engine tinker): Would build Millbrook a better mill if no one told Grukk.
- **Grukk the Younger** (Chief's son): Watches the rookies; will remember who killed his father.

## Quest seeds

- Help Fizzwick build the mill in secret.
- Defeat Grukk in the F-to-E rank trial.
