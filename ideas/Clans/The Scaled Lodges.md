# The Scaled Lodges

> Lizard-folk hunters and archers in jungle camouflage.

Jungle scouts and trappers who know the swamp's paths better than anyone.

## At a glance

- **Home:** [[Regions/Xochitl Mire|Xochitl Mire]]
- **Source board:** Lizard Clan
- **Leader:** Trapper Ssekhet Greenhide
- **Story hook:** A Scaled Lodge trapper has found Xibalbar's nest.

## Origin

The Scaled Lodges are lizard-folk hunters and trappers who have lived in Xochitl Mire's jungles since before the river-cities were built. They joined the cities' warrior orders as scouts.

## Society

Small lodges of related hunters, led by the best trapper. Frill-Speakers advise on the swamp's moods.

## Beliefs

The swamp gives and takes; a hunter who takes too much is taken.

## Relations

Rivals of the Jaguar Lodge, allies of the Sun-Feathered, distant kin of the Mangrove Jaws.

## Conflict and defence

Traps, poisoned darts and camouflage.

## Trade

Hides, poisons, trapping and guiding.

## Strengths

Stealth, poison, swamp-craft.

## Vulnerabilities

Cold; distrust from city-folk.

## Gameplay role

Trapping and poison crafting in Xochitl Mire.

## Members and roles

- **Green-Hides** — Hunter: Camouflaged jungle trappers.
- **Frill-Speakers** — Shaman: Lizard-folk who speak with the swamp.
- **Spine-Backs** — Warrior: Heavy-spined guardians.
- **Tree-Climbers** — Scout: Gecko-footed climbers of the canopy.

## Notable figures

- **Trapper Ssekhet Greenhide** (Leader): Has found Xibalbar's nest.
- **Frill-Speaker Ixa** (Shaman): Hears the swamp's moods and says it is angry.
- **Kesh Treeclimb** (Scout): Gecko-footed and fearless.

## Quest seeds

- Follow Ssekhet to Xibalbar's nest.
- Help Ixa calm the swamp.
