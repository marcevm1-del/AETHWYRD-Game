# The Drowned Crews

> Skeleton pirates on ghost ships.

Crews the wreck-tide brought home dead.

## At a glance

- **Home:** [[Regions/Goldmere Coast|Goldmere Coast]]
- **Source board:** Undead skeleton pirate Clan
- **Leader:** Captain Morrin Bleakwater
- **Story hook:** A Drowned Crew wants to finish its last voyage.

## Origin

The Drowned Crews are sailors the wreck-tide brought home dead: crews of ships that sank far across the sea and drifted to Goldmere's reefs as ghosts, still aboard their ships.

## Society

Each ship keeps its own crew, captain and rules; the crews are bound to finish their last voyage and cannot rest until they do.

## Beliefs

A voyage must be finished; a sailor goes home or goes nowhere.

## Relations

Haunt the pirate stilt-towns; feared by salvagers; fought by the Krakenmother, who wants their wrecks.

## Conflict and defence

Ghost cannon that never run out of shot, cutlasses and boarding parties of the dead.

## Trade

None, though ghost captains sometimes pay in sunken gold for passage home.

## Strengths

Undeath, ghost ships, sea-lore.

## Vulnerabilities

Finished voyages; blessed salt.

## Gameplay role

Ghost-ship questlines in Goldmere; finishing voyages lays crews to rest instead of fighting them.

## Members and roles

- **Ghost Captains** — Leader: Captains of the drowned ships.
- **Bone Gunners** — Warrior: Skeletons with cannon.
- **Kelp-Wrapped** — Minion: Drowned sailors tangled in seaweed.
- **Flag-Bearers** — Officer: Carry the black flags.

## Notable figures

- **Captain Morrin Bleakwater** (Leader): Wants to finish his last voyage, to a port that may no longer exist.
- **Bosun Tide** (Bone Gunner): Fires a cannon that never runs out of shot.
- **Little Wick** (Kelp-Wrapped): A cabin-boy ghost who wants to go home to his mother.

## Quest seeds

- Help Morrin finish his voyage and lay his crew to rest.
- Take Little Wick home.
