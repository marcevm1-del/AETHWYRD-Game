# The Scholar Owls

> Owl-folk scholars and sentinels with feathered robes.

The examination-masters of Haneul, who see in the dark and remember everything.

## At a glance

- **Home:** [[Regions/Haneul|Haneul]]
- **Source board:** Owl Clan
- **Leader:** Examiner Hwang Nightquill
- **Story hook:** A Scholar Owl has failed a candidate who might have saved the wall.

## Origin

The Scholar Owls were Haneul's first examiners, owl-folk scholars who invented the great examinations so that the kingdom would be ruled by learning rather than birth.

## Society

Examiners, archivists, night-sentinels and feather-mages, ranked by the examinations they have passed and set.

## Beliefs

Learning is the only true nobility. A question asked well is half answered.

## Relations

Advisers to the king, rivals of the Tigerguard for influence, respected by every scholar in the world.

## Conflict and defence

Night-Sentinels watch the wall in the dark; feather-mages cast spells written on their own feathers.

## Trade

Education, examination, archives and scholarship.

## Strengths

Knowledge, night vision, magic.

## Vulnerabilities

Arrogance; physical frailty.

## Gameplay role

Examination questline unlocks magic schools in Haneul.

## Members and roles

- **Examiners** — Scholar: Set and judge the great examinations.
- **Night-Sentinels** — Guard: Watch the walls by night.
- **Quill-Keepers** — Archivist: Keep the records of the kingdom.
- **Feather-Mages** — Mage: Owl-folk who cast spells with feathers.

## Notable figures

- **Examiner Hwang Nightquill** (Leader): Failed a candidate who might have saved the wall.
- **Archivist Bom** (Quill-Keeper): Keeps the records of every examination since the first.
- **Sentinel Haneulbit** (Night-Sentinel): Has seen something on the red gate at night.

## Quest seeds

- Find the failed candidate and give them a second chance.
- Help Haneulbit observe the red gate at night.
