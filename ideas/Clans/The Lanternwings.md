# The Lanternwings

> Moth-folk with pale wings and huge dark eyes, drawn to light.

They gather the moon-glass by night and are the first to lose their light to Ilyanthe.

## At a glance

- **Home:** [[Regions/Lustrine Shores|Lustrine Shores]]
- **Source board:** Moth Clan
- **Leader:** Gatherer Mothwyn Silverdust
- **Story hook:** A Lanternwing has flown toward the red moon and not come back.

## Origin

The Lanternwings are moth-folk who appeared on the Lustrine Shores the same year as the moon-glass, drawn by its light. They have gathered it by night ever since.

## Society

Night-flocks of gatherers led by pale-winged elders; dust-seers read the future in the patterns their wings leave.

## Beliefs

Light is to be gathered and given away, never hoarded. The red moon is a hungry light.

## Relations

Partners of the glass-gatherers' guild; the first to lose their light to Ilyanthe.

## Conflict and defence

Blinding wing-dust and swarming.

## Trade

Moon-glass gathering and lamp-making.

## Strengths

Night vision, flight, light-gathering.

## Vulnerabilities

Drawn to bright light; fragile wings.

## Gameplay role

Night-gathering of moon-glass; Lanternwing lamps are light sources that never run out.

## Members and roles

- **Glass-Gatherers** — Worker: Moth-folk who collect moon-glass by night.
- **Dust-Seers** — Mystic: Read the future in wing-dust.
- **Pale Wings** — Noble: White-winged elders.
- **Lightless** — Lost: Moth-folk whose light Ilyanthe has taken.

## Notable figures

- **Gatherer Mothwyn Silverdust** (Leader): Has watched three of her flock lose their light.
- **Seer Pale Ennis** (Dust-Seer): Read the red moon's dust and has not slept since.
- **Flit** (Young gatherer): Flew toward the red moon and did not come back.

## Quest seeds

- Find out what happened to Flit.
- Help Ennis interpret the red moon's dust.
