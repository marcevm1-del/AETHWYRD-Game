# The Glasskin

> Humanoids with skin like polished chrome or crystal, reflecting light in rainbows.

Born where the glass streaks cross; the nomads say they are what is left of people the burning turned to glass. They remember the vanished empire's last day in fragments.

## At a glance

- **Home:** [[Regions/Sunscar|Sunscar]]
- **Source board:** Crystalian / diamond clan
- **Leader:** Elder Shirah of the Seventh Streak
- **Story hook:** A Glasskin elder can remember the empire's last words, but only if she stands in the exact place she was made.

## Origin

The nomads say the Glasskin are people the burning turned to glass on the vanished empire's last day. The Glasskin themselves remember only fragments: a light, a sound and then waking in the sand.

## Society

Small groups bound by the streak where they were made. Elders whose faces have gone to chrome lead them.

## Beliefs

Every Glasskin has a making-place, and standing there brings back memory. Cracks are honourable; shattering is death.

## Relations

Respected by the Well-Keepers, avoided by the Unburied Court, curious about the Colossus.

## Conflict and defence

Prism-Born split light into blades; others fight with glass shards they grow from their skin.

## Trade

Glass, light-lore and memories of the empire.

## Strengths

Light magic, resistance to heat.

## Vulnerabilities

Blunt force can shatter them.

## Gameplay role

Prism-based light puzzles in the Sunken Towers; Glasskin memories unlock lore about the vanished empire.

## Members and roles

- **Chrome-Faced** — Elder: Mirror-skinned elders who remember the burning.
- **Prism-Born** — Mage: Split light into colour and colour into force.
- **Cracked Ones** — Damaged: Glasskin with flaws that leak light.
- **Sandglass Walkers** — Nomad: Wander the streaks, looking for their making-place.

## Notable figures

- **Elder Shirah of the Seventh Streak** (Leader): Can recall the empire's last words, but only at her making-place.
- **Prism Ashaan-Without-Name** (Prism-Born mage): Turns sunlight into force and moonlight into silence.
- **Little Crackle** (Cracked One): A child Glasskin leaking light from a crack, looking for a healer.

## Quest seeds

- Escort Shirah to her making-place before the Colossus walks over it.
- Find a way to mend Little Crackle.
