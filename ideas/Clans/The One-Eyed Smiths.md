# The One-Eyed Smiths

> Huge one-eyed smiths and seers, from scholarly robed cyclopes to brawling forge-giants.

Ancient allies of the dwarves who see the flaws in metal and the cracks in futures. Skarn wants them as his armourers.

## At a glance

- **Home:** [[Regions/Iron Hold|Iron Hold]]
- **Source board:** Cyclops clan
- **Leader:** Master Smith Oculra
- **Story hook:** Skarn has taken a cyclops smith; her apprentice says she can see how the siege ends.

## Origin

The One-Eyed Smiths were the dwarves' first teachers, the story goes: giants who forged in the volcano before Karak Dûn was carved and traded their craft for a home in the mountain.

## Society

Forges are families. Each forge has a master smith, a seer and a crew of brutes who work the bellows; apprentices come from every people.

## Beliefs

The eye sees what is true: the flaw in the blade, the crack in the future. A seer who looks too far loses the sight.

## Relations

Ancient allies of the Hold-clans; Skarn wants them as his armourers; the Scaleborn trade them dragon-bone for lenses.

## Conflict and defence

Hammer-Brutes smash; Forge-Eyes strike exactly where armour is weakest; Far-Seers warn of what is coming.

## Trade

Masterwork steel, lenses, seeing-stones and prophecy (sold reluctantly).

## Strengths

Craft, true sight, great strength.

## Vulnerabilities

No depth perception; Far-Seers go blind with overuse.

## Gameplay role

Masterwork forging service; Far-Seer hints for upcoming sieges.

## Members and roles

- **Forge-Eyes** — Smith: See the flaws inside metal.
- **Far-Seers** — Seer: See cracks in the future, at great cost.
- **Hammer-Brutes** — Warrior: Giant one-eyed brawlers.
- **Lens-Grinders** — Artisan: Craft spectacles, scopes and seeing-stones.

## Notable figures

- **Master Smith Oculra** (Leader): Taken by Skarn; her apprentice says she can see how the siege ends.
- **Brann Wideglass** (Lens-Grinder): Makes spectacles for every dwarf in Karak Dûn and a scope for the High Thane.
- **Ghorm** (Hammer-Brute): Gentle, enormous and terrible in a fight.

## Quest seeds

- Rescue Oculra from Skarn's camp before he forces her to forge.
- Commission a seeing-stone and pay its unusual price.
