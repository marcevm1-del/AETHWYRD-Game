# The Starbodied

> Figures whose bodies are night sky and nebula, with eyes like stars; some wear ordinary clothes over the cosmos.

Children whose Naming Voyage returned them with a name too large for one body. They are calm, kind and slightly far away.

## At a glance

- **Home:** [[Regions/True-Name Isles|True-Name Isles]]
- **Source board:** Ethereal clan
- **Leader:** Little Ellowen-of-the-Long-Name
- **Story hook:** A Starbodied child can hear the Nameless Tide calling names, and one of them is hers.

## Origin

The Starbodied are children whose Naming Voyage returned them with a name too large for one body. Within a year their skin darkens into the night sky, freckled with stars, and their voices grow faint and kind.

## Society

Small and scattered. Each lives with their own family; they gather on clear moonless nights at the Naming Rocks to compare skies.

## Beliefs

A name is a sky, and a sky has no edges. A Starbodied who dims is not dying but becoming distant.

## Relations

Loved by their families, feared by strangers, studied by the namers' council, and hunted by the Nameless Tide.

## Conflict and defence

They rarely fight; when they must, they release starlight that blinds and burns.

## Trade

Navigation by the stars, and a calm that sailors pay to be near.

## Strengths

Starlight, navigation, calm.

## Vulnerabilities

Fading over time; the Nameless Tide.

## Gameplay role

Star-navigation puzzles on the True-Name Isles; Starbodied light reveals hidden paths.

## Members and roles

- **Nebula-Born** — Child: Children whose bodies became the night sky.
- **Comet-Walkers** — Wanderer: Restless Starbodied who never settle.
- **Constellation Elders** — Elder: Old Starbodied whose stars have fixed.
- **Dimming** — Lost: Starbodied whose light is fading.

## Notable figures

- **Little Ellowen-of-the-Long-Name** (Leader by consent): Can hear the Nameless Tide calling names, and one of them is hers.
- **Auren the Comet-Walker** (Wanderer): Never stays anywhere longer than a night, and always knows the way.
- **Grandmother Vespa** (Constellation Elder): Her stars have fixed; she can find the Naming Rocks in a storm.

## Quest seeds

- Protect Ellowen on the night the Tide calls her name.
- Follow Auren to learn where Comet-Walkers go.
