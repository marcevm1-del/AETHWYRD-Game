# The Wyvern Oath

> Riders in light armour bonded to small dragons and wyverns that perch on their shoulders when young.

An order who raise wyverns from eggs in the monastery hatcheries. (Canon: the player cannot ride flying mounts, so riders are NPCs and allies, never mounts the player controls.)

## At a glance

- **Home:** [[Regions/Dragon Spine|Dragon Spine]]
- **Source board:** Dragon/wyvern Rider Clan
- **Leader:** Rider-Abbess Calyx Thornwing
- **Story hook:** A rider's wyvern has refused to fly since Vyrmax's attack; she needs to know why before it decides to leave.

## Origin

The Wyvern Oath began when a monk found an abandoned wyvern egg and raised it in the monastery kitchens. The wyvern stayed, and the order grew from her.

## Society

An order of riders, breeders and retirees; each rider bonds with one wyvern for life.

## Beliefs

A wyvern chooses its rider; a rider never chooses its wyvern.

## Relations

Allies of the monasteries, hunted by Vyrmax, admired by the Thunder-Eyries.

## Conflict and defence

Sky-Lancers dive from above (NPC only; the player cannot ride flying mounts).

## Trade

Wyvern-scale, aerial scouting, egg-guardianship.

## Strengths

Aerial support, scouting.

## Vulnerabilities

Wyverns are vulnerable when grounded.

## Gameplay role

Aerial support summons and scouting; never a player mount.

## Members and roles

- **Hatch-Keepers** — Breeder: Raise wyverns from the egg.
- **Shoulder-Bonded** — Novice: Riders whose wyverns are still small enough to perch.
- **Sky-Lancers** — Rider: Full riders on grown wyverns (NPC only).
- **Grounded Oath** — Retired: Riders whose wyverns have died; they keep the hatcheries.

## Notable figures

- **Rider-Abbess Calyx Thornwing** (Leader): Her wyvern has refused to fly since Vyrmax's attack.
- **Fen Ashwing** (Shoulder-Bonded): His wyvern is still small enough to sit on his shoulder.
- **Grandmother Ysolde** (Grounded Oath): Lost her wyvern and keeps the hatcheries.

## Quest seeds

- Find out why Calyx's wyvern will not fly.
- Protect a clutch of wyvern eggs from drake raiders.
