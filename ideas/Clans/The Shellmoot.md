# The Shellmoot

> Snail-folk, slow and wise, in moss-grown shells.

Greymoor's oldest folk, who remember the abbey on dry ground.

## At a glance

- **Home:** [[Regions/Greymoor|Greymoor]]
- **Source board:** Snail Clan
- **Leader:** Grandfather Spiraleth
- **Story hook:** A Shellmoot elder remembers what the abbey was built to hold down.

## Origin

The Shellmoot are snail-folk who have lived in Greymoor since it was a lake of clear water. The oldest remember the abbey on dry ground and the causeway that led to it.

## Society

Slow moots of elders that take years to decide anything. Slime-Trackers lay trails through the fen; Moss-Shells tend the reed beds; Shell-Sleepers rarely leave their shells at all.

## Beliefs

Haste is the root of every mistake. What sinks slowly can be raised slowly.

## Relations

Friends of the Bogkin, distant cousins of the Capfolk, wary of the Hollow Abbot, whom some of them remember as a young monk.

## Conflict and defence

They barely fight; their slime trails trap enemies and their shells turn blades.

## Trade

Slime-medicine for burns, memory, and the safest paths through the fen.

## Strengths

Memory, patience, armour.

## Vulnerabilities

Slowness; salt.

## Gameplay role

Lore-keepers for Greymoor; Shellmoot trails serve as guided paths through the fog.

## Members and roles

- **Spiral Elders** — Elder: Ancient snails who remember the abbey.
- **Moss-Shells** — Commoner: Snails covered in moss.
- **Slime-Trackers** — Scout: Leave trails that guide travellers.
- **Shell-Sleepers** — Hermit: Snails who rarely leave their shells.

## Notable figures

- **Grandfather Spiraleth** (Leader): Remembers what the abbey was built to hold down.
- **Moss** (Moss-Shell): Covered in moss, very proud of it, and the fastest snail in the fen.
- **Trail** (Slime-Tracker): Leaves glowing trails that guide travellers through the fog.

## Quest seeds

- Sit with Spiraleth long enough to hear the whole story of the abbey (it takes a week).
- Follow Trail's glowing path to the Peat Library.
