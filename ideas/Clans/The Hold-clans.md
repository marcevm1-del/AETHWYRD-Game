# The Hold-clans

> Dwarves of every trade: rune-scholars, smiths in layered plate, engineers with goggles and pistols.

The great clans of Karak Dûn, each with its craft, colours and grudges: smith-clans led by matriarchs, merchant clans who never leave the trade halls, engineer clans with pistols and goggles, and the old warrior clans who guard the gates.

## At a glance

- **Home:** [[Regions/Iron Hold|Iron Hold]]
- **Source board:** Dwarf Clan
- **Leader:** Matriarch Hulda Ironbraid
- **Story hook:** Two Hold-clans are feuding over who forged the Furnace-Heart's grate, while Skarn is at the gate.

## Origin

The Hold-clans trace their descent from the first dwarves, who the legends say were sparks from Thor's first anvil. Each clan was founded by one of the hold's first crafts.

## Society

Clans governed by their heads, who elect a High Thane. Smith-clans are led by matriarchs, warrior clans by the eldest fighter, merchant clans by whoever holds the ledgers.

## Beliefs

Thor and the forge; laws carved in stone; grudges carved deeper.

## Relations

Allied with the One-Eyed Smiths; treaty-bound to Sylvaran; at war with Skarn.

## Conflict and defence

Gate-Wardens in heavy plate, Cog-Engineers with pistols and pumps, Rune-Smiths with enchanted hammers.

## Trade

Steel, armour, fire-crystal, ale and engineering.

## Strengths

Craft, endurance, stubbornness.

## Vulnerabilities

Grudges; slow to change.

## Gameplay role

Main smithing and engineering hub; clan reputation tracks.

## Members and roles

- **Rune-Smiths** — Artisan: Keepers of enchanted steel.
- **Coalbeard Brewers** — Brewer: Masters of dark ale and drinking contests.
- **Gate-Wardens** — Warrior: Plate-clad guardians of the Khazdûr Gate.
- **Cog-Engineers** — Engineer: Goggled dwarves with pistols and pumps.

## Notable figures

- **Matriarch Hulda Ironbraid** (Smith-clan head): Leads the defence of the forges.
- **Brunhild Coalbeard** (Brewer): Keeper of the Anvil's Rest; drinking-contest record holder for three hundred years.
- **Tink Gearsworth** (Cog-Engineer): Has built a cannon that might stop Skarn, if it doesn't explode.

## Quest seeds

- End the feud over the Furnace-Heart's grate before Skarn breaks the gate.
- Test Tink's cannon safely.
