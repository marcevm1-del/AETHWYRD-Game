# The Raywings

> Manta-ray folk gliding through water, sleek and graceful.

Sea-people who carry the Naming Voyagers through the currents to the Naming Rocks.

## At a glance

- **Home:** [[Regions/True-Name Isles|True-Name Isles]]
- **Source board:** Manta clan
- **Leader:** Current-Mother Maraelle
- **Story hook:** The Raywings refuse to carry any more children until the Nameless Tide is gone.

## Origin

The Raywings are manta-ray folk who carried the first islanders to the Naming Rocks, long before there were boats. They still carry every child on the Naming Voyage.

## Society

Current-families led by Current-Mothers, who know every tide and every current between the isles.

## Beliefs

The sea gives names; a Raywing's duty is to carry the children to receive them.

## Relations

Partners of the namers' council, friends of the Starbodied, now refusing to carry children until the Nameless Tide is gone.

## Conflict and defence

Gliding strikes and stinging tails.

## Trade

Carrying passengers and messages between isles.

## Strengths

Speed in water, navigation.

## Vulnerabilities

Land; shallow water.

## Gameplay role

Raywing transport between the True-Name Isles.

## Members and roles

- **Current-Mothers** — Guide: Carry the Naming Voyagers.
- **Gliders** — Messenger: Fast rays who carry news between islands.
- **Deep Rays** — Scout: Dark rays who scout the open sea.
- **Star-Rays** — Mystic: Rays with glowing spots, said to read the stars.

## Notable figures

- **Current-Mother Maraelle** (Leader): Refuses to carry any more children to the Rocks.
- **Glide** (Messenger): The fastest Raywing in the isles.
- **Old Star-Ray Ossian** (Mystic): His glowing spots are said to map the stars.

## Quest seeds

- Convince Maraelle to resume the Naming Voyages once the Tide is dealt with.
- Follow Ossian's star-map to the place the sea keeps stolen names.
