# The Ashborn

> Horned, ash-skinned demons of every shape: farmers, scholars, warlords and priests.

Ordinary demon civilians of Nox Dominion, many of whom fear the Overlord's Generals as much as the heroes do.

## At a glance

- **Home:** [[Regions/Nox Dominion|Nox Dominion]]
- **Source board:** Demon Clan
- **Leader:** Lady Ghresh, Mayor of the Crimson Rest's town
- **Story hook:** A demon scholar wants to defect, and she has brought a library with her.

## Origin

The Ashborn are the ordinary people of Nox Dominion: demons who farm the ash, keep the border towns and teach their children letters. Their histories say they were here before any Overlord.

## Society

Towns ruled by demon nobles who answer to the Generals; families are large and close, and status comes from craft.

## Beliefs

Their own old gods, the endurance of fire, and a quiet hope that the war will end.

## Relations

Afraid of the Generals, wary of humans, and secretly kind to deserters from both sides.

## Conflict and defence

Conscripted into the Overlord's armies; dissenters sabotage from within.

## Trade

Obsidian, sulphur, fire-lily oil and ash-bread.

## Strengths

Heat resistance, resilience.

## Vulnerabilities

Holy magic; suspicion from every side.

## Gameplay role

Demon civilian quests show the war's cost on both sides (Canon: consequences on every side).

## Members and roles

- **Ash Farmers** — Commoner: Grow fire-lilies and ash-wheat on volcanic soil.
- **Obsidian Scholars** — Scholar: Archivists of the demon side of history.
- **Border Keepers** — Merchant: Innkeepers and traders of the border towns.
- **Quiet Dissenters** — Rebel: Secretly oppose the Overlord's Generals.

## Notable figures

- **Lady Ghresh** (Mayor of the border town): Pragmatic, tired and protective of her people.
- **Vael Scholar-of-Ash** (Obsidian Scholar): Wants to defect with a library.
- **Pip-of-the-Forge** (Demon child): Has run away to see what humans are really like.

## Quest seeds

- Escort a defecting scholar and her library across the border.
- Return a runaway demon child to her mother.
