# The Leafsworn

> Court elves in gold-leaf armour, wood elves in green, a few pale exiles in chains.

Court elves of Lothaniel, wood elves of the moonwell hamlets, and a third, quieter strand: exiles of the Grey Hollow, cast out a generation ago for studying the fungus beneath the World Tree.

## At a glance

- **Home:** [[Regions/Sylvaran|Sylvaran]]
- **Source board:** Elven Clan
- **Leader:** Thessaly Grayroot, the Exiled
- **Story hook:** An exiled elf knows how Mycelia thinks, and the court would rather not ask her.

## Origin

The Leafsworn are the elves of Sylvaran in all their strands: court elves of Lothaniel, wood elves of the moonwells, and the exiles of the Grey Hollow.

## Society

Court elves rank by house and age; wood elves by craft; exiles by nothing at all.

## Beliefs

The World Tree first; one's twin tree second; everything else a distant third.

## Relations

Court elves look down on everyone; wood elves are friendly; exiles are bitter.

## Conflict and defence

Longbows, living-wood armour, moonwell magic.

## Trade

Bows, herbs, living wood, moonwell water.

## Strengths

Archery, nature magic, long memory.

## Vulnerabilities

Pride; the blight.

## Gameplay role

Main Sylvaran faction with three sub-reputations.

## Members and roles

- **Court Elves** — Noble: Gold-leaf armoured aristocrats of Lothaniel.
- **Moonwell Folk** — Commoner: Wood elves of the forest floor.
- **Grey Hollow Exiles** — Outcast: Elves cast out for studying the fungus.
- **Twin-Wardens** — Ranger: Protect the twin groves.

## Notable figures

- **Thessaly Grayroot** (The Exiled): Knows how Mycelia thinks; the court would rather not ask.
- **Lord Aerendil Goldleaf** (Court elf): Sees the blight as a political opportunity.
- **Fern Quickbow** (Moonwell ranger): Her twin tree is infected.

## Quest seeds

- Bring Thessaly back to court to advise against Mycelia.
- Save Fern's twin tree.
