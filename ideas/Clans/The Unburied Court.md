# The Unburied Court

> Mummified kings and soldiers in gold and linen.

The vanished empire's dead, waiting in the Sunken Towers.

## At a glance

- **Home:** [[Regions/Sunscar|Sunscar]]
- **Source board:** Undead Egyptian Clan
- **Leader:** Pharaoh-in-Waiting Sekhemrah
- **Story hook:** The Unburied Court wants the Colossus to finish its route.

## Origin

The Unburied Court are the vanished empire's dead, kings and soldiers mummified for an afterlife that never came because the empire burned before the rites were finished.

## Society

A court of the dead under a pharaoh-in-waiting, served by tomb guards, embalmer priests and lesser sand-mummies.

## Beliefs

The rites must be completed; the empire must rise again.

## Relations

Enemies of the Well-Keepers and the Sable Guides; they want the Colossus to finish its route.

## Conflict and defence

Tomb guards with spears and curses; priests with sand-magic.

## Trade

None; they hoard.

## Strengths

Undeath, curses, sand-magic.

## Vulnerabilities

Fire, holy magic, finished rites.

## Gameplay role

Undead faction in Sunscar; finishing the rites is a peaceful alternative.

## Members and roles

- **Pharaohs-in-Waiting** — Royal: Mummified kings waiting to rule again.
- **Tomb Guards** — Warrior: Undead soldiers of the vanished empire.
- **Embalmer Priests** — Priest: Keepers of the death-rites.
- **Sand Mummies** — Minion: Lesser undead of the dunes.

## Notable figures

- **Pharaoh-in-Waiting Sekhemrah** (Leader): Wants the Colossus to finish its route.
- **Embalmer Hesy** (Priest): Knows the unfinished rites.
- **Tomb Guard Ankh-Ka** (Warrior): Has guarded the same door for a thousand years.

## Quest seeds

- Finish the rites and lay the court to rest.
- Learn from Hesy what the Colossus's route leads to.
