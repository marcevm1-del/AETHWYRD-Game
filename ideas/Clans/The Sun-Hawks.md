# The Sun-Hawks

> Falcon-headed guardians in gold collars and blue robes, wings sometimes armoured.

Sky-watchers of the garden cities who guard the aqueducts from the air and were once the div-kings' allies.

## At a glance

- **Home:** [[Regions/Zarvan|Zarvan]]
- **Source board:** Falcon/ Hawk Clan
- **Leader:** Watch-Mother Horeshet
- **Story hook:** A Sun-Hawk elder keeps a feather the div-king gave her, and Zahhar will listen to whoever carries it.

## Origin

The Sun-Hawks were the div-kings' first allies, falcon-headed watchers who guarded the springs from the air in return for nests in the high cliffs. When the shahs rose, the Sun-Hawks swore to them as well, and they have never decided which oath comes first.

## Society

Watch-flocks under Watch-Mothers; priests in gold collars keep the sun-rites; young hunters prove themselves by bringing down a mountain hare in a single stoop.

## Beliefs

The sun sees every theft. Water must flow, and whoever stops it answers to the sky.

## Relations

Old friends of Zahhar, sworn to the shah, close allies of the Coilborn and the Ibis Scribes.

## Conflict and defence

Aerial spears and dives out of the sun; priests blind enemies with reflected light.

## Trade

Aqueduct patrols, trained hunting falcons and sun-charms.

## Strengths

Flight, sight, sun-magic.

## Vulnerabilities

Blind at night; torn loyalties.

## Gameplay role

Aerial scouting reveals Zarvan's map; the feather is a key item in the div negotiation questline.

## Members and roles

- **Aqueduct Watchers** — Guard: Guard the water from the air.
- **Gold-Collared Priests** — Priest: Falcon-headed keepers of the sun-rites.
- **Div-Friends** — Diplomat: Old allies of the div-kings.
- **Hunting Falcons** — Hunter: Swift hunters of the mountains.

## Notable figures

- **Watch-Mother Horeshet** (Leader): Keeps a feather Zahhar gave her a century ago; he will listen to its bearer.
- **Ra-Kemet** (Gold-Collared Priest): Keeps the sun-rites and thinks the shah broke the treaties first.
- **Swift** (Hunting Falcon): The fastest flyer in Zarvan and the worst at taking orders.

## Quest seeds

- Carry Horeshet's feather to Zahhar and speak for the shah.
- Find the breach in the Great Aqueduct before a city runs dry.
