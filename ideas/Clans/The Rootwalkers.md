# The Rootwalkers

> Treefolk and bark-armoured antlered wardens, with moss beards and glowing sap.

Living trees who woke to guard the twin groves. Mycelia's blight is to them what plague is to people.

## At a glance

- **Home:** [[Regions/Sylvaran|Sylvaran]]
- **Source board:** Forest Clan
- **Leader:** Eldest Barkhallow
- **Story hook:** A Rootwalker is infected and asking to be cut down before it spreads.

## Origin

The Rootwalkers are trees of Sylvaran that woke when the elves first planted the twin groves, to guard the children's trees. Some are older than the court itself.

## Society

Each grove has an Eldest who speaks for it; saplings wake slowly over decades and are taught by whichever Rootwalker is nearest.

## Beliefs

Roots remember everything that passes over them. A Rootwalker that cannot heal should be cut down before it spreads.

## Relations

Guardians of the elves' twin trees; the court treats them as furniture, the wood elves as elders; Mycelia is their plague.

## Conflict and defence

Crushing branches, bark armour and roots that grip from below; slow to start, impossible to stop.

## Trade

None; they ask only for water and to be left to grow.

## Strengths

Strength, endurance, regrowth.

## Vulnerabilities

Fire, axes and the blight.

## Gameplay role

Rootwalker allies form living barricades in Sylvaran; their condition tracks the spread of the blight.

## Members and roles

- **Elder Oaks** — Elder: Ancient, slow and wise.
- **Antlered Wardens** — Guardian: Bark-armoured archers of the groves.
- **Saplings** — Young: Newly woken trees, curious and clumsy.
- **Blighted** — Sick: Rootwalkers infected by Mycelia.

## Notable figures

- **Eldest Barkhallow** (Leader): Ancient, slow and asleep for most of the year.
- **Twig** (Sapling): Newly woken and curious about everything, especially feet.
- **Ashbark** (Blighted): Infected by Mycelia and asking to be cut down before it spreads.

## Quest seeds

- Grant Ashbark's request, or find a cure before it is too late.
- Wake Eldest Barkhallow to march on the Grey Hollow.
