# The Coilborn

> Naga-like serpent-folk with swords and spears.

Guardians of the garden springs, old allies of the divs.

## At a glance

- **Home:** [[Regions/Zarvan|Zarvan]]
- **Source board:** Snake Clan
- **Leader:** Warden Nagasha of the Springs
- **Story hook:** A Coilborn guardian knows where the original treaty is carved.

## Origin

The Coilborn are serpent-folk who guarded Zarvan's springs for the div-kings before people came. When the garden treaties were signed in water, they were the witnesses.

## Society

Spring-clans led by Wardens; naga priestesses keep the treaties; cobra guards defend the springs; sand-coils scout the desert edge.

## Beliefs

A treaty witnessed is a treaty bound. Water forgets, but serpents don't.

## Relations

Allies of the Sun-Hawks; servants of the shah and remembered friends of Zahhar; caught between.

## Conflict and defence

Curved swords, hooded charges and venom.

## Trade

Spring-guarding, treaty-witnessing, venom for medicine.

## Strengths

Venom, memory, guarding.

## Vulnerabilities

Cold; divided loyalties.

## Gameplay role

Treaty-recital dialogue unlocks the peaceful resolution with Zahhar.

## Members and roles

- **Spring-Wardens** — Guardian: Guard the garden springs.
- **Cobra Guards** — Warrior: Hooded warriors with curved swords.
- **Naga Priestesses** — Priest: Keepers of the div treaties.
- **Sand-Coils** — Scout: Serpents of the desert edge.

## Notable figures

- **Warden Nagasha of the Springs** (Leader): Knows where the original treaty is carved.
- **Priestess Ssira** (Naga Priestess): Can recite every garden treaty, word for word.
- **Kaal** (Cobra Guard): Has defected to Zahhar's side and believes he is right.

## Quest seeds

- Follow Nagasha to the carved treaty in the Div Springs.
- Bring Kaal back, or hear why he left.
