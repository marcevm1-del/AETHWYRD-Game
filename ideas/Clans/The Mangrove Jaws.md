# The Mangrove Jaws

> Heavy crocodile-folk, scaled and patient; druids in moss cloaks, guards in leather harness.

They guard the river-mouths and mangroves of Lumbaya and remember the temple-builders as friends. They can smell a nightshifter, which makes them very unpopular with the Nightmother.

## At a glance

- **Home:** [[Regions/Lumbaya|Lumbaya]]
- **Source board:** Crocodile Clan
- **Leader:** Old Mother Sumbaru
- **Story hook:** The Mangrove Jaws offer to sniff out shapeshifters in one village, if the villagers will let a crocodile into every house.

## Origin

The Mangrove Jaws have guarded Lumbaya's river-mouths since the temple-builders' time. Their oldest mothers say they were asked to watch the rivers while the builders watched the sky, and that the builders never came back to relieve them.

## Society

Matriarchal clutches led by Old Mothers who barely move and never forget. Young crocodile-folk serve as river guards until they can hold their breath for an hour.

## Beliefs

Patience is the highest virtue; the river always brings what you wait for.

## Relations

Old friends of the temple villages, though the villagers find them unnerving. Feared by the Nightmother, whose children cannot hide their scent from them. Distant cousins of the Scaled Lodges.

## Conflict and defence

Ambush from the water: they wait, they grip, they drag. Moss Druids raise mangrove roots to trap enemies.

## Trade

River tolls, guard work, medicinal mangrove bark and shed teeth used as charms.

## Strengths

Patience, strength, a nose that finds nightshifters.

## Vulnerabilities

Slow on land; cold weather makes them sluggish.

## Gameplay role

A Mangrove Jaws ally reveals disguised nightshifters during Lumbaya's siege; druid allies create root barriers.

## Members and roles

- **River Guards** — Warrior: Heavy-jawed sentinels of the river-mouths.
- **Moss Druids** — Druid: Albino crocodile-folk who speak with the mangroves.
- **Sniffers** — Hunter: Track shapeshifters by scent.
- **Old Mothers** — Elder: Ancient matriarchs who barely move and never forget.

## Notable figures

- **Old Mother Sumbaru** (Leader): Older than the oldest village; speaks once a day.
- **Kesi Riverward** (River Guard): Young, eager and the best shapeshifter-sniffer in the clutch.
- **White Ngoro** (Moss Druid): An albino druid who speaks with the mangroves.

## Quest seeds

- Escort Kesi through a village to sniff out shapeshifters without starting a panic.
- Help White Ngoro wake the mangroves to block a river the nightshifters use.
