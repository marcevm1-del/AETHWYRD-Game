# The Drifting

> Jellyfish-folk with luminous trailing bodies and crowns of light.

They light the Hollow Sea and guide the boat-towns. The Lumen-Eater imitates them.

## At a glance

- **Home:** [[Regions/Hollow Sea|Hollow Sea]]
- **Source board:** Medusae Clan
- **Leader:** Elder Lumessa the Pale Bell
- **Story hook:** A Drifting elder suspects one of her people is the Lumen-Eater's lure.

## Origin

The Drifting are jellyfish-folk who have lit the Hollow Sea since long before the first boat tumbled down the great waterfall. The boat-towns call them the lamps of the deep, and steer by them.

## Society

Blooms of hundreds, each led by a Pale Bell, the oldest and largest of the bloom. Lamp-Drifters travel with the boat-towns; Stingers guard the blooms; the Crowned Drift are born rarely and treated as nobles.

## Beliefs

Light is a gift to be given, never taken. To use light as a lure is the worst blasphemy they know.

## Relations

Guides of the boat-towns, allies of the Inkwardens, and the Lumen-Eater's victims and unwilling models.

## Conflict and defence

Paralysing tendrils and sudden bursts of blinding light.

## Trade

Guidance through the dark, glowing jelly used in lamps, and light itself.

## Strengths

Light, paralysis, numbers.

## Vulnerabilities

Fragile bodies; dry air.

## Gameplay role

Drifting light-guides are the Hollow Sea's navigation system; a betrayal questline reveals the Lumen-Eater's lure.

## Members and roles

- **Pale Bells** — Elder: Great jellyfish elders of the Hollow Sea.
- **Lamp-Drifters** — Guide: Light the way for the boat-towns.
- **Stingers** — Guard: Defenders with paralysing tendrils.
- **Crowned Drift** — Noble: Jellyfish with crowns of light.

## Notable figures

- **Elder Lumessa the Pale Bell** (Leader): Suspects one of her own bloom is helping the Lumen-Eater.
- **Flicker** (Lamp-Drifter): Guides the smallest boat-town and is in love with its navigator's songs.
- **Crowned Aurel** (Crowned Drift): Wears a crown of light and refuses to dim it, even when hunted.

## Quest seeds

- Find the traitor in Lumessa's bloom before another boat-town follows a false light.
- Guide a boat-town past the False Light with Flicker.
