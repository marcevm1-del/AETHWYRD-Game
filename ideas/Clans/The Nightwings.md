# The Nightwings

> Bat-folk with leathery wings and sharp, sorrowful faces; from noble pale elders to snarling hunters.

Umbravel's bat-kin, who carry messages between the silk districts and scout the deep webs. They are gentle with the city and merciless with intruders.

## At a glance

- **Home:** [[Regions/Umbravel|Umbravel]]
- **Source board:** Chiroptera Clan
- **Leader:** Courier-Lord Vesk Duskmantle
- **Story hook:** A Nightwing courier has gone missing in the deep webs with the only copy of the silk contract.

## Origin

The Nightwings came down with Umbravel's founders after the Sundering, carrying the first lanterns into the dark. When the city was spun, they were the ones who flew the first silk cables from wall to wall.

## Society

Roosts ordered by service: couriers carry status, hunters carry respect and the Pale Elders carry the city's memory. A Nightwing who drops a message loses their roost-name until they make it right.

## Beliefs

Every message is a thread in the city's web, and a city with cut threads falls. They sing to the dark before every flight so the dark knows them.

## Relations

Fiercely loyal to Umbravel and its weaver-guilds, though they despise the guild that broke the silk contract. Sworn enemies of the Web Queen's brood, and careful friends of the Hollow Ones.

## Conflict and defence

Silent hunters who drop from the cavern roof with hooked blades and fangs; they fight in pairs, one to strike and one to sing the target's position.

## Trade

Courier work across the city and to the Underworld lifts, echo-maps of the deep webs and guano for the mushroom farms.

## Strengths

Flight in total darkness, echolocation, speed through the webs.

## Vulnerabilities

Bright light blinds them; open sky terrifies them.

## Gameplay role

Unlock an echo-map ability for Umbravel's dark districts; Nightwing couriers deliver mail between Underworld settlements.

## Members and roles

- **Couriers** — Messenger: Carry letters between the silk districts.
- **Deep Hunters** — Warrior: Fanged trackers of the lower webs.
- **Pale Elders** — Noble: Ancient, gentle bat-lords of the oldest district.
- **Echo-Singers** — Navigator: Map the dark by song.

## Notable figures

- **Courier-Lord Vesk Duskmantle** (Leader): Has lost a courier and the silk contract with her, and blames himself.
- **Lirra Softcall** (Echo-Singer): Maps the deep webs by song and hears things there that should not sing back.
- **Old Pallas** (Pale Elder): Remembers the Sundering and will only speak of it in the dark.

## Quest seeds

- Find the missing courier in the deep webs before the Web Queen reads the silk contract.
- Help Lirra map a song-path to the Web Queen's nest.
