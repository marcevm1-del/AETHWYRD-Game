# The Moss-Trolls

> Huge mossy trolls, some carrying boulders.

Trolls of the fjord forests, wary of humans and terrified of Ymgrir.

## At a glance

- **Home:** [[Regions/Frostveil|Frostveil]]
- **Source board:** Troll Clan
- **Leader:** Old Gruumsk Mossback
- **Story hook:** A Moss-Troll has come to the longhouses asking for shelter.

## Origin

The Moss-Trolls are trolls of Frostveil's fjord forests who have slept under the pines since before the longhouses were built. Ymgrir's frost giants hunt them for sport, and this winter they have come down to the longhouses for the first time.

## Society

Loose families; elders grow so mossy they become indistinguishable from boulders. Bridge-Keepers guard crossings and ask riddles; hermits live alone in the deep woods.

## Beliefs

Stay still and the world passes you by. A riddle answered is a friendship made.

## Relations

Protected by the Hill-Kings, wary of humans, terrified of Ymgrir.

## Conflict and defence

Boulder-throwing, crushing blows and regeneration.

## Trade

Bridge-tolls (paid in riddles), mushrooms and moss-medicine.

## Strengths

Regeneration, strength.

## Vulnerabilities

Fire; sunlight makes them sluggish.

## Gameplay role

Troll allies in the Frostveil siege; a riddle minigame.

## Members and roles

- **Mossbacks** — Elder: Ancient trolls covered in moss.
- **Boulder-Throwers** — Warrior: Trolls who throw rocks.
- **Bridge-Keepers** — Guard: Trolls who guard the bridges.
- **Forest Hermits** — Hermit: Trolls who live alone in the woods.

## Notable figures

- **Old Gruumsk Mossback** (Leader): Has come to the longhouses asking for shelter.
- **Grubb** (Bridge-Keeper): Guards a bridge and asks a riddle no one has ever answered.
- **Little Moss** (Young troll): Curious, lost and following the player around.

## Quest seeds

- Convince the longhouses to shelter Gruumsk's family.
- Answer Grubb's riddle to cross the bridge.
