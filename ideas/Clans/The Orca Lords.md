# The Orca Lords

> Orca-folk, black and white, huge and powerful.

Sea-hunters who share the fishing grounds with the villages and fear the Ice-Jaw.

## At a glance

- **Home:** [[Regions/Whitewater Reach|Whitewater Reach]]
- **Source board:** Orcinus Clan
- **Leader:** Pod-King Orvald Blackfin
- **Story hook:** The Orca Lords will hunt Ysgrath if the villages lend them their best harpoons.

## Origin

The Orca Lords are orca-folk who have hunted Whitewater Reach's waters for longer than the villages have stood. The first fishers made a pact with them: the orcas would drive fish to the ice-holes, and the villagers would share the catch.

## Society

Pods led by Pod-Kings and their mothers; Song-Callers keep the pod together across miles of sea; young bulls prove themselves by breaking floes.

## Beliefs

The sea feeds those who share it. A broken pact is a hole in the ice that never freezes.

## Relations

Pact-partners of the fishing villages, allies of the Floe-Knights and the Iceclaws, hunted by Ysgrath.

## Conflict and defence

Floe-breaking charges, coordinated pod hunts and wave-attacks that wash enemies off the ice.

## Trade

Fish-driving, ice-breaking and deep-water escort.

## Strengths

Strength, swimming, coordination.

## Vulnerabilities

Land; the leviathan outsizes them.

## Gameplay role

Orca allies in the Ysgrath fight; pod-calling summons for water combat.

## Members and roles

- **Pod-Kings** — Ruler: Leaders of the orca pods.
- **Floe-Breakers** — Warrior: Orca-folk who break the ice to hunt.
- **Song-Callers** — Mystic: Call the pod together across the sea.
- **Young Bulls** — Novice: Young orcas proving themselves.

## Notable figures

- **Pod-King Orvald Blackfin** (Leader): Will hunt Ysgrath if the villages lend them their best harpoons.
- **Siv Songcaller** (Song-Caller): Can call the pod from across the Reach, and has heard Ysgrath singing back.
- **Young Bull Torrin** (Novice): Eager to prove himself by breaking the Broken Floe.

## Quest seeds

- Broker the harpoon pact between Orvald and the villages.
- Find out why Ysgrath sings back to Siv.
