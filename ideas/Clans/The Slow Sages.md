# The Slow Sages

> Sloth-folk of great patience, in robes and halos.

Lumbaya's canopy-dwelling sages, who keep the old star-records.

## At a glance

- **Home:** [[Regions/Lumbaya|Lumbaya]]
- **Source board:** Sloth Clan
- **Leader:** Sage Bradyon Who-Waits
- **Story hook:** A Slow Sage has been watching the sky for twenty years and finally has something to say.

## Origin

The Slow Sages are sloth-folk who live in Lumbaya's highest canopy and have kept the temple-builders' star-records since the builders vanished. They move slowly because, they say, the stars do too.

## Society

Each sage watches one patch of sky for life; Halo-Sloths keep the rites; Wakers, the rare fast ones, carry messages down to the villages.

## Beliefs

Patience reveals everything. The sky is a book written slowly, and the temples were built to read it.

## Relations

Revered by the temple villages, protected by the Silverbacks, ignored by the Nightmother, who does not think they matter.

## Conflict and defence

They do not fight; Wakers can move astonishingly fast when they must.

## Trade

Star-readings, calendars and very slow advice.

## Strengths

Patience, astronomy, camouflage.

## Vulnerabilities

Slowness; easily overlooked.

## Gameplay role

Astronomy puzzles reveal why the temple roofs were torn off; Sage readings unlock Lumbaya lore.

## Members and roles

- **Star-Watchers** — Scholar: Keep the old star-records.
- **Halo-Sloths** — Priest: Sloths with halos of light.
- **Canopy Dwellers** — Commoner: Live in the highest branches.
- **Wakers** — Rare: Sloths who move fast, rarely.

## Notable figures

- **Sage Bradyon Who-Waits** (Leader): Has watched the sky for twenty years and finally has something to say.
- **Halo-Mother Ama** (Halo-Sloth): Keeps the rites of the Sky Temples.
- **Zip** (Waker): A rare fast sloth who carries messages down from the canopy.

## Quest seeds

- Wait with Bradyon until he says what he has seen.
- Help Zip carry an urgent message before the nightshifters intercept it.
