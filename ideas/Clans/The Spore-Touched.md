# The Spore-Touched

> People and beasts warped by fungus: swollen limbs, bark skin, glowing growths.

Those Mycelia's spores have claimed. They still remember, faintly, who they were.

## At a glance

- **Home:** [[Regions/Sylvaran|Sylvaran]]
- **Source board:** Infected Clan
- **Leader:** The One Who Was Aelindra
- **Story hook:** A Spore-Touched elf wrote a letter home in fungus, and the court has asked someone to read it.

## Origin

The Spore-Touched are elves, wood-folk and beasts claimed by Mycelia's blight. The first were moonwell hamlet families who drank from a grey well, and each wave since has been larger.

## Society

There is no society, only stages. Early cases are hidden by their families; later ones drift toward the forest floor and tend the fungus there in silent groups.

## Beliefs

Early stages remember their own faiths and fear the end; late stages speak of the Spore Lord as a gardener and themselves as its garden.

## Relations

Pitied by the wood elves, feared by the court, studied by the Grey Hollow exiles.

## Conflict and defence

Sporecallers release clouds that slow and confuse; the Hollowed fight without fear or pain.

## Trade

None; families trade everything they have for medicine that does not work.

## Strengths

No fear, no pain, spore clouds.

## Vulnerabilities

Fire, sunlight and moonwell water slow the change.

## Gameplay role

Infection stages map to enemy tiers in Sylvaran; curing named Spore-Touched unlocks the Grey Hollow questline.

## Members and roles

- **Budding** — Early: A few spots of fungus; still themselves.
- **Capped** — Middle: Fungal growths on head and shoulders; slow and dreamy.
- **Sporecallers** — Late: Release spore clouds to spread the blight.
- **Hollowed** — Lost: Nothing left but fungus wearing a shape.

## Notable figures

- **The One Who Was Aelindra** (Spore-Touched elder): A moonwell priestess whose letters home are written in fungus.
- **Brin Mossheart** (Budding): A young wood elf hiding the spots on his arm.
- **Mother Cael** (Grey Hollow exile): Treats the Spore-Touched in secret and keeps careful notes.

## Quest seeds

- Read Aelindra's fungus letter and find what she is trying to say.
- Smuggle moonwell water to Brin before the court discovers him.
