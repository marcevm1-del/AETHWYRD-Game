# The Free Captains

> Pirates of every kind: swashbucklers, quartermasters, gunners.

Goldmere's elected captains and their crews.

## At a glance

- **Home:** [[Regions/Goldmere Coast|Goldmere Coast]]
- **Source board:** Pirate Clan
- **Leader:** Captain Anne Saltgrave
- **Story hook:** A Free Captain wants a truce with the port to fight the Krakenmother.

## Origin

The Free Captains are Goldmere's pirate crews, many of them refugees, deserters and runaways who chose the sea over the law. They elect their captains and remove them by vote.

## Society

Crews under elected captains, with quartermasters, gunners and powder-monkeys; captains meet in a council on the largest stilt-town.

## Beliefs

Freedom first, loot second. A crew that cannot vote out its captain is not free.

## Relations

Rivals and trading partners of Port Goldmere; enemies of the Krakenmother; employers of the Reef-Sharks.

## Conflict and defence

Cannon, cutlass and boarding.

## Trade

Plunder, smuggling, rum.

## Strengths

Ships, cannon, courage.

## Vulnerabilities

Divided; reckless.

## Gameplay role

Pirate faction in Goldmere; ship-to-ship combat events.

## Members and roles

- **Elected Captains** — Leader: Captains chosen by their crews.
- **Quartermasters** — Officer: Keep the loot fair and the crew fed.
- **Gunners** — Warrior: Masters of the cannon.
- **Powder-Monkeys** — Novice: Young crew who carry the powder.

## Notable figures

- **Captain Anne Saltgrave** (Leader): Wants a truce with the port to fight the Krakenmother.
- **Quartermaster Jory Two-Coins** (Officer): Keeps the loot fair and the crew honest.
- **Patch** (Powder-Monkey): A runaway who dreams of being captain.

## Quest seeds

- Broker Anne's truce with the port.
- Help Patch earn a vote.
