# The Gilded

> Warriors in ornate gold armour, on thrones or in phalanx, faces hidden behind gilded helms.

Descendants of Aurumdeep's survivors, sworn to guard their drowned kingdom from treasure-hunters.

## At a glance

- **Home:** [[Regions/Aurumdeep|Aurumdeep]]
- **Source board:** Golden Clan
- **Leader:** Last Steward Aurelion Vane-Gold
- **Story hook:** The Gilded want one specific crown recovered, and they will not say why it matters.

## Origin

The Gilded are descendants of Aurumdeep's survivors, who were outside the kingdom on the night it sank. They swore to guard the drowned kingdom's dead from treasure-hunters.

## Society

A sworn order under a Last Steward; each family guards one district of the sunken city.

## Beliefs

The dead are owed their gold; the show must end.

## Relations

Enemies of the explorers' camp; cautious allies of anyone who respects the dead.

## Conflict and defence

Gold phalanxes; Gilt-Divers who fight underwater.

## Trade

Very little; they live on their ancestors' hidden gold.

## Strengths

Knowledge of the sunken city.

## Vulnerabilities

Few in number; obsessed.

## Gameplay role

Aurumdeep faction; diving routes.

## Members and roles

- **Gold Phalanx** — Warrior: Gilded soldiers in rank and file.
- **Stewards** — Ruler: Keepers of the drowned kingdom's claims.
- **Lamplighters** — Guardian: Tend the enchanted lamps of the deep.
- **Gilt-Divers** — Diver: Recover heirlooms before the treasure-hunters.

## Notable figures

- **Last Steward Aurelion Vane-Gold** (Leader): Wants one specific crown recovered.
- **Oriel Gilt-Diver** (Diver): Has seen the king on his throne.
- **Lamplighter Pell** (Guardian): Tends the enchanted lamps.

## Quest seeds

- Recover the crown for Aurelion and learn why it matters.
- Find out whether the king is truly alive.
