# The Sun-Feathered

> Warriors in feathered headdresses, jade masks and quilted cotton armour, carrying obsidian-edged clubs.

The warrior orders of the river-cities: Jaguar, Eagle and Serpent lodges who play the great ball-games and guard the canoe-roads. Bravery is measured in captives taken alive, not kills.

## At a glance

- **Home:** [[Regions/Xochitl Mire|Xochitl Mire]]
- **Source board:** Aztec Clan
- **Leader:** Speaker Itzcali of the Eagle Lodge
- **Story hook:** The Eagle lodge has challenged an outsider to a ball-game; the prize is the right to hunt Xibalbar.

## Origin

The Sun-Feathered descend from the river-kings' first war-lodges, founded when the cities agreed to settle feuds through the ball-game instead of war.

## Society

Three warrior lodges (Eagle, Jaguar and Serpent) under the river-kings and priests; rank is won on the ball-court and the canoe-road.

## Beliefs

Courage is measured in captives taken alive; the stars keep the calendar of every deed.

## Relations

Allied with the Jaguar Lodge and Scaled Lodges; suspicious of the priests who abandoned the feeding rites.

## Conflict and defence

Feathered shields, obsidian-edged clubs and quilted armour; they fight to capture, not to kill.

## Trade

Feathers, cacao, jade and cotton armour.

## Strengths

Discipline, ritual combat, river mobility.

## Vulnerabilities

Honour codes that forbid ambush.

## Gameplay role

Ball-game minigame; capture-not-kill combat bonuses in Xochitl Mire.

## Members and roles

- **Eagle Lodge** — Warrior order: Sky-watchers and ball-game champions.
- **Jade Priests** — Priest: Keepers of the star-calendar and the Feeding Steps.
- **Obsidian Guard** — Royal guard: Masked guardians of the river-kings.
- **Canoe Runners** — Messenger: Paddlers who carry news along the canoe-roads.

## Notable figures

- **Speaker Itzcali** (Eagle Lodge speaker): Challenges outsiders to the ball-game to test them.
- **Tlaco Brightquill** (Jade priest): Secretly keeps the old feeding rites.
- **Nochi** (Canoe runner): Knows every canoe-road and every rumour.

## Quest seeds

- Win a ball-game to earn the right to hunt Xibalbar.
- Find out what the old feeding rites really fed the swamp.
