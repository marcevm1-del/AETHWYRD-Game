# The Canal Otters

> Otter-folk gondoliers, archers and gentlemen.

Lirian's gondoliers and divers, cheerful and nosy.

## At a glance

- **Home:** [[Regions/Lirian|Lirian]]
- **Source board:** Otter Clan
- **Leader:** Gondolier Pip Slipwater
- **Story hook:** A canal otter has heard the Drowned Choir and wants to follow it, with someone holding the rope.

## Origin

The Canal Otters came to Lirian with the first fishing boats and never left. When the city grew, they became its gondoliers, divers and gossips, and today no deal in Lirian is secret from them for long.

## Society

Family rafts led by the eldest gondolier; the divers' guild and the gondoliers' guild argue about everything except the city's safety.

## Beliefs

Water is home and the canals are family. Every coin in the canal is a prayer, and divers return a tenth of what they find to the water.

## Relations

Friends of everyone, rivals of the Lagoon Folk for the best diving spots, quietly paid informants of the doge.

## Conflict and defence

Otter Archers shoot from gondolas; divers drag attackers under.

## Trade

Gondola fares, diving, gossip and the occasional smuggled letter.

## Strengths

Swimming, agility, information.

## Vulnerabilities

Curiosity; can't keep a secret.

## Gameplay role

Gondola fast travel in Lirian; otter informants sell rumours.

## Members and roles

- **Gondoliers** — Worker: Steer the gondolas of Lirian.
- **Canal Divers** — Diver: Dive for the coins thrown into the canals.
- **Otter Archers** — Warrior: Defend the canals from the Drowned Choir.
- **Gentleman Otters** — Merchant: Well-dressed otter traders.

## Notable figures

- **Gondolier Pip Slipwater** (Leader): Has heard the Drowned Choir and wants to follow it, with someone holding the rope.
- **Madame Whiskerton** (Gentleman Otter): Runs the finest fish restaurant in Lirian and knows every secret spoken at its tables.
- **Splash** (Canal Diver): The youngest diver in the guild and the best at finding lost rings.

## Quest seeds

- Hold Pip's rope while he follows the Drowned Choir's song.
- Use Madame Whiskerton's gossip to find out who in the doge's council deals with the sirens.
