# The Plateback

> Pangolin-folk with overlapping scales, curling into balls for defence.

Riders of Thalassa who patch her hide and polish her barnacles.

## At a glance

- **Home:** [[Regions/Dunewhale Drift|Dunewhale Drift]]
- **Source board:** Pangolin Clan
- **Leader:** Hide-Warden Pango Scalehold
- **Story hook:** A Plateback has found a wound on Thalassa's back that goes very deep.

## Origin

The Plateback are pangolin-folk born on Thalassa's back who have tended her hide for as long as the town has ridden her. They say she was carrying them before she carried anyone else.

## Society

Hide-crews led by Hide-Wardens; each crew is responsible for a patch of Thalassa's back and is named after it.

## Beliefs

Thalassa is mother and home; a wound in her hide is a wound in every Plateback.

## Relations

Partners of the Whale-Singers, wary of the sand pirates, terrified of Glassmaw.

## Conflict and defence

Curl-Guards roll into armoured balls and bowl through enemies; Sand-Diggers ambush from beneath the sand.

## Trade

Hide-care, barnacle-cleaning, shed scales sold as armour.

## Strengths

Armour, digging, loyalty.

## Vulnerabilities

Slow; vulnerable belly.

## Gameplay role

Pangolin scale armour sets; hide-care minigame to keep Thalassa healthy.

## Members and roles

- **Hide-Wardens** — Healer: Tend Thalassa's hide.
- **Curl-Guards** — Warrior: Curl into armoured balls to defend.
- **Sand-Diggers** — Worker: Dig out the barnacles and the parasites.
- **Scale-Traders** — Merchant: Sell shed scales as armour.

## Notable figures

- **Hide-Warden Pango Scalehold** (Leader): Has found a wound on Thalassa's back that goes very deep.
- **Rolla** (Curl-Guard): The best bowler on the whale and very proud of it.
- **Old Scaleface** (Scale-Trader): Sells shed scales to every visitor and swears each one is lucky.

## Quest seeds

- Find out what made the deep wound in Thalassa's back.
- Help Rolla defend the town from sand pirates.
