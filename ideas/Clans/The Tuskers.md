# The Tuskers

> Elephant-folk of great dignity: tusked sages on thrones, gentle giants with clubs, war-elephants in armour.

Keepers of memory along the gold road, partners to the griots. A Tusker never forgets a debt or a kindness.

## At a glance

- **Home:** [[Regions/Zaruma|Zaruma]]
- **Source board:** Elephant Clan
- **Leader:** Grand Memory Bhoomrah
- **Story hook:** A Tusker elder remembers the day Mansacor first ate gold, and what it was hunting before.

## Origin

The Tuskers say they walked the gold road before it was a road, carrying salt to the first towns on their backs. They have never forgotten a single journey, and they can recite every caravan that has crossed Zaruma for three hundred years.

## Society

Herds led by Grand Memories, the eldest matriarchs, who keep the history of every family. Throne-Sages advise the Pride kings; War-Tuskers guard the road; Mammoth-Kin come down from the northern hills in winter.

## Beliefs

Memory is sacred and forgetting is a kind of death. Ivory may only be carved from tusks that have fallen naturally.

## Relations

Partners of the griots, advisers to the Pridelands, protectors of the River-Lords' fords, and the manticore's oldest enemies.

## Conflict and defence

War-Tuskers in armour charge in lines that flatten anything before them; their trumpets can be heard for miles.

## Trade

Memory-keeping, heavy haulage on the gold road, shed ivory and salt.

## Strengths

Strength, memory, endurance.

## Vulnerabilities

Slow to turn; fire panics them.

## Gameplay role

Tusker memories unlock lore and the manticore's weakness; Tusker caravans carry heavy goods along the gold road.

## Members and roles

- **Grand Memories** — Elder: Keep the history of the gold road.
- **Throne-Sages** — Ruler: Ancient elephants who advise the Pride kings.
- **War-Tuskers** — Warrior: Armoured giants of the road garrisons.
- **Mammoth-Kin** — Northern: Shaggy cousins from the far hills.

## Notable figures

- **Grand Memory Bhoomrah** (Leader): Remembers the day Mansacor first ate gold, and what it hunted before.
- **Little Ammu** (Calf): Wants to become a griot and carries a kora twice her size.
- **War-Tusker Dhalgar** (Warrior): Guards the River-Lords' fords and has lost a tusk to the manticore.

## Quest seeds

- Learn from Bhoomrah what Mansacor hunted before gold, and use it as bait.
- Help Little Ammu learn the founding song before the griots' gathering.
