# The Well-Keepers

> Robed scribes, priests and queens in gold collars and linen, carrying crooks and water jars.

The hereditary water-keepers of Qadira, inheritors of the vanished empire's rites. They write everything down and forgive nothing.

## At a glance

- **Home:** [[Regions/Sunscar|Sunscar]]
- **Source board:** Egyptian Clan
- **Leader:** High Keeper Nefrimah
- **Story hook:** A Well-Keeper priestess believes the empire's last pharaoh is still giving the Colossus orders.

## Origin

The Well-Keepers are the hereditary order who survived the vanished empire's fall because they were tending the oasis on the day the sky burned. They inherited the empire's water-rites, its records and its guilt.

## Society

High Keepers lead the order; Shoreline Scribes measure the oasis's edge every dawn; Water Queens head the great families who own the wells' shares; Crook-Bearers guard the springs. Membership passes through the mother's line.

## Beliefs

Water is sacred and wasting it is the worst of crimes. The empire burned because it tried to make itself a god, and pride still burns anyone who touches it.

## Relations

Govern the oasis and every caravan that drinks there; respect the Glasskin as living witnesses; fear the Unburied Court; split over whether to dig up the buried machine.

## Conflict and defence

Crook-Bearers fight with iron-shod staffs and water-whips drawn from the wells; High Keepers can dry a man's mouth with a word.

## Trade

The water-tithe, the Oasis Caravanserai, dates, records and the oasis's famous maps.

## Strengths

Water magic, records, authority over every caravan.

## Vulnerabilities

Tradition; divided leadership.

## Gameplay role

The water-tithe is a recurring cost in Sunscar; Well-Keeper favour unlocks the oasis's map and its secrets.

## Members and roles

- **High Keepers** — Priest: Hereditary masters of the seven wells.
- **Shoreline Scribes** — Archivist: Record the oasis's edge every year.
- **Water Queens** — Ruler: Ceremonial heads of the great families.
- **Crook-Bearers** — Guard: Staff-wielding guardians of the wells.

## Notable figures

- **High Keeper Nefrimah** (Leader): Believes the last pharaoh still commands the Colossus from somewhere.
- **Scribe Hennu** (Shoreline Scribe): Has measured the shrinking shoreline for thirty years and has started to weep while he writes.
- **Water Queen Merit-Sand** (Great-family head): Wants the buried machine dug up, and is willing to defy Nefrimah to do it.

## Quest seeds

- Decide whether to dig up the buried machine, and live with what it does.
- Find the last pharaoh's command-seal in the Sunken Towers.
