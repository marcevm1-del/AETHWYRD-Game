# The Longears

> Rabbit- and hare-folk: farmers, scouts and black-clad duellists with long knives.

Moraveil's fastest messengers and most stubborn farmers, who sit on the joint council beside humans and goblins.

## At a glance

- **Home:** [[Regions/Moraveil|Moraveil]]
- **Source board:** Hare Clan
- **Leader:** Scout-Captain Tansy Quickfoot
- **Story hook:** A Longear scout has seen Grakkus's army and is the only one who knows the route it will take.

## Origin

The Longears have farmed Moraveil's hills longer than the humans or the goblins, and they say the truce was their idea in the first place. Nobody believes them; they keep saying it.

## Society

Warrens of extended families; scout-captains lead in danger, farmers in peace, and Moon-Hares read the weather for everyone.

## Beliefs

Speed is safety, and a full larder is wisdom. Every truce needs a third party to keep the other two honest.

## Relations

Members of the joint truce council beside humans and goblins; scouts for both; terrified of the Hill-Orcs.

## Conflict and defence

Quickfoot Scouts run messages under fire; Black Hares fight with long knives in the dark.

## Trade

Vegetables, messages and weather-readings.

## Strengths

Speed, hearing, scouting.

## Vulnerabilities

Panic in crowds; small and fragile.

## Gameplay role

Longear scouts reveal enemy routes during Moraveil's siege; their warrens provide fast travel.

## Members and roles

- **Quickfoot Scouts** — Scout: The fastest messengers in Moraveil.
- **Burrow Farmers** — Farmer: Stubborn growers of carrots and cabbages.
- **Black Hares** — Duelist: Knife-fighters in black cloaks.
- **Moon-Hares** — Mystic: Hares who read the moon and the weather.

## Notable figures

- **Scout-Captain Tansy Quickfoot** (Leader): Has seen Grakkus's army and is the only one who knows its route.
- **Old Clover** (Burrow Farmer): Stubborn, kind and refuses to leave her field.
- **Shade** (Black Hare): A silent duelist who owes the goblins a life-debt.

## Quest seeds

- Get Tansy's report to the council before Grakkus reaches the bridge.
- Convince Old Clover to evacuate before the army arrives.
