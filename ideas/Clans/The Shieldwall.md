# The Shieldwall

> Hoplites with round bronze shields, crested helms and long spears.

Bastion's phalanx, drilled to hold a line of shields against cavalry. Every ring's captain comes from their ranks.

## At a glance

- **Home:** [[Regions/Bastion|Bastion]]
- **Source board:** Greecian Clan
- **Leader:** Polemarch Kassandros Brightshield
- **Story hook:** The Shieldwall has never broken; Thrakka is planning the charge that will make it.

## Origin

The Shieldwall was founded by the first Warden of Bastion, who taught farmers to stand shoulder to shoulder behind bronze shields against the first horde charge.

## Society

Hoplites drilled from youth; Polemarchs chosen by the ring captains; veterans of broken rings honoured above all.

## Beliefs

The wall is the people; no one breaks ranks.

## Relations

Bastion's heart; allies of the Hornwall; Thrakka's chief target.

## Conflict and defence

Phalanx with long spears and round bronze shields.

## Trade

Military service; shield-smithing.

## Strengths

Discipline, defence.

## Vulnerabilities

Inflexible against flanking.

## Gameplay role

Formation combat; defend-the-ring siege events.

## Members and roles

- **Hoplites** — Infantry: Round-shield spearmen of the phalanx.
- **Polemarchs** — Officer: Captains chosen to hold the outermost ring.
- **Bronze Veterans** — Elite: Survivors of three or more broken rings.
- **Shield-Smiths** — Artisan: Forge and repair the great bronze shields.

## Notable figures

- **Polemarch Kassandros Brightshield** (Leader): Has never broken, and knows Thrakka is planning the charge that will make him.
- **Ione Bronzehand** (Shield-Smith): Can repair a shield in the middle of a battle.
- **Old Leonidas Threerings** (Veteran): Survived three broken rings.
