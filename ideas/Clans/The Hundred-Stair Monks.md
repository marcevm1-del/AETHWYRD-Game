# The Hundred-Stair Monks

> Monks in robes and bandages, martial artists of fire and staff.

The martial monks of Shen-Lu's sects, split into orders devoted to fire, stillness and the staff, who settle doctrine by duel.

## At a glance

- **Home:** [[Regions/Shen-Lu|Shen-Lu]]
- **Source board:** Monk Clan
- **Leader:** Abbot Wen Jinghai of the Still Flame
- **Story hook:** A monk has broken her vow of stillness to fight the Colossus.

## Origin

The Hundred-Stair Monks were a single school before the Hundred-Stair Schism split it into rival sects. Each order still claims to be the true heir of the founder.

## Society

Four orders (Still Flame, Iron Staff, Silent Stair and Wandering Fists), each under an abbot, with disciples ranked by the stairs they have climbed.

## Beliefs

Cultivation of qi leads to oneness with the mountain. Pride is the poison that split the school.

## Relations

Rivals of each other and of the Vanari; allies against the Hunger Colossus, reluctantly.

## Conflict and defence

Fire-palms, staff forms, silent strikes and wandering fist styles.

## Trade

Training, manuals and medicine.

## Strengths

Martial arts, discipline.

## Vulnerabilities

Qi-drain; rivalry.

## Gameplay role

Martial-arts class trainers in Shen-Lu; order choice affects available techniques.

## Members and roles

- **Still Flame** — Order: Monks of fire and meditation.
- **Iron Staff** — Order: Masters of the staff.
- **Silent Stair** — Order: Monks sworn to silence.
- **Wandering Fists** — Order: Travelling martial artists.

## Notable figures

- **Abbot Wen Jinghai of the Still Flame** (Leader): Believes the Colossus can be fought without qi.
- **Sister Mei of the Silent Stair** (Monk): Has broken her vow of silence to warn of the Colossus.
- **Brother Fang** (Wandering Fist): Travels the crossroads challenging every master.

## Quest seeds

- Reunite the four orders against the Colossus.
- Find out which sect built the Colossus.
