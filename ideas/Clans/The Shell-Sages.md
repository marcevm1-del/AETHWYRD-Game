# The Shell-Sages

> Turtle-folk in samurai armour and robes, slow and wise.

Keepers of the islands' oldest contracts.

## At a glance

- **Home:** [[Regions/Kitsune Isles|Kitsune Isles]]
- **Source board:** Turtle Clan
- **Leader:** Elder Kamejiro of the First Promise
- **Story hook:** A Shell-Sage remembers the broken promise that sank an island.

## Origin

The Shell-Sages are turtle-folk who witnessed the first promises between foxes and fishermen that made the Kitsune Isles. They have kept those contracts ever since, carved on the insides of their shells.

## Society

Elders whose shells are covered in contracts; younger turtles serve as samurai or wander as kappa cousins; the most ancient carry islands on their backs.

## Beliefs

A promise is heavier than a shell and lasts longer. The isle that sank was a promise broken.

## Relations

Respected by the fox shrines, consulted by the spirit inns, sorrowful about the Hollow Bride.

## Conflict and defence

Shell Samurai fight in heavy armour; kappa cousins trip enemies into water.

## Trade

Contract-keeping, witnessing and remembering.

## Strengths

Memory, armour, contract-magic.

## Vulnerabilities

Slowness; spilled water (for the kappa cousins).

## Gameplay role

Contract-witnessing unlocks spirit-inn privileges; Shell-Sage memories reveal the Hollow Bride's past.

## Members and roles

- **Promise-Keepers** — Elder: Keep the islands' oldest contracts.
- **Shell Samurai** — Warrior: Turtle-folk in samurai armour.
- **Kappa Cousins** — Trickster: River turtles with water-filled heads.
- **Island-Backs** — Ancient: Turtles so old they have islands on their shells.

## Notable figures

- **Elder Kamejiro of the First Promise** (Leader): Remembers the broken promise that sank an island.
- **Kappa Gawa** (Trickster): A river-cousin owed a cucumber debt from three generations ago.
- **Old Shimajiro** (Island-Back): So old an island grows on his shell; he hasn't moved in a century.

## Quest seeds

- Learn from Kamejiro who broke the promise and how to mend it.
- Settle Kappa Gawa's cucumber debt.
