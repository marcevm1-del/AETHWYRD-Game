# The Ibis Scribes

> Ibis- and stork-folk scribes in robes, with staffs and scrolls.

Zarvan's poets and archivists, keepers of the universities.

## At a glance

- **Home:** [[Regions/Zarvan|Zarvan]]
- **Source board:** Stork/ibis Clan
- **Leader:** Scribe Tahut Reedpen
- **Story hook:** An Ibis Scribe has found a poem that could end the div war.

## Origin

The Ibis Scribes are ibis- and stork-folk who founded Zarvan's universities. Their first scribe wrote down the garden treaties when the shahs and div-kings signed them in water.

## Society

Universities organised by school: reed-pen scribes, poet-ibises, stork messengers and crane dancers.

## Beliefs

A word written outlives the hand. Poetry is prayer, and the best poem ends a war.

## Relations

Scholars of the shah's court, allies of the Sun-Hawks and the Coilborn, respected by Zahhar.

## Conflict and defence

Rarely; a scribe's weapon is a well-placed argument.

## Trade

Scholarship, copying, poetry and teaching.

## Strengths

Knowledge, persuasion.

## Vulnerabilities

Physical frailty.

## Gameplay role

Poetry-recital dialogue options in Zarvan; university library hub.

## Members and roles

- **Reed-Pens** — Scholar: Scribes of the universities.
- **Poet-Ibises** — Poet: Poets of the garden cities.
- **Stork Messengers** — Messenger: Carry letters between cities.
- **Crane Dancers** — Performer: Dancers of the festivals.

## Notable figures

- **Scribe Tahut Reedpen** (Leader): Has found a poem that could end the div war.
- **Poet Shirin** (Poet-Ibis): Writes verses that make roses bloom.
- **Stork Hamid** (Messenger): Carries letters between the cities.

## Quest seeds

- Help Tahut recite the poem to Zahhar.
- Deliver Shirin's verses to the Rose Palace.
