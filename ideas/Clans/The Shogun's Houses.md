# The Shogun's Houses

> Samurai lords in lacquered armour under torii gates, archers in layered silk, an oni-masked general.

The great houses that serve the shogun, each with its own armour colour and crest. Some have quietly accepted Kurogane's masks.

## At a glance

- **Home:** [[Regions/Hoshizora|Hoshizora]]
- **Source board:** Emperor Clan
- **Leader:** Lord Takeda Ryunosuke of the Crimson House
- **Story hook:** A house lord wears Kurogane's mask in secret and wants to take it off before his own retainers notice.

## Origin

The Shogun's Houses descend from the warlords who fought each other for a century until the first shogun united them with a single sword-stroke and a marriage. Each house still carries the crest its founder wore in that war.

## Society

Great houses with crests, armour-colours and retainers, each owing service and hostages to the shogun. Samurai serve lords, ashigaru serve samurai, and everyone serves the shrines.

## Beliefs

Duty to the house, honour in death, respect for the spirits. A lord who neglects a shrine invites the oni.

## Relations

The Crimson House is loyal to the shogun; the Raven House is ambitious and patient; the Jade House trades with the world; the Masked Houses have quietly accepted Kurogane.

## Conflict and defence

Mounted archers, katana duelists, spear-lines of ashigaru and onmyoji advisers who read the spirits before battle.

## Trade

Steel, silk, lacquer, rice and the services of their swordsmiths.

## Strengths

Discipline, steel, organisation, cavalry.

## Vulnerabilities

Rigid hierarchy; rivalries the oni exploit.

## Gameplay role

House reputation tracks in Hoshizora; samurai mentors teach sword-styles tied to each house.

## Members and roles

- **Crimson House** — Great house: Red-lacquered samurai of the capital.
- **Raven House** — Great house: Black-armoured archers of the north.
- **Jade House** — Great house: Coastal lords, masters of the fleets.
- **Masked Houses** — Fallen: Houses that have accepted Kurogane's masks.

## Notable figures

- **Lord Takeda Ryunosuke** (Crimson House lord): Wears Kurogane's mask in secret and wants it off before his retainers notice.
- **Lady Kurosawa Akane** (Raven House heir): Sees the oni war as her path to the shogunate.
- **Old Jiro** (Ashigaru veteran): Has served three lords and outlived two, and knows where all the bodies are.

## Quest seeds

- Remove Takeda's mask without killing him or shaming his house.
- Expose the Raven House's bargain with the Masked Houses.
