# The Sand-Stingers

> Scorpion-bodied folk with human torsos.

Nomads of the deep desert who guard the Colossus's path.

## At a glance

- **Home:** [[Regions/Sunscar|Sunscar]]
- **Source board:** Scorpiones Clan
- **Leader:** Matriarch Qarra Duneclaw
- **Story hook:** The Sand-Stingers want the Colossus stopped without being destroyed.

## Origin

The Sand-Stingers are scorpion-bodied nomads of Sunscar's deep desert who were the vanished empire's border guards. When the empire fell, they kept guarding the borders of a land that no longer existed.

## Society

Matriarchal clans under venom-priestesses; warriors, path-guards and burrowers serve the clan.

## Beliefs

The sacred venom carries the clan's memory; a sting given in anger is a sin.

## Relations

Guardians of the Colossus's path; wary of the Well-Keepers; enemies of treasure-hunters.

## Conflict and defence

Pincers, stingers and ambushes from beneath the sand.

## Trade

Venom for medicine, desert guiding and border tolls.

## Strengths

Poison, armour, desert-craft.

## Vulnerabilities

Cold nights; water.

## Gameplay role

Sand-Stinger antivenom crafting; allies in the Colossus fight.

## Members and roles

- **Dune-Claws** — Warrior: Scorpion warriors of the deep desert.
- **Venom-Priestesses** — Priest: Keepers of the sacred venom.
- **Path-Guards** — Guard: Guard the Colossus's path.
- **Burrowers** — Scout: Hide in the sand and wait.

## Notable figures

- **Matriarch Qarra Duneclaw** (Leader): Wants the Colossus stopped without being destroyed.
- **Priestess Sethra** (Venom-Priestess): Keeps the clan's memory in the sacred venom.
- **Dune-Claw Ruk** (Warrior): Has fought the Colossus and lived.

## Quest seeds

- Find a way to stop the Colossus without destroying it.
- Learn the clan's memory through Sethra's venom ritual.
