# The Sett-folk

> Stout badger-folk in knitted jumpers, waistcoats and reading spectacles; homes full of books, tea and armchairs.

Whisperwood's oldest residents, living in great burrow-halls under the hornbeams. They are the region's librarians, solicitors and grumpy grandparents, and they have watched every rookie come and go.

## At a glance

- **Home:** [[Regions/Whisperwood|Whisperwood]]
- **Source board:** Badger Clan
- **Leader:** Old Barnaby Brockwhistle, Solicitor
- **Story hook:** An old Sett-folk solicitor holds the deed to the ruined waystations and wants to know why the goblins are digging under them.

## Origin

The Sett-folk have lived under Whisperwood since before the waystations, and they hold the deeds to prove it.

## Society

Extended families in burrow-halls, each with its library; disputes go to the burrow solicitors.

## Beliefs

Everything worth keeping should be written down; everything worth saying should be said over tea.

## Relations

Grumpy friends of Brindlewick, wary of the goblins, fond of rookies who return their books.

## Conflict and defence

Rarely fight, but the Honey Badgers are terrifying when they do.

## Trade

Books, legal services, honey and maps of the underground.

## Strengths

Knowledge, tunnels, stubbornness.

## Vulnerabilities

Slow to act; easily offended.

## Gameplay role

Lore library hub for Whisperwood; Sett-folk tunnels provide fast travel in the region.

## Members and roles

- **Burrow Solicitors** — Lawkeeper: Keepers of deeds, wills and grudges.
- **Hedge Librarians** — Scholar: Curators of the great underground book-halls.
- **Night Diggers** — Engineer: Tunnellers who know every sinkhole in the wood.
- **Honey Badgers** — Warrior: Small, furious and impossible to scare off.

## Notable figures

- **Old Barnaby Brockwhistle** (Solicitor): Holds the deed to the waystations and a grudge against everyone.
- **Hazel Brockwhistle** (Hedge Librarian): Barnaby's granddaughter; wants to catalogue the Labyrinth's stone.
- **Grub** (Honey Badger): Small, furious and loyal to whoever feeds him.

## Quest seeds

- Find out why the goblins are digging under the waystations.
- Return thirty years of overdue library books.
