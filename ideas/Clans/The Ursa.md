# The Ursa

> Bear-folk wizards, armoured berserkers and fur-cloaked druids.

Bear-folk of the fjords who hibernate through the darkest weeks and wake to defend the longhouses.

## At a glance

- **Home:** [[Regions/Frostveil|Frostveil]]
- **Source board:** Grizzly Bear Clan
- **Leader:** Sleeping Chieftain Bjornhild
- **Story hook:** Ymgrir's winter has gone on so long that the Ursa have not woken up.

## Origin

The Ursa are bear-folk who lived in Frostveil's pine forests before the first longship was built. The skalds say a bear taught the first humans to keep a fire, and the Ursa never contradict them.

## Society

Dens of related families under a chieftain, traditionally the one who sleeps longest and wakes strongest. Den-Druids stay awake through the winter to watch over the sleepers.

## Beliefs

Winter is a sleep, not a death, and every sleep ends. A bear who wakes early carries the den's luck alone until the others rise.

## Relations

Old allies of the human clans, who leave honey at the dens each autumn. Ymgrir's endless winter means the Ursa have not woken at all this year.

## Conflict and defence

Berserkers charge in fur and iron; Rune-Bears carve frost-runes into their staffs and fight with fire.

## Trade

Honey, furs from shed winter coats, herbs and rune-carving.

## Strengths

Strength, rage, resistance to cold.

## Vulnerabilities

Hibernation leaves the dens defenceless.

## Gameplay role

Waking the dens unlocks Ursa allies for the Frostveil siege and a bear-rune smithing path.

## Members and roles

- **Rune-Bears** — Wizard: Bear-folk mages with staffs and scrolls.
- **Berserkers** — Warrior: Furious fighters in fur and iron.
- **Den-Druids** — Druid: Keepers of the hibernation dens.
- **Honey-Wardens** — Commoner: Beekeepers and foragers of the fjord forests.

## Notable figures

- **Sleeping Chieftain Bjornhild** (Leader): Has not woken, and her den is going cold.
- **Den-Druid Ulla Pinepaw** (Druid): Has kept watch alone for too long and is starting to see things in the snow.
- **Brakki** (Berserker): Woke early, alone and furious, and is guarding the den from frost wolves.

## Quest seeds

- Find the summer-wood that wakes the Ursa before Ymgrir's army reaches the dens.
- Help Brakki hold the den against the frost wolves for one long night.
