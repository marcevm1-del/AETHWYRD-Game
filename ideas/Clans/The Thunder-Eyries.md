# The Thunder-Eyries

> Hawk-headed eagle-folk with storm-grey wings, rifles and spears; some marked with lightning scars.

Bird-folk who nest above the cliff-towns and serve as storm-callers' scouts. Raikaru hunts them first.

## At a glance

- **Home:** [[Regions/Stormreach|Stormreach]]
- **Source board:** Eagle clan
- **Leader:** Wind-Scout Kerrak Boltfeather
- **Story hook:** An eagle-folk scout claims Raikaru can be tricked into chasing a false storm.

## Origin

The Thunder-Eyries arrived on Stormreach's cliffs during a hurricane that drove them from the eastern isles. The storm-callers let them nest above the towns in return for scouting.

## Society

Nest-families ruled by Nest-Mothers; scouts and gunners earn status by flights flown in storms.

## Beliefs

Lightning that strikes and spares you is a blessing. Scar-Bearers are honoured as holy.

## Relations

Allies of the storm-callers; Raikaru hunts them first.

## Conflict and defence

Storm-Scouts dive; Bolt-Rifles shoot lightning-charged rounds.

## Trade

Scouting, lightning-glass, feathers for fletching.

## Strengths

Flight in storms, sharp eyes.

## Vulnerabilities

Calm air makes them clumsy.

## Gameplay role

Lightning-rifle training; storm-reading hints in Stormreach.

## Members and roles

- **Storm-Scouts** — Scout: Eagle-folk who ride the thunderheads.
- **Bolt-Rifles** — Marksman: Lightning-charged gunners of the cliff-towns.
- **Nest-Mothers** — Elder: Keepers of the high eyries.
- **Scar-Bearers** — Blessed: Survivors of lightning strikes, honoured as holy.

## Notable figures

- **Wind-Scout Kerrak Boltfeather** (Leader): Claims Raikaru can be tricked into chasing a false storm.
- **Nest-Mother Aalya** (Elder): Keeper of the highest eyrie.
- **Skree** (Scar-Bearer): Struck by lightning twice; claims to hear it coming.

## Quest seeds

- Build a false storm to lure Raikaru away.
- Recover a fallen scout from the Black Peaks.
