# The Hollow Ones

> Living shadows and silhouettes with long limbs.

Shadow-folk of the deepest webs, who never come into the light.

## At a glance

- **Home:** [[Regions/Umbravel|Umbravel]]
- **Source board:** Shade Clan
- **Leader:** The Unshadowed
- **Story hook:** A Hollow One has started casting a shadow of its own.

## Origin

The Hollow Ones are living shadows from the deepest webs below Umbravel, older than the city. They were there in the dark when the founders arrived, and they let the founders stay.

## Society

No leaders in the usual sense; the oldest, the Unshadowed, are listened to. They gather in silence and communicate by touch.

## Beliefs

Light is a visitor; darkness is home. To cast a shadow is to become something other than what you were.

## Relations

Careful allies of the Nightwings; the Web Queen's brood avoids them; the city treats them as ghosts.

## Conflict and defence

They smother light and strike from the dark with long, stretching limbs.

## Trade

Darkness itself: they can make places unseen, and the weaver-guilds pay for hidden vaults.

## Strengths

Stealth, darkness, immunity to physical harm in shadow.

## Vulnerabilities

Bright light; being seen clearly.

## Gameplay role

Shadow-step abilities in Umbravel; Hollow Ones hide the player from patrols.

## Members and roles

- **Unshadowed** — Elder: The oldest shadows.
- **Long-Limbs** — Hunter: Stretched shadows that hunt in the dark.
- **Drifters** — Scout: Shadows that move between webs.
- **Casting** — Changed: Shadows that have begun to cast shadows.

## Notable figures

- **The Unshadowed** (Elder): The oldest shadow; knows what lived in the cavern before the city.
- **Long-Reach** (Hunter): Stretches across whole districts to hunt the Web Queen's brood.
- **Flicker-Without-Light** (Casting): Has started casting a shadow of its own and is frightened.

## Quest seeds

- Find out why Flicker-Without-Light has begun to cast a shadow.
- Ask the Unshadowed what lived in the cavern before Umbravel.
