# The Stillhorns

> Minotaur and ox-folk, heavy-horned and patient.

Herders and tremor-readers of the Quaking Lands, who feel quakes through their hooves.

## At a glance

- **Home:** [[Regions/Quaking Lands|Quaking Lands]]
- **Source board:** Oxen Clan
- **Leader:** Elder Tauron Deephoof
- **Story hook:** A Stillhorn elder has felt something in the ground that is not Quakeback.

## Origin

The Stillhorns are minotaur and ox-folk who herd the Quaking Lands' stone-horned cattle. They were reading tremors through their hooves long before the human Stillers learned to.

## Society

Herds led by Deep-Hooves elders; tremor-readers and guards serve the spring-towns as well as their own herds.

## Beliefs

The ground speaks to those who listen with their feet. Quakeback is not a monster but a sleeper with bad dreams.

## Relations

Partners of the Stiller spring-towns, uneasy about anyone who wants to fight Quakeback.

## Conflict and defence

Minotaur Guards with great axes; charge attacks that knock enemies down.

## Trade

Cattle, tremor-charts, leather and crystals brought up by quakes.

## Strengths

Strength, tremor-sense, endurance.

## Vulnerabilities

Mazes; confined spaces.

## Gameplay role

Tremor-warning system in the Quaking Lands; Stillhorn allies stabilise ground during fights.

## Members and roles

- **Tremor-Readers** — Mystic: Read the ground through their hooves.
- **Herd-Keepers** — Commoner: Herders of the stone-horned oxen.
- **Minotaur Guards** — Warrior: Defenders of the spring-towns.
- **Deep-Hooves** — Elder: Ancient minotaurs who remember the old maps.

## Notable figures

- **Elder Tauron Deephoof** (Leader): Has felt something in the ground that is not Quakeback.
- **Asterion Quickhoof** (Tremor-Reader): Young and talented; the elders doubt him.
- **Bessa** (Herd-Keeper): Knows every cow by name and every quake by smell.

## Quest seeds

- Find what Tauron felt under the ground.
- Prove Asterion's prediction of a great quake right.
