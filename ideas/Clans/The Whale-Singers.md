# The Whale-Singers

> Whale-folk, huge and gentle.

The singers who speak to Thalassa.

## At a glance

- **Home:** [[Regions/Dunewhale Drift|Dunewhale Drift]]
- **Source board:** Whale Clan
- **Leader:** First Singer Oruna-of-the-Deep-Note
- **Story hook:** A Whale-Singer has heard another sand-whale singing.

## Origin

The Whale-Singers are whale-folk who learned to speak to Thalassa when the first sand-whale towns were built. They are the only ones who can guide her, and the town would be lost without them.

## Society

Singers led by the First Singer; barnacle-riders live closest to Thalassa's hide; spout-watchers keep lookout; calf-keepers remember the other whales.

## Beliefs

Thalassa is mother and her song is the town's heartbeat. When she stops singing, the town will die.

## Relations

Partners of the Plateback, respected by the town captain, terrified of Glassmaw.

## Conflict and defence

Sonic songs that stun and shatter glass, including Glassmaw's teeth.

## Trade

Guiding the whale; songs sold to oasis towns.

## Strengths

Song, guidance, sonic attacks.

## Vulnerabilities

Glassmaw; silence.

## Gameplay role

Whale-song steering of the moving town; sonic attacks crack Glassmaw's teeth.

## Members and roles

- **Deep-Note Singers** — Mystic: Speak to Thalassa.
- **Barnacle-Riders** — Worker: Live on Thalassa's back.
- **Spout-Watchers** — Scout: Watch for Glassmaw.
- **Calf-Keepers** — Elder: Remember the other sand-whales.

## Notable figures

- **First Singer Oruna-of-the-Deep-Note** (Leader): Has heard another sand-whale singing, far away.
- **Spout-Watcher Kel** (Scout): Watches for Glassmaw from Thalassa's Crown.
- **Old Calf-Keeper Ilo** (Elder): Remembers the other sand-whales and their songs.

## Quest seeds

- Find the other singing sand-whale.
- Help Kel track Glassmaw's glass trails.
