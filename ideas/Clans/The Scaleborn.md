# The Scaleborn

> Small and large dragon-folk: kobold monks, bards with frills, gold-scaled sages in robes.

Lay brothers and sisters of the dragon-monasteries who believe they hatched from the sleeper's dreams. Vyrmax calls them traitors to the drakes.

## At a glance

- **Home:** [[Regions/Dragon Spine|Dragon Spine]]
- **Source board:** Dragonkin Clan
- **Leader:** Brother Pyrrin Emberscale
- **Story hook:** A Scaleborn monk has started dreaming the sleeper's dreams, and they are getting shorter.

## Origin

The Scaleborn say they hatched from the sleeper's dreams, and the dragon-monks do not argue. They have served the monasteries since the first bone was hollowed into a hall.

## Society

Lay brothers and sisters ranked by scale-colour as they age, from kobold-brown to sage-gold.

## Beliefs

Keep the sleeper asleep; tend the lullabies; never wake what dreams you.

## Relations

Loyal to the monks; Vyrmax calls them traitors to the drakes; friendly with the Wyvern Oath.

## Conflict and defence

Ember-Wings guard the Bone Road with fire-breath and spears.

## Trade

Monastery crafts, lullaby-scrolls and incense.

## Strengths

Fire breath, scholarship, devotion.

## Vulnerabilities

Cold; divided loyalties.

## Gameplay role

Lore-keepers for Dragon Spine; dream sequences reveal hints about the sleeper.

## Members and roles

- **Kobold Lay-Brothers** — Monk: Small dragon-folk who tend the monasteries.
- **Frill-Bards** — Bard: Song-keepers of the sleeper's lullabies.
- **Gold-Scale Sages** — Scholar: Eldest dragonkin, keepers of the bone-lore.
- **Ember-Wings** — Guardian: Winged dragonkin who guard the Bone Road.

## Notable figures

- **Brother Pyrrin Emberscale** (Leader): Has started dreaming the sleeper's dreams.
- **Sister Tamsa Frillsong** (Frill-Bard): Sings the lullabies and adds new verses no one taught her.
- **Old Aurum-Scale** (Gold-Scale Sage): Remembers when the drakes and the Scaleborn were one people.

## Quest seeds

- Interpret Pyrrin's dreams before they end.
- Broker a truce with Vyrmax using Old Aurum-Scale's memory.
