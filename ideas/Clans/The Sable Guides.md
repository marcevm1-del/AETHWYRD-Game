# The Sable Guides

> Black jackal-headed figures in suits or linen, with staffs and gold.

Guides of the dead and of caravans, who know every safe path through the dunes.

## At a glance

- **Home:** [[Regions/Sunscar|Sunscar]]
- **Source board:** Jackal Clan
- **Leader:** Guide-Master Inpu Nightpath
- **Story hook:** A Sable Guide has been asked to guide a caravan to a city that no longer exists.

## Origin

The Sable Guides are jackal-headed people who guided the vanished empire's dead into the afterlife. When the empire burned, they turned to guiding the living through the desert instead.

## Society

Guides, priests, traders and tomb-watchers, each a hereditary role. A Sable Guide's path is passed from parent to child along with their staff.

## Beliefs

Every road leads somewhere, even for the dead. A guide who loses a traveller must walk the road again until they find them.

## Relations

Respected by the Well-Keepers, distrusted by the Unburied Court, hired by every caravan.

## Conflict and defence

Staffs, curved daggers and a talent for leading enemies astray.

## Trade

Guiding caravans, guarding tombs and funeral rites.

## Strengths

Navigation, endurance, rapport with the dead.

## Vulnerabilities

Water; superstition.

## Gameplay role

Fast-travel guides in Sunscar; funeral rites that unlock lore from the dead.

## Members and roles

- **Path-Walkers** — Guide: Lead caravans through the dunes.
- **Death-Guides** — Priest: Guide the dead to rest.
- **Night-Suits** — Merchant: Jackal-headed traders in black.
- **Sable Watchers** — Guard: Guardians of the tombs.

## Notable figures

- **Guide-Master Inpu Nightpath** (Leader): Has been asked to guide a caravan to a city that no longer exists.
- **Anpu the Younger** (Path-Walker): Inpu's son, eager to earn his own staff.
- **Mother Seshet** (Death-Guide): Speaks for the dead at funerals, and sometimes the dead answer.

## Quest seeds

- Join the caravan to the city that no longer exists.
- Help Anpu earn his staff by finding a lost traveller.
