# The River-Lords

> Hippo-folk in naval coats and generals' uniforms, enormously dignified.

They control the river fords on the gold road and charge tolls in gold, which makes them Mansacor's next target.

## At a glance

- **Home:** [[Regions/Zaruma|Zaruma]]
- **Source board:** Hippo Clan
- **Leader:** Admiral Hippolyte Fordwarden
- **Story hook:** The River-Lords will close the fords to everyone until the manticore is dead.

## Origin

The River-Lords are hippo-folk who settled the fords of the gold road when it was still a cattle-track. They charged the first caravans a toll, and they have charged every caravan since.

## Society

Ford-houses led by an Admiral, with Ford-Wardens collecting tolls and Tusk-Guard enforcing them. Wealth is counted in fords held.

## Beliefs

A crossing has a price; a debt unpaid sinks you. They are scrupulously honest about tolls and ruthless about debts.

## Relations

Advised by the Tuskers, taxed by the Pride kings, despised by the caravaneers, and now Mansacor's next target.

## Conflict and defence

Tusk-Guard in armour charge through water; Mud-Bathers wait under the surface.

## Trade

River tolls, barges and ferrying.

## Strengths

Strength, water combat, wealth.

## Vulnerabilities

Slow on land; greedy.

## Gameplay role

Ford tolls are a gold sink in Zaruma; the River-Lords are key to the manticore hunt.

## Members and roles

- **Ford-Wardens** — Tollkeeper: Hippos who own the gold road's river crossings.
- **Admirals** — Officer: Commanders of the river barges.
- **Mud-Bathers** — Commoner: Easy-going river folk, very dangerous when crossed.
- **Tusk-Guard** — Warrior: Armoured enforcers of the toll.

## Notable figures

- **Admiral Hippolyte Fordwarden** (Leader): Will close every ford until the manticore is dead.
- **Ford-Warden Bembe** (Tollkeeper): Has never let anyone cross without paying, including a king.
- **Old Mudlark** (Mud-Bather): Lazy, cheerful and secretly the best fighter on the river.

## Quest seeds

- Persuade Hippolyte to reopen the fords before the northern cities starve.
- Use the fords to trap Mansacor in deep water.
