# The Capfolk

> Walking mushrooms of every size, from tiny sprouts to towering bruisers.

Mushroom-folk who live alongside the Thimblefolk and the beetles; Mycelia is their nightmare cousin.

## At a glance

- **Home:** [[Regions/Mossdeep|Mossdeep]]
- **Source board:** Mushroom clan
- **Leader:** Old Puffball Morel
- **Story hook:** A Capfolk elder says the Spore Lord was once one of them.

## Origin

The Capfolk are walking mushrooms who sprouted in Mossdeep's warm, mana-rich soil. They say they grew from the same root-web as Mycelia, long ago, before the Spore Lord went wrong.

## Society

Rings of mushrooms that grow from one spore-mother. The oldest Puffballs speak for each ring, and new Sprouts appear after every warm rain.

## Beliefs

Everything rots and everything returns; the soil remembers. To spread without asking is the worst crime a fungus can commit.

## Relations

Gentle neighbours of the Thimblefolk, allies of the Carapace, and horrified by Mycelia, whom they call the Cousin.

## Conflict and defence

Toadstool Guards fight with staves; Morel Mystics release sleeping-spores that never harm.

## Trade

Medicinal mushrooms, soil-reading and composting for the Thimblefolk gardens.

## Strengths

Spore magic, regrowth, soil-sense.

## Vulnerabilities

Dry heat, fire and salt.

## Gameplay role

The Capfolk link Mossdeep to the Sylvaran blight questline; spore-crafting recipes.

## Members and roles

- **Puffballs** — Elder: Round, slow and wise.
- **Toadstool Guards** — Warrior: Red-capped fighters.
- **Morel Mystics** — Mage: Mushroom-folk who speak with the soil.
- **Sprouts** — Young: Tiny new mushrooms, very curious.

## Notable figures

- **Old Puffball Morel** (Leader): Says the Spore Lord was once one of them and knows its true name.
- **Inky Cap** (Morel Mystic): Can hear the root-web, and the Cousin's voice in it.
- **Sprig** (Sprout): Newly grown and asking everyone what it is for.

## Quest seeds

- Learn Mycelia's true name from Old Puffball and carry it to Sylvaran.
- Help Inky Cap listen to the root-web without being taken by it.
