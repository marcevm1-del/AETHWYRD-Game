# The Pipers

> Satyrs and fauns with goat legs, pipes and spears.

Musicians and messengers of both courts, who are allowed to cross between them.

## At a glance

- **Home:** [[Regions/Everdusk|Everdusk]]
- **Source board:** Satyr/Faun Clan
- **Leader:** Fennick Reedsong
- **Story hook:** A Piper has been forbidden from playing, and the forest is going quiet.

## Origin

The Pipers are satyrs and fauns who wandered into Everdusk playing music so beautiful that both courts claimed them. They are the only folk allowed to cross freely between the Summer and Winter Courts.

## Society

Loose bands of musicians, messengers and wild fauns; each Piper's tune is their passport.

## Beliefs

Music is older than the courts; a forest without song is a dead forest.

## Relations

Messengers for both courts, friends of the Antlered Court, now forbidden to play by the Briar King.

## Conflict and defence

Spears and enchanting tunes that make enemies dance until they drop.

## Trade

Music, messages and fey-goods.

## Strengths

Music, charm, mobility between courts.

## Vulnerabilities

Cannot fight without music.

## Gameplay role

Music-based puzzles; Pipers open paths between the courts.

## Members and roles

- **Court Pipers** — Musician: Play for both courts.
- **Goat-Runners** — Messenger: Carry messages between the courts.
- **Wild Fauns** — Druid: Live in the deep woods.
- **Satyr Spears** — Warrior: Defend the glades.

## Notable figures

- **Fennick Reedsong** (Leader): Has been forbidden to play, and the forest is going quiet.
- **Melisande** (Court Piper): Plays for both courts and spies for neither.
- **Old Billygrim** (Wild Faun): Lives in the deep woods and knows the forest's oldest songs.

## Quest seeds

- Lift the Briar King's ban on Fennick's music.
- Carry a message between the courts with Melisande.
