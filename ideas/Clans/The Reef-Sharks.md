# The Reef-Sharks

> Shark-folk, from naval officers to gaping monsters.

Hunters and enforcers of the reefs, hired by both port and pirates.

## At a glance

- **Home:** [[Regions/Goldmere Coast|Goldmere Coast]]
- **Source board:** Shark Clan
- **Leader:** Commodore Mako Grinwater
- **Story hook:** The Reef-Sharks have stopped hunting near the wreck-tide; they are afraid.

## Origin

The Reef-Sharks are shark-folk who hunted Goldmere's reefs before the port was built. When the salvagers came, the sharks found that enforcing claims paid better than hunting fish.

## Society

Commodores lead squadrons of Grinwaters, Hammerheads and Great Whites; rank is shown by teeth worn on a cord.

## Beliefs

Keep moving or drown. Never bite the hand that pays you, until the contract ends.

## Relations

Hired by both port and pirates; old rivals of the Shellguard; now too frightened of the wreck-tide to hunt there.

## Conflict and defence

Fast, brutal strikes in the water; Hammerheads scout, Great Whites finish.

## Trade

Enforcement, protection and deep-water escort.

## Strengths

Speed and power in water, smell for blood.

## Vulnerabilities

Must keep moving; poor on land.

## Gameplay role

Hireable underwater escorts in Goldmere; their fear is an early warning of the Krakenmother.

## Members and roles

- **Commodores** — Officer: Shark-folk naval officers.
- **Grinwaters** — Enforcer: Enforcers of the reefs.
- **Hammerheads** — Scout: Wide-eyed scouts.
- **Great Whites** — Elite: The largest and most feared.

## Notable figures

- **Commodore Mako Grinwater** (Leader): Has stopped hunting near the wreck-tide and won't say why.
- **Hammer** (Hammerhead scout): Saw something in the deep that made him swim home.
- **Big Grey** (Great White): The largest shark-folk on the coast, retired and bored.

## Quest seeds

- Find out what frightened the Reef-Sharks away from the wreck-tide.
- Hire Big Grey out of retirement for one last job.
