# The Heavenfolk

> Broad-winged people in layered robes and light armour; feathers range from gold to storm-grey.

The winged people of the eyries, divided into lantern-bearers who guide the dead to the Ledges of Last Flight and armoured wingguard who once escorted royal messengers and now serve no king.

## At a glance

- **Home:** [[Regions/Heavenreach|Heavenreach]]
- **Source board:** Angel Clan
- **Leader:** Elder Aeryth Highwind
- **Story hook:** A Heavenfolk lantern-bearer has lost her lantern, and the dead on her route have stopped arriving.

## Origin

The Heavenfolk say the Titans taught their ancestors to fly. They served every kingdom as messengers until a king executed one of them for the contents of a letter.

## Society

Ruled by a council of eldest flyers; status rests on flight, oath-keeping and lineage of messages delivered.

## Beliefs

A message once taken must be delivered; the dead fly one last time from the Ledges.

## Relations

Distant from the ground kingdoms, allied with the Snowmanes, hostile to Aquilon's rocs.

## Conflict and defence

Wingguard fight in tight aerial formations with spears and bows.

## Trade

Feathers, falcons, high-altitude herbs and, rarely, scouting.

## Strengths

Flight, sight, unbreakable oaths.

## Vulnerabilities

Hollow bones; pride; old grudges.

## Gameplay role

Courier and scouting quests; they provide flight-based abilities, never flying mounts.

## Members and roles

- **Lantern-Bearers** — Guide: Carry the dead's lanterns to the Ledges of Last Flight.
- **Wingguard** — Warrior: Armoured flyers who once escorted royal couriers.
- **Sky-Scribes** — Messenger: Keepers of the old courier-cant and the oath-ledgers.
- **Grounded** — Mourned: Heavenfolk who can no longer fly, treated as living ghosts.

## Notable figures

- **Elder Aeryth Highwind** (Council elder): Refuses to fly messages for any crown again.
- **Ilsa Brightquill** (Young lantern-bearer): Wants to fly messages again and is looking for a partner.
- **Captain Halvor Galeward** (Wingguard captain): Lost his wing to a roc; commands from the ground.

## Quest seeds

- Carry the first Heavenfolk message in a generation.
- Retrieve a child taken as tribute by Aquilon.
