# The Hearthbound

> Ordinary humans: scholars, knights, archers, climbers.

The baseline people of the heartland, varied and stubborn, alongside the Veilborn who were summoned among them.

## At a glance

- **Home:** [[Regions/Aldmere|Aldmere]]
- **Source board:** Human clan
- **Leader:** Reeve Maud Ashby
- **Story hook:** A family claims a Veilborn ancestor and has proof no one wants to see.

## Origin

The Hearthbound are the ordinary people of Aldmere: farmers, crafters, clerks and soldiers whose families have lived on the same land for generations. Among them live the Veilborn, summoned from another world, and their descendants.

## Society

Villages run by reeves, towns by guilds and the whole by the crown. Family names come from trades and places, and land passes to whoever works it.

## Beliefs

The seven day-gods, the Church and the harvest. A guest at the door is fed first and questioned later.

## Relations

Loyal to the crown, suspicious of nobles, generous to rookies, and weary of the barrow-dead.

## Conflict and defence

Crown Knights lead militia levies with spears and bows; every village has a bell and a muster-list.

## Trade

Wheat, wool, timber, honey and the business of adventurers.

## Strengths

Numbers, resilience, adaptability.

## Vulnerabilities

No magic of their own; easily divided.

## Gameplay role

The baseline human faction; Hearthbound reputation affects prices and quests across the heartland.

## Members and roles

- **Crown Knights** — Warrior: Sworn to the Aldmerian throne.
- **Valley Farmers** — Commoner: Families on the same land for generations.
- **Guild Scholars** — Scholar: Clerks, mapmakers and archivists.
- **Veilborn Descendants** — Mixed: Families with a summoned ancestor.

## Notable figures

- **Reeve Maud Ashby** (Leader of Ashby): Keeps the gates shut by day and the families fed by night.
- **Sir Edric Thornfield** (Crown Knight): Has sworn to stand at Greyhollow until Ashgrave falls.
- **Wren Hollis** (Veilborn descendant): Has her great-grandmother's diary from another world and doesn't know what to do with it.

## Quest seeds

- Help Maud evacuate the outer farms before the next barrow-march.
- Read Wren's diary with her and decide who else should see it.
