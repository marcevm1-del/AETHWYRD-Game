# The Antlered Court

> Stag-, elk- and antelope-folk with great antlers, in formal coats or bark armour; some carry swords, some staffs.

Mortal-blooded courtiers who serve the Summer Court as heralds and duellists. Their antlers shed each spring and are given as tokens of oath.

## At a glance

- **Home:** [[Regions/Everdusk|Everdusk]]
- **Source board:** Elk-kin/Antelopion Clan
- **Leader:** Herald Cervaine of the Summer Glades
- **Story hook:** An Antlered herald has offered an outsider his shed antler; accepting it means accepting a duel.

## Origin

The Antlered Court were mortal deer-folk who wandered into Everdusk in a hard winter and were given shelter by the Summer Court. Their price was service, and they have served ever since.

## Society

Ranked by antler-tines; antlers shed each spring are given as tokens of oath.

## Beliefs

An oath is a bond the fey cannot break; a duel settles what words cannot.

## Relations

Servants of the Summer Court; envoys to the Winter Court; friends of the Pipers.

## Conflict and defence

Duelists with swords; Elk Wardens with antler-charges.

## Trade

Diplomacy, heraldry, oath-witnessing.

## Strengths

Speed, dignity, oath-magic.

## Vulnerabilities

Bound by courtesy even in danger.

## Gameplay role

Fey-court diplomacy; duel minigame.

## Members and roles

- **Heralds** — Diplomat: Carry the Summer Court's messages.
- **Antler Duelists** — Duelist: Fight with swords and with oaths.
- **Elk Wardens** — Guard: Heavy-antlered guardians of the glades.
- **Antelope Runners** — Messenger: Swift courtiers who cross between courts.

## Notable figures

- **Herald Cervaine** (Leader): Has offered an outsider his shed antler and a duel.
- **Lady Brisket Swiftfoot** (Antelope Runner): Carries messages faster than any fey.
- **Old Thornbranch** (Elk Warden): Guards the Last Glade's gate.

## Quest seeds

- Accept Cervaine's antler and win the duel.
- Carry a Summer Court message to the Thorn Throne.
