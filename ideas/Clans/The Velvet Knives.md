# The Velvet Knives

> Panther-folk assassins and thieves in black.

Cinderhole's quietest guild, who sell secrets to Vasque and his enemies alike.

## At a glance

- **Home:** [[Regions/Cinderhole|Cinderhole]]
- **Source board:** Panthar Clan
- **Leader:** The Velvet Mother, Noirelle
- **Story hook:** A Velvet Knife has been hired to kill the alchemist who could put out the fires.

## Origin

The Velvet Knives began as a guild of panther-folk thieves who learned to breathe Cinderhole's smoke when no one else could. They now sell secrets to Vasque and to his enemies alike.

## Society

A matriarchal guild under the Velvet Mother, with silent assassins, whisperer spies and shadow-dancer thieves.

## Beliefs

Every secret has a price; every price has a buyer. Never kill a child; never break a contract.

## Relations

Vasque's informants and his greatest danger; rivals of the Undercity Whiskers.

## Conflict and defence

Silent blades, poison and smoke.

## Trade

Secrets, theft and assassination.

## Strengths

Stealth, information.

## Vulnerabilities

Mistrust; contracts.

## Gameplay role

Stealth faction in Cinderhole; spy network.

## Members and roles

- **Silent Ones** — Assassin: The guild's killers.
- **Whisperers** — Spy: The guild's information-sellers.
- **Shadow-Dancers** — Thief: The guild's thieves.
- **Velvet Mothers** — Leader: The guild's matriarchs.

## Notable figures

- **The Velvet Mother, Noirelle** (Leader): Has accepted a contract to kill the alchemist who could put out the fires.
- **Shade-Paw** (Silent One): Has started to doubt the contract.
- **Whisper** (Whisperer): Knows every secret in Cinderhole.

## Quest seeds

- Protect the alchemist from the Velvet Knives.
- Turn Shade-Paw.
