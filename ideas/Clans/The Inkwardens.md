# The Inkwardens

> Octopus-folk with tentacled lower bodies, tridents and ink.

Deep sea-folk of the Hollow Sea, keepers of the deepest charts.

## At a glance

- **Home:** [[Regions/Hollow Sea|Hollow Sea]]
- **Source board:** Octopoda Clan
- **Leader:** Chartmaster Octavian Deepink
- **Story hook:** An Inkwarden has charted the Lumen-Eater's hunting grounds.

## Origin

The Inkwardens are octopus-folk of the Hollow Sea who were there before the boat-towns arrived. They charted the lightless ocean by touch and taste, and their charts are the best in the world below.

## Society

Solitary scholars who gather only to share charts. Chartmasters hold the deepest maps; Ink-Mages and Trident Lords protect them.

## Beliefs

Knowledge is a current; hoard it and it stagnates. Every chart must be copied before its maker dies.

## Relations

Chart-sellers to the boat-towns, allies of the Drifting, hunted by the Lumen-Eater.

## Conflict and defence

Ink clouds, constriction, tridents and camouflage.

## Trade

Charts of the Hollow Sea, pearls and deep-salvage.

## Strengths

Intelligence, camouflage, regrowth.

## Vulnerabilities

Short-lived; solitary.

## Gameplay role

Chart-buying reveals the Hollow Sea map; Inkwarden allies blind enemies with ink.

## Members and roles

- **Chartmasters** — Scholar: Keepers of the deep charts.
- **Ink-Mages** — Mage: Octopus-folk who fight with ink.
- **Trident Lords** — Warrior: Tentacled warriors with tridents.
- **Shadow-Swimmers** — Scout: Camouflaged scouts of the deep.

## Notable figures

- **Chartmaster Octavian Deepink** (Leader): Has charted the Lumen-Eater's hunting grounds.
- **Murex** (Ink-Mage): Writes spells in ink that glow in the dark.
- **Sable-Arm** (Trident Lord): Lost three arms to the Lumen-Eater and grew them back angrier.

## Quest seeds

- Use Octavian's chart to plan an ambush on the Lumen-Eater.
- Copy a dying Chartmaster's maps before they are lost.
