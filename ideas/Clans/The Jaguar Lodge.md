# The Jaguar Lodge

> Spotted cat-warriors in jade and feathers, fast and fierce.

The Jaguar lodge of the river-cities' warrior orders, night-hunters on the canoe-roads.

## At a glance

- **Home:** [[Regions/Xochitl Mire|Xochitl Mire]]
- **Source board:** Leopard/Cheetah Clan
- **Leader:** War-Captain Balam Nightclaw
- **Story hook:** The Jaguar lodge has lost a warrior to Xibalbar and wants the head that took him.

## Origin

The Jaguar Lodge is the oldest of the river-cities' warrior orders, founded by the first river-king's bodyguard. Its warriors hunt the canoe-roads at night.

## Society

Ranked by hunts completed; cubs train for seven years before they are named.

## Beliefs

The night belongs to the jaguar; a warrior who kills without need loses their spots.

## Relations

Allies of the Sun-Feathered, rivals of the Scaled Lodges, at war with Xibalbar.

## Conflict and defence

Night ambushes with obsidian clubs and spears.

## Trade

Escort duty, pelts, jade.

## Strengths

Stealth, speed, night vision.

## Vulnerabilities

Pride; open water.

## Gameplay role

Stealth training; night-hunting bonuses in Xochitl Mire.

## Members and roles

- **Night-Claws** — Hunter: Night hunters of the canoe-roads.
- **Jade-Spotted** — Elite: Lodge champions in jade armour.
- **Cub-Runners** — Novice: Young warriors proving themselves.
- **Shadow-Coats** — Scout: Black jaguars who scout unseen.

## Notable figures

- **War-Captain Balam Nightclaw** (Leader): Lost a warrior to Xibalbar and wants the head that took him.
- **Ixchel Shadowcoat** (Scout): A black jaguar who can vanish in the reeds.
- **Cub Tikal** (Novice): Young, brave and too eager.

## Quest seeds

- Hunt the head of Xibalbar that killed Balam's warrior.
- Keep Cub Tikal alive on his first hunt.
