# The Iron Legions

> Roman-style legionaries in segmented armour with red cloaks.

Vharlen's legions, drilled from childhood, who carry their founders' pledge into every battle and bury their standards with their dead.

## At a glance

- **Home:** [[Regions/Vharlen|Vharlen]]
- **Source board:** Romanian Clan
- **Leader:** Legate Severa Marcellan
- **Story hook:** A legion has lost its standard to Morvane.

## Origin

The Iron Legions were founded with the empire itself, when the first emperor swore every citizen to serve and carved the pledge beneath the barracks. They have held the demon frontier for generations.

## Society

Legions of legionaries under centurions and legates; Praetorians guard the emperor; auxiliaries scout the passes. Every citizen serves a term, and rank in the legion shapes rank at home.

## Beliefs

The pledge first, the empire second, the self last. A legion that loses its standard is disbanded in shame.

## Relations

The empire's sword and shield; wary of the guild; respectful of Morvane as an enemy.

## Conflict and defence

Shield walls, short swords, javelins and disciplined manoeuvre; auxiliaries skirmish in the passes.

## Trade

Military service; the legions consume half the empire's grain and iron.

## Strengths

Discipline, numbers, organisation.

## Vulnerabilities

Rigid; slow to adapt.

## Gameplay role

Legion reputation track in Vharlen; standard-recovery questline.

## Members and roles

- **Legionaries** — Infantry: The backbone of Vharlen's army.
- **Centurions** — Officer: Commanders of a hundred.
- **Praetorians** — Elite: Guards of the emperor.
- **Frontier Auxiliaries** — Scout: Light troops of the passes.

## Notable figures

- **Legate Severa Marcellan** (Leader): Lost a standard to Morvane and means to take it back.
- **Centurion Gaius Brannock** (Officer): Twenty years on the frontier and still alive.
- **Lucan** (Auxiliary scout): Knows every pass and every shortcut.

## Quest seeds

- Recover the lost standard from Morvane's camp.
- Scout a fallen fort with Lucan.
