# Brindlewick Buildings and Structures

> **Built.** Generated by `Tools/blender/town_buildings.py` (Blender 5.x, no add-ons) and placed by the scene builder (`Game/Assets/_Game/Editor/Ring0SceneBuilder.Town.cs`, plus the forest pieces in `Ring0SceneBuilder.Whisperwood.cs`). Models: `Game/Assets/_Game/Art/Models/Buildings/<id>.fbx`. Source: `Art/Blender/town_buildings.blend`. Preview: `13_Production/screenshots/town_buildings_blender.png`. Aerial view of the placed town: `13_Production/screenshots/brindlewick_aerial.png`. Style comparison with anime towns: [[anime_building_style]].

## Style

A bright anime storybook town (the mood of the concept art, none of its assets):
- **Walls and footings:** stone footings; plaster walls in cream, warm, pink, blue and mint; dark timber framing.
- **Roofs:** steep (1.35× rise), flared at the eaves, in saturated blue, red, green, teal, orange and purple.
- **Windows and doors:** shuttered, sometimes arched windows with flower boxes; plank doors with iron rings and stone steps.
- **Detail:** turrets, dormers, balconies, bay windows, ivy, bunting, weathervanes, chimney pots, and shop signs with icons.
- **In game:** the `Ascend/Toon` shader gives two-tone cel shading, a violet shadow tint and a dark ink outline.

Every model is a single mesh with a few flat colours.

**Conventions:**
- Units are metres; ground at z = 0; each model is centred on its origin.
- The front door faces −Y in Blender, which is +Z in Unity at yaw 0.
- FBX exports with Unity axes and transforms applied, the same settings as the Ring 0 monsters.

## Where they stand (Ring 0 scene)

The plaza where you start stays open for the NPCs and the movement test course; the buildings ring it. All 89 placements fit.

| Area | What's there |
|---|---|
| Plaza | Guild hall, the Sleeping Griffon, the smithy, the temple, the orphanage, houses, the gatehouse and two guard towers |
| Market Street | South of the plaza: bakery, apothecary, tailor and bookshop to the west; general store, tavern and teahouse to the east |
| Hall Square | The fountain, the town hall, the clock tower and the mage tower; the observatory to the east |
| Rose Lane | The new quarter behind the hall: row houses, the turret corner house, the flower shop, the carpenter, the bathhouse, the round cottage, the mushroom house, the gazebo, the manor, the library and the guard barracks |
| Farmstead | East: farmhouse, stable, granary silo, chicken coop, greenhouse, the watermill with its stone bridge, a roadside shrine and the well |
| Millbrook | The barn and the windmill |
| Treeline camp | Tents, Lyra's campfire, Pell's wagon; the trees leave the camp clear |
| Out in the wood | Millbrook's old well, the Old Watchtower ruin, and the ruins and goblin camp in Grukk's clearing |

**How the streets are filled:** the builder's street packer lines up each frontage's models side by side with a 1.2 m gap. A model that doesn't fit is left out and logged.

**Names and colliders:**
- **Waystones** keep their object names (`Waystone`, `Waystone_Whisperwood`), so saved attunements still match.
- **Colliders:** buildings and props get mesh colliders; the plaza and roads are flat and walkable with none.

**Town ward:** it now covers x −75…75 and z −98…33, which takes in the new quarter and the farmstead.

## Regenerating

```bash
blender -b --factory-startup -P Tools/blender/town_buildings.py
```
(Add `-- --only guild_hall,well` to build just a few.) Then rebuild the scene with *Ascend ▸ Ring 0 ▸ Build Graybox Scene*, or in batch mode with `Ascend.EditorTools.Ring0SceneBuilder.BuildBatch`.

If no model has been exported, the builder falls back to the old gate blockout and primitive stand-ins, so the scene always builds.
