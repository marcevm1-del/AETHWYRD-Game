# Clans, Guardians & Fauna of Aetheria

_Draft v4 · full clan profiles · Canon.md wins on conflict._

## Clans by region

### Aldmere

#### The Hearthbound _(Human clan)_
Led by **Reeve Maud Ashby**.

Ordinary humans: scholars, knights, archers, climbers. The baseline people of the heartland, varied and stubborn, alongside the Veilborn who were summoned among them.

**Origin.** The Hearthbound are the ordinary people of Aldmere: farmers, crafters, clerks and soldiers whose families have lived on the same land for generations. Among them live the Veilborn, summoned from another world, and their descendants.

**Society.** Villages run by reeves, towns by guilds and the whole by the crown. Family names come from trades and places, and land passes to whoever works it.

**Beliefs.** The seven day-gods, the Church and the harvest. A guest at the door is fed first and questioned later.

**Relations.** Loyal to the crown, suspicious of nobles, generous to rookies, and weary of the barrow-dead.

**At war.** Crown Knights lead militia levies with spears and bows; every village has a bell and a muster-list.

**Trade.** Wheat, wool, timber, honey and the business of adventurers.

**Kinds**
- **Crown Knights** (Warrior) — Sworn to the Aldmerian throne.
- **Valley Farmers** (Commoner) — Families on the same land for generations.
- **Guild Scholars** (Scholar) — Clerks, mapmakers and archivists.
- **Veilborn Descendants** (Mixed) — Families with a summoned ancestor.

**Notable people**
- **Reeve Maud Ashby** (Leader of Ashby) — Keeps the gates shut by day and the families fed by night.
- **Sir Edric Thornfield** (Crown Knight) — Has sworn to stand at Greyhollow until Ashgrave falls.
- **Wren Hollis** (Veilborn descendant) — Has her great-grandmother's diary from another world and doesn't know what to do with it.

**Strengths.** Numbers, resilience, adaptability. **Weaknesses.** No magic of their own; easily divided.

**Quests**
- A family claims a Veilborn ancestor and has proof no one wants to see.
- Help Maud evacuate the outer farms before the next barrow-march.
- Read Wren's diary with her and decide who else should see it.

**In play.** The baseline human faction; Hearthbound reputation affects prices and quests across the heartland.

#### The Barrow-Host _(Undead Skeleton Clan)_
Led by **Ashgrave, the Oathless Baron**.

Skeletal knights and kings in rusted crowns. Ashgrave's host from the Greyhollow barrows.

**Origin.** The Barrow-Host are the dead of Greyhollow: the knights and soldiers who kept the oath their lord broke and died holding the line. When the barrows opened, they rose and followed the one man who could not follow them in life.

**Society.** An army of the dead under Ashgrave, organised as they were in life: knights, wights, foot-soldiers and the ancient crowned dead from older barrows.

**Beliefs.** The oath is all that is left. The living forgot them; they will make the living remember.

**Relations.** The siege-army of Aldmere; hated and feared by the Hearthbound; some still wear the colours of living families.

**At war.** Disciplined ranks of the dead, wights who drain the living and knights in rusted plate.

**Trade.** None.

**Kinds**
- **Oathless Knights** (Elite) — Ashgrave's skeletal knights.
- **Barrow-Wights** (Mage) — Undead who drain the living.
- **Bone Soldiers** (Infantry) — The marching dead of the barrows.
- **Crowned Dead** (Royal) — Ancient kings in rusted crowns.

**Notable people**
- **Ashgrave, the Oathless Baron** (Leader and Tyrant) — Leads the dead who kept the oath he broke.
- **Sir Edwyn the Faithful** (Oathless Knight) — Still wears the colours of a living family in Ashby.
- **The Barrow-Crown** (Crowned Dead) — An ancient king from a barrow older than Aldmere, who serves no one.

**Strengths.** Numbers, no fear, no fatigue. **Weaknesses.** Silver, holy magic, remembering their names.

**Quests**
- A skeleton knight in Ashgrave's host still wears the colours of a living family.
- Return Sir Edwyn to his family's memory and lay him to rest.
- Learn the Barrow-Crown's story before Ashgrave uses it.

**In play.** Aldmere's siege faction; naming the dead weakens them and is a non-violent counter.

### Whisperwood

#### The Sett-folk _(Badger Clan)_
Led by **Old Barnaby Brockwhistle, Solicitor**.

Stout badger-folk in knitted jumpers, waistcoats and reading spectacles; homes full of books, tea and armchairs. Whisperwood's oldest residents, living in great burrow-halls under the hornbeams. They are the region's librarians, solicitors and grumpy grandparents, and they have watched every rookie come and go.

**Origin.** The Sett-folk have lived under Whisperwood since before the waystations, and they hold the deeds to prove it.

**Society.** Extended families in burrow-halls, each with its library; disputes go to the burrow solicitors.

**Beliefs.** Everything worth keeping should be written down; everything worth saying should be said over tea.

**Relations.** Grumpy friends of Brindlewick, wary of the goblins, fond of rookies who return their books.

**At war.** Rarely fight, but the Honey Badgers are terrifying when they do.

**Trade.** Books, legal services, honey and maps of the underground.

**Kinds**
- **Burrow Solicitors** (Lawkeeper) — Keepers of deeds, wills and grudges.
- **Hedge Librarians** (Scholar) — Curators of the great underground book-halls.
- **Night Diggers** (Engineer) — Tunnellers who know every sinkhole in the wood.
- **Honey Badgers** (Warrior) — Small, furious and impossible to scare off.

**Notable people**
- **Old Barnaby Brockwhistle** (Solicitor) — Holds the deed to the waystations and a grudge against everyone.
- **Hazel Brockwhistle** (Hedge Librarian) — Barnaby's granddaughter; wants to catalogue the Labyrinth's stone.
- **Grub** (Honey Badger) — Small, furious and loyal to whoever feeds him.

**Strengths.** Knowledge, tunnels, stubbornness. **Weaknesses.** Slow to act; easily offended.

**Quests**
- An old Sett-folk solicitor holds the deed to the ruined waystations and wants to know why the goblins are digging under them.
- Find out why the goblins are digging under the waystations.
- Return thirty years of overdue library books.

**In play.** Lore library hub for Whisperwood; Sett-folk tunnels provide fast travel in the region.

#### The Six Clans of Hollowpine _(Goblin Clan)_
Led by **Grukk, Goblin Chief of Hollowpine**.

Goblins with goggles, flamethrowers, mechanical arms and cockerel mounts. Grukk's people are not all raiders. Hollowpine is a tinkering culture of scrap engines, fire-pots and cockerel cavalry, and its best inventors would rather build than burn.

**Origin.** Hollowpine's six clans came to Whisperwood as refugees from a war in the north, generations ago, and settled in the sinkhole caves. Each kept its own craft, mark and recipe.

**Society.** Six clans with six chiefs, now united under Grukk. Grukk shares food fairly and punishes theft, which is why they follow him.

**Beliefs.** Full bellies first; clever hands second; a goblin who invents something gets to name it after themselves.

**Relations.** Raiders of Brindlewick and Millbrook; secretly envious of the Moraveil goblins' truce; wary of the Sett-folk.

**At war.** Firepots, flamethrowers, cockerel cavalry and scrap engines; coordinated raids, never two days running.

**Trade.** Mushrooms, pelts, scrap machines and gems.

**Kinds**
- **Firepot Clan** (Tinkerer) — Makers of fire-pots and flamethrowers.
- **Cockerel Riders** (Cavalry) — Goblins on giant fighting roosters.
- **Scrap-Engine Clan** (Engineer) — Builders of goggled war-engines.
- **Mushroom Clan** (Farmer) — Growers in the sinkhole caves.
- **Wolfskin Clan** (Hunter) — Trappers who wear wolf pelts.
- **Gem-Hoard Clan** (Merchant) — Collectors and traders of shiny things.

**Notable people**
- **Grukk** (Goblin Chief of Hollowpine) — Clever, fair to his own and the region's Tyrant.
- **Fizzwick** (Scrap-Engine tinker) — Would build Millbrook a better mill if no one told Grukk.
- **Grukk the Younger** (Chief's son) — Watches the rookies; will remember who killed his father.

**Strengths.** Ingenuity, numbers, coordination. **Weaknesses.** Fragile; clan rivalries.

**Quests**
- A goblin tinker will build a better mill for Millbrook, if the village promises not to tell Grukk.
- Help Fizzwick build the mill in secret.
- Defeat Grukk in the F-to-E rank trial.

**In play.** Ring 1 Tyrant faction; Grukk the Younger becomes a redeemable villain later (Canon).

### Greymoor

#### The Bogkin _(Amphibian Clan)_
Led by **Choir-Mother Gullagrum**.

Frog- and toad-folk in reed cloaks and wide hats, from samurai-straight duelists to round, cheerful herbalists. Greymoor's frogfolk live in the deeper meres and trade with the stilt-villages by night. They croak in harmonies that carry under the fog, and the Hollow Abbot's silence is killing their songs.

**Origin.** Frogfolk lived in the clear lake that became Greymoor. When the abbey sank, they moved to the deeper meres and kept singing.

**Society.** Families of a few dozen around a single choir-mother, whose song holds the family together.

**Beliefs.** Song is life; silence is death. Every child gets a note of its own at birth.

**Relations.** Night-traders with the stilt-villages, friends with the Shellmoot, terrified of the Hollow Abbot.

**At war.** Reed duelists strike from the water; choirs stun enemies with harmonies.

**Trade.** Medicine, frog-silk and reed-craft.

**Kinds**
- **Reed Duelists** (Warrior) — Long-legged frog swordsmen who strike from the water.
- **Toad Herbalists** (Healer) — Round, cheerful brewers of swamp medicine.
- **Choir-Croakers** (Singer) — Harmony-singers whose songs carry under the fog.
- **Glass-Skins** (Scout) — Translucent tree-frogs nearly invisible in the reeds.

**Notable people**
- **Choir-Mother Gullagrum** (Leader) — Ancient and enormous; her voice is fading.
- **Pell Swiftreed** (Reed Duelist) — Young and reckless; wants to fight the Abbot alone.
- **Mossy Gip** (Toad Herbalist) — Brews the fen's best medicine and worst tea.

**Strengths.** Water, song, poison immunity. **Weaknesses.** Dry heat and silence.

**Quests**
- A Bogkin choir-master wants to sing against the Choir of Silence, and needs someone to stand with her while she does.
- Stand with Gullagrum as she sings against the Choir of Silence.
- Recover a choir-mother's note stolen by the silence.

**In play.** Bard-style song mechanics; Bogkin choirs can counter silence effects in Greymoor.

#### The Shellmoot _(Snail Clan)_
Led by **Grandfather Spiraleth**.

Snail-folk, slow and wise, in moss-grown shells. Greymoor's oldest folk, who remember the abbey on dry ground.

**Origin.** The Shellmoot are snail-folk who have lived in Greymoor since it was a lake of clear water. The oldest remember the abbey on dry ground and the causeway that led to it.

**Society.** Slow moots of elders that take years to decide anything. Slime-Trackers lay trails through the fen; Moss-Shells tend the reed beds; Shell-Sleepers rarely leave their shells at all.

**Beliefs.** Haste is the root of every mistake. What sinks slowly can be raised slowly.

**Relations.** Friends of the Bogkin, distant cousins of the Capfolk, wary of the Hollow Abbot, whom some of them remember as a young monk.

**At war.** They barely fight; their slime trails trap enemies and their shells turn blades.

**Trade.** Slime-medicine for burns, memory, and the safest paths through the fen.

**Kinds**
- **Spiral Elders** (Elder) — Ancient snails who remember the abbey.
- **Moss-Shells** (Commoner) — Snails covered in moss.
- **Slime-Trackers** (Scout) — Leave trails that guide travellers.
- **Shell-Sleepers** (Hermit) — Snails who rarely leave their shells.

**Notable people**
- **Grandfather Spiraleth** (Leader) — Remembers what the abbey was built to hold down.
- **Moss** (Moss-Shell) — Covered in moss, very proud of it, and the fastest snail in the fen.
- **Trail** (Slime-Tracker) — Leaves glowing trails that guide travellers through the fog.

**Strengths.** Memory, patience, armour. **Weaknesses.** Slowness; salt.

**Quests**
- A Shellmoot elder remembers what the abbey was built to hold down.
- Sit with Spiraleth long enough to hear the whole story of the abbey (it takes a week).
- Follow Trail's glowing path to the Peat Library.

**In play.** Lore-keepers for Greymoor; Shellmoot trails serve as guided paths through the fog.

### Sylvaran

#### The Leafsworn _(Elven Clan)_
Led by **Thessaly Grayroot, the Exiled**.

Court elves in gold-leaf armour, wood elves in green, a few pale exiles in chains. Court elves of Lothaniel, wood elves of the moonwell hamlets, and a third, quieter strand: exiles of the Grey Hollow, cast out a generation ago for studying the fungus beneath the World Tree.

**Origin.** The Leafsworn are the elves of Sylvaran in all their strands: court elves of Lothaniel, wood elves of the moonwells, and the exiles of the Grey Hollow.

**Society.** Court elves rank by house and age; wood elves by craft; exiles by nothing at all.

**Beliefs.** The World Tree first; one's twin tree second; everything else a distant third.

**Relations.** Court elves look down on everyone; wood elves are friendly; exiles are bitter.

**At war.** Longbows, living-wood armour, moonwell magic.

**Trade.** Bows, herbs, living wood, moonwell water.

**Kinds**
- **Court Elves** (Noble) — Gold-leaf armoured aristocrats of Lothaniel.
- **Moonwell Folk** (Commoner) — Wood elves of the forest floor.
- **Grey Hollow Exiles** (Outcast) — Elves cast out for studying the fungus.
- **Twin-Wardens** (Ranger) — Protect the twin groves.

**Notable people**
- **Thessaly Grayroot** (The Exiled) — Knows how Mycelia thinks; the court would rather not ask.
- **Lord Aerendil Goldleaf** (Court elf) — Sees the blight as a political opportunity.
- **Fern Quickbow** (Moonwell ranger) — Her twin tree is infected.

**Strengths.** Archery, nature magic, long memory. **Weaknesses.** Pride; the blight.

**Quests**
- An exiled elf knows how Mycelia thinks, and the court would rather not ask her.
- Bring Thessaly back to court to advise against Mycelia.
- Save Fern's twin tree.

**In play.** Main Sylvaran faction with three sub-reputations.

#### The Rootwalkers _(Forest Clan)_
Led by **Eldest Barkhallow**.

Treefolk and bark-armoured antlered wardens, with moss beards and glowing sap. Living trees who woke to guard the twin groves. Mycelia's blight is to them what plague is to people.

**Origin.** The Rootwalkers are trees of Sylvaran that woke when the elves first planted the twin groves, to guard the children's trees. Some are older than the court itself.

**Society.** Each grove has an Eldest who speaks for it; saplings wake slowly over decades and are taught by whichever Rootwalker is nearest.

**Beliefs.** Roots remember everything that passes over them. A Rootwalker that cannot heal should be cut down before it spreads.

**Relations.** Guardians of the elves' twin trees; the court treats them as furniture, the wood elves as elders; Mycelia is their plague.

**At war.** Crushing branches, bark armour and roots that grip from below; slow to start, impossible to stop.

**Trade.** None; they ask only for water and to be left to grow.

**Kinds**
- **Elder Oaks** (Elder) — Ancient, slow and wise.
- **Antlered Wardens** (Guardian) — Bark-armoured archers of the groves.
- **Saplings** (Young) — Newly woken trees, curious and clumsy.
- **Blighted** (Sick) — Rootwalkers infected by Mycelia.

**Notable people**
- **Eldest Barkhallow** (Leader) — Ancient, slow and asleep for most of the year.
- **Twig** (Sapling) — Newly woken and curious about everything, especially feet.
- **Ashbark** (Blighted) — Infected by Mycelia and asking to be cut down before it spreads.

**Strengths.** Strength, endurance, regrowth. **Weaknesses.** Fire, axes and the blight.

**Quests**
- A Rootwalker is infected and asking to be cut down before it spreads.
- Grant Ashbark's request, or find a cure before it is too late.
- Wake Eldest Barkhallow to march on the Grey Hollow.

**In play.** Rootwalker allies form living barricades in Sylvaran; their condition tracks the spread of the blight.

#### The Spore-Touched _(Infected Clan)_
Led by **The One Who Was Aelindra**.

People and beasts warped by fungus: swollen limbs, bark skin, glowing growths. Those Mycelia's spores have claimed. They still remember, faintly, who they were.

**Origin.** The Spore-Touched are elves, wood-folk and beasts claimed by Mycelia's blight. The first were moonwell hamlet families who drank from a grey well, and each wave since has been larger.

**Society.** There is no society, only stages. Early cases are hidden by their families; later ones drift toward the forest floor and tend the fungus there in silent groups.

**Beliefs.** Early stages remember their own faiths and fear the end; late stages speak of the Spore Lord as a gardener and themselves as its garden.

**Relations.** Pitied by the wood elves, feared by the court, studied by the Grey Hollow exiles.

**At war.** Sporecallers release clouds that slow and confuse; the Hollowed fight without fear or pain.

**Trade.** None; families trade everything they have for medicine that does not work.

**Kinds**
- **Budding** (Early) — A few spots of fungus; still themselves.
- **Capped** (Middle) — Fungal growths on head and shoulders; slow and dreamy.
- **Sporecallers** (Late) — Release spore clouds to spread the blight.
- **Hollowed** (Lost) — Nothing left but fungus wearing a shape.

**Notable people**
- **The One Who Was Aelindra** (Spore-Touched elder) — A moonwell priestess whose letters home are written in fungus.
- **Brin Mossheart** (Budding) — A young wood elf hiding the spots on his arm.
- **Mother Cael** (Grey Hollow exile) — Treats the Spore-Touched in secret and keeps careful notes.

**Strengths.** No fear, no pain, spore clouds. **Weaknesses.** Fire, sunlight and moonwell water slow the change.

**Quests**
- A Spore-Touched elf wrote a letter home in fungus, and the court has asked someone to read it.
- Read Aelindra's fungus letter and find what she is trying to say.
- Smuggle moonwell water to Brin before the court discovers him.

**In play.** Infection stages map to enemy tiers in Sylvaran; curing named Spore-Touched unlocks the Grey Hollow questline.

### Mossdeep

#### The Carapace _(Insecta Clan)_
Led by **Queen-Mantis Zirrikh**.

Mantis-, wasp- and beetle-folk in chitin armour with gauze wings. Insect-folk who live in Mossdeep's ferns and are uneasy allies of the Thimblefolk; Mother Webcrawler hunts them too.

**Origin.** The Carapace are insect-folk who live in Mossdeep's ferns: mantises, wasps and beetles grown large on the valley's mana-rich soil. They were here before the Thimblefolk and consider the Thimblefolk recent arrivals.

**Society.** A hive-court under a Queen-Mantis, with duelists, riders and guards in castes. Each caste has its own hall in the hollow fern-stems.

**Beliefs.** The hive is one body; a body defends itself. Aphid-keeping is slavery, and they will not forgive it.

**Relations.** Uneasy neighbours of the Thimblefolk, prey of Mother Webcrawler, allies of the Capfolk.

**At war.** Mantis Duelists strike from ambush; Wasp Riders attack from the canopy; Beetle Guards hold the halls.

**Trade.** Silk, wax and the services of the wasp riders.

**Kinds**
- **Mantis Duelists** (Warrior) — Blade-armed hunters of the ferns.
- **Wasp Riders** (Cavalry) — Ride giant wasps through the canopy.
- **Beetle Guards** (Heavy) — Armoured defenders of the hive-halls.
- **Queen's Gauze** (Noble) — Winged attendants of Queen-Mantis Zirrikh.

**Notable people**
- **Queen-Mantis Zirrikh** (Leader) — Will help fight Mother Webcrawler if the Thimblefolk free the aphids.
- **Kzzt** (Wasp Rider) — The fastest flier in the valley, and fiercely proud of it.
- **Old Shellback** (Beetle Guard) — Has guarded the queen's hall for forty years.

**Strengths.** Armour, speed, numbers. **Weaknesses.** Cold, smoke and spider venom.

**Quests**
- The Carapace will help fight Mother Webcrawler if the Thimblefolk stop keeping aphids.
- Broker a deal between Zirrikh and the Thimblefolk council over the aphids.
- Ride with Kzzt to scout the Silk Canopy.

**In play.** Alliance choice in Mossdeep: siding with the Carapace changes the Webcrawler fight.

#### The Capfolk _(Mushroom clan)_
Led by **Old Puffball Morel**.

Walking mushrooms of every size, from tiny sprouts to towering bruisers. Mushroom-folk who live alongside the Thimblefolk and the beetles; Mycelia is their nightmare cousin.

**Origin.** The Capfolk are walking mushrooms who sprouted in Mossdeep's warm, mana-rich soil. They say they grew from the same root-web as Mycelia, long ago, before the Spore Lord went wrong.

**Society.** Rings of mushrooms that grow from one spore-mother. The oldest Puffballs speak for each ring, and new Sprouts appear after every warm rain.

**Beliefs.** Everything rots and everything returns; the soil remembers. To spread without asking is the worst crime a fungus can commit.

**Relations.** Gentle neighbours of the Thimblefolk, allies of the Carapace, and horrified by Mycelia, whom they call the Cousin.

**At war.** Toadstool Guards fight with staves; Morel Mystics release sleeping-spores that never harm.

**Trade.** Medicinal mushrooms, soil-reading and composting for the Thimblefolk gardens.

**Kinds**
- **Puffballs** (Elder) — Round, slow and wise.
- **Toadstool Guards** (Warrior) — Red-capped fighters.
- **Morel Mystics** (Mage) — Mushroom-folk who speak with the soil.
- **Sprouts** (Young) — Tiny new mushrooms, very curious.

**Notable people**
- **Old Puffball Morel** (Leader) — Says the Spore Lord was once one of them and knows its true name.
- **Inky Cap** (Morel Mystic) — Can hear the root-web, and the Cousin's voice in it.
- **Sprig** (Sprout) — Newly grown and asking everyone what it is for.

**Strengths.** Spore magic, regrowth, soil-sense. **Weaknesses.** Dry heat, fire and salt.

**Quests**
- A Capfolk elder says the Spore Lord was once one of them.
- Learn Mycelia's true name from Old Puffball and carry it to Sylvaran.
- Help Inky Cap listen to the root-web without being taken by it.

**In play.** The Capfolk link Mossdeep to the Sylvaran blight questline; spore-crafting recipes.

### Lirian

#### The Lagoon Folk _(Merfolk Clan)_
Led by **Queen Corallin of the Water-Gates**.

Merfolk with fins, tridents and coral crowns; some with octopus hair. The sea-elves and merfolk of Lirian's outer lagoon, trading through water-gates.

**Origin.** The Lagoon Folk are the merfolk and sea-elves of Lirian's outer lagoon, who were living in the old city before it drowned. When the sea rose, they stayed; the humans built the new city on top.

**Society.** A water-court under a queen, with noble houses, trident guards and coral-weaver guilds. They trade with the city through carved water-gates.

**Beliefs.** The sea keeps what it is given; the old city is a grave, and graves are not for sirens.

**Relations.** Trading partners of the doge, rivals of the Canal Otters for diving rights, enemies of the Drowned Choir.

**At war.** Trident Guard fight in currents; sea-elf mages call the tide.

**Trade.** Pearls, coral jewellery, sea-silk and secrets of the drowned city.

**Kinds**
- **Water-Gate Nobles** (Ruler) — Merfolk aristocracy of the outer lagoon.
- **Sea-Elves** (Mage) — Elves who live beneath the water.
- **Trident Guard** (Warrior) — Defenders of the lagoon.
- **Coral-Weavers** (Artisan) — Grow coral into jewellery and buildings.

**Notable people**
- **Queen Corallin of the Water-Gates** (Leader) — Knows where the Drowned Choir sleeps by day.
- **Lord Nerith Saltblood** (Sea-elf noble) — Thinks the city should be left to the sirens.
- **Pearl** (Coral-Weaver) — A young merfolk artist who sneaks into the canals to sell her work.

**Strengths.** Water combat, tide-magic, knowledge of the drowned city. **Weaknesses.** Dry land; city politics.

**Quests**
- The Lagoon Folk know where the Drowned Choir sleeps by day.
- Lead a raid on the Drowned Belltowers by day with Corallin's guard.
- Expose Nerith's dealings with the sirens.

**In play.** Underwater sections in Lirian; water-gate fast travel.

#### The Canal Otters _(Otter Clan)_
Led by **Gondolier Pip Slipwater**.

Otter-folk gondoliers, archers and gentlemen. Lirian's gondoliers and divers, cheerful and nosy.

**Origin.** The Canal Otters came to Lirian with the first fishing boats and never left. When the city grew, they became its gondoliers, divers and gossips, and today no deal in Lirian is secret from them for long.

**Society.** Family rafts led by the eldest gondolier; the divers' guild and the gondoliers' guild argue about everything except the city's safety.

**Beliefs.** Water is home and the canals are family. Every coin in the canal is a prayer, and divers return a tenth of what they find to the water.

**Relations.** Friends of everyone, rivals of the Lagoon Folk for the best diving spots, quietly paid informants of the doge.

**At war.** Otter Archers shoot from gondolas; divers drag attackers under.

**Trade.** Gondola fares, diving, gossip and the occasional smuggled letter.

**Kinds**
- **Gondoliers** (Worker) — Steer the gondolas of Lirian.
- **Canal Divers** (Diver) — Dive for the coins thrown into the canals.
- **Otter Archers** (Warrior) — Defend the canals from the Drowned Choir.
- **Gentleman Otters** (Merchant) — Well-dressed otter traders.

**Notable people**
- **Gondolier Pip Slipwater** (Leader) — Has heard the Drowned Choir and wants to follow it, with someone holding the rope.
- **Madame Whiskerton** (Gentleman Otter) — Runs the finest fish restaurant in Lirian and knows every secret spoken at its tables.
- **Splash** (Canal Diver) — The youngest diver in the guild and the best at finding lost rings.

**Strengths.** Swimming, agility, information. **Weaknesses.** Curiosity; can't keep a secret.

**Quests**
- A canal otter has heard the Drowned Choir and wants to follow it, with someone holding the rope.
- Hold Pip's rope while he follows the Drowned Choir's song.
- Use Madame Whiskerton's gossip to find out who in the doge's council deals with the sirens.

**In play.** Gondola fast travel in Lirian; otter informants sell rumours.

### Fanghold

#### The Hoofbound _(Centaur Clan)_
Led by **Khan-Mare Saruhan Windmane**.

Centaurs from horse-backed steppe archers to antlered forest variants, painted in clan colours. The horse-clans of the steppe, older than the yurt camps, who keep the Wolf Stones and witness the water-peace. Kade's capture of the Deep Well is an insult they mean to answer.

**Origin.** The Hoofbound are the oldest people of the steppe; the clans say they were raising the Wolf Stones when wolves still walked on four legs.

**Society.** Herds of related families led by a Khan-Mare; elders keep the stones.

**Beliefs.** The wind carries the ancestors; the water-peace is holy.

**Relations.** Witnesses of the water-peace; allies of the Greyhowl clans; at war with Kade.

**At war.** Mounted archers firing at full gallop; Thunder-Hooves carry the camps.

**Trade.** Horses, felt and herbs.

**Kinds**
- **Steppe Archers** (Cavalry) — Horse-bodied archers who shoot at full gallop.
- **Antlered Wardens** (Druid) — Deer-bodied centaurs from the forest edges.
- **Stone-Keepers** (Elder) — Witnesses of the Wolf Stones and the water-peace.

### Nox Dominion
