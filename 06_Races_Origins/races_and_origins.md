# 06 — Races, Cultures & Origins (v0.2)

## 1. Playable Races
### HUMAN — *"Short lives, long ambitions."*
- **Bonus:** +1 all stats, +10% XP, +1 extra skill slot at Lv 30.
- **Subcultures:** *Aldmerian* (knights and farmers), *Sunscar Nomads* (desert riders, +heat resist), *Isles-folk* (Eastern; katana, tea, spirit shrines), *Northfolk* (+cold resist, axes, sagas).
- **Racial skill:** *Adaptable*: once per day, copy a racial passive of an ally for 1 hour.

### ELF (Sylvan) — *"We remember when your mountains were young."*
- **Bonus:** +INT +DEX, Nature affinity, see ley lines.
- **Subraces:** *High Elf* (court mages, arrogant), *Wood Elf* (rangers, humble), *Moon Elf* (night magic, Maelis devotees).
- **Racial skill:** *Elven Grace*: perfect-dodge window +30%.
- **Culture:** live 1,000+ years; humans are "the mayflies"; strict politics around the World Tree.

### DARK ELF — *"Exiled for the crime of asking questions."*
- **Bonus:** +AGI +INT, Dark affinity, night vision.
- **Racial skill:** *Shade Step*: short teleport in darkness.
- **Culture:** exiled after the Long Night; underground city *Umbravel*; matriarchal houses.

### DWARF — *"Steel remembers every blow."*
- **Bonus:** +VIT +STR, +2 crafting quality, poison resist.
- **Subraces:** *Deep Dwarf* (miners), *Forge Dwarf* (smiths), *Hill Dwarf* (brewers/traders).
- **Racial skill:** *Stoneblood*: cannot be knocked down below 50% HP.
- **Culture:** clan honor, ale, ancestor halls, beards and braids (all genders, by clan tradition).

### BEASTKIN — *"Our blood sings with the old wild."*
| Subtype | Bonus | Racial skill |
|---|---|---|
| Wolf-kin | +STR +AGI, smell | *Pack Howl* (party buff) |
| Cat-kin | +AGI +LUK, night sight | *Nine Lives* (one free revive per day) |
| Fox-kin | +INT +CHA, illusion | *Fox Fire*; tails grow with level (1 → 9) |
| Rabbit-kin | +AGI, hearing | *Moon Leap* (super jump) |
| Bear-kin | +VIT +STR | *Hibernate* (massive regen) |
| Bird-kin (Harpy-blood) | +AGI +DEX | *Glide* from level 1 |
| Lizard-kin | +VIT, water breathing | *Shed Skin* (cleanse debuffs) |
- **Culture:** Beastkin Federation; arena honor; some regions still enslave beastkin, which drives part of the story.

### DRAGONKIN — *"An echo of the Age of Dragons."*
- **Bonus:** +STR +VIT, breath attack (element by bloodline), scales = natural armor.
- **Bloodlines:** Crimson (fire), Azure (lightning), Frost, Jade (earth/poison), Umbral (void).
- **Racial skill:** *Draconic Awakening* at C-Rank (wings, flight bursts later).
- **Downside:** −20% XP gain, dragons judge you harshly.

### DEMONKIN — *"Born of the Abyss, raised by the world that hates us."*
- **Bonus:** +INT +STR, Dark/Fire affinity, +night power.
- **Racial skill:** *Infernal Pact*: sacrifice HP for MP.
- **Downside:** Church-controlled towns are Hostile by default; disguises needed.

### HALFLING — *"Small feet, large appetites, larger luck."*
- **Bonus:** +LUK +DEX, stealth, cooking bonus. **Skill:** *Second Breakfast* (food buffs last 2×).

## 3. Character Creator
- Anime-styled 3D creator: face presets + sliders, hair (60+ styles, physics), eyes (heterochromia, glowing), marks/tattoos, horns/ears/tails by race, height/body sliders, 20 voice sets (VA lines), personality tone for chosen dialogue (*Polite / Cocky / Cold / Cheerful*).
- **Aura color** (how your skills glow).
- **Old-world memory** (pick 3: student, cook, gamer, nurse, engineer, athlete, otaku, office worker…). This gives small perks and unique dialogue: an engineer learns Otherworld Knowledge faster, a cook gets a cooking bonus, a gamer can "read" the System better.
