# Open-world RPG discovery and world systems

## Discovery, guidance and fatigue

### Takeaway
Strong discovery systems let players choose between authored guidance and self-directed curiosity, while ensuring that exploration earns legible value. For Ascend, the most transferable pattern is a small number of strong regional prompts and layered optional discoveries, with liberation and ecology providing the world’s persistent narrative payoff.

### Cited Findings
- Nintendo’s developers said Tears of the Kingdom added sky and cave layers to familiar Hyrule; a new valuable feature in a familiar cliff could make players wonder whether other cliffs held caves too. They explicitly described discoveries as changing how the known landscape is perceived. Interview dated May 9, 2023: [Nintendo, Ask the Developer Vol. 9, Part 3](https://www.nintendo.com/en-ca/whatsnew/ask-the-developer-vol-9-the-legend-of-zelda-tears-of-the-kingdom-part-3/).
- Nintendo also reported that their experimental sky islands became visually cluttered when too many were placed; the team then adjusted placement and appearance. That is direct evidence that density is a visual-composition problem as well as a content-count problem. Same interview, above.
- Elden Ring’s official player guide explains that Sites of Grace give directional guidance toward important areas, while discovered map fragments reveal guidance on the map. The same guide presents combat avoidance and returning later as valid responses to a difficult area. [Bandai Namco, Elden Ring Gameplay Overview](https://www.bandainamcoent.com/es_mx/news/elden-ring-introduction-part-3-gameplay-overview) (published 2022; guide page, current retrieval date 2026-10-23).
- Miyazaki described Elden Ring’s design goals as a sense of accomplishment plus a sense of adventure: players engage with a huge world of unknowns and threats at their liberty. He also said the larger scale meant greater responsibility delegated to staff with experience. [Xbox Wire, May 27, 2022](https://news.xbox.com/en-us/2022/05/27/from-softwares-hidetaka-miyazaki-on-the-secrets-of-elden-rings-development/).
- Genshin Impact’s launch-era official presentation described freeform exploration with fast travel, scattered encampments/dungeons/treasures, and seven city-states distinguished through aesthetics, architecture and landscape. It positioned both active discovery and simply enjoying scenery as valid. [PlayStation Blog, August 1, 2019](https://blog.playstation.com/archive/2019/08/01/get-your-first-look-at-genshin-impact-a-new-open-world-adventure-coming-to-playstation-next-year).

### Inferences
- Avoid a uniform checklist by mixing three information states: visible/obvious landmarks, rumors or environmental clues that suggest a search area, and truly unmarked surprises. Let map tools record what the player notices rather than auto-populating every authored point.
- Ascend’s weekly boards and liberation systems can serve as player-facing guidance: during siege, a board can identify a concrete regional pressure; after liberation, it can shift toward hunts and recovery. This turns the already-canonical state change into navigation and pacing rather than another completion ledger.
- Use “one discovery reframes several places” as a low-cost content multiplier. A new sign, migratory trace, magical phenomenon or route clue can teach the player to notice similar evidence elsewhere without cloning the actual reward or location.
- Build explicit quiet intervals into routes. Warm inns, safe overlooks, ordinary NPC routines and scenic passages should separate danger clusters, so the world’s threat feels punctuated and meaningful rather than constant.

### Gaps
- Publicly available official materials do not provide comparable fatigue telemetry, completion rates or a validated ideal number of discoveries per hour for these games.
- The cited Genshin source is a launch-era overview, not a current developer postmortem on exploration fatigue. It supports the stated launch framing only.

## Traversal, landmarks, verticality and pacing

### Takeaway
Traversal tools are most valuable when they alter route choice and perception, not merely travel speed. Landmarks can orient the player while hidden approaches, occlusion and height changes preserve the pleasure of discovery; Ascend should tune its climb/glide kit as a network of choices with clear limits.

### Cited Findings
- Tears of the Kingdom’s developers said they designed around seamless travel from surface to sky and deliberately pursued more three-dimensional gameplay. They noted that cliffs previously used mainly for climbing gained new meaning when caves were added to cliff faces. [Nintendo, May 9, 2023](https://www.nintendo.com/en-ca/whatsnew/ask-the-developer-vol-9-the-legend-of-zelda-tears-of-the-kingdom-part-3/).
- In that same interview, Nintendo described seamless environments, including interiors with visible views back to the outside, as a long-running production goal. Developers also discussed the difficulty of making sky/ground transitions work consistently for audio, illustrating that seamlessness has cross-discipline costs. [Nintendo, May 9, 2023](https://www.nintendo.com/en-ca/whatsnew/ask-the-developer-vol-9-the-legend-of-zelda-tears-of-the-kingdom-part-3/).
- The Dragon’s Dogma 2 director said the team placed intriguing destination landmarks within the player’s visible field to reduce uncertainty about where to go, while shaping the route to that destination with blind/occluded sections to strengthen the feeling of adventure. In the same interview, he said quality, quantity and frequency of experiences mattered more than map area, and that they tried to minimize stretches players would find boring. [PlayStation Blog Japan, March 19, 2024](https://blog.ja.playstation.com/2024/03/19/20240319-dragonsdogma2/). The article is in Japanese; the paraphrase here is from the published interview.
- Miyazaki said Elden Ring’s world was intended to support player freedom and adventure, with unknowns and threats. PlayStation Blog’s interview introduction also describes the summonable mount as enabling faster, more vertical traversal. [Xbox Wire, May 27, 2022](https://news.xbox.com/en-us/2022/05/27/from-softwares-hidetaka-miyazaki-on-the-secrets-of-elden-rings-development/); [PlayStation Blog, January 28, 2022](https://blog.playstation.com/?p=359935).
- Capcom’s official release information describes Dragon’s Dogma 2 as a single-player adventure whose AI-controlled Pawns create the taste of group adventuring. [Capcom, November 29, 2023](https://www.capcom.co.jp/ir/english/news/html/e231129.html). This is relevant as a production/content pattern for solo play, not a recommendation to add online play.

### Inferences
- Use a traversal “verb budget”: each ability should have at least two distinct route consequences (e.g., climb to a ledge or reach a cave mouth; glide across a ravine or descend safely into a basin). Avoid adding tools that only remove walking time.
- For climbing, tune stamina/handholds/rest points around readable commitment: players should see a plausible route, make a few meaningful choices, and be able to identify a safe fallback. For gliding, use wind, height and landing zones to create decisions without permitting sustained flight or flying mounts (Canon forbids flying mounts; flight/teleport are abilities only).
- Establish a regional landmark hierarchy: one distant anchor visible from multiple approaches, a few mid-range sub-landmarks that reveal route branches, and close-range tells for local secrets. Deliberate occlusion can hide the next stretch, but the destination or a compelling clue should remain available often enough to prevent aimless wandering.
- Make travel encounter frequency stateful and authored: a dangerous crossing can contain a threat, a social encounter, a resource/trace, or a calm stretch. Reuse a small encounter grammar across regions, changing the ecology and stakes rather than commissioning a unique set piece for every path.

### Gaps
- No official quantitative guidance was found for ideal landmark spacing, climbing duration, glide distance or encounter frequency. These require graybox play observation; the figures should be treated as tuning questions, not borrowed constants.
- The Dragon’s Dogma 2 cited interview is an official Sony platform interview with Capcom developers, not a Capcom-hosted design postmortem. Its direct design claims are attributed to the director, but the page’s translation and platform framing should be kept in mind.

## Density, persistent state and two-person production

### Takeaway
A two-person team should scope world richness through reusable rules, regional identity and authored exceptions, rather than a promise that every meter contains bespoke content. The best opportunity for Ascend is to make its canonical liberation state visibly and durably change a region, while keeping settlement safety and the hand-authored cast intact.

### Cited Findings
- Dragon’s Dogma 2’s director said the map was shaped around the quality, quantity and frequency of experiences, rather than size alone; the team sought to avoid stretches players would consider boring. The interview also describes landmarks in view and occluded route segments. [PlayStation Blog Japan, March 19, 2024](https://blog.ja.playstation.com/2024/03/19/20240319-dragonsdogma2/).
- In the same interview, the director linked seamless loading to monsters pursuing the player and large enemies naturally entering towns. This describes a systemic consequence of seamless simulation, not a guarantee that such simulation is cheap or appropriate for smaller teams. [PlayStation Blog Japan, March 19, 2024](https://blog.ja.playstation.com/2024/03/19/20240319-dragonsdogma2/).
- Miyazaki said Elden Ring’s much larger production delegated more responsibility to experienced staff; the team’s capacity to entrust work to them was among the reasons they chose that project at that time. [Xbox Wire, May 27, 2022](https://news.xbox.com/en-us/2022/05/27/from-softwares-hidetaka-miyazaki-on-the-secrets-of-elden-rings-development/).
- Obsidian’s official Xbox coverage describes Avowed’s Living Lands as having distinct regions, factions, characters and story choices; its story begins in the port city of Paradis. This supports region identity and story embedded in a particular place, though it is promotional coverage rather than a detailed world-design postmortem. [Xbox Wire, June 9, 2024](https://news.xbox.com/en-us/2024/06/09/avowed-story-companion-xbox-games-showcase-2024/).
- An official Dragon’s Dogma 2 interview describes AI companions operating inside a single-player game, while Capcom’s press release identifies them as AI-controlled Pawns. [PlayStation Blog Japan, March 19, 2024](https://blog.ja.playstation.com/2024/03/19/20240319-dragonsdogma2/); [Capcom, November 29, 2023](https://www.capcom.co.jp/ir/english/news/html/e231129.html).

### Inferences
- Ascend already has unusually strong scope anchors in Canon: 40 surface regions, permanent liberation, siege schedules that do not destroy settlements, and post-story recovery. Protect those rules by defining a “region kit” that can be authored once and varied: landmark silhouette, traversal obstacle, local ecology, settlement routine, one optional story thread, one signature encounter, and a small set of state changes.
- A persistent liberation delta can be compact but visible: safer roads, changed patrol/merchant presence, repaired but never destroyed defenses, new ambient conversation, altered encounter tables, and a changed board. Store these as region-state variants and reuse existing props/dialogue/encounter templates. Do not make the player ruler, guild owner or settlement destroyer; those conflict with Canon.
- Treat “living world” as consequences the player can perceive, not a simulation of every creature at all times. Use scheduled/high-level ecology states and local encounter activation, with authored vignettes to make the rules emotionally legible. Named monsters may reflect regional ecology/story flags but must not develop personal memory from defeating the player (Canon §4d).
- For a two-person team, prototype one representative region slice before scaling the 40-region promise: graybox one route network with a landmark, one climb/glide choice, one hidden branch, one safe social beat, one dangerous beat, and a liberation state transition. Then reuse the validated authoring template and art/encounter vocabulary.
- Use asset reuse with strong composition and regional variants (palette, dressing, signage, sound, weather, NPC schedule, encounter mix). Hand-author the few moments players use to infer the world’s rules; systematize the connective tissue. Avoid assuming that large studios’ continuous simulation, content volume or bespoke landmark density is achievable for two people.

### Gaps
- The Avowed source located in official Xbox material is a story/setting reveal rather than an in-depth developer design talk. Public official sources found in this pass do not substantiate particular Avowed discovery or fatigue systems.
- No current primary HoYoverse source located here provided an in-depth production account of Genshin’s exploration density or fatigue. The official launch-era PlayStation Blog material gives only the product’s stated design framing.
- None of the developer sources provides a directly comparable estimate of labor per region or content density. The two-person recommendations above are inferences based on the project’s team size and the documented scope/production observations, not developer-provided prescriptions.
