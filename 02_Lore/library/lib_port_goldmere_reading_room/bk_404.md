# Songs Heard in Port Goldmere

*Kwame Adeyemi of Goldmere Coast*

**Shelf:** Ballad sheet  
**Region:** Goldmere Coast  
**In-world date:** 944 SC  
**Status:** Draft. Player-facing. Not Canon until reviewed. Written as what a person in Aethwyrd could know.
## Sung at the table

Collected at pirate stilt-towns. Change the name of the bridge if your bridge is the one that is out. A song that cannot survive a correction is a poster.
