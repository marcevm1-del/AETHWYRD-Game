## How this copy is used

I set this down at Port Goldmere because a child asked a straight question and the adults answered with a proverb. The guild chalk stub on the table is not a relic. It is how this copy stayed honest.

Yelena Okafor of Goldmere Coast keeps this book in the kitchen at Port Goldmere, where the houses are beacon posts, salt, and tide tables. The everyday pot already knows fried fish, citrus, and tavern gravy. What follows is the version you can cook when a harbour tax and a story about a retired cook has made the market short, and when you still mean to sit down.

A note on faith, because someone always asks: Vex before a sailing, Solenne on the customs house. None of these dishes require a blessing to be edible. A blessing is for the table, after the salt is in.

Prices move. A copper is still the coin of a small bite, and a silver is still a bed. If a stall quotes you a mithril piece for supper, you are in the wrong story or the wrong street.

## Market Pepper Stew

*As cooked when Port Goldmere is being ordinary.*

**You need**

- beans
- a hot pepper
- fried bread
- oil

Simmer the beans until they slump. Fry the pepper in oil and stir it through. The bread is for people who pretended they could take the heat.
