*Amina Pell of Greymoor*

**Shelf:** Ballad sheet  
**Region:** Greymoor  
**In-world date:** 939 SC  
**Status:** Draft. Player-facing. Not Canon until reviewed. Written as what a person in Aethwyrd could know.

Not for glory, not for blame.
