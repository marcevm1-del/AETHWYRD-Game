## The short version on the board

I set this down at Greymoor stilt-village because the shelf copy had been edited by someone with a tidy mind and a bad memory. The folded tide-table on the table is not a relic. It is how this copy stayed honest.

At Greymoor stilt-village the work of the day sits inside reed thatch on stilts above peat. People eat eel, bog-berries, and black bread and they talk about lights that are not lanterns out on the peat the way coastal towns talk about weather: often, and with corrections.
