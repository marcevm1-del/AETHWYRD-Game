# The House Book of the Moot Hall
*Elder Ruth Hallam, who keeps the house book of the largest hall in the stilt-village*

**Shelf:** Household book  
**Region:** Greymoor  
**In-world date:** 1007 SC  
**Status:** Draft. Player-facing. Not Canon until reviewed. Written as what a person in Aethwyrd could know.

The moot hall is the one house in the village that is never empty. I write its rules down because I have been told too often that "everybody knows," and nobody does.

## Who sits where

The oldest elder sits nearest the fire. The youngest sits nearest the door, because they are the one who will have to go out in the night and fetch what is wanted. The rest sit in between, in an order that has no name and that nobody has ever broken.

## The lamp

A jar of lantern-moths burns in the window from dusk to dawn. We do not put it out. A house with no lamp is a house the dead cannot find. The moths are farmed, fed on a sugar-water and let out into the reeds once a year, and they do not mind.

## The bell

At dusk, the hand-bell is rung three times. It does not matter whether anyone is listening. It matters that somebody has rung it.

## Guests

A guest is given a bowl, a place at the fire and a quiet hour. They are not asked their business until they have eaten. If they are a traveller, we ask where they are going. If they are a Guild member, we ask what they are hunting. We do not ask what they are carrying.

## What we do not do

- We do not cut peat on Morrowsday.
- We do not whistle on the water.
- We do not argue with a witch.
- We do not lie to the moot. We have been known to say less than the whole truth, but we have never said less than we know.

## What the house is for

It is for people who disagree and live together anyway. That is not as hard as it sounds. You need a pot, a fire and a rule that the fire is not to be broken over a quarrel. I have kept the house for twenty-three years and the fire has not gone out once.
