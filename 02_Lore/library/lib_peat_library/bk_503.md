# Neighbors Near Greymoor stilt-village

*Amira Flint of Greymoor*

**Shelf:** Field guide  
**Region:** Greymoor  
**In-world date:** 1010 SC  
**Status:** Draft. Player-facing. Not Canon until reviewed. Written as what a person in Aethwyrd could know.
## Before the entries

I set this down at the Walking Hut market because the Guild card was right about the danger and wrong about the cause. The blue lamp-glass on the table is not a relic. It is how this copy stayed honest.

This guide is for walking country: reed thatch on stilts above peat. You will smell eel, bog-berries, and black bread in the settlements and you will be told about lights that are not lanterns out on the peat. The entries below are neighbors, not trophies.

Shrines out here lean toward this habit: Morrowyn's blue lamps along the causeway. A prayer is not a ward you can spend like a potion. Learn the creature anyway.

If a poster and a hunter disagree, copy both and date them. The halls of the Labyrinth reset. Ordinary hills do not, and a path can be wrong twice.

## Naga Warrior

Around the Walking Hut market, people file the Naga Warrior near guild rank C+. A rank is a licence hint. It is not a measurement of your courage, and it goes stale if the ground has changed.

The ground is the hunter. Look at the low places before the skyline.

Folk talk says its nature runs toward Water, and that Lightning unsettles it. Treat that as a rumor you can test from farther away than its reach. If the test fails, you have learned something, provided you can still leave.

What the meat-stall will not put on a sign: do not take a young one to prove a point, do not block the line it uses to flee, and do not trust a core that is still warm as if it were a lamp. Cores light cities. They also belong to a creature that was here first. One of them is remembering a person. The other is remembering a rule.
