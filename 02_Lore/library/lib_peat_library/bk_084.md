# Soup for the Moot
*Hanne Reedwright, who has fed the elders of three villages and outlived two of their arguments*

**Shelf:** Cookery  
**Region:** Greymoor  
**In-world date:** 1013 SC  
**Status:** Draft. Player-facing. Not Canon until reviewed. Written as what a person in Aethwyrd could know.

In Greymoor no one rules, and so everything is decided at a table. A moot of elders will sit for a day and a night over a boundary stake or a stolen punt, and the soup is what keeps them civil. I make the soup.

## The pot

It must be large enough that nobody is tempted to take a second helping before everyone has had a first. Mine holds fourteen bowls. I know that number because in my experience a moot is seven elders, seven grievances and one cook.

## The stock

Begin with eel bones and a smoked head, browned in the pot with a little fat. Add water, a whole onion with its skin on and a handful of dried reed-root. Simmer until the fen smell has left the room. That takes about as long as it takes the first elder to say, "I do not wish to be unreasonable."

## What goes in

- Black bread, cubed and toasted. It thickens as it sits.
- Bog-berries, a small handful. They make a soup that is a little sour and a little sweet, which is the temperature of most disputes.
- Peat-smoked eel, flaked.
- Salt. Always salt. Do not trust anyone who says the soup does not need it.

## How to serve

Serve in the order that the elders sit, and never in the order of their age. Give the angriest elder the first bowl. They cannot speak while they eat, and for a short time the room is quiet.

If an argument has started, bring the bread. Bread is a truce.

## What the soup has settled

A boundary stake near the Crooked Lantern. The name of a child. Whose turn it was to ring the bell at dusk. The ownership of a punt that had been stolen, and returned, and stolen again by the same person for the same reason. I have never known a dispute to survive a second bowl of this soup.

I do not claim credit. I claim only that it is hard to keep your temper hot when you are eating something that is hot.
