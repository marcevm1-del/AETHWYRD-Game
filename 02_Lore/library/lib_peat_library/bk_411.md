# Songs Heard in Greymoor stilt-village

*Corin Ellison of Greymoor*

**Shelf:** Ballad sheet  
**Region:** Greymoor  
**In-world date:** 951 SC  
**Status:** Draft. Player-facing. Not Canon until reviewed. Written as what a person in Aethwyrd could know.
## Sung at the table

Collected at Greymoor stilt-village. Change the name of the bridge if your bridge is the one that is out. A song that cannot survive a correction is a poster.

Latch the door, but leave the light,
Somebody’s walking home tonight.

Somebody’s walking home tonight.
