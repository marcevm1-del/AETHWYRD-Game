*Soren Reed of Greymoor*

**Shelf:** Local history

**In-world date:** 932 SC  
**Status:** Draft. Player-facing. Not Canon until reviewed. Written as what a person in Aethwyrd could know.
## The short version on the board

I set this down at the Walking Hut market because the Guild card was right about the danger and wrong about the cause. The guild chalk stub on the table is not a relic. It is how this copy stayed honest.

At the Walking Hut market the work of the day sits inside reed thatch on stilts above peat. People eat eel, bog-berries, and black bread and they talk about lights that are not lanterns out on the peat the way coastal towns talk about weather: often, and with corrections.

This chapter listens by way of a reeve's paper. A speaker at the Walking Hut market says one thing. A speaker at Greymoor stilt-village says the part that was left on the road between them. One of them is remembering a person. The other is remembering a rule.

The shrine mark nearby belongs to Morrowyn: a lantern with a blue flame, kept for the dead, and the living who remember them. That is public devotion, the kind you can see from the street. Morrowyn's blue lamps along the causeway. I am not writing what a god intends. I am writing what the keeper swept this morning.
