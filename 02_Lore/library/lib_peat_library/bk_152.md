# The Walking Hut: A Short History
*Pim Fenwick, apprentice to the Walking Hut's trader, learning to tell it straight*

**Shelf:** Local history  
**Region:** Greymoor  
**In-world date:** 1018 SC  
**Status:** Draft. Player-facing. Not Canon until reviewed. Written as what a person in Aethwyrd could know.

I have been told to write the history of the Hut so that the next apprentice has something to read besides the trader's face. The trader has agreed to this. The trader has also told me that there is no history, only a route.

## What the Hut is

It is a trader's hut on great bird legs. It walks. It walks a slow circuit of the fen, and where it stops, there is a market. I have never seen it hurry, and I have never seen it late. It is, as far as anyone can tell, older than the oldest person who has bought from it.

## What it sells

Medicine, news, salt, cloth, iron nails, candles and a very small selection of things that the trader will not name. The medicine is good. The news is better. The salt is at a price that is neither fair nor unfair, but regular, and regularity is worth more than fairness in a place where nothing else is.

## How it stops

I have asked the trader how the Hut decides where to stand. The answer was, "It knows." I asked how it knows. The answer was, "Better than I do." I asked whether it could be made to walk faster. The trader looked at me for a long time and said, "Why?"

I have not asked a fourth question.

## The year it stopped

There was a winter, some years before I came, when the Hut stood still for forty days. The fen went without salt and without news. People began to talk, and in the fen, when people talk, it is as though a wind has risen. The Hut began to walk again of its own accord. Nobody asked why it had stopped; nobody has asked since.

## What I have learned

A hut that walks is a hut that remembers where you live. That is the oldest kind of trade. I write this down so that you will not think it is magic. It is not magic. It is manners, kept up for a very long time.
