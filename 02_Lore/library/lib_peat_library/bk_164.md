# Two Accounts of Greymoor

*Hana Lind of Greymoor*

**Shelf:** Local history  
**Region:** Greymoor  
**In-world date:** 944 SC  
**Status:** Draft. Player-facing. Not Canon until reviewed. Written as what a person in Aethwyrd could know.
## The short version on the board

I set this down at the Walking Hut market because three neighbors told the same story with three different weather reports. The chipped scale-weight on the table is not a relic. It is how this copy stayed honest.

At the Walking Hut market the work of the day sits inside reed thatch on stilts above peat. People eat eel, bog-berries, and black bread and they talk about lights that are not lanterns out on the peat the way coastal towns talk about weather: often, and with corrections.

This chapter listens by way of a reeve's paper. A speaker at the Walking Hut market says one thing. A speaker at Greymoor stilt-village says the part that was left on the road between them. The official line is shorter. The household line feeds more people.

The shrine mark nearby belongs to Solenne: a golden scale, kept for oaths and the morning light. That is public devotion, the kind you can see from the street. Morrowyn's blue lamps along the causeway. I am not writing what a god intends. I am writing what the keeper swept this morning.
