## How this copy is used

I set this down at the Walking Hut market because the Guild card was right about the danger and wrong about the cause. The guild chalk stub on the table is not a relic. It is how this copy stayed honest.

Bramble Holt of Greymoor keeps this book in the kitchen at the Walking Hut market, where the houses are reed thatch on stilts above peat. The everyday pot already knows eel, bog-berries, and black bread. What follows is the version you can cook when lights that are not lanterns out on the peat has made the market short, and when you still mean to sit down.

A note on faith, because someone always asks: Morrowyn's blue lamps along the causeway. None of these dishes require a blessing to be edible. A blessing is for the table, after the salt is in.

Prices move. A copper is still the coin of a small bite, and a silver is still a bed. If a stall quotes you a mithril piece for supper, you are in the wrong story or the wrong street.

## Ink-Stone Dumplings

*As cooked when the Walking Hut market is being ordinary.*

**You need**

- dumpling wrappers
- greens
- a little meat or mushroom
- tea

Fill the wrappers less than you want to. Boil them until they float and then a little longer. Tea after, not with, if you care about the broth.

## Adobe Noon Bread

*As cooked when the Walking Hut market is being ordinary.*

**You need**

- flour
- a little oil
- dates
- salt

Make a soft dough. Bake it on a hot plate. Split it and put the dates in while it is too hot for your fingers. That is the point.

## Gold-Road Millet

*As cooked when the Walking Hut market is being ordinary.*

**You need**

## Mayonnaise, and the Argument

*As cooked when the Walking Hut market is being ordinary.*

**You need**

- an egg yolk
- oil
- a little vinegar
- salt

Whisk the yolk. Add oil by drops until it looks like a mistake, then in a thread. Vinegar and salt. Serve it with fried fish or be prepared to defend your character.
