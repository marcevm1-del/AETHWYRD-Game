# Letters Left in Lirian canal cities

*Saoirse Okeke of Lirian*

**Shelf:** Letter book  
**Region:** Lirian  
**In-world date:** 998 SC  
**Status:** Draft. Player-facing. Not Canon until reviewed. Written as what a person in Aethwyrd could know.
## Who kept these

Saoirse Okeke of Lirian wrote from Lirian canal cities. The houses are canal stucco and painted bridges. The letters smell faintly of river fish, citrus peel, and oil. They mention a licence argument at every lock the way you mention a cough in the next room: not the main sentence, never absent.

## Letter 1 — Maelsday, to a sibling

Lirian canal cities, in the year this copy claims.

I arrived. The bed is clean and the lock is a suggestion. Send the blue thread, not money.

The rest of the page is ordinary: river fish, citrus peel, and oil, a price, a name spelled two ways. Maelis reflected in the canals. The official line is shorter. The household line feeds more people.

— Saoirse Okeke of Lirian

## Letter 2 — Thorsday, to a teacher

Lirian canal cities, in the year this copy claims.
