# Neighbors Near Velmark ducal towns

*Corin Ibarra of Velmark*

**Shelf:** Field guide  
**Region:** Velmark  
**In-world date:** 1004 SC  
**Status:** Draft. Player-facing. Not Canon until reviewed. Written as what a person in Aethwyrd could know.
## Before the entries

I set this down at Velmark ducal towns because the Guild card was right about the danger and wrong about the cause. The copper nail on the table is not a relic. It is how this copy stayed honest.

This guide is for walking country: grain barns and white roads. You will smell porridge, new bread, and onion in the settlements and you will be told about carts that must move before the bell. The entries below are neighbors, not trophies.

Shrines out here lean toward this habit: Solenne at the barn door. A prayer is not a ward you can spend like a potion. Learn the creature anyway.

If a poster and a hunter disagree, copy both and date them. The halls of the Labyrinth reset. Ordinary hills do not, and a path can be wrong twice.

## Blade Mantis

Around Velmark ducal towns, people file the Blade Mantis near guild rank D+. A rank is a licence hint. It is not a measurement of your courage, and it goes stale if the ground has changed.

The ground is the hunter. Look at the low places before the skyline.

Folk talk says its nature runs toward Wind, and that Fire unsettles it. Treat that as a rumor you can test from farther away than its reach. If the test fails, you have learned something, provided you can still leave.

What the meat-stall will not put on a sign: do not take a young one to prove a point, do not block the line it uses to flee, and do not trust a core that is still warm as if it were a lamp. Cores light cities. They also belong to a creature that was here first. Neither is lying. Each left out the part that made their own choice look worse.
