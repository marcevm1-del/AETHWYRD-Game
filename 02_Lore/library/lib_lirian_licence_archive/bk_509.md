# Neighbors Near Moraveil goblin village

*Bramble Mori of Moraveil*

**Shelf:** Field guide  
**Region:** Moraveil  
**In-world date:** 1001 SC  
**Status:** Draft. Player-facing. Not Canon until reviewed. Written as what a person in Aethwyrd could know.
## Before the entries

I set this down at Moraveil market town because the shrine keeper and the miller had stopped speaking, and the road still needed both. The wooden spoon on the table is not a relic. It is how this copy stayed honest.

This guide is for walking country: market awnings and goblin-mended stalls. You will smell pepper stew and fried bread in the settlements and you will be told about a bargain that changes if you blink. The entries below are neighbors, not trophies.

Shrines out here lean toward this habit: Vex's dice on the counter. A prayer is not a ward you can spend like a potion. Learn the creature anyway.

If a poster and a hunter disagree, copy both and date them. The halls of the Labyrinth reset. Ordinary hills do not, and a path can be wrong twice.

## Highwayman Captain

Around Moraveil market town, people file the Highwayman Captain near guild rank D. A rank is a licence hint. It is not a measurement of your courage, and it goes stale if the ground has changed.

It comes on. Distance is the tool, not a speech.

Folk talk says its nature runs toward None, and that Fire unsettles it. Treat that as a rumor you can test from farther away than its reach. If the test fails, you have learned something, provided you can still leave.

What the meat-stall will not put on a sign: do not take a young one to prove a point, do not block the line it uses to flee, and do not trust a core that is still warm as if it were a lamp. Cores light cities. They also belong to a creature that was here first. One of them is remembering a person. The other is remembering a rule.
