# Goods Through Velmark ducal towns

*Noor Novak of Velmark*

**Shelf:** Trade notes  
**Region:** Velmark  
**In-world date:** 981 SC  
**Status:** Draft. Player-facing. Not Canon until reviewed. Written as what a person in Aethwyrd could know.
## The stall

At Velmark ducal towns the goods sit against grain barns and white roads. People are buying porridge, new bread, and onion and talking about carts that must move before the bell. Faith is present as habit: Solenne at the barn door. None of that is a price list. It is why the price list is tense.

The wallet of this world counts copper, silver, gold, platinum, and mithril, each a hundred of the one before. Most of a life is lived in the first two.

## Grain

Grain moves because it keeps, and it makes other arguments possible.

In Velmark, ask who carried it, not only who owns the stall. A thing can arrive by marriage, refuge, a contract, or a raid, and those are not the same story. Neither is lying. Each left out the part that made their own choice look worse.

Write the price you saw and the day. A book that says 'always' is trying to be a lord. If the Guild’s buyback for cores tempts you to hunt past your rank, this page is the friend who takes the chalk out of your hand.
