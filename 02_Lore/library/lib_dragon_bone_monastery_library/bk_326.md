# Goods Through Sky Archipelago

*Tamsin Ibarra of Sky Archipelago*

**Shelf:** Trade notes  
**Region:** Sky Archipelago  
**In-world date:** 986 SC  
**Status:** Draft. Player-facing. Not Canon until reviewed. Written as what a person in Aethwyrd could know.
## The stall

At Sky Archipelago the goods sit against eyries, cloud, and the Long Stair. People are buying bird-broth and hard bread and talking about a height that walking can reach and wings are not offered. Faith is present as habit: Kaelthas above, Thorgrim in the stair-nails. None of that is a price list. It is why the price list is tense.

The wallet of this world counts copper, silver, gold, platinum, and mithril, each a hundred of the one before. Most of a life is lived in the first two.

## Grain

Grain moves because it keeps, and it makes other arguments possible.

In Sky Archipelago, ask who carried it, not only who owns the stall. A thing can arrive by marriage, refuge, a contract, or a raid, and those are not the same story. Both saw the same hour. They were standing on different banks.
