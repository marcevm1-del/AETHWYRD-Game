## What this fragment refuses

Valdrisia is a name for an old empire of towers and ley-line farms, dated by scholars near six thousand years before the Summoning Calendar, and gone, on the public record, in a single night about three thousand years before that calendar’s year zero. Sunscar keeps the ruins people can walk to. Vharlen keeps whatever this copy actually found, which may be less romantic.

I will not write the night as if I were there. I will not name a single cause to please a patron. A vanished city is not a riddle you are owed. It is a wound in the record, and the people who live on that ground now have suppers to cook. If your theory needs them to be props, leave the theory in your pocket.
