## Limits, said early

Street lamps burn core-light. Pumps run on small rune engines. This makes a town look effortless. It is not effortless. A core is a spent life of a monster, bought, sold, and argued over. A rune repeats what it was taught. It does not become wise, and it does not forgive a sloppy circle.

Divine magic is borrowed from the Eight through devotion. The Church licenses healers. An unlicensed healer is illegal even when the patient stands up afterward. That is a law about power, not a proof that the patient should have stayed down. Know which argument you are in.

The official teaching says Ilvaris gave Status so mortals could grow enough to face calamities. This lecture repeats the official teaching and then returns to the work: what the mark on your sheet allows you to practice today. Wonder belongs in the room. Convenience does not. If a working solves your trouble by ignoring its own cost, you skipped a page.
