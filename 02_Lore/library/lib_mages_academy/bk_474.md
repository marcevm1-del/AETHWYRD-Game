## A potsherd, not a dynasty

I set this down at Summer Court glades because a child asked a straight question and the adults answered with a proverb. The blue lamp-glass on the table is not a relic. It is how this copy stayed honest.

This chapter listens by way of a walker. A speaker at Summer Court glades says one thing. A speaker at Everdusk says the part that was left on the road between them. Both saw the same hour. They were standing on different banks.

## What this fragment refuses

Valdrisia is a name for an old empire of towers and ley-line farms, dated by scholars near six thousand years before the Summoning Calendar, and gone, on the public record, in a single night about three thousand years before that calendar’s year zero. Sunscar keeps the ruins people can walk to. Everdusk keeps whatever this copy actually found, which may be less romantic.

I will not write the night as if I were there. I will not name a single cause to please a patron. A vanished city is not a riddle you are owed. It is a wound in the record, and the people who live on that ground now have suppers to cook. If your theory needs them to be props, leave the theory in your pocket.
