# The Knight and the Wyvern Princess: The Cheerful Poster

*Ilyra Penn of Aldmere*

**Shelf:** Romance serial  
**Region:** Aldmere  
**In-world date:** 882 SC, printer's copy circulating in 1024 SC  
**Status:** Draft. Player-facing. Not Canon until reviewed. Written as what a person in Aethwyrd could know.
## What this volume is

A Church clerk wants the wyvern cleared. The Guild wants a species report. Seryth wants the road.

A note from the Aldmere printer: this is a novel. The canton, the manor, and the persons are the author’s. Do not ride to Dragon Spine expecting Princess Seryth to owe you an appearance. Do not nail a bounty because a book was tender.

## Part 1

The clerk spoke of safety. The clerk’s eyes spoke of a tidy page. Edwyn had learned, from his own order, how a tidy page can march. He asked what 'cleared' would leave in the stable. The clerk did not enjoy the question.

The road around them went on being a road: bread, a price, a bell somewhere that was not theirs to own. The book stays with the bowl, the buckle, the sock, the lantern. Large destinies can find another shelf.

## Part 2

The Guild’s letter was better. It asked for a report, a witness, and no trophy. Seryth could live with a report if the wyvern’s name stayed off it. They wrote the report together, arguing about adjectives, which is a kind of peace.

The road around them went on being a road: bread, a price, a bell somewhere that was not theirs to own. The book stays with the bowl, the buckle, the sock, the lantern. Large destinies can find another shelf.

## Part 3

At the end of the day the poster was still nailed up. Edwyn took it down, copied the useful warnings onto a plain sheet, and burned the cheerful one in the cook-fire. Seryth did not praise him. She passed him the next sheet for the Guild, which was better.

The road around them went on being a road: bread, a price, a bell somewhere that was not theirs to own. The book stays with the bowl, the buckle, the sock, the lantern. Large destinies can find another shelf.
