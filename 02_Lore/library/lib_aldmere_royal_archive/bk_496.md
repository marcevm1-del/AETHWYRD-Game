# The Knight and the Wyvern Princess: The Wrong Rumor

*Ilyra Penn of Aldmere*

**Shelf:** Romance serial  
**Region:** Aldmere  
**In-world date:** 887 SC, printer's copy circulating in 1024 SC  
**Status:** Draft. Player-facing. Not Canon until reviewed. Written as what a person in Aethwyrd could know.
## What this volume is

A letter is opened on the road. By the time they reach the next inn, the story is that Edwyn has stolen a princess and a monster.

A note from the Aldmere printer: this is a novel. The canton, the manor, and the persons are the author’s. Do not ride to Dragon Spine expecting Princess Seryth to owe you an appearance. Do not nail a bounty because a book was tender.

## Part 1

Rumors travel lighter than people. The inn had already chosen a version in which Seryth did not have a will. She corrected it once, clearly, and then ate her soup. Repeating herself would have made the room feel owed her life.

The road around them went on being a road: bread, a price, a bell somewhere that was not theirs to own. The book stays with the bowl, the buckle, the sock, the lantern. Large destinies can find another shelf.

## Part 2

Edwyn wanted to fight the sentence. She asked him to fight the part that would follow them into the next town: the poster, copied badly. They pinned a plain account beside it and signed both names.
