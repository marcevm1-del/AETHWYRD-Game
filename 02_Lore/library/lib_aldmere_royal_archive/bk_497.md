# The Knight and the Wyvern Princess: Chosen

*Ilyra Penn of Aldmere*

**Shelf:** Romance serial  
**Region:** Aldmere  
**In-world date:** 888 SC, printer's copy circulating in 1024 SC  
**Status:** Draft. Player-facing. Not Canon until reviewed. Written as what a person in Aethwyrd could know.
## What this volume is

In a later storm the wyvern chooses Edwyn’s side of the camp. It is a choice, and it can be undone.

A note from the Aldmere printer: this is a novel. The canton, the manor, and the persons are the author’s. Do not ride to Dragon Spine expecting Princess Seryth to owe you an appearance. Do not nail a bounty because a book was tender.

## Part 1

The wyvern had many reasons to prefer Seryth, and did, most hours. This hour it put its weight against Edwyn’s knee and sighed like a fire settling. Seryth watched the way a person watches a friend be trusted by someone they love.

The road around them went on being a road: bread, a price, a bell somewhere that was not theirs to own. The book stays with the bowl, the buckle, the sock, the lantern. Large destinies can find another shelf.

## Part 2

Edwyn put his hand on the harness buckle and left it unfastened. 'You can go,' he said, which is a sentence that only counts if you mean the going.

## Part 3

The wyvern stayed. Seryth sat down on his other side. The three of them made a shape against the weather that was not a crest and not a claim. It was company.
