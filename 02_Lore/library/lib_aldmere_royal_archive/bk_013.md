# Counting Kobolds
*Neris Quill, who watched the Salvage Warren from the Chalk Landing for eleven winters*

**Shelf:** Field guide  
**Region:** Great Labyrinth (Floors 1-40)  
**In-world date:** 1009 SC  
**Status:** Draft. Player-facing. Not Canon until reviewed. Written as what a person in Aethwyrd could know.

Under the third and fourth floors there is a kobold den where the little dungeon-born things stack whatever adventurers lose. People call it the Salvage Warren. I call it my neighbours. I have sat on the Chalk Landing with a flask and a tally-board for eleven winters and I have grown fond of them in the way you grow fond of a leaking roof: I know every drip.

The Guild pays me a small stipend to count. I count them badly and I tell the truth about the arithmetic.

## Kobold Sneak

Rank E, Dark, weak to Light. It lives in the dark edge of a room and takes the thing you set down for a moment. It is skittish: you will never fight one to the end, because it leaves. Carry a lamp and the Sneak has no hall to hide in. I have seen a whole shift of them stand about the Scrap Hall in the lamp's yellow circle looking like clerks who forgot their forms.

## Kobold Shaman

D, a Fire creature, weak to Water, and skittish. It does not charge. It stands at the back and makes a small angry fire that is more nuisance than danger, and it vanishes when the fire is put out. A flask of water ends the lesson. I carry two.

## Kobold Trapmaster

Rank D, skittish, weak to Fire. It does not fight; it builds. The Weighing Gallery is its work: little traps that wait for the unwary. The rule I give to everyone is to look at the floor before you look at the wall. The floor tells you what a kobold thinks of you.

## Kobold Packleader

D+, skittish, weak to Fire. It does not command anyone; kobolds keep no ranks of that kind. It is simply the one the others follow when the stacks are moved from one hall to the next, because it remembers the way. Do not take what is stacked, and do not pick a fight over a boot.

## Armor Beetle

E+, Earth, weak to Wind, an ambusher. It sleeps in the Beetle Vault and wakes to the sound of boots. Strike its legs, not its back, and do not bring a hammer to a shell.

## What I have learned

The old tally-keepers left a rusted door below the stacks, and a way past it for people who can read their chalk. The kobolds do not read it. They keep it as one keeps a grandmother's chair: cleaned, avoided and not quite understood. If you can read the marks, you will find the way quicker than the Guild's map does. I will not say more, because the lesson is in finding.

Every winter I have fewer kobolds on my board than I did the winter before, and every winter there are more stacks in the Scrap Hall. I do not know what that means, and I am content not to know.
