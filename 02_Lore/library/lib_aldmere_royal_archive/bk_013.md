## Before the entries

I set this down at Great Labyrinth (Floors 1-40) because three neighbors told the same story with three different weather reports. The chipped scale-weight on the table is not a relic. It is how this copy stayed honest.

This guide is for walking country: cut stairs and chalk marks. You will smell whatever the pack still holds in the settlements and you will be told about floors that reset their halls and keep their lessons. The entries below are neighbors, not trophies.

Shrines out here lean toward this habit: Ilvaris's spindle scratched beside practical arrows. A prayer is not a ward you can spend like a potion. Learn the creature anyway.

If a poster and a hunter disagree, copy both and date them. The halls of the Labyrinth reset. Ordinary hills do not, and a path can be wrong twice.

## Bone Weevil

Around Great Labyrinth (Floors 1-40), people file the Bone Weevil near guild rank C. A rank is a licence hint. It is not a measurement of your courage, and it goes stale if the ground has changed.

It comes on. Distance is the tool, not a speech.

Folk talk says its nature runs toward Dark, and that Light unsettles it. Treat that as a rumor you can test from farther away than its reach. If the test fails, you have learned something, provided you can still leave.

What the meat-stall will not put on a sign: do not take a young one to prove a point, do not block the line it uses to flee, and do not trust a core that is still warm as if it were a lamp. Cores light cities. They also belong to a creature that was here first. Both saw the same hour. They were standing on different banks.
