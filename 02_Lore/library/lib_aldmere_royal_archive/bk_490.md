# The Knight and the Wyvern Princess: Seryth

*Ilyra Penn of Aldmere*

**Shelf:** Romance serial  
**Region:** Aldmere  
**In-world date:** 881 SC, printer's copy circulating in 1024 SC  
**Status:** Draft. Player-facing. Not Canon until reviewed. Written as what a person in Aethwyrd could know.
## What this volume is

Princess Seryth of a Dragon Spine canton comes for the wyvern, who is kin in the way her people reckon kin, not a pet and not a prize.

A note from the Aldmere printer: this is a novel. The canton, the manor, and the persons are the author’s. Do not ride to Dragon Spine expecting Princess Seryth to owe you an appearance. Do not nail a bounty because a book was tender.

## Part 1

Seryth arrived without a trumpet. She had dust on her hem and a law in her mouth. The wyvern put its head under her hand and Edwyn understood that he had been hosting a person with wings, which he had almost known.

The road around them went on being a road: bread, a price, a bell somewhere that was not theirs to own. The book stays with the bowl, the buckle, the sock, the lantern. Large destinies can find another shelf.

## Part 2

She thanked him without giving him the wyvern’s true name. Names, she said, are not a receipt. He agreed so quickly that she looked at him again, revising the manor-knight she had expected.

## Part 3

They shared bread because the day was long and the stable was not a court. Neither called it anything. The wyvern slept between them and the door, which was trust arranged as furniture.
