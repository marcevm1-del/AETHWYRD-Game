# The Knight and the Wyvern Princess: No Trophy

*Ilyra Penn of Aldmere*

**Shelf:** Romance serial  
**Region:** Aldmere  
**In-world date:** 890 SC, printer's copy circulating in 1024 SC  
**Status:** Draft. Player-facing. Not Canon until reviewed. Written as what a person in Aethwyrd could know.
## What this volume is

Edwyn answers the baron. He does not become a ruler. He does not bring a piece of the wyvern. The cost is real and limited.

A note from the Aldmere printer: this is a novel. The canton, the manor, and the persons are the author’s. Do not ride to Dragon Spine expecting Princess Seryth to owe you an appearance. Do not nail a bounty because a book was tender.

## Part 1

The hall wanted a scene. Edwyn gave it a report, a burned poster, and his resignation from the particular duty of fetching trophies. He did not resign from being a neighbor. Those are different offices.

The road around them went on being a road: bread, a price, a bell somewhere that was not theirs to own. The book stays with the bowl, the buckle, the sock, the lantern. Large destinies can find another shelf.

## Part 2

The baron was angry in a way that would last a season, not a legend. Good. Legends are how this sort of anger recruits a choir. Edwyn accepted the season.

The road around them went on being a road: bread, a price, a bell somewhere that was not theirs to own. The book stays with the bowl, the buckle, the sock, the lantern. Large destinies can find another shelf.

## Part 3

Seryth waited outside on purpose, so no one could say she had bewitched the hall. When he came out she looked at his face, read the season, and said, 'We can walk.' They walked.

The road around them went on being a road: bread, a price, a bell somewhere that was not theirs to own. The book stays with the bowl, the buckle, the sock, the lantern. Large destinies can find another shelf.
