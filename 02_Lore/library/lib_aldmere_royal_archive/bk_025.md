# Pocket-Book of a Floor-Walker
*Brandt Kessel, hunter, scrawled between jobs*

**Shelf:** Field guide  
**Region:** Great Labyrinth (Floors 1-40)  
**In-world date:** 1014 SC  
**Status:** Draft. Player-facing. Not Canon until reviewed. Written as what a person in Aethwyrd could know.

Short entries. I write them in the dark with a pencil. Forgive the spelling; I forgive yours.

## Killer Ant

E. No Element. Weak to Fire. Swarms. Never fight them in a corridor you cannot leave. Fire at the front, run at the back. One is a snack, forty is a funeral.

## Needle Wasp

E+. Poison. Weak to Light. Aggressive, and fast. Carry an antidote or do not carry yourself. It stings through leather. It does not stay in one place; it goes for your face. A cloak over the head while you retreat works better than courage.

## Bone Crawler

F. Dark. Weak to Light. It drops on you from the ceiling and calls itself an accident. Look up when the hall goes quiet.

## Mud Doll

F. Earth. Weak to Wind. Territorial. It is a puddle that learned to stand. Do not step in the mud on Floor Two. Do not step in the mud on Floor Three. It does not matter which floor.

## Floor Crawler

F. No Element. Weak to Fire. Aggressive and dull. Good for a first-day kill and a poor teacher. The ones who learn from Floor Crawlers learn the wrong lesson.

## Notes to myself

- Never go in with two empty flasks. I have done it once.
- The best hall on Floor Three is the one nobody names. It has no ants.
- Thorgrim keeps a forge on Forge Lane and makes a fair blade. Kaelthas keeps the storm that follows you out of the gate. Say both names and mean one of them.
- Torches on the walls are for people who forgot their own. Bring your own.
- If you hear laughter behind a wall, nobody is laughing. Walk on.

## For whoever finds this

It is not a manual. It is the list of mistakes I made, in the order I made them. Learn from the list and make different ones. There are enough to go round.
