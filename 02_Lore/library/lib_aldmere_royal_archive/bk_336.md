# Letters Left in Ashby

*Kwame Ndlovu of Aldmere*

**Shelf:** Letter book  
**Region:** Aldmere  
**In-world date:** 996 SC  
**Status:** Draft. Player-facing. Not Canon until reviewed. Written as what a person in Aethwyrd could know.
## Who kept these

Kwame Ndlovu of Aldmere wrote from Royal Valley farms. The houses are stone manors and new bath-houses. The letters smell faintly of bread, gravy, and the endless mayonnaise argument. They mention undead on the valley roads the way you mention a cough in the next room: not the main sentence, never absent.

## Letter 1 — Maelsday, to a sibling

Royal Valley farms, in the year this copy claims.

I arrived. The bed is clean and the lock is a suggestion. Send the blue thread, not money.

The rest of the page is ordinary: bread, gravy, and the endless mayonnaise argument, a price, a name spelled two ways. Solenne's sun-domes and the Guild's spindle banner. The official line is shorter. The household line feeds more people.

— Kwame Ndlovu of Aldmere

## Letter 2 — Thorsday, to a teacher

Royal Valley farms, in the year this copy claims.

Your lesson about measuring twice survived the road. Your lesson about people did not, and I am rewriting it.

The rest of the page is ordinary: bread, gravy, and the endless mayonnaise argument, a price, a name spelled two ways. Solenne's sun-domes and the Guild's spindle banner. One of them is remembering a person. The other is remembering a rule.

— Kwame Ndlovu of Aldmere

## Letter 3 — Sylvasday, to the guild clerk

Royal Valley farms, in the year this copy claims.

The poster is missing the part where the bridge is out. I am not asking for a refund. I am asking for a corrected board.

The rest of the page is ordinary: bread, gravy, and the endless mayonnaise argument, a price, a name spelled two ways. Solenne's sun-domes and the Guild's spindle banner. Both saw the same hour. They were standing on different banks.

— Kwame Ndlovu of Aldmere

## Letter 4 — Kaelsday, to a parent

Royal Valley farms, in the year this copy claims.

Do not come. I am well. I am also not ready to perform being well at your table. The next letter will be longer.

The rest of the page is ordinary: bread, gravy, and the endless mayonnaise argument, a price, a name spelled two ways. Solenne's sun-domes and the Guild's spindle banner. Neither is lying. Each left out the part that made their own choice look worse.

— Kwame Ndlovu of Aldmere

## Letter 5 — Morrowsday, to a friend on another road

Royal Valley farms, in the year this copy claims.

The innkeeper remembers you and pretends not to. Leave the spoon you stole the next time you pass. They are using a ladle and suffering.

The rest of the page is ordinary: bread, gravy, and the endless mayonnaise argument, a price, a name spelled two ways. Solenne's sun-domes and the Guild's spindle banner. Neither is lying. Each left out the part that made their own choice look worse.

— Kwame Ndlovu of Aldmere

## Letter 6 — Vexday, to nobody, never sent

Royal Valley farms, in the year this copy claims.

If I do not come back, the knot-cord in the tin is the real tally. The paper tally is for the reeve.

The rest of the page is ordinary: bread, gravy, and the endless mayonnaise argument, a price, a name spelled two ways. Solenne's sun-domes and the Guild's spindle banner. One of them is remembering a person. The other is remembering a rule.
