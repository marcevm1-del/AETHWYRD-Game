# The Knight and the Wyvern Princess: Longhouse

*Ilyra Penn of Aldmere*

**Shelf:** Romance serial  
**Region:** Aldmere  
**In-world date:** 889 SC, printer's copy circulating in 1024 SC  
**Status:** Draft. Player-facing. Not Canon until reviewed. Written as what a person in Aethwyrd could know.
## What this volume is

Winter at Frostveil. A turf longhouse, smoked fish, and an argument about courage that does not humiliate either of them.

A note from the Aldmere printer: this is a novel. The canton, the manor, and the persons are the author’s. Do not ride to Dragon Spine expecting Princess Seryth to owe you an appearance. Do not nail a bounty because a book was tender.

## Part 1

The longhouse was warm because many people decided to be warm together. Edwyn tried to take the worst pallet. A grandmother told him to stop performing and sleep where he was put.

The road around them went on being a road: bread, a price, a bell somewhere that was not theirs to own. The book stays with the bowl, the buckle, the sock, the lantern. Large destinies can find another shelf.

## Part 2

Seryth and he argued, quietly, about a risky road. She thought his caution was a kind of fear wearing manners. He thought her certainty was a kind of fear wearing speed. Both were a little true, and they said so before bed, which kept the argument from becoming a wall.

The road around them went on being a road: bread, a price, a bell somewhere that was not theirs to own. The book stays with the bowl, the buckle, the sock, the lantern. Large destinies can find another shelf.

## Part 3

In the morning she handed him tea. He handed her the drier socks. Romance, this volume suggests, is logistics performed with affection.
