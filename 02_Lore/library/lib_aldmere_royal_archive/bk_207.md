## The bench

Copied at Great Labyrinth (Floors 1-40) from a maker who was willing to be watched. Nothing here makes you a master. Mastery is someone else’s trust, repeated.

## Terrace Wall

**For:** A wall that holds soil and still lets water down.

Stones with faces that touch. A weep-gap every few paces. Build it a little in from the fall, so the mountain can be the mountain and the potatoes can stay.

Stop when the work is true. Continuing in order to feel like a craftsperson is how a good nail becomes a bent one.

## Stair Nail

**For:** The nail that keeps a walked stair from becoming a rumor.

On a long stair, the nail is the ethics. Check the one at hand height and the one at the turn. Do not offer to carry a person up a height they can walk. Walking is the route.

Stop when the work is true. Continuing in order to feel like a craftsperson is how a good nail becomes a bent one.

## Book Press

**For:** A press for a wet page, a flower you are allowed to take, or a map.

Boards, a strap, and patience. Do not press a page that is not yours. Margins are a conversation. A press is not a way to flatten someone else’s note out of existence.

Stop when the work is true. Continuing in order to feel like a craftsperson is how a good nail becomes a bent one.

## Nail

**For:** A straight nail, headed, that will take a hammer without folding.

Heat the rod to the color your teacher named, not the color you find exciting. Draw it out. Head it. Quench as you were taught for this metal and not for the metal in a ballad. Forty honest nails teach a hand more than one anxious blade.

Stop when the work is true. Continuing in order to feel like a craftsperson is how a good nail becomes a bent one.

## Reed Thatch Tie

**For:** A bundle that sheds rain and can be replaced without heroics.

Cut reeds after the wet week, not during it. Lay them thick enough that light does not prize through. Tie where the next person can reach. A roof is a promise to someone who is asleep.

Stop when the work is true. Continuing in order to feel like a craftsperson is how a good nail becomes a bent one.

## Knot-Cord Tally

**For:** A record that works when the paper is gone.

One knot, one debt or one gift, and a space of cord between families of knots. Never one cord for two kinds of promise. Hang it where the household can see it and a thief must be obvious.

Stop when the work is true. Continuing in order to feel like a craftsperson is how a good nail becomes a bent one.
