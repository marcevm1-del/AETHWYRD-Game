# The Knight and the Wyvern Princess: The Bowl

*Ilyra Penn of Aldmere*

**Shelf:** Romance serial  
**Region:** Aldmere  
**In-world date:** 880 SC, printer's copy circulating in 1024 SC  
**Status:** Draft. Player-facing. Not Canon until reviewed. Written as what a person in Aethwyrd could know.
## What this volume is

Edwyn Carr, a knight of a small manor on the Grain Road, finds a wounded wyvern and a bounty poster with the same description. He puts the poster under a bowl of water and keeps his hands where the wyvern can see them.

A note from the Aldmere printer: this is a novel. The canton, the manor, and the persons are the author’s. Do not ride to Dragon Spine expecting Princess Seryth to owe you an appearance. Do not nail a bounty because a book was tender.

## Part 1

The bounty was written in a cheerful hand. Cheerful hands should not be trusted with other people’s bodies. Edwyn read it twice, then looked at the wing, which was torn in a way that matched a careless rope and not a duel.

The road around them went on being a road: bread, a price, a bell somewhere that was not theirs to own. The book stays with the bowl, the buckle, the sock, the lantern. Large destinies can find another shelf.

## Part 2

He set a bowl down and stepped back. The wyvern drank like a person who expected the bowl to be a trick. When it was not, some tightness went out of the air. Edwyn did not name the feeling. Naming it would have made him brave in the wrong direction.

The road around them went on being a road: bread, a price, a bell somewhere that was not theirs to own. The book stays with the bowl, the buckle, the sock, the lantern. Large destinies can find another shelf.

## Part 3

By evening the wing was wrapped in a clean cloth. No chain was in the picture. A passing carter called him soft. Edwyn said, 'The poster can wait until the wing cannot.' That sentence was the start of his trouble with men who like a short story.

The road around them went on being a road: bread, a price, a bell somewhere that was not theirs to own. The book stays with the bowl, the buckle, the sock, the lantern. Large destinies can find another shelf.
