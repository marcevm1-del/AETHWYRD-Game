# Neighbors Near Ashby

*Corin Cho of Aldmere*

**Shelf:** Field guide  
**Region:** Aldmere  
**In-world date:** 1015 SC  
**Status:** Draft. Player-facing. Not Canon until reviewed. Written as what a person in Aethwyrd could know.
## Before the entries

I set this down at Royal Valley farms because the shelf copy had been edited by someone with a tidy mind and a bad memory. The blue lamp-glass on the table is not a relic. It is how this copy stayed honest.

This guide is for walking country: stone manors and new bath-houses. You will smell bread, gravy, and the endless mayonnaise argument in the settlements and you will be told about undead on the valley roads. The entries below are neighbors, not trophies.

Shrines out here lean toward this habit: Solenne's sun-domes and the Guild's spindle banner. A prayer is not a ward you can spend like a potion. Learn the creature anyway.

If a poster and a hunter disagree, copy both and date them. The halls of the Labyrinth reset. Ordinary hills do not, and a path can be wrong twice.

## Headless Rider

Around Royal Valley farms, people file the Headless Rider near guild rank C. A rank is a licence hint. It is not a measurement of your courage, and it goes stale if the ground has changed.

Something here is being kept. It may be a nest, a stone, or a grudge.

Folk talk says its nature runs toward Dark, and that Light unsettles it. Treat that as a rumor you can test from farther away than its reach. If the test fails, you have learned something, provided you can still leave.

What the meat-stall will not put on a sign: do not take a young one to prove a point, do not block the line it uses to flee, and do not trust a core that is still warm as if it were a lamp. Cores light cities. They also belong to a creature that was here first. Neither is lying. Each left out the part that made their own choice look worse.
