# The Knight and the Wyvern Princess: The Harness That Is Not a Chain

*Ilyra Penn of Aldmere*

**Shelf:** Romance serial  
**Region:** Aldmere  
**In-world date:** 883 SC, printer's copy circulating in 1024 SC  
**Status:** Draft. Player-facing. Not Canon until reviewed. Written as what a person in Aethwyrd could know.
## What this volume is

They go to Iron Hold to have a harness made that helps a healing wing and does not pretend to own it.

A note from the Aldmere printer: this is a novel. The canton, the manor, and the persons are the author’s. Do not ride to Dragon Spine expecting Princess Seryth to owe you an appearance. Do not nail a bounty because a book was tender.

## Part 1

The smith at the hold refused the first drawing because it was a chain that had learned longer words. 'If it cannot be taken off by the one who wears it,' she said, 'it is not a harness. It is a mood you forged.'

The road around them went on being a road: bread, a price, a bell somewhere that was not theirs to own. The book stays with the bowl, the buckle, the sock, the lantern. Large destinies can find another shelf.

## Part 2

Edwyn, who had worn other people’s moods for years, sat down and redrew the buckles. Seryth corrected the place where the wing would hate it. The wyvern bit the practice strap and then, offended by nothing, let go.

## Part 3

They paid in silver and in a promise to send back the design if it galled. Thorgrim’s mark went on the new tool because the smith kept that custom, not because a god had been summoned to referee a romance.
