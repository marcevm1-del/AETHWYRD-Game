# The Knight and the Wyvern Princess: The Mill Bell

*Ilyra Penn of Aldmere*

**Shelf:** Romance serial  
**Region:** Aldmere  
**In-world date:** 886 SC, printer's copy circulating in 1024 SC  
**Status:** Draft. Player-facing. Not Canon until reviewed. Written as what a person in Aethwyrd could know.
## What this volume is

A siege bell catches them at a mill. They hold a door and a roof, then give the nails back to the miller.

A note from the Aldmere printer: this is a novel. The canton, the manor, and the persons are the author’s. Do not ride to Dragon Spine expecting Princess Seryth to owe you an appearance. Do not nail a bounty because a book was tender.

## Part 1

The bell was not romantic. It was a timetable. Edwyn took the door because he knew doors. Seryth took the yard because the wyvern would follow her voice and not a stranger’s heroics.

The road around them went on being a road: bread, a price, a bell somewhere that was not theirs to own. The book stays with the bowl, the buckle, the sock, the lantern. Large destinies can find another shelf.

## Part 2

When it was over, the miller was alive and angry about the flour. Anger was allowed. Edwyn swept. Seryth stacked sacks. The wyvern was kept back from the grain, because kinship is not an excuse to ruin someone’s year.

The road around them went on being a road: bread, a price, a bell somewhere that was not theirs to own. The book stays with the bowl, the buckle, the sock, the lantern. Large destinies can find another shelf.

## Part 3

Nobody knelt. The miller said, 'Come back when you are buying flour.' That was the blessing available. They took it.

The road around them went on being a road: bread, a price, a bell somewhere that was not theirs to own. The book stays with the bowl, the buckle, the sock, the lantern. Large destinies can find another shelf.
