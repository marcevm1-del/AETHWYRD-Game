# The Knight and the Wyvern Princess: Two Laws

*Ilyra Penn of Aldmere*

**Shelf:** Romance serial  
**Region:** Aldmere  
**In-world date:** 885 SC, printer's copy circulating in 1024 SC  
**Status:** Draft. Player-facing. Not Canon until reviewed. Written as what a person in Aethwyrd could know.
## What this volume is

Edwyn’s oath to his baron and Seryth’s ridge law ask for different endings. They do not solve this by becoming rulers of anything.

A note from the Aldmere printer: this is a novel. The canton, the manor, and the persons are the author’s. Do not ride to Dragon Spine expecting Princess Seryth to owe you an appearance. Do not nail a bounty because a book was tender.

## Part 1

The baron’s letter wanted a return and a proof. Proof, in that dialect, meant a part of the wyvern. Edwyn folded the letter until it was small and still there.

The road around them went on being a road: bread, a price, a bell somewhere that was not theirs to own. The book stays with the bowl, the buckle, the sock, the lantern. Large destinies can find another shelf.

## Part 2

Seryth’s law wanted the wyvern free to leave even if Seryth herself stayed. She said this without testing whether Edwyn would fail it. That was respect. He felt it land.

The road around them went on being a road: bread, a price, a bell somewhere that was not theirs to own. The book stays with the bowl, the buckle, the sock, the lantern. Large destinies can find another shelf.

## Part 3

They wrote, side by side, a third page: Edwyn would answer the baron in person, without a trophy, and Seryth would not be offered as an explanation. If that broke the oath, the oath was smaller than he had been told. He did not feel like a king. He felt like a man with a true sentence.

The road around them went on being a road: bread, a price, a bell somewhere that was not theirs to own. The book stays with the bowl, the buckle, the sock, the lantern. Large destinies can find another shelf.
