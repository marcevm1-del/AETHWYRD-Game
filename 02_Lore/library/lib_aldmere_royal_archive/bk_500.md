# The Knight and the Wyvern Princess: Two Lanterns

*Ilyra Penn of Aldmere*

**Shelf:** Romance serial  
**Region:** Aldmere  
**In-world date:** 891 SC, printer's copy circulating in 1024 SC  
**Status:** Draft. Player-facing. Not Canon until reviewed. Written as what a person in Aethwyrd could know.
## What this volume is

the Lamp Vigil. They light two lanterns. Nobody dies to prove the feeling. The serial leaves them in a life, not on a pedestal.

A note from the Aldmere printer: this is a novel. The canton, the manor, and the persons are the author’s. Do not ride to Dragon Spine expecting Princess Seryth to owe you an appearance. Do not nail a bounty because a book was tender.

## Part 1

They lit one lantern for Seryth’s dead, whose names Edwyn did not ask to hold, and one for Edwyn’s teacher, whose name he did say, because the teacher had liked being said. The wyvern stood behind them and was not asked to symbolize anything.

The road around them went on being a road: bread, a price, a bell somewhere that was not theirs to own. The book stays with the bowl, the buckle, the sock, the lantern. Large destinies can find another shelf.

## Part 2

Afterward there were biscuits, because the living have to eat, and a road, because the living have somewhere to be in the morning. Seryth took his hand where the town could see, not as a trophy and not as a secret.

The road around them went on being a road: bread, a price, a bell somewhere that was not theirs to own. The book stays with the bowl, the buckle, the sock, the lantern. Large destinies can find another shelf.

## Part 3

This is the last volume the printer in Aldmere has. It ends with a kettle. If you wanted a coronation, the wrong series found you. If you wanted two people and a free wyvern still choosing the same road in the morning, you may close the book. They are already walking.

The road around them went on being a road: bread, a price, a bell somewhere that was not theirs to own. The book stays with the bowl, the buckle, the sock, the lantern. Large destinies can find another shelf.
