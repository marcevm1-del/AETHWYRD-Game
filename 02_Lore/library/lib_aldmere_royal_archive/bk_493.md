# The Knight and the Wyvern Princess: Watch on the Pass

*Ilyra Penn of Aldmere*

**Shelf:** Romance serial  
**Region:** Aldmere  
**In-world date:** 884 SC, printer's copy circulating in 1024 SC  
**Status:** Draft. Player-facing. Not Canon until reviewed. Written as what a person in Aethwyrd could know.
## What this volume is

A storm pins them on the Karak Dûn Pass. The watch is shared. The conversation is careful and warm.

A note from the Aldmere printer: this is a novel. The canton, the manor, and the persons are the author’s. Do not ride to Dragon Spine expecting Princess Seryth to owe you an appearance. Do not nail a bounty because a book was tender.

## Part 1

The pass did not care about their errand. Wind took the loose end of every plan. They sat with their shoulders not quite touching, because touch should be chosen in daylight as well as in weather.

The road around them went on being a road: bread, a price, a bell somewhere that was not theirs to own. The book stays with the bowl, the buckle, the sock, the lantern. Large destinies can find another shelf.

## Part 2

Seryth told a small story about a monastery kitchen. Edwyn told a small story about a baron who loved trumpets. Neither story was a confession of the deep kind. They were practice in being a person beside another person.

The road around them went on being a road: bread, a price, a bell somewhere that was not theirs to own. The book stays with the bowl, the buckle, the sock, the lantern. Large destinies can find another shelf.

## Part 3

Near dawn the wyvern tucked its healed wing along Edwyn’s back, casual as a cloak. He went very still, so the gift would not be startled into meaning more than it did. Seryth saw, and her smile was quick and private.

The road around them went on being a road: bread, a price, a bell somewhere that was not theirs to own. The book stays with the bowl, the buckle, the sock, the lantern. Large destinies can find another shelf.
