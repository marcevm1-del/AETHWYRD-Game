# Letters Left in Umbravel

*Rafi Mori of Umbravel*

**Shelf:** Letter book  
**Region:** Umbravel  
**In-world date:** 1013 SC  
**Status:** Draft. Player-facing. Not Canon until reviewed. Written as what a person in Aethwyrd could know.
## Who kept these

Rafi Mori of Umbravel wrote from Umbravel. The houses are dark spires and borrowed lamp-light. The letters smell faintly of mushroom flour and bitter tea. They mention a street that ends where the map still continues the way you mention a cough in the next room: not the main sentence, never absent.

## Letter 1 — Maelsday, to a sibling

Umbravel, in the year this copy claims.

I arrived. The bed is clean and the lock is a suggestion. Send the blue thread, not money.

The rest of the page is ordinary: mushroom flour and bitter tea, a price, a name spelled two ways. Morrowyn, tended by people who disagree about her. Both saw the same hour. They were standing on different banks.

— Rafi Mori of Umbravel

## Letter 2 — Thorsday, to a teacher

Umbravel, in the year this copy claims.

Your lesson about measuring twice survived the road. Your lesson about people did not, and I am rewriting it.

## Letter 3 — Sylvasday, to the guild clerk

Umbravel, in the year this copy claims.
