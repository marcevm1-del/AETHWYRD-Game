## How this copy is used

I set this down at Hollow Sea boat-towns because I had walked the route twice and the second time the first notes looked vain. The copper nail on the table is not a relic. It is how this copy stayed honest.

Idris Adeyemi of Hollow Sea keeps this book in the kitchen at Hollow Sea boat-towns, where the houses are black water under a stone sky. The everyday pot already knows kelp-cake and lamp-oil tea. What follows is the version you can cook when a tide with no moon to explain it has made the market short, and when you still mean to sit down.

A note on faith, because someone always asks: Morrowyn's lantern, hooded. None of these dishes require a blessing to be edible. A blessing is for the table, after the salt is in.

Prices move. A copper is still the coin of a small bite, and a silver is still a bed. If a stall quotes you a mithril piece for supper, you are in the wrong story or the wrong street.

## Core-Lamp Custard, Carefully

*As cooked when Hollow Sea boat-towns is being ordinary.*

**You need**

- milk
- egg
- a spoon of sugar or honey
- no core-shard in the bowl

Heat the milk. Temper the egg. Bake it in a water dish. The lamp may warm the room. It does not go in the custard. Anyone who tells you otherwise is selling shards.
