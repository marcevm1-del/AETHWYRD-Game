## How this copy is used

I set this down at Hollow Sea boat-towns because the shelf copy had been edited by someone with a tidy mind and a bad memory. The wooden spoon on the table is not a relic. It is how this copy stayed honest.

Safiya Costa of Hollow Sea keeps this book in the kitchen at Hollow Sea boat-towns, where the houses are black water under a stone sky. The everyday pot already knows kelp-cake and lamp-oil tea. What follows is the version you can cook when a tide with no moon to explain it has made the market short, and when you still mean to sit down.

A note on faith, because someone always asks: Morrowyn's lantern, hooded. None of these dishes require a blessing to be edible. A blessing is for the table, after the salt is in.

Prices move. A copper is still the coin of a small bite, and a silver is still a bed. If a stall quotes you a mithril piece for supper, you are in the wrong story or the wrong street.

## Spray Trout

*As cooked when Hollow Sea boat-towns is being ordinary.*

**You need**

- a trout
- flatbread
- pine honey
- salt

Gut the trout. Salt it. Cook it until the eye goes white. Honey on the bread, not on the fish, unless you are alone and will not be judged.
