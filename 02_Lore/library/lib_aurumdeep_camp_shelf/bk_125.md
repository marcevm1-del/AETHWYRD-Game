# Boots Toward Cinderhole

*Amina Novak of Hollow Sea*

**Shelf:** Road book  
**Region:** Hollow Sea  
**In-world date:** 905 SC  
**Status:** Draft. Player-facing. Not Canon until reviewed. Written as what a person in Aethwyrd could know.
## How to use this notebook

I walked out of Whisperwood with a dry pair of socks and a worse map than this one. The country begins as black water under a stone sky. Inns will feed you kelp-cake and lamp-oil tea if you pay like a person and not like a raid.

Trouble on the posters: a tide with no moon to explain it. A siege bell is a local fact. It is not your invitation to become the story of the town. Help if you are asked and able. Do not invent a command.

Where people put their hands before a gate: Morrowyn's lantern, hooded.

A copper buys a bite. Silver buys a bed. Write the price you actually paid in the margin so the next reader is not braver than the menu.

## Solenday — the border track, Whisperwood to Sylvaran (15.5 km)

Leave while the lamps are still a choice. the border track, Whisperwood to Sylvaran (15.5 km) is walkable on a fair day. Count river crossings if the note gives them: this leg records no numbered count.

Stop at the first inn that will write your name without a speech. Regulars are made by returning, not by spending like a lord. If the room is dear, the common table still counts as rest, and your pack stays in your sight.

What I got wrong the first time: I trusted a straight pencil line. Neither is lying. Each left out the part that made their own choice look worse. Ask the person who mends the road, not only the person who sells the view.

Night note. Monster hours are not a moral. They are a clock. Be inside a wall, a yurt ring, a longhouse, or a lit camp with a watch you trust. A core-lamp in a window means someone is awake. It does not mean you are welcome to decide their defence.
