# Songs Heard in Hollow Sea boat-towns

*Amira Diallo of Hollow Sea*

**Shelf:** Ballad sheet  
**Region:** Hollow Sea  
**In-world date:** 946 SC  
**Status:** Draft. Player-facing. Not Canon until reviewed. Written as what a person in Aethwyrd could know.
## Sung at the table

Collected at Hollow Sea boat-towns. Change the name of the bridge if your bridge is the one that is out. A song that cannot survive a correction is a poster.

## Copper for a Bite

Copper for a bite and silver for a bed,
Gold is for the armor, platinum for the shed,

Pass the bread to the person who is standing in the wet.
