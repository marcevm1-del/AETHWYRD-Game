## The charter, in a human voice

I set this down at Thimblefolk Mushroom Town because the shelf copy had been edited by someone with a tidy mind and a bad memory. The folded tide-table on the table is not a relic. It is how this copy stayed honest.

At Thimblefolk Mushroom Town the work of the day sits inside toadstool eaves and moss steps. People eat mushroom stew and last year's honey and they talk about spores that sweeten a closed room the way coastal towns talk about weather: often, and with corrections.

This chapter listens by way of the Wanderer's Charter: no birth, race, or crown between a soul and its rising. A speaker at Thimblefolk Mushroom Town says one thing. A speaker at Mossdeep says the part that was left on the road between them. The official line is shorter. The household line feeds more people.

## Words, learned in a room

At Thimblefolk Mushroom Town you will hear 'card-burner' for someone who died at F, 'goblin-chewed' for a bad beating, 'silver-sick' for a C-rank who has stalled, and 'a brightlamp' for someone whose growth frightens the table. The nickname is not always a compliment. Do not use the death-words to be colorful. The Hall of Cards is a wall of people.
