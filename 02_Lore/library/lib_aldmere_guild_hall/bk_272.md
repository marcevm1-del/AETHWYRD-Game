## The charter, in a human voice

I set this down at Moraveil goblin village because the shrine keeper and the miller had stopped speaking, and the road still needed both. The copper nail on the table is not a relic. It is how this copy stayed honest.

## Words, learned in a room

At Moraveil market town you will hear 'card-burner' for someone who died at F, 'goblin-chewed' for a bad beating, 'silver-sick' for a C-rank who has stalled, and 'a brightlamp' for someone whose growth frightens the table. The nickname is not always a compliment. Do not use the death-words to be colorful. The Hall of Cards is a wall of people.
