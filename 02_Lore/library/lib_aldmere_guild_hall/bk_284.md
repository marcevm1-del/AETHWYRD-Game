# Weapons at the Door
*Marta Quillfeather, who keeps the weapons-rack of the Friendly Goblin and has opinions about each blade*

**Shelf:** Guild manual  
**Region:** Moraveil  
**In-world date:** 1019 SC  
**Status:** Draft. Player-facing. Not Canon until reviewed. Written as what a person in Aethwyrd could know.

I keep the rack. It is by the door, under the lantern, and it has fourteen pegs, which is thirteen more than any inn really needs, and just enough for a Guild party on a rainy night.

## The rule

At the Friendly Goblin, a human who is polite and keeps their weapons at the door is welcome. That is the whole rule. It is posted in both languages over the rack, in a large, kind hand.

## How it works

You arrive. You put your sword on a peg. I give you a tag. You go in and have supper. When you leave, you hand me the tag and I hand you the sword. In between, I count the pegs, and I do not touch anything.

## What I have heard

"You do not trust us."

"I trust you. I do not trust the third cup."

"What if I need it?"

"For what? There is a stew."

## What I have seen

A veteran, an old hand, with a scar from brow to jaw, who put down a pair of daggers and a short sword and then, after a moment, a tiny knife from his boot. He looked at me. I looked at him. He sighed, and unfastened a second tiny knife from his sleeve.

"There," he said. "That is all."

"That is not all," I said.

He unstrapped a throwing star from his hair.

## A note for the Guild

The rule is not about danger. It is about manners. A table where everyone is armed is a table where everyone is a little braced, and a table where everyone is a little braced is not a table. It is a negotiation.

## For a visitor

If you do not have anything to leave, say so. I will give you a peg anyway. It is the polite thing, and it is also a very good way to find out who is lying.
