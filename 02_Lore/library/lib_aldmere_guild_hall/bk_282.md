# Lost, Stacked and Found
*Tobias Wren, clerk of the Guild's lost-and-found shelf*

**Shelf:** Guild manual  
**Region:** Great Labyrinth (Floors 1-40)  
**In-world date:** 1016 SC  
**Status:** Draft. Player-facing. Not Canon until reviewed. Written as what a person in Aethwyrd could know.

I have the smallest desk in the Guild and, I think, the most contented. A man who keeps the lost-and-found shelf is a man who spends his days giving things back.

## How it works

Everything you drop in the Great Labyrinth goes somewhere. On the third and fourth floors it goes to a kobold, who stacks it in a den we call the Salvage Warren. The kobolds do not steal, as such; they hoard, and they are not clever about the difference. What they stack comes back to us in two ways.

1. **Through the gate.** Sometimes a kobold shift drops a pile near the Chalk Landing and forgets it. These are the easy ones.
2. **Through a party.** A party that reaches the Scrap Hall takes what they recognise and hands the rest to me.

## Rules for the shelf

- **The loss slip is everything.** I can return a boot to a person who wrote down "left boot, brown, mended at the heel." I cannot return "a boot."
- **Two weeks, then the box.** After two weeks I put unclaimed goods in a box by the stair for anyone to take. I would rather a thing be used than shelved.
- **No receipts for things you picked up yourself.** If you took it from the stacks, it is yours until someone writes a loss slip, and then it is theirs. This is not unfair; it is just slow.

## What I have returned

A pair of spectacles. A wooden spoon. Three swords, two of them the same sword. A brass key that opened nothing I could find. A letter, unsealed, which I read aloud to a man who did not know how and cried for a minute. I will not say what it said.

## For the clerk who follows me

Do not be proud of your shelf. Be proud of the empty days, when somebody has come in and taken their things and gone home. Solenne keeps the oath: she is the god of those who say "I will give it back" and then do.

Sylvara has the beasts; the kobolds are not her business. They are mine.
