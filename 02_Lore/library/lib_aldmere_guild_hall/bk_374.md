## A plain note on bondage

In Aldmere, slavery is outlawed, and has been since the time of the second Hero. That sentence is the law of the kingdom. It is not the whole map. Border baronies and the Shadow Syndicate still buy and sell people, and beastkin are the ones the posters pretend not to count. If you are writing a pretty history in which this is already over, you are writing for someone who benefits from the pretty version.

A person who escapes is not a plot. They are hungry, and they may not owe you their story. Food, a door, and a message to someone they trust are help. Questions can wait. The Guild’s charter says no birth, race, or crown stands between a soul and its rising. Hold the charter up to the street you are actually standing in.
