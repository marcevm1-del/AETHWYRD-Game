## Words, learned in a room

At Thimblefolk Mushroom Town you will hear 'card-burner' for someone who died at F, 'goblin-chewed' for a bad beating, 'silver-sick' for a C-rank who has stalled, and 'a brightlamp' for someone whose growth frightens the table. The nickname is not always a compliment. Do not use the death-words to be colorful. The Hall of Cards is a wall of people.
