# The Big-Folk Desk: Notes for the Newly Appointed
*Hazel Tuft, clerk of the Guild's small desk in Thimblefolk Mushroom Town*

**Shelf:** Guild manual  
**Region:** Mossdeep  
**In-world date:** 1017 SC  
**Status:** Draft. Player-facing. Not Canon until reviewed. Written as what a person in Aethwyrd could know.

Congratulations. You have been appointed to the only Guild desk in Aethwyrd that is lower than the customer.

## What the desk is

A toadstool counter, a stool and a stack of forms. The forms are the usual ones, but each has been rewritten, by hand, at a scale the Thimblefolk can read. The big folk cannot read them. This is not a mistake.

## How to receive a big folk

Big folk arrive at the valley mouth and are brought in by a beetle-rider, who is polite to them in the way one is polite to a friendly bear. They are shown to a chair that has been made for them: a bench of woven reed at the edge of the town, where they can sit without breaking anything.

You must go to them. They cannot come to you. This is the first rule, and the hardest.

## Forms

1. **The entry slip.** One for each big folk, and one for each beetle they bring. We do not need to know the weight.
2. **The courtesy slip.** The big folk signs that they will not step on the town. We have a stamp for it. The stamp is a very small footprint.
3. **The return slip.** Handed in on leaving. If a big folk has taken a honey-jar without paying, it is noted.

## Courtesy

Never say "little." Never say "tiny." Say "close" or "near" or "within reach." The big folk do not know they are large, and it is not our place to tell them. It is our place to make them comfortable.

## When the big folk ask for help

There is a lively debate in the town over whether the big folk should be asked. I recommend that you not take sides. When one asks what the Guild can do, say what the Guild can do. When the town asks what the Guild thinks, say that the Guild thinks it is very glad to be here.

## A last note

There is a thing called the Silk Canopy, over the ferns, that is dim and quiet. It is not for big folk or small. If someone asks to be taken, say the desk is closed.
