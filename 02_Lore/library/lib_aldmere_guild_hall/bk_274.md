## The charter, in a human voice

I set this down at ice-fishing villages because the shrine keeper and the miller had stopped speaking, and the road still needed both. The folded tide-table on the table is not a relic. It is how this copy stayed honest.

This chapter listens by way of the Wanderer's Charter: no birth, race, or crown between a soul and its rising. A speaker at ice-fishing villages says one thing. A speaker at Whitewater Reach says the part that was left on the road between them. The official line is shorter. The household line feeds more people.

## Words, learned in a room

At ice-fishing villages you will hear 'card-burner' for someone who died at F, 'goblin-chewed' for a bad beating, 'silver-sick' for a C-rank who has stalled, and 'a brightlamp' for someone whose growth frightens the table. The nickname is not always a compliment. Do not use the death-words to be colorful. The Hall of Cards is a wall of people.
