## How this copy is used

I set this down at Bastion outer ring because I had walked the route twice and the second time the first notes looked vain. The blue lamp-glass on the table is not a relic. It is how this copy stayed honest.

Kwame Okafor of Bastion keeps this book in the kitchen at Bastion outer ring, where the houses are open steppe and a low ring wall. The everyday pot already knows horse-meat soup and black tea. What follows is the version you can cook when wind with nowhere to hide has made the market short, and when you still mean to sit down.

A note on faith, because someone always asks: Kaelthas, and a fire that is everybody's job. None of these dishes require a blessing to be edible. A blessing is for the table, after the salt is in.

Prices move. A copper is still the coin of a small bite, and a silver is still a bed. If a stall quotes you a mithril piece for supper, you are in the wrong story or the wrong street.

## Beacon Fry

*As cooked when Bastion outer ring is being ordinary.*

**You need**

- small fish
- salt
- a lemon or a sour herb
- bread

Dry the fish. Fry it hot. Salt after, not before, or it weeps and the pan goes bitter.
