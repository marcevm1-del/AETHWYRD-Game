## How this copy is used

I set this down at Bastion outer ring because the Guild card was right about the danger and wrong about the cause. The dry orange peel on the table is not a relic. It is how this copy stayed honest.

Tamsin Haddad of Bastion keeps this book in the kitchen at Bastion outer ring, where the houses are open steppe and a low ring wall. The everyday pot already knows horse-meat soup and black tea. What follows is the version you can cook when wind with nowhere to hide has made the market short, and when you still mean to sit down.

A note on faith, because someone always asks: Kaelthas, and a fire that is everybody's job. None of these dishes require a blessing to be edible. A blessing is for the table, after the salt is in.

Prices move. A copper is still the coin of a small bite, and a silver is still a bed. If a stall quotes you a mithril piece for supper, you are in the wrong story or the wrong street.

## Shell-Lane Clams

*As cooked when Bastion outer ring is being ordinary.*

**You need**

- clams
- greens
- rice
- a splash of the cooking liquor

Steam the clams until they open. Discard the ones that do not. Greens in the liquor. Rice in a bowl beside.
