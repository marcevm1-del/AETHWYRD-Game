## How this copy is used

I set this down at Bastion outer ring because the shrine keeper and the miller had stopped speaking, and the road still needed both. The tea-stained map on the table is not a relic. It is how this copy stayed honest.

Soren Ndlovu of Bastion keeps this book in the kitchen at Bastion outer ring, where the houses are open steppe and a low ring wall. The everyday pot already knows horse-meat soup and black tea. What follows is the version you can cook when wind with nowhere to hide has made the market short, and when you still mean to sit down.

A note on faith, because someone always asks: Kaelthas, and a fire that is everybody's job. None of these dishes require a blessing to be edible. A blessing is for the table, after the salt is in.

Prices move. A copper is still the coin of a small bite, and a silver is still a bed. If a stall quotes you a mithril piece for supper, you are in the wrong story or the wrong street.

## Barn Porridge

*As cooked when Bastion outer ring is being ordinary.*

**You need**

- oats or millet
- water or milk
- a thread of honey
- salt

Start it cold. Stir until it sighs. Honey is for children and for adults who have already done the morning's work.
