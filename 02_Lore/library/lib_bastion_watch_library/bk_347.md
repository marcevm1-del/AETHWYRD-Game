# Letters Left in Nox Dominion

*Ren Farid of Nox Dominion*

**Shelf:** Letter book  
**Region:** Nox Dominion  
**In-world date:** 1007 SC  
**Status:** Draft. Player-facing. Not Canon until reviewed. Written as what a person in Aethwyrd could know.
## Who kept these

Ren Farid of Nox Dominion wrote from Nox Dominion. The houses are black glass and a red horizon. The letters smell faintly of travelers' rations and bitter greens. They mention the Overlord's country, spoken of quietly the way you mention a cough in the next room: not the main sentence, never absent.

## Letter 1 — Maelsday, to a sibling

Nox Dominion, in the year this copy claims.

I arrived. The bed is clean and the lock is a suggestion. Send the blue thread, not money.

The rest of the page is ordinary: travelers' rations and bitter greens, a price, a name spelled two ways. almost no open shrine, and a private coin to Vex. Neither is lying. Each left out the part that made their own choice look worse.

— Ren Farid of Nox Dominion
