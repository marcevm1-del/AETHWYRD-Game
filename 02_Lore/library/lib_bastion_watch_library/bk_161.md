## The short version on the board

I set this down at Bastion outer ring because the shrine keeper and the miller had stopped speaking, and the road still needed both. The guild chalk stub on the table is not a relic. It is how this copy stayed honest.

At Bastion outer ring the work of the day sits inside open steppe and a low ring wall. People eat horse-meat soup and black tea and they talk about wind with nowhere to hide the way coastal towns talk about weather: often, and with corrections.

This chapter listens by way of a reeve's paper. A speaker at Bastion outer ring says one thing. A speaker at Bastion says the part that was left on the road between them. One of them is remembering a person. The other is remembering a rule.

The shrine mark nearby belongs to Morrowyn: a lantern with a blue flame, kept for the dead, and the living who remember them. That is public devotion, the kind you can see from the street. Kaelthas, and a fire that is everybody's job. I am not writing what a god intends. I am writing what the keeper swept this morning.
