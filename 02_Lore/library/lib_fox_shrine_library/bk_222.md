# Bench Notes from Fox Shrine Isle

*Imani Flint of Kitsune Isles*

**Shelf:** Craft notes  
**Region:** Kitsune Isles  
**In-world date:** 1002 SC  
**Status:** Draft. Player-facing. Not Canon until reviewed. Written as what a person in Aethwyrd could know.
## The bench

Copied at spirit inns from a maker who was willing to be watched. Nothing here makes you a master. Mastery is someone else’s trust, repeated.

## Yurt Felt Patch

**For:** A patch that keeps weather out and smoke going up.

Match the felt. Stitch small. Leave the smoke hole alone unless you are the person who will sleep under your own cleverness.

Stop when the work is true. Continuing in order to feel like a craftsperson is how a good nail becomes a bent one.

## Rune of Fastening

**For:** A mark that holds a lid, a strap, or a door, and nothing romantic.

Copy the circle from a working tool, not from memory and pride. A rune repeats. If you teach it a sloppy lesson, it will be loyal to the sloppiness. It will not guard your soul, pay your tab, or choose your friends.

Stop when the work is true. Continuing in order to feel like a craftsperson is how a good nail becomes a bent one.

## Dye from a Known Plant

**For:** Color you can name, on cloth you can wash.

If you cannot name the plant, you are not dyeing. You are gambling with someone’s skin. Mordant as the dyer who lives here does it. Write the plant’s name on the hem in chalk before you brag.

Stop when the work is true. Continuing in order to feel like a craftsperson is how a good nail becomes a bent one.

## Smoke Rack

**For:** Fish that keeps, and does not taste of panic.

Salt, air, then smoke that is thin and cool enough to walk through. Thick smoke is how you make a bitter brick. Label the rack with the day.

Stop when the work is true. Continuing in order to feel like a craftsperson is how a good nail becomes a bent one.

## Terrace Wall

**For:** A wall that holds soil and still lets water down.

Stones with faces that touch. A weep-gap every few paces. Build it a little in from the fall, so the mountain can be the mountain and the potatoes can stay.

Stop when the work is true. Continuing in order to feel like a craftsperson is how a good nail becomes a bent one.

## Stair Nail

**For:** The nail that keeps a walked stair from becoming a rumor.

On a long stair, the nail is the ethics. Check the one at hand height and the one at the turn. Do not offer to carry a person up a height they can walk. Walking is the route.

Stop when the work is true. Continuing in order to feel like a craftsperson is how a good nail becomes a bent one.
