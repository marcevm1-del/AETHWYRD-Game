# Goods Through Naming Voyage harbor

*Noor Park of True-Name Isles*

**Shelf:** Trade notes  
**Region:** True-Name Isles  
**In-world date:** 985 SC  
**Status:** Draft. Player-facing. Not Canon until reviewed. Written as what a person in Aethwyrd could know.
## The stall

At Naming Voyage harbor the goods sit against salt cedar and named coves. People are buying fish stew and coconut and talking about a name given carefully, or not at all. Faith is present as habit: Maelis, who is asked before a child is named. None of that is a price list. It is why the price list is tense.

The wallet of this world counts copper, silver, gold, platinum, and mithril, each a hundred of the one before. Most of a life is lived in the first two.

## Grain

Grain moves because it keeps, and it makes other arguments possible.

In True-Name Isles, ask who carried it, not only who owns the stall. A thing can arrive by marriage, refuge, a contract, or a raid, and those are not the same story. Neither is lying. Each left out the part that made their own choice look worse.
