## How this copy is used

I set this down at The Maw because the shelf copy had been edited by someone with a tidy mind and a bad memory. The reed pen on the table is not a relic. It is how this copy stayed honest.

Bramble Sato of The Maw keeps this book in the kitchen at The Maw, where the houses are a throat of stone and warm wind. The everyday pot already knows cave-fungus and melted tallow. What follows is the version you can cook when a depth the Guild does not post on a beginner's board has made the market short, and when you still mean to sit down.

A note on faith, because someone always asks: Morrowyn, if anyone still has a lantern. None of these dishes require a blessing to be edible. A blessing is for the table, after the salt is in.

Prices move. A copper is still the coin of a small bite, and a silver is still a bed. If a stall quotes you a mithril piece for supper, you are in the wrong story or the wrong street.

## Mayonnaise, and the Argument

*As cooked when The Maw is being ordinary.*

**You need**

- an egg yolk
- oil
- a little vinegar
- salt

Whisk the yolk. Add oil by drops until it looks like a mistake, then in a thread. Vinegar and salt. Serve it with fried fish or be prepared to defend your character.

## Peat Eel with Berries

*As cooked when The Maw is being ordinary.*

**You need**

- two small eels
- bog-berries
- rye crust
- a pinch of pepper

Coil the eel in a low pan. Crush the berries over it. Set the crust on top and bake at the edge of the fire until the crust sounds hollow.

## Spray Trout

*As cooked when The Maw is being ordinary.*

**You need**

- a trout
- flatbread
- pine honey
- salt

Gut the trout. Salt it. Cook it until the eye goes white. Honey on the bread, not on the fish, unless you are alone and will not be judged.
