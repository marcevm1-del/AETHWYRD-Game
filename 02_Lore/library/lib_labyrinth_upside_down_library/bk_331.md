## The stall

At Great Labyrinth (Floors 91-120) the goods sit against camp-lamps and a stair you must find yourself. People are buying food counted in days and talking about rumors of a deeper stair, and no map in this book. Faith is present as habit: the lantern you carried in. None of that is a price list. It is why the price list is tense.

The wallet of this world counts copper, silver, gold, platinum, and mithril, each a hundred of the one before. Most of a life is lived in the first two.
