## How this copy is used

I set this down at The Maw because a child asked a straight question and the adults answered with a proverb. The wooden spoon on the table is not a relic. It is how this copy stayed honest.

Maren Okeke of The Maw keeps this book in the kitchen at The Maw, where the houses are a throat of stone and warm wind. The everyday pot already knows cave-fungus and melted tallow. What follows is the version you can cook when a depth the Guild does not post on a beginner's board has made the market short, and when you still mean to sit down.

A note on faith, because someone always asks: Morrowyn, if anyone still has a lantern. None of these dishes require a blessing to be edible. A blessing is for the table, after the salt is in.

Prices move. A copper is still the coin of a small bite, and a silver is still a bed. If a stall quotes you a mithril piece for supper, you are in the wrong story or the wrong street.

## Ink-Stone Dumplings

*As cooked when The Maw is being ordinary.*

**You need**

- dumpling wrappers
- greens
- a little meat or mushroom
- tea

Fill the wrappers less than you want to. Boil them until they float and then a little longer. Tea after, not with, if you care about the broth.

## Adobe Noon Bread

*As cooked when The Maw is being ordinary.*

**You need**

- flour
- a little oil
- dates
- salt

Make a soft dough. Bake it on a hot plate. Split it and put the dates in while it is too hot for your fingers. That is the point.

## Gold-Road Millet

*As cooked when The Maw is being ordinary.*

**You need**

- millet
- a spice you can name
- stew meat or squash
- salt

Steam the millet. Stew the rest until it gives. Serve them touching, not stirred, so a guest can refuse the meat and still be fed.

## Giant-Step Beans

*As cooked when The Maw is being ordinary.*

**You need**

- beans
- smoked chili
- plantain
- water

Soak the beans. Cook them with the chili until the broth is red-brown. Fry the plantain to the side.

*As cooked when The Maw is being ordinary.*

**You need**

- onions
- water or a bone broth
- grit-bread
- salt

Cook the onions until they go sweet and then a shade darker. Add water. The bread dips. Do not serve it grey.

- oil

## Mayonnaise, and the Argument

*As cooked when The Maw is being ordinary.*

**You need**

- an egg yolk
- oil
- a little vinegar
- salt

Whisk the yolk. Add oil by drops until it looks like a mistake, then in a thread. Vinegar and salt. Serve it with fried fish or be prepared to defend your character.
