## How this copy is used

I set this down at The Maw because the shrine keeper and the miller had stopped speaking, and the road still needed both. The reed pen on the table is not a relic. It is how this copy stayed honest.

Amina Okeke of The Maw keeps this book in the kitchen at The Maw, where the houses are a throat of stone and warm wind. The everyday pot already knows cave-fungus and melted tallow. What follows is the version you can cook when a depth the Guild does not post on a beginner's board has made the market short, and when you still mean to sit down.

A note on faith, because someone always asks: Morrowyn, if anyone still has a lantern. None of these dishes require a blessing to be edible. A blessing is for the table, after the salt is in.

Prices move. A copper is still the coin of a small bite, and a silver is still a bed. If a stall quotes you a mithril piece for supper, you are in the wrong story or the wrong street.

## Spray Trout

*As cooked when The Maw is being ordinary.*

**You need**

- a trout
- flatbread
- pine honey
- salt

Gut the trout. Salt it. Cook it until the eye goes white. Honey on the bread, not on the fish, unless you are alone and will not be judged.
