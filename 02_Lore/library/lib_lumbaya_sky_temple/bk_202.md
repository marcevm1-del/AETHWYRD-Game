*Edda Sato of Ashira Vale*

## The bench

Copied at Ashira from a maker who was willing to be watched. Nothing here makes you a master. Mastery is someone else’s trust, repeated.

## Core-Lamp Mend

**For:** A street lamp that burns a core without wasting it.

Seat the core dry. Clean the glass. Do not crack the housing to get a brighter hour tonight if tomorrow’s street goes dark. Brightness is not the same as care.

Stop when the work is true. Continuing in order to feel like a craftsperson is how a good nail becomes a bent one.

## Canal Lock Grease

**For:** A lock that opens for a boat and not for a speech.

Grease the gates on the day the schedule already names. If you wait for a poet, the poet will be on the boat that is stuck.

## Rune of Fastening

**For:** A mark that holds a lid, a strap, or a door, and nothing romantic.

Copy the circle from a working tool, not from memory and pride. A rune repeats. If you teach it a sloppy lesson, it will be loyal to the sloppiness. It will not guard your soul, pay your tab, or choose your friends.

## Dye from a Known Plant

**For:** Color you can name, on cloth you can wash.

If you cannot name the plant, you are not dyeing. You are gambling with someone’s skin. Mordant as the dyer who lives here does it. Write the plant’s name on the hem in chalk before you brag.

## Smoke Rack

**For:** Fish that keeps, and does not taste of panic.

Salt, air, then smoke that is thin and cool enough to walk through. Thick smoke is how you make a bitter brick. Label the rack with the day.
