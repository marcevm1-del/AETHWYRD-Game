# Neighbors Near Lumbaya temple villages

*Dugan Park of Lumbaya*

**Shelf:** Field guide  
**Region:** Lumbaya  
**In-world date:** 1018 SC  
**Status:** Draft. Player-facing. Not Canon until reviewed. Written as what a person in Aethwyrd could know.
## Before the entries

I set this down at Lumbaya temple villages because I had walked the route twice and the second time the first notes looked vain. The spare button on the table is not a relic. It is how this copy stayed honest.

This guide is for walking country: vine over old giant-stone. You will smell plantain, beans, and smoked chili in the settlements and you will be told about a stair that was not built for human legs. The entries below are neighbors, not trophies.

Shrines out here lean toward this habit: stories of builders, told as stories. A prayer is not a ward you can spend like a potion. Learn the creature anyway.

If a poster and a hunter disagree, copy both and date them. The halls of the Labyrinth reset. Ordinary hills do not, and a path can be wrong twice.

## Glowfin Angler

Around Lumbaya temple villages, people file the Glowfin Angler near guild rank C. A rank is a licence hint. It is not a measurement of your courage, and it goes stale if the ground has changed.

It keeps a patch and announces it. Stepping through the middle is the mistake.

Folk talk says its nature runs toward Dark, and that Light unsettles it. Treat that as a rumor you can test from farther away than its reach. If the test fails, you have learned something, provided you can still leave.

What the meat-stall will not put on a sign: do not take a young one to prove a point, do not block the line it uses to flee, and do not trust a core that is still warm as if it were a lamp. Cores light cities. They also belong to a creature that was here first. Both saw the same hour. They were standing on different banks.
