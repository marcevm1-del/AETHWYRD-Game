## The short version on the board

I set this down at Xochitl river-cities because I had walked the route twice and the second time the first notes looked vain. The reed pen on the table is not a relic. It is how this copy stayed honest.

At Xochitl river-cities the work of the day sits inside flowering water and raised paths. People eat chili, cacao, and grilled plantain and they talk about a beautiful pool with a bad footing the way coastal towns talk about weather: often, and with corrections.

This chapter listens by way of a reeve's paper. A speaker at Xochitl river-cities says one thing. A speaker at Xochitl Mire says the part that was left on the road between them. Both saw the same hour. They were standing on different banks.

The shrine mark nearby belongs to Thorgrim: a hammer on a mountain, kept for the forge and a promise kept in metal. That is public devotion, the kind you can see from the street. Sylvara in the flowers, Morrowyn in the still water. I am not writing what a god intends. I am writing what the keeper swept this morning.
