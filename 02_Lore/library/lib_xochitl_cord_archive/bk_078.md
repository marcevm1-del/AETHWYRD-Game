## How this copy is used

I set this down at Xochitl river-cities because the shrine keeper and the miller had stopped speaking, and the road still needed both. The copper nail on the table is not a relic. It is how this copy stayed honest.

Edda Sato of Xochitl Mire keeps this book in the kitchen at Xochitl river-cities, where the houses are flowering water and raised paths. The everyday pot already knows chili, cacao, and grilled plantain. What follows is the version you can cook when a beautiful pool with a bad footing has made the market short, and when you still mean to sit down.

A note on faith, because someone always asks: Sylvara in the flowers, Morrowyn in the still water. None of these dishes require a blessing to be edible. A blessing is for the table, after the salt is in.

Prices move. A copper is still the coin of a small bite, and a silver is still a bed. If a stall quotes you a mithril piece for supper, you are in the wrong story or the wrong street.

## Lamp-Gold Dried Fruit

*As cooked when Xochitl river-cities is being ordinary.*

**You need**

- dried fruit
- a canteen
- a heel of bread

This is a diver's meal. Soak the fruit until it bends. Eat it before the stair, not after, if your hands will be busy.

## Ash Onion Soup

*As cooked when Xochitl river-cities is being ordinary.*

**You need**

- onions
- water or a bone broth
- grit-bread
- salt

Cook the onions until they go sweet and then a shade darker. Add water. The bread dips. Do not serve it grey.

## Mayonnaise, and the Argument

*As cooked when Xochitl river-cities is being ordinary.*

**You need**

- an egg yolk
- oil
- a little vinegar
- salt

Whisk the yolk. Add oil by drops until it looks like a mistake, then in a thread. Vinegar and salt. Serve it with fried fish or be prepared to defend your character.

## Peat Eel with Berries

*As cooked when Xochitl river-cities is being ordinary.*

**You need**

- two small eels
- bog-berries
- rye crust
- a pinch of pepper

Coil the eel in a low pan. Crush the berries over it. Set the crust on top and bake at the edge of the fire until the crust sounds hollow.
