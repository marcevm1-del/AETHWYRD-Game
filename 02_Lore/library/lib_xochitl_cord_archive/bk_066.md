The everyday pot already knows chili, cacao, and grilled plantain. What follows is the version you can cook when a beautiful pool with a bad footing has made the market short, and when you still mean to sit down.

A note on faith, because someone always asks: Sylvara in the flowers, Morrowyn in the still water. None of these dishes require a blessing to be edible. A blessing is for the table, after the salt is in.

Prices move. A copper is still the coin of a small bite, and a silver is still a bed. If a stall quotes you a mithril piece for supper, you are in the wrong story or the wrong street.

## Millbrook Rabbit Pot

*As cooked when Xochitl river-cities is being ordinary.*

**You need**

- one horned rabbit, jointed
- an onion
- a heel of bread
- thyme if the hedge allows
- water and salt

Brown the joints in a little fat. Add onion. Cover with water and the bread so the broth goes thick. Keep it under a boil. Eat with the spoons you have.

## Yurt Smoke Meat

*As cooked when Xochitl river-cities is being ordinary.*

**You need**

- a strip of dried meat
- salted milk
- flat stone bread

Soften the meat in the milk over a low fire. Do not boil the milk. Fold it into the bread. This is a riding meal, not a feast.
