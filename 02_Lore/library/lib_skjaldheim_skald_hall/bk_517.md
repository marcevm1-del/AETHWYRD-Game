# Neighbors Near ice-fishing villages

*Bramble Okeke of Whitewater Reach*

**Shelf:** Field guide  
**Region:** Whitewater Reach  
**In-world date:** 1007 SC  
**Status:** Draft. Player-facing. Not Canon until reviewed. Written as what a person in Aethwyrd could know.
## Before the entries

I set this down at ice-fishing villages because the shelf copy had been edited by someone with a tidy mind and a bad memory. The dry orange peel on the table is not a relic. It is how this copy stayed honest.

This guide is for walking country: spray, rope bridges, and wet stone. You will smell trout, flatbread, and pine honey in the settlements and you will be told about a crossing that is safe until it is not. The entries below are neighbors, not trophies.

Shrines out here lean toward this habit: Kaelthas, because the river is louder than a priest. A prayer is not a ward you can spend like a potion. Learn the creature anyway.

If a poster and a hunter disagree, copy both and date them. The halls of the Labyrinth reset. Ordinary hills do not, and a path can be wrong twice.

## Abyssal Kraken Queen

Around ice-fishing villages, people file the Abyssal Kraken Queen near guild rank SSS. A rank is a licence hint. It is not a measurement of your courage, and it goes stale if the ground has changed.

The ground is the hunter. Look at the low places before the skyline.

Folk talk says its nature runs toward Void, and that Light unsettles it. Treat that as a rumor you can test from farther away than its reach. If the test fails, you have learned something, provided you can still leave.

What the meat-stall will not put on a sign: do not take a young one to prove a point, do not block the line it uses to flee, and do not trust a core that is still warm as if it were a lamp. Cores light cities. They also belong to a creature that was here first. Neither is lying. Each left out the part that made their own choice look worse.
