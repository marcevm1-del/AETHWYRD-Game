# Letters Left in Skjaldheim

*Ilyra Flint of Frostveil*

**Shelf:** Letter book  
**Region:** Frostveil  
**In-world date:** 1000 SC  
**Status:** Draft. Player-facing. Not Canon until reviewed. Written as what a person in Aethwyrd could know.
## Who kept these

Ilyra Flint of Frostveil wrote from Skjaldheim. The houses are turf longhouses and fjord wind. The letters smell faintly of smoked fish, sour cream, and rye. They mention ice that sounds like a door the way you mention a cough in the next room: not the main sentence, never absent.

## Letter 1 — Maelsday, to a sibling

Skjaldheim, in the year this copy claims.

I arrived. The bed is clean and the lock is a suggestion. Send the blue thread, not money.

The rest of the page is ordinary: smoked fish, sour cream, and rye, a price, a name spelled two ways. Kaelthas in the weather, Morrowyn for the lost. Both saw the same hour. They were standing on different banks.

— Ilyra Flint of Frostveil

## Letter 2 — Thorsday, to a teacher

Skjaldheim, in the year this copy claims.

Your lesson about measuring twice survived the road. Your lesson about people did not, and I am rewriting it.

The rest of the page is ordinary: smoked fish, sour cream, and rye, a price, a name spelled two ways. Kaelthas in the weather, Morrowyn for the lost. Both saw the same hour. They were standing on different banks.

— Ilyra Flint of Frostveil

## Letter 3 — Sylvasday, to the guild clerk

Skjaldheim, in the year this copy claims.

The poster is missing the part where the bridge is out. I am not asking for a refund. I am asking for a corrected board.

The rest of the page is ordinary: smoked fish, sour cream, and rye, a price, a name spelled two ways. Kaelthas in the weather, Morrowyn for the lost. Both saw the same hour. They were standing on different banks.

— Ilyra Flint of Frostveil

## Letter 4 — Kaelsday, to a parent

Skjaldheim, in the year this copy claims.

Do not come. I am well. I am also not ready to perform being well at your table. The next letter will be longer.

The rest of the page is ordinary: smoked fish, sour cream, and rye, a price, a name spelled two ways. Kaelthas in the weather, Morrowyn for the lost. The official line is shorter. The household line feeds more people.

— Ilyra Flint of Frostveil

## Letter 5 — Morrowsday, to a friend on another road

Skjaldheim, in the year this copy claims.

The innkeeper remembers you and pretends not to. Leave the spoon you stole the next time you pass. They are using a ladle and suffering.

The rest of the page is ordinary: smoked fish, sour cream, and rye, a price, a name spelled two ways. Kaelthas in the weather, Morrowyn for the lost. One of them is remembering a person. The other is remembering a rule.

— Ilyra Flint of Frostveil

## Letter 6 — Vexday, to nobody, never sent

Skjaldheim, in the year this copy claims.

If I do not come back, the knot-cord in the tin is the real tally. The paper tally is for the reeve.

The rest of the page is ordinary: smoked fish, sour cream, and rye, a price, a name spelled two ways. Kaelthas in the weather, Morrowyn for the lost. The official line is shorter. The household line feeds more people.

— Ilyra Flint of Frostveil
