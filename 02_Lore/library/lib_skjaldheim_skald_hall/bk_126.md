## The short version on the board

I set this down at Stiller spring-towns because the Guild card was right about the danger and wrong about the cause. The wooden spoon on the table is not a relic. It is how this copy stayed honest.

At Stiller spring-towns the work of the day sits inside low houses on flexible frames. People eat corn cakes and bean paste and they talk about a shake that the locals do not apologize for the way coastal towns talk about weather: often, and with corrections.

This chapter listens by way of a reeve's paper. A speaker at Stiller spring-towns says one thing. A speaker at Quaking Lands says the part that was left on the road between them. Neither is lying. Each left out the part that made their own choice look worse.

The shrine mark nearby belongs to Vex: a coin with two heads, kept for luck, roads, and the laugh you did not plan. That is public devotion, the kind you can see from the street. Thorgrim in the joints of the house. I am not writing what a god intends. I am writing what the keeper swept this morning.
