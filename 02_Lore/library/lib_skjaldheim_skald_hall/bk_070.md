## How this copy is used

I set this down at Stiller spring-towns because I had walked the route twice and the second time the first notes looked vain. The copper nail on the table is not a relic. It is how this copy stayed honest.

Maren Costa of Quaking Lands keeps this book in the kitchen at Stiller spring-towns, where the houses are low houses on flexible frames. The everyday pot already knows corn cakes and bean paste. What follows is the version you can cook when a shake that the locals do not apologize for has made the market short, and when you still mean to sit down.

A note on faith, because someone always asks: Thorgrim in the joints of the house. None of these dishes require a blessing to be edible. A blessing is for the table, after the salt is in.

Prices move. A copper is still the coin of a small bite, and a silver is still a bed. If a stall quotes you a mithril piece for supper, you are in the wrong story or the wrong street.

## Sluice Garden Plate

*As cooked when Stiller spring-towns is being ordinary.*

**You need**

- chickpeas
- mint
- oil
- a garden herb

Cook the chickpeas until they crush under a spoon. Oil and mint. This is what you eat when the water argument is still going on outside.
