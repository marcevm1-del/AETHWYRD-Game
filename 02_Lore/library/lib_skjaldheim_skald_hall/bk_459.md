# The Latch at Stiller spring-towns

*Haru Okafor of Quaking Lands*

**Shelf:** A short play  
**Region:** Quaking Lands  
**In-world date:** 999 SC  
**Status:** Draft. Player-facing. Not Canon until reviewed. Written as what a person in Aethwyrd could know.
## Persons

- THE KEEPER, of a shrine or an inn. Tired, precise.
- THE WALKER, new on this road. Trying not to perform bravery.
- THE NEIGHBOR, who was here last year and will be here next year.

The scene is Stiller spring-towns. low houses on flexible frames. A pot of corn cakes and bean paste is on somewhere just off the stage. The trouble outside is a shake that the locals do not apologize for, and it stays outside until someone opens the door.

## Act I

KEEPER: If you are here for a legend, the legend eats at the other table.

WALKER: I am here because the board was wrong about the bridge.

NEIGHBOR: The board is often wrong. The river is consistent. Sit down before you correct the country.

KEEPER: There is bread. There is a price. There is also a blanket if your pride will move over.

WALKER: And a blessing? People said Thorgrim in the joints of the house.

KEEPER: People say a great many useful things and then go home. You may wash your hands. That is the rite I am offering.

## Act II

NEIGHBOR: Last year a party came through and slew the trouble on the poster and left the hole in the roof. We are still dry when it does not rain from that corner.

WALKER: I did not come to leave you a hole.

KEEPER: Then ask what is needed before you ask what is glorious. Glorious is a long word. Nails are short.

NEIGHBOR: Stay for the bell or go before it. Do not stand in the doorway making a speech. Doorways are for people who have decided.

WALKER: I will take the nails as far as the bridge. I will not take the town’s voice with me.

KEEPER: Good. Eat first. Heroes who will not eat become other people’s problem, and I have a pot to watch.

*(They eat. The door stays latched until the Walker has finished the bowl. Lights down on the lamp, not on a moral.)*

## A note for the company

Play it warm. Nobody is a fool for wanting a safe night. If you add a song, it should be one the Neighbor already knows.
