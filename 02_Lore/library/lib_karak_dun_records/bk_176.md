# A Smith's Hearth-Stone
*Dagny Emberhand, smith of the Third Hall, writing the rules of her house for her apprentice*

**Shelf:** Household book  
**Region:** Iron Hold  
**In-world date:** 1008 SC  
**Status:** Draft. Player-facing. Not Canon until reviewed. Written as what a person in Aethwyrd could know.

Every smith keeps a hearth-stone from their first forge. Lose it, and you have lost your luck, your craft and, by some accounts, your name. I do not entirely believe this. I also do not take chances.

## The stone

Mine is a fist of grey granite, a little scorched on one side. It sits on the shelf above the hearth. I touch it with my left hand every morning before I touch the bellows, and I tell it what I mean to make. It is the only person I know who has never contradicted me.

## The house

A smith's house has three rooms. The forge is the first. The kitchen is the second. The third is the room where the apprentice sleeps, and it is the one I think of most.

An apprentice sleeps near the fire, on a bench, with a blanket that smells of old iron. This is the custom. By the end of the first winter, they sleep through the hammer. By the end of the third, they cannot sleep without it. That is how you know a smith has been made.

## Meals

We eat at the long table, all together, and we eat what there is. Bread. Stew. Roasted roots. Dark ale, in the evening, one cup for the apprentice and as many as the rest of us can justify.

No one speaks of work at the table. It is the rule. It is also the rule that everyone speaks of it anyway, and that the person who notices first pays for the next round.

## Safety

- Tie your hair.
- Wear the apron.
- Never reach across a hot piece.
- Do not leave a hammer on the anvil. It is not a courtesy; it is a good way to lose a toe.

## What I tell the apprentice

A forge is not a place for hurry. If you are in a hurry, put the work down and go for a walk. The metal will be there when you come back, and so, I hope, will you.

Thorgrim, whose day is Thorsday, is the god of the promise made in iron. I have made a few. The stone remembers them.
