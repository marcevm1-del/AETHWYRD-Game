## How this copy is used

I set this down at Karak Dûn because the Guild card was right about the danger and wrong about the cause. The dry orange peel on the table is not a relic. It is how this copy stayed honest.

Idris Okafor of Iron Hold keeps this book in the kitchen at Karak Dûn, where the houses are basalt halls and anvil-heat. The everyday pot already knows stout, stone-bread, and roast roots. What follows is the version you can cook when a vow hammered where a stranger can hear it has made the market short, and when you still mean to sit down.

A note on faith, because someone always asks: Thorgrim, whose mark is on every new tool. None of these dishes require a blessing to be edible. A blessing is for the table, after the salt is in.

Prices move. A copper is still the coin of a small bite, and a silver is still a bed. If a stall quotes you a mithril piece for supper, you are in the wrong story or the wrong street.

## Guild-Round Punch, Nonalcoholic Pot

*As cooked when Karak Dûn is being ordinary.*

**You need**

- tea
- citrus peel
- honey
- water

The round you buy the hall is often beer. This pot is for the watcher, the child, and the person who is about to walk. Brew it strong.
