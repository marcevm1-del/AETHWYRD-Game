# The Brewer's House and the Three-Hundred-Year Cup
*Hagen Stoutbarrel, brewer, on the household of a man who has, in a manner of speaking, competed*

**Shelf:** Household book  
**Region:** Iron Hold  
**In-world date:** 1019 SC  
**Status:** Draft. Player-facing. Not Canon until reviewed. Written as what a person in Aethwyrd could know.

A brewer's house smells of malt, and of the particular sort of damp that comes from a cellar. I am fond of both.

## The cellar

The house is built round it. Four stairs down and the air changes: cooler, still, and faintly sweet. There are barrels in rows, and each is marked in chalk with the date and the name of the one who tapped it. A barrel is a diary. You can read a family's fortune from its labels.

## The brewing

It is done at the turn of the season, by the whole house. The grain is soaked, the water is heated, and the great paddle is passed from hand to hand until everyone has stirred. It is an honour for the child who is old enough to lift it.

## The cups

A dwarf's cup is a serious matter. Ours are carved from black oak and rimmed in bronze. A cup is given at the naming and kept for life. It is never washed with soap, only with water and a cloth, and it never loses its flavour.

## The drinking contests

They are a sacred tradition. They are conducted by the rules of a thousand years. A contest begins with a toast, proceeds by rounds and ends when one contestant sets down a cup and says, quite calmly, "I think I have had enough."

The record holder is three hundred years old. I will not name them. I will say that the last time they entered, they did so in the middle of a conversation about the price of coal, and won without raising their voice.

## What I would say to a visitor

Do not enter. Watch. If you must drink, drink slowly, eat bread and ask for water between the cups. Nobody will think the less of you. They will think the more.

Thorgrim, whose day is Thorsday, has a fondness for the brewer. I think it is because we also work with fire and patience, and we also hope.
