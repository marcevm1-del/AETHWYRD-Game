# Letters Left in Gearholm Undercity

*Amina Rahman of Gearholm*

**Shelf:** Letter book  
**Region:** Gearholm  
**In-world date:** 1017 SC  
**Status:** Draft. Player-facing. Not Canon until reviewed. Written as what a person in Aethwyrd could know.
## Who kept these

Amina Rahman of Gearholm wrote from Gearholm Undercity. The houses are brick, copper, and clock-chime. The letters smell faintly of sausage, mustard, and beer. They mention a gear that is also a law the way you mention a cough in the next room: not the main sentence, never absent.

## Letter 1 — Maelsday, to a sibling

Gearholm Undercity, in the year this copy claims.

I arrived. The bed is clean and the lock is a suggestion. Send the blue thread, not money.

The rest of the page is ordinary: sausage, mustard, and beer, a price, a name spelled two ways. Thorgrim in the workshop, Vex in the patent queue. Neither is lying. Each left out the part that made their own choice look worse.

— Amina Rahman of Gearholm
