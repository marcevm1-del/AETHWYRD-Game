**Status:** Draft. Player-facing. Not Canon until reviewed. Written as what a person in Aethwyrd could know.
## The short version on the board

I set this down at Ashen Mines camps because I had walked the route twice and the second time the first notes looked vain. The blue lamp-glass on the table is not a relic. It is how this copy stayed honest.

At Ashen Mines camps the work of the day sits inside basalt halls and anvil-heat. People eat stout, stone-bread, and roast roots and they talk about a vow hammered where a stranger can hear it the way coastal towns talk about weather: often, and with corrections.

This chapter listens by way of a reeve's paper. A speaker at Ashen Mines camps says one thing. A speaker at Karak Dûn says the part that was left on the road between them. The official line is shorter. The household line feeds more people.

The shrine mark nearby belongs to Vex: a coin with two heads, kept for luck, roads, and the laugh you did not plan. That is public devotion, the kind you can see from the street. Thorgrim, whose mark is on every new tool. I am not writing what a god intends. I am writing what the keeper swept this morning.

Evidence you can touch beats a speech you can repeat. A mended wall, a card that nobody will take down, a field that will not take the old crop, a song that changes when the singer notices who just walked in. Copy the object. Then copy who benefits if you only believe the short version.
