# Letters Left in border baronies

*Imani Reed of Strife Marches*

**Shelf:** Letter book  
**Region:** Strife Marches  
**In-world date:** 999 SC  
**Status:** Draft. Player-facing. Not Canon until reviewed. Written as what a person in Aethwyrd could know.
## Who kept these

Imani Reed of Strife Marches wrote from mercenary forts. The houses are broken walls used as sheepfolds. The letters smell faintly of barley soup and hard cheese. They mention bones of a war nobody dates the same way the way you mention a cough in the next room: not the main sentence, never absent.

## Letter 1 — Maelsday, to a sibling

mercenary forts, in the year this copy claims.

I arrived. The bed is clean and the lock is a suggestion. Send the blue thread, not money.

The rest of the page is ordinary: barley soup and hard cheese, a price, a name spelled two ways. two shrines that will not share a yard. Both saw the same hour. They were standing on different banks.

— Imani Reed of Strife Marches
