# Neighbors Near Fanghold

*Amina Ibarra of Fanghold*

**Shelf:** Field guide  
**Region:** Fanghold  
**In-world date:** 1015 SC  
**Status:** Draft. Player-facing. Not Canon until reviewed. Written as what a person in Aethwyrd could know.
## Before the entries

I set this down at nomad yurt camps because three neighbors told the same story with three different weather reports. The dry orange peel on the table is not a relic. It is how this copy stayed honest.

This guide is for walking country: felt yurts and horse-lines. You will smell smoked meat and salted milk in the settlements and you will be told about raid dust on the steppe edge. The entries below are neighbors, not trophies.

Shrines out here lean toward this habit: Kaelthas named before a ride. A prayer is not a ward you can spend like a potion. Learn the creature anyway.

If a poster and a hunter disagree, copy both and date them. The halls of the Labyrinth reset. Ordinary hills do not, and a path can be wrong twice.

## Mana Fox

Around nomad yurt camps, people file the Mana Fox near guild rank E. A rank is a licence hint. It is not a measurement of your courage, and it goes stale if the ground has changed.

It would rather leave. Corner it, and the leaving is over.

Folk talk says its nature runs toward Light, and that Dark unsettles it. Treat that as a rumor you can test from farther away than its reach. If the test fails, you have learned something, provided you can still leave.

What the meat-stall will not put on a sign: do not take a young one to prove a point, do not block the line it uses to flee, and do not trust a core that is still warm as if it were a lamp. Cores light cities. They also belong to a creature that was here first. Neither is lying. Each left out the part that made their own choice look worse.
