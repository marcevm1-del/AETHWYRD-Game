# Neighbors Near border baronies

*Bruni Flint of Strife Marches*

**Shelf:** Field guide  
**Region:** Strife Marches  
**In-world date:** 1014 SC  
**Status:** Draft. Player-facing. Not Canon until reviewed. Written as what a person in Aethwyrd could know.
## Before the entries

I set this down at border baronies because a child asked a straight question and the adults answered with a proverb. The copper nail on the table is not a relic. It is how this copy stayed honest.

This guide is for walking country: broken walls used as sheepfolds. You will smell barley soup and hard cheese in the settlements and you will be told about bones of a war nobody dates the same way. The entries below are neighbors, not trophies.

Shrines out here lean toward this habit: two shrines that will not share a yard. A prayer is not a ward you can spend like a potion. Learn the creature anyway.

If a poster and a hunter disagree, copy both and date them. The halls of the Labyrinth reset. Ordinary hills do not, and a path can be wrong twice.

## Skeleton Knight

Around border baronies, people file the Skeleton Knight near guild rank D+. A rank is a licence hint. It is not a measurement of your courage, and it goes stale if the ground has changed.

Something here is being kept. It may be a nest, a stone, or a grudge.

Folk talk says its nature runs toward Dark, and that Light unsettles it. Treat that as a rumor you can test from farther away than its reach. If the test fails, you have learned something, provided you can still leave.

What the meat-stall will not put on a sign: do not take a young one to prove a point, do not block the line it uses to flee, and do not trust a core that is still warm as if it were a lamp. Cores light cities. They also belong to a creature that was here first. One of them is remembering a person. The other is remembering a rule.
