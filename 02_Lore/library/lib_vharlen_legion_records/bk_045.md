## Before the entries

I set this down at Vharlen frontier legions because a child asked a straight question and the adults answered with a proverb. The guild chalk stub on the table is not a relic. It is how this copy stayed honest.

This guide is for walking country: legion stone above a wide river. You will smell beans, oil, and barrack bread in the settlements and you will be told about a pass that is really a fortified gap. The entries below are neighbors, not trophies.

Shrines out here lean toward this habit: Kaelthas on the standard, Solenne in the court. A prayer is not a ward you can spend like a potion. Learn the creature anyway.

If a poster and a hunter disagree, copy both and date them. The halls of the Labyrinth reset. Ordinary hills do not, and a path can be wrong twice.

## Dark Cultist

Around Vharlen frontier legions, people file the Dark Cultist near guild rank C. A rank is a licence hint. It is not a measurement of your courage, and it goes stale if the ground has changed.

It comes on. Distance is the tool, not a speech.

Folk talk says its nature runs toward Blood, and that Light unsettles it. Treat that as a rumor you can test from farther away than its reach. If the test fails, you have learned something, provided you can still leave.

What the meat-stall will not put on a sign: do not take a young one to prove a point, do not block the line it uses to flee, and do not trust a core that is still warm as if it were a lamp. Cores light cities. They also belong to a creature that was here first. Both saw the same hour. They were standing on different banks.
