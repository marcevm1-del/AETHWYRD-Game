# Goods Through Calamity Steppe

*Leila Reed of Calamity Steppe*

**Shelf:** Trade notes  
**Region:** Calamity Steppe  
**In-world date:** 989 SC  
**Status:** Draft. Player-facing. Not Canon until reviewed. Written as what a person in Aethwyrd could know.
## The stall

At Calamity Steppe the goods sit against scorched grass and warning stones. People are buying smoked meat and canteen water and talking about a horizon that has already ended once. Faith is present as habit: Kaelthas, asked for nerve and not for glory. None of that is a price list. It is why the price list is tense.

The wallet of this world counts copper, silver, gold, platinum, and mithril, each a hundred of the one before. Most of a life is lived in the first two.

## Grain

Grain moves because it keeps, and it makes other arguments possible.

In Calamity Steppe, ask who carried it, not only who owns the stall. A thing can arrive by marriage, refuge, a contract, or a raid, and those are not the same story. One of them is remembering a person. The other is remembering a rule.

Write the price you saw and the day. A book that says 'always' is trying to be a lord. If the Guild’s buyback for cores tempts you to hunt past your rank, this page is the friend who takes the chalk out of your hand.
