## The story told to children

I set this down at nomad yurt camps because I had walked the route twice and the second time the first notes looked vain. The blue lamp-glass on the table is not a relic. It is how this copy stayed honest.

This chapter listens by way of children. A speaker at nomad yurt camps says one thing. A speaker at Fanghold says the part that was left on the road between them. One of them is remembering a person. The other is remembering a rule.
