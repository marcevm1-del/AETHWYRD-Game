# Songs Heard in Canopy City Lothaniel

*Vesna Mori of Sylvaran*

**Shelf:** Ballad sheet  
**Region:** Sylvaran  
**In-world date:** 940 SC  
**Status:** Draft. Player-facing. Not Canon until reviewed. Written as what a person in Aethwyrd could know.
## Sung at the table

Collected at Moonwell hamlets. Change the name of the bridge if your bridge is the one that is out. A song that cannot survive a correction is a poster.
