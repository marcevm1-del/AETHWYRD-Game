# The Seed That Chose Late
*A story for reading aloud under the leaves of Sylvaran, as told to children who have not yet been named*

**Shelf:** Stories for reading aloud  
**Region:** Sylvaran  
**In-world date:** 1012 SC  
**Status:** Draft. Player-facing. Not Canon until reviewed. Written as what a person in Aethwyrd could know.

*(Read this one slowly. The seed is a small voice. Give it a small voice.)*

In the Twin Grove there was a seed that had been planted, and had not yet decided what to become.

All round it, the other seeds were busy. The oak seed was becoming an oak. The birch was becoming a birch. The beech was being very serious about its roots, and the yew was thinking about the next thousand years.

"What will you be?" they asked the little seed.

"I haven't decided," it said.

"But you are in the ground," said the oak.

"I know."

"The child has a name," said the birch. "The child will be a year old tomorrow."

"I know."

"It is a very long time to wait," said the beech.

"Perhaps," said the seed. "But I do not want to be the wrong tree."

---

A year passed. The seed lay in the dark and the damp and thought. It thought about light. It thought about rain. It thought about a child it had never met, who was learning to walk, and fell down, and laughed.

*I should like to be that,* thought the seed. *I should like to fall down and laugh.*

A tree cannot fall down and laugh. But a tree can bend in the wind and shake its leaves, which is nearly the same.

So the seed put out a small green shoot, and then a leaf, and then a branch, and it grew into a willow, which is a tree that bends and shakes and has never in its life been serious.

The child grew up to be a person who laughed in difficult places, and the willow was beside her all the way.

*(The reader says:)* **"There is no wrong tree, only a late one."**
