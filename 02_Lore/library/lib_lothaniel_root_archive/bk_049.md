## How this copy is used

I set this down at Canopy City Lothaniel because three neighbors told the same story with three different weather reports. The dry orange peel on the table is not a relic. It is how this copy stayed honest.

Imani Costa of Sylvaran keeps this book in the kitchen at Canopy City Lothaniel, where the houses are living timber and root-stairs. The everyday pot already knows waybread, honey, and leaf-tea. What follows is the version you can cook when the old argument over felled heartwood has made the market short, and when you still mean to sit down.

A note on faith, because someone always asks: Sylvara, spoken to as a neighbor. None of these dishes require a blessing to be edible. A blessing is for the table, after the salt is in.

Prices move. A copper is still the coin of a small bite, and a silver is still a bed. If a stall quotes you a mithril piece for supper, you are in the wrong story or the wrong street.

## March Barley Soup

*As cooked when Canopy City Lothaniel is being ordinary.*

**You need**

- barley
- a bone if you have one
- hard cheese
- water

Boil the barley longer than you think. Grate the cheese in at the end so it becomes a line, not a lump.

**You need**
