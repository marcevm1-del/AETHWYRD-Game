## The week as people actually live it

I set this down at Thimblefolk Mushroom Town because the Guild card was right about the danger and wrong about the cause. The knot-cord on the table is not a relic. It is how this copy stayed honest.

At Thimblefolk Mushroom Town the work of the day sits inside toadstool eaves and moss steps. People eat mushroom stew and last year's honey and they talk about spores that sweeten a closed room the way coastal towns talk about weather: often, and with corrections.

## Days, without a whip

The week runs Solenday, Maelsday, Thorsday, Sylvasday, Kaelsday, Morrowsday, Vexday. Ilvaris has no day. People notice, and they do not all explain it the same way. You can miss a festival and still be a neighbor. You cannot miss a promised watch and call it piety.

Status Appraisal Day comes at twelve. It is a festival and it is a sorting, and adults who joke through it are remembering their own. Bring the honey cakes before the reading. Afterward, walk the child home whether the sheet was kind or not.

The Lamp Vigil is for the dead, with offerings and a light that is not a dare. Eat the biscuits before the lighting. Speak the names you have a right to speak. A red moon in the sky is a thing sailors mention without raising their voice. This book does not explain it. The night is already full.
