# Small Tools, Done Properly
*Pip Cogsworth, tool-smith of Thimblefolk Mushroom Town, who is eleven fingers tall*

**Shelf:** Craft notes  
**Region:** Mossdeep  
**In-world date:** 1008 SC  
**Status:** Draft. Player-facing. Not Canon until reviewed. Written as what a person in Aethwyrd could know.

The big folk buy our tools because they are small and perfect. They do not understand that they are small *because* they are perfect. A thing made at our size cannot hide a flaw. There is nowhere for it to go.

## The bench

My bench is a toadstool cap, flattened, with a rim. It is eight fingers across. On it I keep a file, a hammer the size of a seed, a vice made from a clothes-peg and a lamp made from a firefly in a jar. That is the whole of the smithy.

## What I make

- **Needles**, for the moss-weavers. A good needle has no burr and no bend, and I have never sold one that did.
- **Hinges**, for the toadstool doors. They must swing without a sound, for a creak at our size is a roar.
- **Honey-knives**, for the honey-keepers. Thin as a leaf and sharp as a thought.
- **Beetle-bits**, for the beetle-riders. These are the ones I am proudest of, and the ones I worry about most.

## The rule of the file

Never file toward yourself. Never file in haste. Never file a thing you have not first looked at for the length of a breath. The file removes what the eye has already decided to remove, and a smith who files before looking is a smith who files away the thing that was right.

## Tempering

We do not have forges. We have the Warm Springs, where the beetles bathe, and a patient hand. We heat a thing in a spirit lamp and cool it in the spring, a little at a time, until it rings. A tool that rings is a tool that will not break. A tool that thuds is a tool that has told you something.

## For the big folk

If you buy one of my needles, do not use it on canvas. If you buy a hinge, do not fit it to a gate. They are made for the work they are made for, and they will refuse any other.

Thorgrim, they say, made the world from a hammer. I have made a good deal of it from a pin.
