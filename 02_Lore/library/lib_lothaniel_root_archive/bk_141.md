## The short version on the board

I set this down at Canopy City Lothaniel because the shelf copy had been edited by someone with a tidy mind and a bad memory. The copper nail on the table is not a relic. It is how this copy stayed honest.

At Canopy City Lothaniel the work of the day sits inside living timber and root-stairs. People eat waybread, honey, and leaf-tea and they talk about the old argument over felled heartwood the way coastal towns talk about weather: often, and with corrections.
