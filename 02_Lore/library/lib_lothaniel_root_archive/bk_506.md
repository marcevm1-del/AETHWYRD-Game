# Neighbors Near Canopy City Lothaniel

*Hana Holt of Sylvaran*

**Shelf:** Field guide  
**Region:** Sylvaran  
**In-world date:** 1004 SC  
**Status:** Draft. Player-facing. Not Canon until reviewed. Written as what a person in Aethwyrd could know.
## Before the entries

I set this down at Moonwell hamlets because three neighbors told the same story with three different weather reports. The wooden spoon on the table is not a relic. It is how this copy stayed honest.

This guide is for walking country: living timber and root-stairs. You will smell waybread, honey, and leaf-tea in the settlements and you will be told about the old argument over felled heartwood. The entries below are neighbors, not trophies.

Shrines out here lean toward this habit: Sylvara, spoken to as a neighbor. A prayer is not a ward you can spend like a potion. Learn the creature anyway.

If a poster and a hunter disagree, copy both and date them. The halls of the Labyrinth reset. Ordinary hills do not, and a path can be wrong twice.

## Pixie

Around Moonwell hamlets, people file the Pixie near guild rank E. A rank is a licence hint. It is not a measurement of your courage, and it goes stale if the ground has changed.

It would rather leave. Corner it, and the leaving is over.

Folk talk says its nature runs toward Wind, and that Fire unsettles it. Treat that as a rumor you can test from farther away than its reach. If the test fails, you have learned something, provided you can still leave.

What the meat-stall will not put on a sign: do not take a young one to prove a point, do not block the line it uses to flee, and do not trust a core that is still warm as if it were a lamp. Cores light cities. They also belong to a creature that was here first. The official line is shorter. The household line feeds more people.
