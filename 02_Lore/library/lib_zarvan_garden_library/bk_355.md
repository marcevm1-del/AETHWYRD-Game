# Letters Left in Stormreach cliff-towns

*Bramble Adeyemi of Stormreach*

**Shelf:** Letter book  
**Region:** Stormreach  
**In-world date:** 1015 SC  
**Status:** Draft. Player-facing. Not Canon until reviewed. Written as what a person in Aethwyrd could know.
## Who kept these

Bramble Adeyemi of Stormreach wrote from Stormreach cliff-towns. The houses are gorge wind and cliff terraces. The letters smell faintly of goat stew and barley. They mention a cliff that the maps once called a coast the way you mention a cough in the next room: not the main sentence, never absent.

## Letter 1 — Maelsday, to a sibling

Stormreach cliff-towns, in the year this copy claims.

I arrived. The bed is clean and the lock is a suggestion. Send the blue thread, not money.

The rest of the page is ordinary: goat stew and barley, a price, a name spelled two ways. Kaelthas in every eaves. Both saw the same hour. They were standing on different banks.

— Bramble Adeyemi of Stormreach

## Letter 2 — Thorsday, to a teacher

Stormreach cliff-towns, in the year this copy claims.

Your lesson about measuring twice survived the road. Your lesson about people did not, and I am rewriting it.

The rest of the page is ordinary: goat stew and barley, a price, a name spelled two ways. Kaelthas in every eaves. The official line is shorter. The household line feeds more people.

— Bramble Adeyemi of Stormreach

## Letter 3 — Sylvasday, to the guild clerk

Stormreach cliff-towns, in the year this copy claims.

The poster is missing the part where the bridge is out. I am not asking for a refund. I am asking for a corrected board.

The rest of the page is ordinary: goat stew and barley, a price, a name spelled two ways. Kaelthas in every eaves. Neither is lying. Each left out the part that made their own choice look worse.

— Bramble Adeyemi of Stormreach
