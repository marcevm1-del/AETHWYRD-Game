# Letters Left in Zaruma gold-road towns

*Bramble Haddad of Zaruma*

**Shelf:** Letter book  
**Region:** Zaruma  
**In-world date:** 1014 SC  
**Status:** Draft. Player-facing. Not Canon until reviewed. Written as what a person in Aethwyrd could know.
## Who kept these

Bramble Haddad of Zaruma wrote from Zaruma gold-road towns. The houses are gold-road courtyards and library shade. The letters smell faintly of spiced stew, kola, and millet. They mention a king who is expected to be generous the way you mention a cough in the next room: not the main sentence, never absent.

## Letter 1 — Maelsday, to a sibling

Zaruma gold-road towns, in the year this copy claims.

I arrived. The bed is clean and the lock is a suggestion. Send the blue thread, not money.

The rest of the page is ordinary: spiced stew, kola, and millet, a price, a name spelled two ways. elders, archivists, and Solenne's scale. Both saw the same hour. They were standing on different banks.

— Bramble Haddad of Zaruma

## Letter 2 — Thorsday, to a teacher

Zaruma gold-road towns, in the year this copy claims.

Your lesson about measuring twice survived the road. Your lesson about people did not, and I am rewriting it.

The rest of the page is ordinary: spiced stew, kola, and millet, a price, a name spelled two ways. elders, archivists, and Solenne's scale. Neither is lying. Each left out the part that made their own choice look worse.

— Bramble Haddad of Zaruma

## Letter 3 — Sylvasday, to the guild clerk

Zaruma gold-road towns, in the year this copy claims.
