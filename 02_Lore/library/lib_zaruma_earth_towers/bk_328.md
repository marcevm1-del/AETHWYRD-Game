# Goods Through Lustrine glass beaches

*Amina Sato of Lustrine Shores*

**Shelf:** Trade notes  
**Region:** Lustrine Shores  
**In-world date:** 988 SC  
**Status:** Draft. Player-facing. Not Canon until reviewed. Written as what a person in Aethwyrd could know.
## The stall

At Lustrine glass beaches the goods sit against pale sand and shell-lanes. People are buying clams, greens, and rice and talking about a tide that uncovers a road twice a day. Faith is present as habit: Maelis when the water is silver. None of that is a price list. It is why the price list is tense.

The wallet of this world counts copper, silver, gold, platinum, and mithril, each a hundred of the one before. Most of a life is lived in the first two.
