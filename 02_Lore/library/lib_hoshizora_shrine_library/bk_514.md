# Neighbors Near Hoshizora City

*Nia Park of Hoshizora*

**Shelf:** Field guide  
**Region:** Hoshizora  
**In-world date:** 1017 SC  
**Status:** Draft. Player-facing. Not Canon until reviewed. Written as what a person in Aethwyrd could know.
## Before the entries

I set this down at Bamboo Sea villages because a child asked a straight question and the adults answered with a proverb. The spare button on the table is not a relic. It is how this copy stayed honest.

This guide is for walking country: curved tile and cedar rain. You will smell rice, grilled fish, and pickles in the settlements and you will be told about a river mouth that fogs the streets. The entries below are neighbors, not trophies.

Shrines out here lean toward this habit: Maelis and a household shrine. A prayer is not a ward you can spend like a potion. Learn the creature anyway.

If a poster and a hunter disagree, copy both and date them. The halls of the Labyrinth reset. Ordinary hills do not, and a path can be wrong twice.

## Griffon

Around Bamboo Sea villages, people file the Griffon near guild rank C. A rank is a licence hint. It is not a measurement of your courage, and it goes stale if the ground has changed.

It keeps a patch and announces it. Stepping through the middle is the mistake.

Folk talk says its nature runs toward Wind, and that Fire unsettles it. Treat that as a rumor you can test from farther away than its reach. If the test fails, you have learned something, provided you can still leave.

What the meat-stall will not put on a sign: do not take a young one to prove a point, do not block the line it uses to flee, and do not trust a core that is still warm as if it were a lamp. Cores light cities. They also belong to a creature that was here first. Neither is lying. Each left out the part that made their own choice look worse.
