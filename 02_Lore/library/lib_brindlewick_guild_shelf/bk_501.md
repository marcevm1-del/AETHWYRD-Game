# Neighbors Near Brindlewick

*Nia Farid of Whisperwood*

**Shelf:** Field guide  
**Region:** Whisperwood  
**In-world date:** 1000 SC  
**Status:** Draft. Player-facing. Not Canon until reviewed. Written as what a person in Aethwyrd could know.
## Before the entries

I set this down at Millbrook because the shrine keeper and the miller had stopped speaking, and the road still needed both. The blue lamp-glass on the table is not a relic. It is how this copy stayed honest.

This guide is for walking country: half-timber and hornbeam shade. You will smell mill bread, tea, and a rabbit stew in the settlements and you will be told about goblin tracks on the mill path. The entries below are neighbors, not trophies.

Shrines out here lean toward this habit: Sylvara's groves and a small Solenne window. A prayer is not a ward you can spend like a potion. Learn the creature anyway.

If a poster and a hunter disagree, copy both and date them. The halls of the Labyrinth reset. Ordinary hills do not, and a path can be wrong twice.

## Gremlin

Around Millbrook, people file the Gremlin near guild rank F. A rank is a licence hint. It is not a measurement of your courage, and it goes stale if the ground has changed.

It starts at motion. A quiet watcher sees more than a sprinter.

Folk talk says its nature runs toward Lightning, and that Earth unsettles it. Treat that as a rumor you can test from farther away than its reach. If the test fails, you have learned something, provided you can still leave.

What the meat-stall will not put on a sign: do not take a young one to prove a point, do not block the line it uses to flee, and do not trust a core that is still warm as if it were a lamp. Cores light cities. They also belong to a creature that was here first. Neither is lying. Each left out the part that made their own choice look worse.
