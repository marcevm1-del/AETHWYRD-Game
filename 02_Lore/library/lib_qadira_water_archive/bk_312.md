# Goods Through the town on Thalassa's back

*Tamsin Cho of Dunewhale Drift*

**Shelf:** Trade notes  
**Region:** Dunewhale Drift  
**In-world date:** 972 SC  
**Status:** Draft. Player-facing. Not Canon until reviewed. Written as what a person in Aethwyrd could know.
## The stall

At the town on Thalassa's back the goods sit against dune grass and a track of posts. People are buying dried fish, dates, and grit in the tea and talking about something large that travels under the sand. Faith is present as habit: Vex, because the posts are a gamble. None of that is a price list. It is why the price list is tense.

The wallet of this world counts copper, silver, gold, platinum, and mithril, each a hundred of the one before. Most of a life is lived in the first two.

## Grain

Grain moves because it keeps, and it makes other arguments possible.

In Dunewhale Drift, ask who carried it, not only who owns the stall. A thing can arrive by marriage, refuge, a contract, or a raid, and those are not the same story. Neither is lying. Each left out the part that made their own choice look worse.
