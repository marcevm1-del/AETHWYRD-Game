# Neighbors Near the town on Thalassa's back

*Elowen Flint of Dunewhale Drift*

**Shelf:** Field guide  
**Region:** Dunewhale Drift  
**In-world date:** 992 SC  
**Status:** Draft. Player-facing. Not Canon until reviewed. Written as what a person in Aethwyrd could know.
## Before the entries

I set this down at the town on Thalassa's back because the Guild card was right about the danger and wrong about the cause. The dry orange peel on the table is not a relic. It is how this copy stayed honest.

This guide is for walking country: dune grass and a track of posts. You will smell dried fish, dates, and grit in the tea in the settlements and you will be told about something large that travels under the sand. The entries below are neighbors, not trophies.

Shrines out here lean toward this habit: Vex, because the posts are a gamble. A prayer is not a ward you can spend like a potion. Learn the creature anyway.

If a poster and a hunter disagree, copy both and date them. The halls of the Labyrinth reset. Ordinary hills do not, and a path can be wrong twice.

## Sand Skink

Around the town on Thalassa's back, people file the Sand Skink near guild rank F. A rank is a licence hint. It is not a measurement of your courage, and it goes stale if the ground has changed.

The ground is the hunter. Look at the low places before the skyline.

Folk talk says its nature runs toward Earth, and that Wind unsettles it. Treat that as a rumor you can test from farther away than its reach. If the test fails, you have learned something, provided you can still leave.

What the meat-stall will not put on a sign: do not take a young one to prove a point, do not block the line it uses to flee, and do not trust a core that is still warm as if it were a lamp. Cores light cities. They also belong to a creature that was here first. The official line is shorter. The household line feeds more people.
