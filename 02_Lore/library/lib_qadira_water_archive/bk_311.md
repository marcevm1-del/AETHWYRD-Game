# Goods Through Oasis of Qadira

*Hana Ellison of Sunscar*

**Shelf:** Trade notes  
**Region:** Sunscar  
**In-world date:** 971 SC  
**Status:** Draft. Player-facing. Not Canon until reviewed. Written as what a person in Aethwyrd could know.
## The stall

At Oasis of Qadira the goods sit against adobe shade and hot dust. People are buying flatbread, dates, and goat cheese and talking about a ruin that was a city, and a night nobody can date cleanly. Faith is present as habit: Solenne at noon, and a silence about the old empire. None of that is a price list. It is why the price list is tense.

The wallet of this world counts copper, silver, gold, platinum, and mithril, each a hundred of the one before. Most of a life is lived in the first two.

## Grain

Grain moves because it keeps, and it makes other arguments possible.

In Sunscar, ask who carried it, not only who owns the stall. A thing can arrive by marriage, refuge, a contract, or a raid, and those are not the same story. One of them is remembering a person. The other is remembering a rule.

Write the price you saw and the day. A book that says 'always' is trying to be a lord. If the Guild’s buyback for cores tempts you to hunt past your rank, this page is the friend who takes the chalk out of your hand.
