# Letters Left in terrace cities

*Dugan Ellison of Apu Highlands*

**Shelf:** Letter book  
**Region:** Apu Highlands  
**In-world date:** 1006 SC  
**Status:** Draft. Player-facing. Not Canon until reviewed. Written as what a person in Aethwyrd could know.
## Who kept these

Dugan Ellison of Apu Highlands wrote from terrace cities. The houses are stone terraces and thin soil. The letters smell faintly of potatoes, chili, and herb salt. They mention a landslide year somebody's grandparent still cooks around the way you mention a cough in the next room: not the main sentence, never absent.

## Letter 1 — Maelsday, to a sibling

terrace cities, in the year this copy claims.

I arrived. The bed is clean and the lock is a suggestion. Send the blue thread, not money.

The rest of the page is ordinary: potatoes, chili, and herb salt, a price, a name spelled two ways. the mountain, thanked before the god. The official line is shorter. The household line feeds more people.

— Dugan Ellison of Apu Highlands

terrace cities, in the year this copy claims.
