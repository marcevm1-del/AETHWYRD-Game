I set this down at Haneul gate-towns because the Guild card was right about the danger and wrong about the cause. The hammer-mark stamp on the table is not a relic. It is how this copy stayed honest.

This chapter listens by way of swept stone. A speaker at Haneul gate-towns says one thing. A speaker at Haneul says the part that was left on the road between them. One of them is remembering a person. The other is remembering a rule.

The shrine mark nearby belongs to Vex: a coin with two heads, kept for luck, roads, and the laugh you did not plan. That is public devotion, the kind you can see from the street. Kaelthas on the wall, Solenne in the oath. I am not writing what a god intends. I am writing what the keeper swept this morning.
