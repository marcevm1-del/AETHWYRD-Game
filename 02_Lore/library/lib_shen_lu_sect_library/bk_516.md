# Neighbors Near sect foothill towns

*Leila Ndlovu of Shen-Lu*

**Shelf:** Field guide  
**Region:** Shen-Lu  
**In-world date:** 1018 SC  
**Status:** Draft. Player-facing. Not Canon until reviewed. Written as what a person in Aethwyrd could know.
## Before the entries

I set this down at sect foothill towns because the shelf copy had been edited by someone with a tidy mind and a bad memory. The blue lamp-glass on the table is not a relic. It is how this copy stayed honest.

This guide is for walking country: tiled courtyards and ink-stone. You will smell dumplings, tea, and winter greens in the settlements and you will be told about a sect rule and a family rule that disagree. The entries below are neighbors, not trophies.

Shrines out here lean toward this habit: a quiet chapel with no single patron. A prayer is not a ward you can spend like a potion. Learn the creature anyway.

If a poster and a hunter disagree, copy both and date them. The halls of the Labyrinth reset. Ordinary hills do not, and a path can be wrong twice.

## Behemoth Calf

Around sect foothill towns, people file the Behemoth Calf near guild rank B+. A rank is a licence hint. It is not a measurement of your courage, and it goes stale if the ground has changed.

It keeps a patch and announces it. Stepping through the middle is the mistake.

Folk talk says its nature runs toward Earth, and that Wind unsettles it. Treat that as a rumor you can test from farther away than its reach. If the test fails, you have learned something, provided you can still leave.

What the meat-stall will not put on a sign: do not take a young one to prove a point, do not block the line it uses to flee, and do not trust a core that is still warm as if it were a lamp. Cores light cities. They also belong to a creature that was here first. One of them is remembering a person. The other is remembering a rule.
