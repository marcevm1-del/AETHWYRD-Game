# Boots Toward Stormreach

*Corin Farid of Haneul*

**Shelf:** Road book  
**Region:** Haneul  
**In-world date:** 986 SC  
**Status:** Draft. Player-facing. Not Canon until reviewed. Written as what a person in Aethwyrd could know.
## How to use this notebook

I walked out of Haneul with a dry pair of socks and a worse map than this one. The country begins as gate-towns under a high pass. Inns will feed you buckwheat, chili, and broth if you pay like a person and not like a raid.

Trouble on the posters: the wind at the Red Gate. A siege bell is a local fact. It is not your invitation to become the story of the town. Help if you are asked and able. Do not invent a command.

Where people put their hands before a gate: Kaelthas on the wall, Solenne in the oath.

A copper buys a bite. Silver buys a bed. Write the price you actually paid in the margin so the next reader is not braver than the menu.

## Solenday — the border track, Haneul to Shen-Lu (17.1 km)

Leave while the lamps are still a choice. the border track, Haneul to Shen-Lu (17.1 km) is walkable on a fair day. Count river crossings if the note gives them: this leg records no numbered count.

Stop at the first inn that will write your name without a speech. Regulars are made by returning, not by spending like a lord. If the room is dear, the common table still counts as rest, and your pack stays in your sight.

What I got wrong the first time: I trusted a straight pencil line. The official line is shorter. The household line feeds more people. Ask the person who mends the road, not only the person who sells the view.

Night note. Monster hours are not a moral. They are a clock. Be inside a wall, a yurt ring, a longhouse, or a lit camp with a watch you trust. A core-lamp in a window means someone is awake. It does not mean you are welcome to decide their defence.
