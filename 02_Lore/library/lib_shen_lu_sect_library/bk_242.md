## The story told to children

I set this down at sect foothill towns because the Guild card was right about the danger and wrong about the cause. The guild chalk stub on the table is not a relic. It is how this copy stayed honest.

At sect foothill towns the work of the day sits inside tiled courtyards and ink-stone. People eat dumplings, tea, and winter greens and they talk about a sect rule and a family rule that disagree the way coastal towns talk about weather: often, and with corrections.

This chapter listens by way of children. A speaker at sect foothill towns says one thing. A speaker at Shen-Lu says the part that was left on the road between them. One of them is remembering a person. The other is remembering a rule.
