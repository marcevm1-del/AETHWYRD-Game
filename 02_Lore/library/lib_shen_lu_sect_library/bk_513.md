# Neighbors Near Haneul gate-towns

*Chenna Cho of Haneul*

**Shelf:** Field guide  
**Region:** Haneul  
**In-world date:** 995 SC  
**Status:** Draft. Player-facing. Not Canon until reviewed. Written as what a person in Aethwyrd could know.
## Before the entries

I set this down at Haneul gate-towns because the shrine keeper and the miller had stopped speaking, and the road still needed both. The dry orange peel on the table is not a relic. It is how this copy stayed honest.

This guide is for walking country: gate-towns under a high pass. You will smell buckwheat, chili, and broth in the settlements and you will be told about the wind at the Red Gate. The entries below are neighbors, not trophies.

Shrines out here lean toward this habit: Kaelthas on the wall, Solenne in the oath. A prayer is not a ward you can spend like a potion. Learn the creature anyway.

If a poster and a hunter disagree, copy both and date them. The halls of the Labyrinth reset. Ordinary hills do not, and a path can be wrong twice.

## Lich

Around Haneul gate-towns, people file the Lich near guild rank A. A rank is a licence hint. It is not a measurement of your courage, and it goes stale if the ground has changed.

The ground is the hunter. Look at the low places before the skyline.

Folk talk says its nature runs toward Ice, and that Fire unsettles it. Treat that as a rumor you can test from farther away than its reach. If the test fails, you have learned something, provided you can still leave.

What the meat-stall will not put on a sign: do not take a young one to prove a point, do not block the line it uses to flee, and do not trust a core that is still warm as if it were a lamp. Cores light cities. They also belong to a creature that was here first. One of them is remembering a person. The other is remembering a rule.
