# Goods Through sect foothill towns

*Safiya Farid of Shen-Lu*

**Shelf:** Trade notes  
**Region:** Shen-Lu  
**In-world date:** 994 SC  
**Status:** Draft. Player-facing. Not Canon until reviewed. Written as what a person in Aethwyrd could know.
## The stall

At sect foothill towns the goods sit against tiled courtyards and ink-stone. People are buying dumplings, tea, and winter greens and talking about a sect rule and a family rule that disagree. Faith is present as habit: a quiet chapel with no single patron. None of that is a price list. It is why the price list is tense.

The wallet of this world counts copper, silver, gold, platinum, and mithril, each a hundred of the one before. Most of a life is lived in the first two.

## Grain

Grain moves because it keeps, and it makes other arguments possible.

In Shen-Lu, ask who carried it, not only who owns the stall. A thing can arrive by marriage, refuge, a contract, or a raid, and those are not the same story. Both saw the same hour. They were standing on different banks.
