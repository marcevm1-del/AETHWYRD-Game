# 02 — Lore & Cosmology (v0.2)

> *"Before the world, there was the Loom. Before the Loom, there was the Thread. And the Thread was lonely."*
> — Opening line of the *Canticle of Weaving*, sung in every temple of Aetheria

## 1. Creation Myth — The Weaving
1. **The Thread:** a single endless strand of **Aether**, the raw substance of possibility, drifted through the void.
2. **The Two Hands:** the Thread folded and became two primordials: **Aurelion, the Hand of Dawn** (order, form, light) and **Nyxara, the Hand of Dusk** (dream, change, dark).
3. **The Loom:** together they wove the world on a cosmic loom. Every mountain, sea and soul is a knot in the **Great Weave**.
4. **The Children:** from leftover threads came **the Five Elder Dragons** (guardians of the Weave's pillars), **the Titans** (builders of continents), and **the Eight** (the gods mortals worship).
5. **The Fraying:** the Weave was never perfect. Where it frays, **Aether leaks**. Leaks become ley lines, monsters, dungeons, and eventually **Calamities**.

## 2. The Pantheon — "The Eight"
| God | Domain | Symbol | Worshipped by | Blessing (player boon) | Personality |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Solenne, the Radiant Judge** | Sun, justice, oaths | Golden scale | Holy Church, paladins, the Kingdom | +Holy damage, oath buffs | Righteous, rigid, can't tolerate lies |
| **Maelis, the Silver Dreamer** | Moon (Lumen), dreams, prophecy | Crescent and eye | Oracles, elves | Prophetic dreams reveal secrets | Gentle, sad, knows too much |
| **Thorgrim, the Anvil Father** | Forge, earth, craft | Hammer on mountain | Dwarves, smiths | Crafting quality up | Gruff, loves hard work and ale |
| **Sylvara, the Green Mother** | Nature, growth, beasts | World Tree leaf | Elves, druids, farmers, tamers | Taming and growth bonuses | Warm but wild; nature has teeth |
| **Kaelthas, the Storm Banner** | War, storms, courage | Lightning spear | Soldiers, beastkin, sailors | +crit, courage aura | Loud, honorable, hates cowards |
| **Morrowyn, the Lantern Keeper** | Death, rebirth, memory | Lantern with blue flame | Gravekeepers, (secretly) necromancers | Revive chance, ghost-sight | Calm, kind, misunderstood |
| **Vex, the Laughing Coin** | Luck, trickery, travel, trade | Coin with two heads | Merchants, thieves, gamblers | +LUK, random miracles | Chaotic, mischievous, secretly wise |
| **Ilvaris, the Weaver of Fates** | Fate, the System, summoning | Spindle and stars | Adventurers' Guild (officially) | Grants Status itself | Distant, perfect, *wrong somehow* |

**The Forgotten Ninth:** temple murals show **nine** thrones, and one is scratched out. Who sat there is one of the great mysteries (Act V: **Ezrath, the Unbound**, god of freedom, erased by Ilvaris).

**Devotion:** every region has shrines to 2–3 gods. Daily prayer builds Devotion with 1 patron + 1 minor god. It unlocks blessings, divine quests, a god-specific Legendary item, and an audience with the god's **Avatar** late in the game.

## 4. The System (Status)
- **Official truth:** Ilvaris created Status after the Sundering so that mortals could grow strong enough to face Calamities.
- **What people notice:** only beings with Status level up; monsters grow by absorbing Aether. That's why the Predator path is heresy.
- **The secret (Act V):**
  - When a soul reaches SSS, part of its growth is **harvested** to re-stitch the fraying Weave.
  - Otherworld souls are summoned because they have **unbound growth capacity**. They're fuel.
  - The current Demon Lord is the **second summoned Hero** (the smashed statue). He learned the truth and chose to become a monster instead.
  - Ilvaris isn't evil. She's desperately keeping the world from unraveling, at a terrible price.
- This gives all five endings real moral weight.

## 5. Magic Theory
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Aether** | Raw substance of the Weave |
| **Mana** | Aether filtered through a soul; what spells use |
| **Ley Lines** | Rivers of Aether underground; towns grow on them; glow faintly at night |
| **Magic Stones / Cores** | Crystallized Aether in monsters; currency, fuel, crafting |
| **Dungeons** | Aether wells that grow a core and a mind; they lure adventurers to feed |
| **Chanting** | Shaping mana with **Old Weave-tongue**; mastery shortens it |
| **Forbidden Magic** | Space, Time, Void; touches the Weave directly; hunted by the Church |
| **Divine Magic** | Borrowed from the Eight via Devotion |
| **Spirit Magic** | Contracts with elemental spirits; no mana, uses Bond |

## 6. Moons, Sky & Calendar
- **Lumen** (silver, large): 30-day cycle. Full Lumen means healing +, undead −.
- **Vesper** (red, small): erratic cycle. **Blood Vesper** (full red moon) means all monsters +1 sub-rank, rare spawns, demon activity.
- **Twin Eclipse** (both moons cover the sun): yearly world event. The veil thins and spirits walk.
- **The Shattered Sky:** a scar across the northern sky from the Sundering; it shimmers like an aurora at night.

| Season | Months | Festival |
|---|---|---|
| Spring (*Bloomtide*) | Firstleaf, Rainsong, Petalfall | **Festival of Blossoms** (romance events, flower crowns) |
| Summer (*Highsun*) | Goldlight, Embermonth, Stormcall | **Grand Tournament** (Arena, all ranks) |
| Autumn (*Harvestwane*) | Reaping, Lanternfall, Ashmoon | **Lantern Night** (spirits of the dead visit) |
| Winter (*Deepfrost*) | Hearthmonth, Starfrost, Lastlight | **Starfire Eve** (gifts, snow festival, Twin Eclipse) |

12 months × 30 days. Seasons drive crop cycles, migrations, spawns and festivals.

Otherworlders get **[Comprehension]** for spoken Common, but reading other scripts must be learned. Lore-reading becomes a skill-based adventure.
