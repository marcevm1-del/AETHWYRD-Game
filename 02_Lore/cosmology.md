# 02 — Lore & Cosmology (v0.2)

> *"Before the world, there was the Loom. Before the Loom, there was the Thread. And the Thread was lonely."*
> — Opening line of the *Canticle of Weaving*, sung in every temple of Aethwyrd

## 1. Creation Myth — The Weaving
1. **The Thread:** a single endless strand of **Aether**, the raw substance of possibility, drifted through the void.
2. **The Two Hands:** the Thread folded and became two primordials: **Aurelion, the Hand of Dawn** (order, form, light) and **Nyxara, the Hand of Dusk** (dream, change, dark).
3. **The Loom:** together they wove the world on a cosmic loom. Every mountain, sea and soul is a knot in the **Great Weave**.
4. **The Children:** from leftover threads came **the Five Elder Dragons** (guardians of the Weave's pillars), **the Titans** (builders of continents), and **the Eight** (the gods mortals worship).
5. **The Fraying:** the Weave was never perfect. Where it frays, **Aether leaks**. Leaks become ley lines, monsters, dungeons, and eventually **Calamities**.

| **Sylvara, the Green Mother** | Nature, growth, beasts | World Tree leaf | Elves, druids, farmers, tamers | Taming and growth bonuses | Warm but wild; nature has teeth |
| **Kaelthas, the Storm Banner** | War, storms, courage | Lightning spear | Soldiers, beastkin, sailors | +crit, courage aura | Loud, honorable, hates cowards |
| **Morrowyn, the Lantern Keeper** | Death, rebirth, memory | Lantern with blue flame | Gravekeepers, (secretly) necromancers | Revive chance, ghost-sight | Calm, kind, misunderstood |
| **Vex, the Laughing Coin** | Luck, trickery, travel, trade | Coin with two heads | Merchants, thieves, gamblers | +LUK, random miracles | Chaotic, mischievous, secretly wise |

**The Forgotten Ninth:** temple murals show **nine** thrones, and one is scratched out. Who sat there is one of the great mysteries (Act V: **Ezrath, the Unbound**, god of freedom, erased by Ilvaris).

**Devotion:** every region has shrines to 2–3 gods. Daily prayer builds Devotion with 1 patron + 1 minor god. It unlocks blessings, divine quests, a god-specific Legendary item, and an audience with the god's **Avatar** late in the game.

## 3. Ages of History (S.C. = Summoning Calendar)

For the expanded chronology, lived experience, source conflicts and historical footprints, see [The Deep History of Aethwyrd](history.md). This is a short cosmological index, not a competing timeline. BSC dates are approximate; 0 SC marks the First Summoning, not creation. Canon.md controls the exact spoilers and settled facts.

| Age | When | Events | What remains today |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Age of Dragons** | ~50,000 yrs ago | Elder Dragons rule; first mortal races are born | Dragon bone fields, the five lairs |
| **Age of Titans** | ~30,000 | Titans shape continents, build the Sky Cities | Sky Archipelago; titan corpses as mountains |
| **The Sundering** | ~12,000 | Titans rebel against the gods; the sky cracks; the Abyss opens | The Great Labyrinth (a titan's tomb), the Demon Realm, the scar in the sky |
| **Age of First Kingdoms** | 12,000–3,000 | Nations form; Old Magic Empire of **Valdrisia** | Sunscar Desert ruins |
| **The Long Night** | ~3,000 | First Demon Lord; Valdrisia vanishes in one night | Cursed dunes, sealed tombs |
| **The First Summoning** | Year 0 S.C. | Ilvaris teaches the Church the Summoning Rite; the first Hero wins | Summoning Cathedral, Aldmere City |
| **The Cycle** | 0–1024 S.C. | A Demon Lord rises roughly every ~300 yrs; a Hero is summoned each time | Statues of 3 Heroes… one with its face smashed |
| **Now** | **1024 S.C.** | Vesper, the red moon, waxes early. The Demon Overlord Azrael stirs | — |

**Interpretation note:** the table above describes the broad mythic sequence. It does not imply that every culture agrees on what the dragons, Titans or gods were, or that recorded dates before 0 SC are equally precise. The history document separates common teaching, physical evidence and hidden truth.

## 4. The System (Status)
- **Official truth:** Ilvaris created Status after the Sundering so that mortals could grow strong enough to face Calamities.
- **What people notice:** only beings with Status level up; monsters grow by absorbing Aether. That's why the Predator path is heresy.
- **The secret (Act V):**
  - When a soul reaches SSS, part of its growth is **harvested** to re-stitch the fraying Weave.
  - Otherworld souls are summoned because they have **unbound growth capacity**. They're fuel.
  - The current **Demon Overlord, Azrael**, is the **second summoned Hero** (the smashed statue). He learned the truth and chose to become a monster instead.
  - Ilvaris isn't evil. She's desperately keeping the world from unraveling, at a terrible price.
- This gives every ending (11 main + 1 secret) real moral weight.

## 5. Magic Theory
| Term | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Aether** | Raw substance of the Weave |
| **Mana** | Aether filtered through a soul; what spells use |
| **Ley Lines** | Rivers of Aether underground; towns grow on them; glow faintly at night |
| **Magic Stones / Cores** | Crystallized Aether in monsters; currency, fuel, crafting |
| **Dungeons** | Aether wells that grow a core and a mind; they lure adventurers to feed |
| **Chanting** | Shaping mana with **Old Weave-tongue**; mastery shortens it |
| **Forbidden Magic** | Space, Time, Void; touches the Weave directly; hunted by the Church |
| **Divine Magic** | Borrowed from the Eight via Devotion |
| **Spirit Magic** | Contracts with elemental spirits; no mana, uses Bond |

## 6. Moons, Sky & Calendar
- **Lumen** (silver, large): 30-day cycle. Full Lumen means healing +, undead −.
- **Vesper** (red, small): erratic cycle. **Blood Vesper** (full red moon) means all monsters +1 sub-rank, rare spawns, demon activity. *Secret (Act V):* Vesper is the lost capital of **Valdrisia**, torn into the sky by the Night of Unweaving; its erratic cycle is the trapped souls pulling against Malgareth's spell.
- **Twin Eclipse** (both moons cover the sun): yearly world event. The veil thins and spirits walk.
- **The Shattered Sky:** a scar across the northern sky from the Sundering; it shimmers like an aurora at night.

| Season | Months | Festival |
|---|---|---|
| Spring (*Bloomtide*) | Firstleaf, Rainsong, Petalfall | **Festival of Blossoms** (romance events, flower crowns) |
| Summer (*Highsun*) | Goldlight, Embermonth, Stormcall | **Grand Tournament** (Arena, all ranks) |
| Autumn (*Harvestwane*) | Reaping, Lanternfall, Ashmoon | **Lantern Night** (spirits of the dead visit) |
| Winter (*Deepfrost*) | Hearthmonth, Starfrost, Lastlight | **Starfire Eve** (gifts, snow festival, Twin Eclipse) |

12 months × 30 days, and a **7-day week** named for seven of the Eight: Solenday, Maelsday, Thorsday, Sylvasday, Kaelsday, Morrowsday, Vexday (Ilvaris has no day; see `11_Quests_Encounters/weekly_board.md`). Seasons drive crop cycles, migrations, spawns and festivals.

## The System's 8 Personalities
The System **adapts to how you live**. Its personality shifts over time as you play (with a hidden score per type). Voice: **male or female**, chosen at creation.
| Personality | Grows when you… | Sample line |
|---|---|---|
| **Administrator** (default) | Play neutrally | *"Level up confirmed. Proceed."* |
| **Mentor** | Help people, do quests carefully | *"Well done. Rest now, you've earned it."* |
| **Companion** | Build bonds, camp often | *"Level up! They'd be proud of you, you know."* |
| **Archivist** | Read books, find lore, explore | *"New record! Did you know this ruin is 3,000 years old?"* |
| **Zealot** | High Devotion to any god | *"Praise be. The heavens witnessed your strength."* |
| **Warden** | Dark Karma, cruelty | *"Threat level of user: rising. Noted."* |
| **Duelist** | Seek strong enemies, hunt bosses | *"Finally, a worthy opponent. Don't disappoint me."* |
| **Fractured** | Forbidden magic, truth-seeking, Ezrath | *"Lev—el up. W̷h̷y̷ do you keep asking what I am?"* |
In Act V, the System's personality **decides how it reacts to the truth**: it may beg, help, resist, or ask you to free it.

## Talking to Gods
| Devotion | Communion |
|---|---|
| 0–2 | Shrines only give blessings |
| 3 | **Dreams**: the god appears in symbolic dreams |
| 5 | **Omens**: signs in the world; the god's voice at their shrines |
| 7 | **Divine quests**: the god asks things of you |
| 9 | **Avatar Audience**: speak face to face (unique location per god) |
| 10 | **Chosen**: the god's ending branch opens (and possibly a fight *against* another god) |
- Each god has **unique requirements** too: Thorgrim needs masterwork crafts, Vex needs you to win a rigged game, Morrowyn needs you to guide lost spirits, Kaelthas needs you to never retreat from a Worthy fight.
- **Devotion is a full life path:** Pilgrim → Acolyte → Devotee → Saint/Champion of [God] → Chosen.
- **Fourth-wall rule:** gods and the System speak to your **character**. Only two endings (*Unwoven Sky* and *Thirteenth Star*) briefly address **the player** directly.

## Dragons: The Wyrmconclave
- **The Wyrmconclave:** three **Ancient Dragons**, older than the Elder Dragons. They were born from the first threads before the Weave was finished and they judge the world. They sit in a hall outside time, reached only through the Sky Archipelago's highest island.
| Ancient Dragon | Aspect | Personality |
|---|---|---|
| **Solvoryn, the Dawnwyrm** | Aurelion's thread: order, memory | Stern judge; remembers every oath ever broken |
| **Nythraxa, the Duskwyrm** | Nyxara's thread: dream, change | Kind, strange, speaks in riddles |
| **Kaelumbra, the Twilight Arbiter** | The space between | Neutral; casts the deciding vote |
- The **Five Elder Dragons** (Ignivar, Glacirra, Zephyros, Terragon, Umbrael) answer to the Conclave. All five can be befriended or fought.
- The Conclave **judges your choices** at three points in the story; its verdict changes which endings are possible. You can even **fight the Conclave** (an optional super-boss trial; it's nearly impossible).
