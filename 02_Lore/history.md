# 02b — The Deep History of Aetheria (v0.3)

> *"History is the Weave remembering itself. Badly, and in its own favor."*
> — Magistra Eolande Fairweather, *Lectures I Was Fired For*

Dates: **BSC** = Before the Summoning Calendar · **SC** = Summoning Calendar. **Now = 1024 SC.**

---

## I. THE AGE BEFORE AGES (∞ → ~50,000 BSC)
- **The Thread** drifts alone through the void. Aether, raw possibility.
- It folds into the **Two Hands**: **Aurelion** (Dawn: order, form) and **Nyxara** (Dusk: dream, change).
- Together they build **the Loom** and weave **the Great Weave**: land, sea, sky and souls.
- The Weave has a flaw at its center: a knot that never tightened, called **the Unclosed Knot**. Every later disaster leaks from it.
- Exhausted, the Two Hands fall into a sleep. Aurelion becomes the **sun's light**, Nyxara the **space between stars**. Some scholars believe the two moons are their dreaming eyes.

## II. THE AGE OF DRAGONS (~50,000 → ~30,000 BSC)
- The **Five Elder Dragons** wake: **Ignivar** (Fire), **Glacirra** (Ice), **Zephyros** (Storm), **Terragon** (Earth), **Umbrael** (Void). Each one is a living **pillar** that holds part of the Weave taut.
- The first mortals (proto-elves, proto-dwarves, the "First Folk") live in the dragons' shadow, worshipping them as gods.
- **The Draconic Covenant:** dragons protect mortals; mortals keep the ley lines clean. The Covenant holds for 20,000 years.
- Draconic becomes the first written language. Its runes still appear on the oldest ruins.

## III. THE AGE OF TITANS (~30,000 → ~12,000 BSC)
- **The Titans** wake: giants of stone, crystal and machinery, the Weave's leftover craftsmen. They are led by **Atharos, the First Titan**.
- They **reshape continents**, raise mountains, and build the **Sky Cities** (today's Sky Archipelago) powered by Aether engines.
- **The Eight gods** rise in this age as Aurelion and Nyxara's youngest threads: Solenne, Maelis, Thorgrim, Sylvara, Kaelthas, Morrowyn, Vex and Ilvaris. With them is a **ninth: Ezrath the Unbound**, god of freedom and choice.
- The gods teach mortals magic, craft and faith. Elves learn from Sylvara, dwarves from Thorgrim, humans from Solenne and Kaelthas.
- **Atharos discovers the Unclosed Knot** and learns the Weave is slowly fraying. His solution: **unweave fate itself**, so that every being has full free will and the Weave reorganizes around choices instead of destiny. **Ezrath agrees.**
- **Ilvaris disagrees.** She believes unbinding fate will make the Weave collapse.

## IV. THE SUNDERING (~12,000 BSC)
- **The War of Heaven and Stone:** Titans and Ezrath against the other gods, who are joined by most dragons.
- **Umbrael** (Void Dragon) sides with the Titans, then betrays them at the final battle. It's unclear for whom.
- Atharos is struck down. His body falls and becomes the bedrock beneath the central continent; his tomb later becomes **the Great Labyrinth**.
- The final blow **cracks the sky**: the **Shattered Sky**, the scar visible in the north.
- The crack opens **the Abyss**: a space beneath the Weave where loose Aether pools. From it the first **demons** are born. They are not evil by nature, but made of raw, hungry possibility. The Abyss becomes the **Nox Dominion**.
- **Ezrath is erased.** Ilvaris unweaves his name from history. Murals are scratched, prayers forgotten. He survives only as a **sealed, wandering echo** (the Hooded Traveler).
- **The World-Eater Wolf, Garmrok**, a beast born from the war's wounds, is chained to the Shattered Sky by the gods.
- **The Status is born.** To stop the Weave from fraying further, **Ilvaris weaves the System**: a lattice of fate laid over every mortal soul. It gives mortals levels, skills and growth, *and* quietly channels the growth of the strongest souls back into the Weave to re-stitch it.
- Most mortals experience this as a **miracle**: *"The gods have given us the power to grow."*

## V. THE AGE OF FIRST KINGDOMS (~12,000 → ~3,000 BSC)
| Era | Event |
|---|---|
| ~11,000 BSC | Elves plant the **World Tree Lothaniel** on Sylvara's grave-seed; the **Sylvaran Court** forms |
| ~10,500 BSC | Dwarves found **Karak Dûn** in the mountains; Thorgrim's forge is lit |
| ~9,000 BSC | Humans form tribal kingdoms in the central plains |
| ~8,000 BSC | **Beastkin** emerge, born where Sylvara's wild magic touched ordinary animals and people. Their origin myth: "the First Hunt" |
| ~7,000 BSC | **The Eastern Isles** close themselves off behind a spirit barrier; the Fox Shrine is founded |
| ~6,000 BSC | **Valdrisia** is founded in the (then green) Sunscar region: humans and elves who learned to read **Old Weave-tongue** |
| ~5,000 BSC | **The Felled Grove War**: dwarves dig into the World Tree's roots for *Heartwood ore*. The elves go to war. The wound becomes the first trace of the **Blight**. The feud still lasts |
| ~4,500 BSC | Valdrisia becomes the **Old Magic Empire**: towers that touch the clouds, enslaved beastkin labor, ley-line farms |
| ~3,200 BSC | Valdrisian archmagi decode the System and realize it **harvests souls** |

## VI. THE LONG NIGHT (~3,000 BSC)
- The Valdrisian Archmage **Malgareth Vey** tries to **break the System** with a ritual using an entire empire's mana: the **Night of Unweaving**.
- The ritual fails catastrophically. **Valdrisia vanishes in a single night.** Its people are pulled into the Weave as threads. The land turns to desert; glass deserts mark the ritual circles.
- Malgareth survives as something else: the **first Demon Lord**, a mortal who merged with the Abyss.
- **The Long Night:** 40 years of darkness as demons pour from the Abyss.
- An alliance of elves, dwarves, humans and beastkin defeats Malgareth at **the Battle of Ashen Gate**, at a horrible cost.
- **Morthis, Queen of Valdrisia**, was away from the capital that night. She's the only royal survivor. She spends the next three thousand years trying to bring her people back (today's General of Rot).

## VII. THE STAGNATION (~3,000 BSC → 0 SC)
- After the Long Night, **native mortals stop growing.** Souls hit invisible ceilings. The strongest natives cap around **B–A rank**.
- *Real reason:* the System, starved after Valdrisia's collapse, tightened its harvest to keep the Weave together.
- Every ~300–400 years a new **Demon Lord** rises from the Abyss, wherever a powerful soul touches it. Each one is beaten back more narrowly.
- Mortal kingdoms rise and fall: **the Thousand Crowns Era**.

## VIII. THE FIRST SUMMONING (Year 0 SC)
- A Demon Lord called **Seraxis, the Crimson Mother** (a demon born of the Abyss itself) rises and threatens to drown the central continent.
- **Ilvaris speaks to the Church** for the first time in millennia and gives them the **Summoning Rite**: a ritual that pulls a soul from **the World Beyond the Veil** at the moment of its death.
- **Hero #1: Kaede Aoyama** (an otherworlder) is summoned. Her soul has **no growth ceiling**.
  - She was a **video gamer** in her old world. She calls her Status a "game UI," levels obsessively, and gives the Guild its **F-to-S letter ranks** (later extended to SS and SSS). Most people today don't know the ranks come from an otherworlder's game.
  - Her companions: **Aldric the Steadfast** (human knight), **Lirael** (elven archer) and **Borin Ashhand** (dwarf smith).
  - **Year 7 SC:** she defeats Seraxis.
  - **Year 12 SC:** she writes the **Wanderer's Charter** and founds the **Adventurers' Guild**: "a place for anyone, of any race or birth, to rise by deeds alone."
  - **Year 52 SC:** Aldric becomes **King Aldric I**; the **Kingdom of Aldmere** ("Aldric's Mere") is founded.
  - **Year 61 SC:** Kaede reaches **SSS** and "ascends." Officially she became a star. In truth, **the System harvested her**. Her echo still lingers on Labyrinth Floor 110.

## XII. OPEN MYSTERIES (truths hidden in the world)
1. **Did Malgareth really die?** His glass-desert circles still hum.
2. **Whose side was Umbrael on** in the Sundering?
3. **What is the Unclosed Knot**, and can it be closed? (Ending: *The Thirteenth Star*)
4. **Is Ezrath good?** He gives truth and freedom, but freedom could unravel the world.
5. **Why can otherworld souls grow without limit?** (Theory: the World Beyond the Veil has no System.)
6. **What happened to Kaede's companions' descendants?** (The current royal family, and someone else.)
