# 02c — Main Story Beat Sheet (v0.3)

Tone curve: **warm, funny and adventurous (Acts I–II) → political and tragic (III–IV) → cosmic and personal (V–VI).** Choices are tracked as **Story Flags** and change scenes, companions and endings.

---

## ACT I — *The Wooden Card* (Rank F → E · ~10–30 hrs)
**Theme:** belonging, a new home, first friends. **Region:** Brindlewick, Whisperwood, Millbrook.

| # | Beat | Notes / choices |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Registration.** Mina hands you an oak card. The crystal appraisal flickers strangely | Mina covers it up: *"Equipment's old, ha ha."* |
| 2 | **First contracts:** rabbits, herbs, the crying mandrake | Tutorial loops; meet Hollis, Granny Mabel, Torvald |
| 3 | **Lyra joins.** Her village owes a debt; she needs money fast | Bond starts |
| 4 | **"The Goblin Who Shouldn't Exist"**: a hobgoblin with a glowing red eye leads organized raids | First hint of Red Moon Cult experiments |
| 5 | **Night of the Smiling Scarecrow** (first horror quest) | Teaches the Menace system |
| 6 | **Whisperwood ruins**: a mural with nine thrones, one scratched | Mystery #1 seeded |
| 7 | **Rank trial: The Goblin Chief of Hollowpine** | You find the red-eyed hobgoblin was **made**: cult runes carved into him |
| 8 | **Choice:** report the runes to the Guild or the Church | *Guild:* Mina and Rodrick investigate quietly · *Church:* an Inquisitor arrives and is suspiciously interested in **you** |
| 9 | **Rank E ceremony.** Party at the Sleeping Griffon. Whiskers the cat stares at you a little too long | — |
| 10 | **Recruitment choice:** Kiri (rescue from slavers on the Ashby road) **or** Sir Aldric (the drunk knight at the inn) | Mutually exclusive in Act I (the other returns later, changed) |

**Act I ending:** the red moon Vesper is seen **swelling** in daylight. Old Hollis whispers: *"That's not supposed to happen for another hundred years."*

## ACT II — *The Red Moon Rises* (D → C · ~30–140 hrs)
**Theme:** the world is bigger and more dangerous than you thought. **Regions:** Aldmere City, Greymoor, the Labyrinth's first floors.

| # | Beat | Notes / choices |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Advanced Class change**: your first real power spike | — |
| 2 | **To the capital.** Aldmere City, the Academy, the Cathedral, the three Hero statues (one smashed) | Mystery #2 seeded |
| 3 | **Meet Kaito** (rival summoned hero). He mocks you, then saves you, then mocks you for needing saving | Rival meter starts |
| 4 | **The Academy Exam**: Seraphine joins (after you humiliate her) | Academy questline opens |
| 5 | **The Labyrinth opens to you.** Floors 1–20 | The long-haul path begins |
| 6 | **Greymoor**: you find **Tomás Reyes's diary** in a witch's hut | He writes: *"The Lantern holds him. But the Lantern isn't a lock. It's a promise."* |
| 7 | **The Cult strikes**: Lantern Night festival, cultists try to steal Morrowyn's relics from the Cathedral | Big set-piece battle in the city |
| 8 | **Elira** (excommunicated Saint) helps you; the Church hunts her | **Choice:** protect her (Church reputation ↓) or hand her over (she may die, permanently) |
| 9 | **Siege of Marrowgate** (B-rank trial, if you're ready) or the Stampede world event | The first mass battle |
| 10 | **Twin Eclipse**: you meet **the Hooded Traveler** at a crossroads | He says your old-world name. Then: *"They'll tell you you're a hero. Ask them what heroes are for."* |

**Act II ending:** the **Lantern Seal cracks**. Across the world every Status window glitches for one second, and a voice that isn't the System speaks to everyone: *"I'm coming home."* (Azrael.)

## ACT III — *Kingdoms of Ash and Leaf* (C → B · ~140–240 hrs)
**Theme:** politics, old grudges, the cost of alliances. **Regions:** Sylvaran Forest, Iron Hold, Beastkin Plains (open order).

| # | Beat | Notes / choices |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **The Grand Council in Aldmere**: nations argue over the coming war. Grand Guildmaster **Celestine** asks you to unite them | Hub of Act III |
| 2 | **Sylvaran arc:** the World Tree's **Blight** is spreading. The elves blame the dwarves | Prince Thalien vs the Dark Elf exiles; restore or burn the blighted root |
| 3 | **Iron Hold arc:** dwarves mining deeper find Valdrisian engines. Torvald's past | Forge-city politics; choose a clan |
| 4 | **Beastkin arc:** beastkin **refuse to fight Azrael**. He freed their ancestors | You learn Hero #2's real story here. Kiri's arc peaks |
| 5 | **The Treaty of Ash and Leaf** summit | Your choices in arcs 2–4 decide who signs |
| 6 | **The Betrayal:** someone close to the party is a **Red Moon Cult** agent | Who depends on flags: Bram (debt-blackmailed), Sir Aldric (if his name was never cleared), or Kaito (if the rivalry went bad) |
| 7 | **Vexmor's capture**: a Demon General's daughter is taken prisoner | **Choice:** execute, imprison, or free her (opens the Demon side) |
| 8 | **The fall of Marrowgate**: whatever you do, one town falls to the first demon army (permanent Settlement change) | First big tragedy; a companion can die here if Mortal Bonds conditions are met |

**Act III ending:** the four **Demon Generals** are revealed. Each one strikes a region at once. The world map turns red in four places.

## ACT IV — *The Four Generals* (A → S · ~240–560 hrs)
**Theme:** war, sacrifice, understanding your enemy. **Structure:** fully **open**, tackle the Generals in any order. Each one you defeat changes the others' tactics.

| General | Region | What you learn | Big choice |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Ignarok**, the Flame Unbroken | Iron Hold | He was a dwarf champion who chose to become a demon rather than be harvested | Honorable death, or redemption (Torvald's lost brother) |
| **Morthis**, the Queen Under Sand | Sunscar | Valdrisia's people live **as threads in the Weave**: the System's first great harvest | Destroy her, or release her people's souls (Morrowyn path) |
| **Zephiel**, the Storm Architect | Sky Archipelago | Titan tech could replace the System, at enormous risk | Destroy his engine, or steal his research (Unwoven Sky path) |
| **Rhaka**, the Beast Mother | Beastkin Plains | The Guild farms monster cores; monsters are partly people | Kill her, or recruit her (changes the war and Monster Council) |

**Mid-act events:** Dragon Migration (meet the Elder Dragons), the Grand Tournament (Kaito's showdown), Nyx's true identity revealed, the Eastern Isles reopen their spirit barrier.
**Act IV ending:** with the Generals resolved, you're named **S-Rank, "Hero of the Age."** At the ceremony Celestine collapses and tells you everything she knows about Adrian.

## ACT V — *The Weaver's Lie* (S → SS · ~560–780 hrs)
**Theme:** truth, and what heroes are *for*.

| # | Beat |
|---|---|
| 1 | **Kaede's Journal**: you find Hero #1's Status journal in the Guild Vault. Her last entry: *"Level 300. The UI's turning gold. It feels like falling upward. If you're reading this, I didn't come back."* |
| 2 | **The Hall of Heroes** (Labyrinth 101–110): Kaede's echo confirms the harvest |
| 3 | **Archbishop Crowe's confession** (or his attempt to have you killed): the Church knew, and summons heroes **to feed the Weave** |
| 4 | **Azrael parleys with you**, in a dream, in your old-world body. He shows you his memories as Adrian: the betrayal, Celestine, the beastkin |
| 5 | **Ezrath reveals himself.** The Forgotten Ninth. He offers to help you destroy the System entirely |
| 6 | **Garmrok breaks its chain.** The World-Eater Wolf hunts across regions (Calamity event) |
| 7 | **The Choice of Allegiance:** Gods · Azrael · Ezrath · Nobody (Slow-Life withdrawal) · or the hidden path: **close the Unclosed Knot** |

## ACT VI — *Ascension* (SS → SSS · ~780–1,000+ hrs)
- Path-specific final arcs (each 30–80 hrs), the Calamities, Labyrinth 111–120, the Elder Dragons' judgment.
- **Endings** (see Vision doc) plus **variants**: who lived, who died, which towns thrived, your romance, your Named monster companions, the fate of Kaito, Vexmor and Celestine.
