# Regional encounter tables

Generated by `node Tools/world/build_encounters.js` into `Data/World/encounters.json`. Do not edit by hand: change the source data or the `THEMES` block in the generator and rerun.

Every table row names a species that actually lives in that region (from `Data/Monsters/population.json`), stays inside the region's rank band, and excludes Tyrants and Uniques. The random-encounter director reads the JSON; this page is the human view.

## How a region is cut into tables
| Zone | Where | Who |
|---|---|---|
| roadside | Within 60 m of a road, pass or settlement gate. | Calm creatures only. Never inside a settlement. |
| wild | Open land in the region. | The middle of the rank band. |
| deep | Ruins, dens, lairs, caves and deep forest. | The top of the band, hostile first. |
| night | Replaces wild/deep between dusk and dawn. | Hostile residents seen at night. |

Group size comes from behaviour (Pack 3-5, Swarm 4-8, Territorial 1-2, Ambush 1, Aggressive 1-3, Passive 1-3, Skittish 1-2, Guardian 1) and shrinks with rank: B- and above at most 3 (Pack) or 2, A- and above at most 2 or 1, S- and above always 1.

**Safe rule:** No random combat inside settlements, within 150 m of an inn or waystone, or on a road within noCombatWithinMeters of either end.

**Tyrants:** A region's Tyrant is never in a random table. It appears only through the siege system (siege_waves.json) and its lair.
