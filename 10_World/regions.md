# 10 — The World of Aetheria: Regions (v0.2)

> *"Every road in Aetheria leads somewhere wonderful. Most of them also lead somewhere that wants to eat you."*


Each region has: **a leitmotif**, **a postcard moment**, **3 story dungeons**, **10–15 dens**, **1–2 ruins**, **1–2 field bosses**, **3–5 Named monsters**, **20–40 side quests**, **secrets**.

---

## 1. KINGDOM OF ALDMERE — *"The Heartland"* (F–C)
**Mood:** rolling green hills, windmills, stone bridges, cherry orchards, golden wheat at sunset. Classic fantasy, warm and safe-looking (until night).
**Leitmotif:** pastoral flute + strings; becomes brass and choir in the capital.
**Postcard moment:** cresting Hollowpine Hill at dawn. Brindlewick below, Aldmere City's white towers on the horizon, Lumen still visible in the morning sky.

| Sub-zone | Rank | Feature |
|---|---|---|
| **Brindlewick & Meadows** | F | Starter town; horned rabbits, slimes; the training grounds |
| **Whisperwood** | F–E | Old forest; goblins, wolves, wisps; elven ruins; the White Stag |
| **Millbrook Farmlands** | E | Farms, cockatrices, Old Ironhide the beetle; farm plot purchase |
| **Hollowpine Highlands** | E–D | Goblin camps, bandit forts; first dungeon |
| **Greymoor Marsh** | D | Fog, undead, the Lantern Widow; witch's hut |
| **Aldmere City & Royal Valley** | D–C | Capital, Summoning Cathedral, Academy, Labyrinth entrance |
| **Greyhollow Keep** | C | Haunted castle; Sir Aldric's past |

**Settlements:**
- **Brindlewick:** guild branch, the *Sleeping Griffon Inn* (with Whiskers the cat), Hollis's training yard, Granny Mabel's farm, Torvald's forge.
- **Millbrook:** windmills, harvest festival.
- **Ashby:** plague village quest.
- **Aldmere City:** 5 districts (Noble, Market, Temple, Academy, Slums).

**Dungeons:** Hollowpine Goblin Camp (E), Proving Cave (E–D), Silkhollow Cave (D), Greyhollow Keep (C), Cathedral Catacombs (C).
**Secrets:** the 400-year-old cat; a hidden fairy ring that opens only during Festival of Blossoms; Hollis's buried sword; a "lost otherworlder's diary" with notes from a previous summoned person.

## 2. SYLVARAN FOREST — *"The Green Cathedral"* (D–B)
**Mood:** trees as tall as castles, bioluminescent mushrooms, fairy lights, mist, waterfalls, a canopy city.
**Leitmotif:** harp, choir, wooden flutes.
**Postcard moment:** the first sight of the **World Tree Lothaniel**, its crown above the clouds, glowing leaves drifting down like snow.

| Sub-zone | Rank | Feature |
|---|---|---|
| Fairy Glades | D | Pixies, wisps, the fairy court |
| Moonwell Lake | D+ | Undines, a lake library underwater |
| The Weeping Roots | C | Corrupted area: the World Tree's sickness |
| Canopy City Lothaniel | C | Elven capital in the branches |
| Thornwild | B | Treants, man-eater plants, blight |
| Elder Grove | B+ | Ancient Treant, Sylvara's shrine |

**Story:** the World Tree is dying of the **Blight** (Blighted World Root Calamity). Elven politics are split between purists and the Dark Elf exiles' return.
**Secrets:** the drowned library of Moonwell; a Dark Elf entrance to Umbravel; a unicorn glade (Karma 500+).

## 3. BEASTKIN PLAINS — *"The Endless Gold"* (D–B)
**Mood:** savannah, endless grass, herds of thunder-elk, stone monoliths, nomad yurt cities, huge sunsets.
**Leitmotif:** drums, throat singing, wind instruments.
**Postcard moment:** thousands of thunderhorn elk stampeding beneath a lightning storm.

| Sub-zone | Rank | Feature |
|---|---|---|
| Fanghold | D | Federation capital; **Grand Arena** |
| Thunder Steppe | C | Elk herds, Thunderhorn Behemoth field boss |
| Bone Valley | C+ | Ancient beast graveyard |
| Slaver's Road | C | Kiri's arc |
| Monolith Circle | B | Rhaka's domain |

**Systems showcased:** arena ladder (F–SS brackets), mounted combat, beast taming haven.

## 4. IRON HOLD PEAKS — *"The Hammer Under the Mountain"* (C–A)
**Mood:** volcanic mountains, lava rivers, huge dwarven halls carved into mountains, rune-lit doors, mine carts.
**Leitmotif:** anvil percussion, deep male choirs, war horns.
**Postcard moment:** the **Great Forge of Karak Dûn** opening, a lava waterfall feeding a forge the size of a city.

Sub-zones: Karak Dûn (capital), the Ashen Mines, Ember Rift, Ancestor Halls, the Molten Throne (Ignarok's seat).
**Systems:** legendary forging, rune crafting, minecart races, a dwarven brewing contest.

## 5. EASTERN ISLES (Shogunate of Hoshizora) — *"Islands of a Thousand Shrines"* (C–A)
**Mood:** pagodas, torii-style gates (original design), bamboo forests, hot springs, cherry blossoms, mountain shrines, lantern festivals, spirit world overlay.
**Leitmotif:** koto, shakuhachi, taiko.
**Postcard moment:** a lantern festival on the water, thousands of floating lights under a full Lumen.

Sub-zones: Hoshizora City, Bamboo Sea, Spirit Mountain, the Oni Volcano, Fox Shrine Isle (Suzune's home), Pirate Cove.
**Monsters:** original yokai-inspired spirits: umbrella-ghosts, fox spirits, lantern spirits, kappa-like river imps (original names and designs), Oni.
**Systems:** Ninja/Samurai classes, spirit world shift (see invisible spirits by night), hot spring rest scenes (wholesome, no fanservice).

## 6. FROSTVEIL NORTH — *"Where the Sky Is Broken"* (B–A)
**Mood:** tundra, glaciers, frozen waterfalls, longhouses, aurora (the Shattered Sky glows here).
**Leitmotif:** solo female vocal, cello, wind.
**Postcard moment:** standing on a glacier at night as the **Shattered Sky** ripples above in every color.

Sub-zones: Skjaldheim (Northfolk city), Glacier Teeth, the Frozen Sea, Giant's Rest, Glacirra's Lake.
**Systems:** survival-lite (cold meter, warmth from fire spells/food), dog-sled or Fenrir mount, giant diplomacy.

## 7. SUNSCAR DESERT — *"The Empire That Vanished"* (B–A)
**Mood:** endless dunes, ruined Valdrisian cities half-buried, oasis towns, sandstorms, pyramids, glass deserts (from ancient magic).
**Leitmotif:** oud, darbuka, haunting vocals.
**Postcard moment:** a sandstorm clears to reveal a **ghost city**, Valdrisia as it was, shimmering for one minute.

Sub-zones: Oasis of Qadira, Glass Wastes, the Sunken Capital, Pyramid of Morthis, Sphinx Gate.
**Systems:** heat/water survival-lite, sand-ship travel, tomb puzzles, Sphinx riddles.

## 8. ABYSSAL COAST — *"The Edge of the Deep"* (B–S)
**Mood:** jagged coasts, pirate towns on stilts, coral reefs, an underwater merfolk kingdom, storms.
**Leitmotif:** sea shanties, accordion, deep brass for the abyss.
**Postcard moment:** diving through a reef as a sky whale passes overhead, its shadow on the water.

Sub-zones: Port Goldmere (Consortium), Saltwater Grotto, Coral Kingdom of Ys, Shipwreck Graveyard, the Abyssal Trench.
**Systems:** sailing (own a ship), underwater exploration (breathing magic), naval battles, fishing legends.

## 9. DRAGON SPINE MOUNTAINS — *"The Mountain That Breathes"* (A–S)
**Mood:** cloud-piercing peaks, dragon bone bridges, abandoned dragon monasteries, eagles' nests, thin air.
**Postcard moment:** realizing the mountain range you've climbed for an hour is **Terragon**, and he just opened an eye.
**Systems:** climbing, flying mounts, dragon diplomacy, Draconic language.

## 10. SKY ARCHIPELAGO — *"The Cities of the Titans"* (S–SS)
**Mood:** floating islands with waterfalls pouring into the void, ancient Titan machinery, crystal spires, airships, sky whales.
**Postcard moment:** your airship rising through the clouds into a sunrise over a hundred floating islands.
**Systems:** airship ownership and combat, gliding between islands, Titan tech crafting.

## 11. NOX DOMINION (Demon Realm) — *"Under the Red Moon"* (S–SSS)
**Mood:** permanent twilight, Vesper huge in the sky, black-glass forests, rivers of red light, and surprisingly a **living demon civilization** (markets, children, poets). Not just evil.
**Postcard moment:** the Nox Citadel silhouetted against a full Blood Vesper.
**Systems:** demon reputation, Crimson Throne path, morality inversion (here, Holy acts make you suspicious).

## 12. THE GREAT LABYRINTH — *"The Titan's Tomb"* (F → EX)
- Entrance beneath Aldmere City, guarded by the Guild.
- **120 floors**, 12 biome bands (10 floors each):

| Floors | Biome | Rank |
|---|---|---|
| 1–10 | Stone maze | F–D |
| 11–20 | Sunken temple | D–C |
| 21–30 | Fungal swamp | C |
| 31–40 | Bone cathedral | C–B |
| 41–50 | Lava fields | B–A |
| 51–60 | Frozen ocean | A |
| 61–70 | Mirror world | A–S |
| 71–80 | Clockwork Titan machinery | S |
| 81–90 | Void edge | S–SS |
| 91–100 | Inside the Titan's heart | SS |
| 101–110 | Hall of Heroes (hidden) | SS+ |
| 111–120 | The Loom (secret) | EX |

- **Safe-floor towns** every 10 floors (underground merchants, a dwarven tavern on floor 30, a ghost market on 40).
- Each floor mixes **handcrafted rooms with procedural layouts**.
- **Labyrinth Records**: personal best times, deepest floor (local leaderboard + Steam achievements).

## 14. Dynamic World
- **Day/night**, **weather** (rain, storm, fog, snow, sandstorm, ash), **two moons** and **seasons** all change spawns, NPC schedules and quests.
- **Settlement states:** thriving / threatened / under siege / ruined / rebuilt. Your actions change them.
- **Monster migrations** and ecosystems (see Bestiary).

## v0.3 Notes
- **One seamless world**, expanding to **25+ regions** (human, beastkin, elven, monster-dominated, and more), to be detailed in a dedicated pass.
