# 10c — Culture Research Pass v0.5 (Q5: "go mad, look everywhere")

Research covered: community worldbuilding lists on MyAnimeList, anime and manga worldbuilding articles, fantasy literature discussions (Goodreads), mythology guides for under-used folklore, and murim/xianxia genre conventions. **12 new cultures and regions** are below, plus the cultivation track (Q4) and the Underworld entrance network (Q6).

> **Respect rule:** for living traditions (Inuit, Andean, Filipino, Khmer and others), we draw on *themes and atmosphere*, invent our own names and spirits, and avoid using sacred figures or rites directly.

---

## A. NEW CULTURES & REGIONS

### 31. 🌀 The Dunewhale Drift (B–A): *a town on the back of a whale, sailing a sea of sand*
- **Inspired by:** *Kujira no Kora wa Sajou ni Utau* (*Children of the Whales*): the Mud Whale, a floating island drifting over an endless sea of sand, where an ability shortens its users' lives. Also the *Dune* sandworm.
- **Our version:** **Thalassa**, a colossal sand-leviathan whose back holds a nomad town. It swims through the Sunscar dune-sea on a yearly migration. Its people, the **Driftfolk**, can "sing" to sand (**Sandsong** magic), but every song shortens their lives. They keep an **Archive of the Short-Lived**.
- **Gameplay:** a moving region (it's in a different place each season); find it by tracking its song.

### 32. 🌀 Lustrine Shores (A–S): *the gem people*
- **Inspired by:** *Land of the Lustrous* (*Houseki no Kuni*): a world where gem-like beings live.
- **Our version:** the **Lustrine**, crystalline people (each a different gem with its own hardness and fragility) living on glass beaches. They shatter rather than die and can be **reassembled**. On every **Blood Vesper**, **Vesperite raiders** (Malgareth's moon-servants) descend to harvest them.
- **Gameplay:** a playable **Lustrine race** unlock (high magic, fragile; you can lose and replace limbs with other gems for new powers).

### 33. 🌀 The Mossdeep of the Thimblefolk (D–C): *the tiny world*
- **Inspired by:** *Hakumei to Mikochi* (tiny people living in the forest), *The Borrowers*, *Arrietty*.
- **Our version:** a forest where the **Thimblefolk** (9 cm tall) live in mushrooms and hollow logs, ride beetles and trade with mice. A fairy-ring potion shrinks **you**. Suddenly a horned rabbit is a kaiju.
- **Gameplay:** a cozy and terrifying scale-shift region. F-rank monsters become boss-sized. One of the most memorable places in the game.

### 34. 🏰 The Mana-Tithe Duchies of Velmark (C–B)
- **Inspired by:** *Ascendance of a Bookworm*, where noble rank follows mana capacity rather than wealth and noble society is severe and political; also its feystones and mana-fed weapons.
- **Our version:** small duchies where **nobles must pour their mana into the land's Foundation Stones** every season or the crops die. Mana-rich commoners are "adopted" into nobility, and some are trapped there. Your Veilborn mana is **politically priceless**: three dukes compete for you.
- **Gameplay:** political intrigue, mana-tithe rituals (minigame), and a court mystery arc.

### 35. 🌀 The Serene Vale of Ashira (B): *the perfect village (horror)*
- **Inspired by:** *Shinsekai Yori* (*From the New World*): a seemingly utopian future with psychic powers in small, isolated villages.
- **Our version:** a valley where everyone has gentle telekinesis, everyone smiles, and children are tested at 12. **Children who fail disappear.** The village's "monsters" are the descendants of those who failed.
- **Gameplay:** a slow-burn horror mystery; Menace 4–5; a hard moral choice at the end.

### 36. 🏰 Cinderhole, the Smoke City (A)
- **Inspired by:** *Dorohedoro* (sorcerers who use the non-magical as test subjects), *Arcane*'s Zaun.
- **Our version:** a city below Gearholm where rogue mages test spells on the magic-less; smoke-breathing mages; masked gangs; a lizard-headed vigilante legend (an original character inspired by the genre).
- **Gameplay:** black-market magic, the Abyssal Pit Fights, a revenge storyline.

### 37. 🌀 The Isles of Lumbaya (B–A): *jungle temples and night terrors*
- **Inspired by:** Southeast Asian folklore and settings: a Khmer-inspired sword-and-sorcery setting, and Filipino creatures like the *aswang* (shapeshifting vampire) and *tikbalang* (horse-headed trickster spirit).
- **Our version:** jungle archipelago with **giant stone-faced temple-cities** swallowed by roots, rice terraces, longboat races, shaman-healers (**Babaylans**). Night brings **Nightshifters** (our own shapeshifting vampires who pretend to be villagers) and **Crossroad Steeds** (horse-headed tricksters who make you lose your way).
- **Gameplay:** jungle exploration, temple puzzles, a night-horror village mystery.

### 38. 🌀 The Whitewater Reach (A–S): *the sea-mother's ice*
- **Inspired by:** Arctic folklore themes: a sea spirit who controls marine life and weather, ice giants, transforming shamans.
- **Our version:** frozen coasts where the **Deep Mother**, an original sea spirit, withholds fish and seals when angered. **Spirit-walkers** dive to comb her tangled hair to calm her. **Frost giants** sleep under the ice. Dog-sled travel (ground transport).
- **Gameplay:** survival-lite, a sea-spirit appeasement quest, ice-fishing legends.

### 39. 🌀 The Apu Highlands (A–S): *mountains that are gods*
- **Inspired by:** Andean cosmology themes: a three-tiered cosmos (upper, middle and inner worlds), mountain spirits, a creator who made and destroyed giants before forming humans from stone.
- **Our version:** terraced mountain cities; three-world shrines (Sky, Earth, Deep), with doors to the **Underworld**. **Mountain Lords**, actual living mountains, can be spoken to. **Stone Giants of the First Making** tie into the Titans. Woolly **vicuñal** pack-beasts (pack animals that can die, Q45).
- **Gameplay:** high-altitude exploration, a mountain-spirit council, an Underworld gateway region.

### 40. 🏰 The Peacock Throne of Zarvan (B–A): *the garden-epic empire*
- **Inspired by:** Persian epic tradition (the *Shahnameh*, an influence even on early Slavic fantasy), paradise gardens, the simurgh myth, *Magi*.
- **Our version:** rose-garden cities, fire temples, poets' duels, hero lineages recorded in an endless **Book of Kings** that still writes itself. **Divs** (horned giants) in the mountains. A great healing bird-spirit, **Simurrah**, guards the high peaks.
- **Gameplay:** epic duels, poetry battles (Bard heaven), and the chance to get **written into the Book of Kings** (a legacy system).

### 41. 🌀 The Quaking Lands (S): *the earth that wants to break*
- **Inspired by:** N. K. Jemisin's *Broken Earth* (praised for blending many themes): a world of recurring catastrophes and feared earth-calmers.
- **Our version:** a region where **earthquakes come every hour**. **Stillers**, mages who can calm or cause quakes, are feared, enslaved and drafted. Cities are built on springs and floating platforms.
- **Gameplay:** dynamic terrain (the map literally shifts), a Stiller liberation arc.

### 42. 🏰 Shen-Lu's Martial World (inside the Jade Dynasty)
- **Inspired by:** **murim** manhwa: an orthodox alliance against a demonic cult led by a feared "Heavenly Demon"; demonic sects shown as political organizations with internal struggles; protagonists who are wrongly accused of demonic arts.
- **Our version:** the **Righteous Alliance of Nine Sects** vs the **Crimson Heaven Cult** (no relation to the Red Moon Cult: they hate each other). The Cult is a real political power with factions; its leader, the **Heavenly Sovereign**, may be more honorable than the Alliance.
- **Gameplay:** join either side; a false-accusation questline; martial tournaments.

## B. Q4: THE CULTIVATION TRACK (decided: full parallel progression, available everywhere, centered in Shen-Lu)
Research: xianxia realm lists vary by work. Common stages are Body Refinement, Qi Condensation, Foundation Establishment, Core Formation, Nascent Soul, Soul/Spirit Formation and Void Refinement, each with early/middle/late/peak sub-stages, and breakthroughs that can require a heavenly lightning tribulation.

| Realm | Sub-stages | Unlocks | Breakthrough trial |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 · **Body Tempering** | Early/Mid/Late/Peak | Body passives | Waterfall training |
| 2 · **Qi Condensation** | ×4 | Qi meter, Qi skills | Meditation minigame |
| 3 · **Foundation Establishment** | ×4 | Flying sword *techniques* (combat only, not travel) | **Lightning Tribulation** boss fight |
| 4 · **Core Formation** | ×4 | Golden Core: +regen, pill alchemy | Inner-demon duel |
| 5 · **Nascent Soul** | ×4 | Soul projection (scout as a spirit) | Tribulation II |
| 6 · **Spirit Severing** | ×4 | Domains | Sever an attachment (story choice) |
| 7 · **Void Refinement** | ×4 | Space techniques | Tribulation III |
| 8 · **Immortal Ascension** | — | Sect Ancestor title | The Heavens' Trial (ties into the System's truth: the System *fears* cultivators) |
- **Lore:** cultivation was taught by the Veilborn Chen Wei. It grows power **without the System's harvest channel**, so the Church and Ilvaris quietly fear it. That gives it a big role in the *Unwoven Sky* ending.
- **Balance:** cultivation realms add their own power layer. Guild rank stays the world's main "language," but sects respect only realms.

## C. Q6: UNDERWORLD ENTRANCE NETWORK ("Deepgates")
The whole map connects to the Underworld, mostly through **mountains and specific landmark sites**. Target: **60+ entrances.**
| Entrance type | Where | Count |
|---|---|---|
| **Mountain caverns** | Iron Hold, Dragon Spine, Apu Highlands, Frostveil | ~20 |
| **Volcanic vents** | Iron Hold, Oni volcano (Isles), Cinderfall | ~6 |
| **Sinkholes & cenotes** | Mire Kingdoms, Lumbaya jungles, Quaking Lands | ~8 |
| **Ancient wells & tombs** | Sunscar, Aldmere crypts, Zarvan | ~8 |
| **Titan scars** | Calamity Steppe, Bastion | ~4 |
| **World Tree roots** | Sylvaran Forest | 2 |
| **Labyrinth back-doors** | Hidden on floors 30/60/90 | 3 |
| **The Maw** | One great vertical pit | 1 |
| **Apu three-world shrines** | Apu Highlands | 3 |
| **Secret / player-dug** | Mining skill can open some | ~5+ |
- Each **Deepgate** has a **guardian**, a **camp** after first clear (fast-travel point), and a **layer tag** (which Underworld layer it reaches).
