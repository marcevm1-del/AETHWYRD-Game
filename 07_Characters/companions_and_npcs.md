# 07 — Companions, NPCs, Villains & Factions (v0.2)

## 1. Companion Rules
- **Party:** you + 1 (F) → 2 (E) → 3 (D) → 4 (B). Extra companions wait at your home/guild house.
- Each companion has: a **class tree** (you guide it), a **personal questline** (5–8 quests), a **Bond meter** (0–10), a **Loyalty** state (can leave), **opinions** on your choices, **Union Skills** with you, and an optional **romance** (no gameplay gating).
- **Camp scenes:** at night you can camp. Party banter, cooking, stories by the fire. This is the heart of the fantasy feel.

## 2. The Twelve Companions
### 1. LYRA BRIGHTWOOD — *the First Party Member*
- **Race/Class:** Human, Swordsman → (Blademaster → Sword Saint)
- **Personality:** loud, cheerful, terrible at money, eats like three people; dreams of being S-Rank to save her poor village.
- **Recruit:** registers at the guild the same day you do.
- **Arc:** *"The Girl Who Couldn't Win"*: she keeps losing duels to her childhood rival; you help her find her own sword style. Ends with her inventing a new technique named after your party.
- **Union Skill:** *Twin Blossom Edge*.
- **Theme:** upbeat flute and strings.

### 2. SERAPHINE VALCREST — *the Tsundere Prodigy*
- **Race/Class:** High Elf, Mage → Elementalist → Archmage
- **Personality:** noble, sharp-tongued, secretly insecure; "it's not like I wanted to join your party."
- **Recruit:** humiliated in the Mages' Academy entrance duel by you, demands a rematch, then joins "to study you."
- **Arc:** *"The Ninth Element"*: her family's forbidden research (Void magic). She chooses between family honor and the truth.
- **Union Skill:** *Prism Requiem*.

### 3. KIRI — *the Freed Wolf*
- **Race/Class:** Wolf-kin, Rogue → Assassin or Beastwarden (your influence decides)
- **Personality:** quiet, fierce, food-motivated; learns to smile over the course of the story.
- **Recruit:** rescue her from slavers on the Ashby road (or buy her freedom, which has different Karma and dialogue).
- **Arc:** *"Silver Fang"*: finding her lost pack and confronting the slaver lord. Choice: revenge or mercy.
- **Union Skill:** *Moonfang Crossing*.

### 4. SIR ALDRIC VOSS — *the Disgraced Knight*
- **Race/Class:** Human, Guardian → Paladin → Holy Knight (or Crusader if his faith breaks)
- **Personality:** stoic, dry humor, drinks too much; once captain of the Royal Guard.
- **Arc:** *"The Oath at Greyhollow"*: the massacre he was blamed for. The real culprit is a current noble. Clear his name or let him become a vigilante.

### 5. NYX — *the 900-Year-Old Dragon*
- **Race/Class:** Appears as a child; actually **Umbrael's daughter**, an Elder-Dragon spawn (hidden SS-Rank)
- **Personality:** smug, ancient, lazy; calls you "little mortal"; loves sweets.
- **Recruit:** found asleep in a ruined temple, follows you because "you smell interesting."
- **Arc:** *"Scales of the Void"*: her father is a Calamity. In Act V she regains her true dragon form (fights beside you; never rideable).

### 6. BRAM COPPERPOT — *the Lucky Coward*
- **Race/Class:** Halfling, Merchant (hidden: Gambler)
- **Personality:** greedy, cowardly, absurdly lucky, loyal underneath; runs away from every fight and somehow lands a crit.
- **Arc:** *"The Debt of a Thousand Coins"*: he owes the Syndicate. Shenanigans, heists, a casino arc.

### 7. KAITO HAYASE — *the Rival Hero*
- **Race/Class:** Human (summoned), Hero candidate
- **Personality:** arrogant, desperate to be "the main character"; secretly terrified.
- **Arc:** rival → enemy → ally *or* fallen villain (corrupted by the Demon Lord) depending on your choices. One of the most branching characters.

### 8. MOCHI — *the Slime*
- **Race/Class:** Slime, evolving familiar
- **Personality:** no words; bounces; expressive. Becomes humanoid (C-rank) and learns to talk, very badly at first.
- **Recruit:** Day 1 (Transported) or found later.
- **Arc:** *"What Mochi Ate"*: Mochi once devoured something important from the Age of Titans. It can evolve into a Primordial being.

### 9. ELIRA SANCTIS — *the Fallen Saint*
- **Race/Class:** Human, Cleric → Saint (or Necromancer branch)
- **Personality:** gentle, guilt-ridden, quietly funny.
- **Arc:** *"The Heresy of Mercy"*: excommunicated for healing a demon child. The Church hunts her. The Inquisition arc.

### 10. VEXMOR AZHARRA — *the Demon General's Daughter*
- **Race/Class:** Demonkin, Warlock → Hexblade
- **Personality:** proud, teasing, honorable, hates hypocrites.
- **Recruit:** captured in Act III. Spare her, or don't.
- **Arc:** opens the Demon Realm side; she can become Demon Queen in the Crimson Throne ending.

### 11. TORVALD STONEBEARD — *the Old Dwarf Smith*
- **Race/Class:** Dwarf, Artisan → Runesmith
- **Personality:** grumpy grandpa; secretly sentimental; sings forge songs.
- **Arc:** *"The Unfinished Blade"*: forging the sword that killed his son's killer. Unlocks a Legendary weapon.

### 12. SUZUNE KAGURA — *the Shrine Maiden of the Isles*
- **Race/Class:** Fox-kin, Cleric/Spirit Summoner → Spirit King's Contractor
- **Personality:** serene, mischievous, tails reveal her emotions.
- **Arc:** *"Nine Tails, Nine Debts"*: each tail she gains comes with a spirit's debt to repay. Opens the Eastern Isles spirit world.
