# Monster lines, variations, guilds and contracts (system doc)

**Status:** rules and data built and tested; **UI not built** (Unity screens below are the spec). Approved by the user 2026-11-09 as Canon section 6 rows M1 to M6. Source: the user's *Monster Classification & Guild System* bible v1.0. Assessment and conflicts: `13_Production/monster_guild_integration.md`.

## 1. What the player sees, in one paragraph
Monsters sit in one of **30 groups** with nine named stages (Lesser Goblin to Ancient Goblin King). Some carry one or more of **12 variations** (Horned, Frozen, Corrupted and so on) that change looks, stats, element and a weakness, never threat rank. Beyond the Adventurers' Guild there are **29 more guilds**; the player joins as many as they like, earns reputation with each separately, climbs an 8-step ladder (Applicant to Master), takes guild contracts and bounties, and gets small, capped discounts. Monster groups hold a **standing** with the player (Hostile to Allied) that quests and choices move.

## 2. Rules that never bend
| Rule | Source |
|---|---|
| Threat rank is the species' own rating. Stage never sets it; variations never shift it (`rankShift` is 0 and validated) | Bible section 6; Canon section 2 |
| Monsters never evolve or grow because they defeated the player; those triggers are refused in code | Canon 4d (`MonsterLines.IsTriggerAllowed`) |
| No enemy hierarchy or autonomous faction war. Stage 7 to 9 names (Lord, King, Emperor) are species names. Group-to-group ally and rival lists are flavour only | Canon 4d, proposal M4 |
| The Adventurers' Guild keeps its own F to SSS ranks and cards; the other 29 use the member ladder | `GuildRank`, proposal M3 |
| The player holds up to Master (tier 7). Grandmaster is story-only. The player never owns or runs a guild | Canon section 4 |
| Reputation is per guild. Every reputation event has a daily cap per guild; every contract has a cooldown. No farming loops | Bible section 14 |
| Discounts are bounded (12% at Master, none while distrusted) and shown | Canon section 6 item economy guardrails |
| Tamed partners still take one permanent evolution at Bond 5 (not nine stages) | Canon section 6 taming rules |
| Awakened is a scripted state, not a spawn modifier. Succubus/Incubus is not a playable path. Veiled Hand contracts are implied, never graphic | Teen rating, proposal M6 |

## 4. Numbers (placeholder tuning; all in data)
- **Member ladder** (reputation / contracts / gate): Applicant 0/0; Initiate 10/1; Member 40/3; Veteran 120/8; Expert 300/15 (mastery test); Officer 600/25 (appointment); Master 1,200/40 (appointment); Grandmaster 2,500/60 (story only, player cannot hold).
- **Discount:** 0, 0, 2, 4, 6, 8, 10, 12 percent by tier.
- **Standing:** below -50 distrusted (services refused); below -150 banned (membership revoked, tier resets).
- **Reputation events** (delta, daily cap): contract +10/40; donation +2 per 100c/10; discovery +8/16; rescue +15/30; fair conduct +2/6; abandoned mission -15/45; stole property -60/120; harmed protected creature -40/80; broke contract -30/60; betrayed member -80/160.
- **Contract access by rank:** F needs tier 1; E tier 2; D and C tier 3; B and A tier 4; S tier 5; SS tier 6; SSS tier 7. Copper at mid band: F 50, E 250, D 900, C 3,000, B 8,000, A 20,000, S 50,000, SS 120,000, SSS 300,000; low 0.6x, high 1.5x. A player above a template's range is paid at its top rank.
- **Variation stacking:** up to 3; the 2nd and 3rd apply 60% and 35% of the part of each multiplier above 1.
- **Bounty claims:** reward in escrow; a claimant needs 15% of the work to be paid; split by contribution, remainder to the largest.
- **Standing points:** Hostile -100, Wary -40, Neutral 0, Friendly 40, Allied 80 (start values -70, -20, 0, 50, 85).

## 5. Screens to build (ux-designer spec for ui-engineer; none exist yet)
All in the existing "anime System window" style (`hud_and_ui.md`), keyboard and pad, no new input mode.
1. **Guild Hall tab** (Journal or town guild building): the 30 guilds in a list with a standing seal; locked ones show "???" until found (hidden guilds) or their rank note (restricted). A guild page shows purpose, tier and progress to the next tier (reputation and contracts as two bars, the gate as a line of text), the discount, rivals, and the open contracts.
2. **Contract board** per guild: template title, category icon, rank range, tier needed (grey with the reason when not met), cooldown date, reward at the player's rank.
3. **Bounty detail**: target identity, last known place, threat estimate, behaviour, known weaknesses, accepted proofs, deadline, reward and the escrow note; after claiming, the split by contribution.
4. **Bestiary additions**: a group badge and stage label on each species page (for example "Goblins, stage 6: Goblin Champion"), the nine-stage strip with the species' own stage lit, branch paths as plain text, and variation chips with their cue and weakness note. Threat shown separately as its own rating.
5. **Standing page**: the 18 tracked monster groups with a state word and a bar; "why" text from the last change.
6. **Material tooltips**: show the grade name (Cracked to Mythic) beside the item rarity colour.
Accessibility: every state is a word plus a shape, not colour alone.
