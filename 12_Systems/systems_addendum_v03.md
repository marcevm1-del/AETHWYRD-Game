# 12b — Systems Addendum v0.3 (from Questionnaire #1)

## 1. Difficulty: Player Choice + the S-Rank Floor (Q9)
| Setting | F → A content | S → SSS content |
|---|---|---|
| Story | Gentle | **Brutal** |
| Adventurer | Balanced | **Brutal** |
| Veteran | Hard | **Brutal+** |
| Hardcore (permadeath) | Hard | **Nightmare** |
- **"Brutal"** = full enemy AI aggression, unique mechanics, unblockable combos, and boss enrage timers. Easy-mode assists (auto-dodge, damage reduction) **switch off** in S+ zones and fights.
- The game tells you this ahead of time: an in-world Guild warning at A-rank. *"Beyond this point, the world will not go easy on you."*

## 2. Respec (Q19)
| Level | Stat/skill reset |
|---|---|
| 1–10 | **Free**, unlimited (learning phase) |
| 11+ | **Tome of Forgetting** (rare drop or craft) + gold scaling with level + a 1-day cooldown |
| 100+ | Also requires a **Shrine of Ilvaris trial** |

## 3. Relics & Chase Items (Q20: replaces the "secretly OP" focus)
The "overpowered" fantasy now comes from **grinding for legendary-tier items**. They're hard to get, worth it, and every one has a story.

| Tier | How you get it | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Relic** (Legendary+) | Multi-step chain: rare drop → quest → forge | *Fang of the First Wolf*: 0.5% drop from Alpha wolves → hunt the Silver Fenrir → Torvald forges it |
| **Grand Relic** (Mythic) | 3–5 chains across regions + a boss at Worthy+ rating | *Starfall Edge*: collect 7 meteor shards that fall during Twin Eclipses |
| **Apex Relic** (Mythic+, 50+ hrs each) | Account-long chase: crafting, taming, Labyrinth floors, a world event | *Crown of the Beast God* |
| **Divine Artifact** | Story + endgame | *Solgrace* |

- **Relic Codex** (in the Journal) shows silhouettes of undiscovered relics with cryptic hints, so players know the chase exists.
- Relics **level up** with you (Relic Lv 1 → 50) through use and feeding materials.
- **Rank concealment** stays only as one optional relic (*Mask of the Humble*). It's a fun extra, not a theme.

## 4. Exceptional Promotion (Q17)
- Completing a quest or boss **2+ ranks above your rank** (rated Perilous or higher) triggers **"Exceptional Feat"**.
- The Guild Master summons you to a **Trial of Merit**: a duel against the Guild Master or an SS-ranked examiner. Win (any grade) → **skip one rank**.
- Maximum **one skip per rank band**. It can't be used to reach SS or SSS (those require their full trials).

## 5. Unique Skills: Chosen vs Fated (Q23, Q25)
| Pool | How | Power | Count |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Chosen** | Pick freely at creation | Strong, reliable | 10 |
| **Fated** | **Roll the Fate Dice** once (no reroll). You may refuse it and pick Chosen instead, but only before seeing the result | Game-changing | 10 |

**Chosen:** Infinite Storage, Appraisal Eye, Growth Acceleration, Monster Speech, Otherworld Knowledge, Crafting God's Hands, Charm Aura, Hero's Light, Gravity Heart, Lucky Star.
**Fated:** Predator, Second Dawn, Copy, Great Sage's Voice, Status Manipulation, Time Sense, Shade Harvest, Demon Heart, Blood Pact, **Nothing → Zero**.

**Limits on game-changers (examples):**
| Skill | Limits |
|---|---|
| Second Dawn (time rewind) | 3 rewinds per in-game day; each one leaves a "Fate Scar" (−5% max HP until you rest); doesn't work in Trials |
| Copy | Slots are limited; copied skills run at 70% power; Ultimates can't be copied until Stage 3 |
| Predator | Must consume the body within 10 s; skill gain chance 5–25%; bosses only drop "fragments" |
| Status Manipulation | Once a day; can't exceed rank caps |
| Time Sense | Long cooldown; the time stop gets shorter against higher-rank enemies |

## 6. Taming Roster (Q33)
- **3 active monsters max** (always), plus a **Ranch** for stored monsters (capacity grows with home upgrades).
- **Trade-up loop:** tame a low-rank partner → evolve it, or release it, or retire it to the ranch → tame higher-ranked ones as you climb.
- **Taming rank cap:** you can tame monsters up to **your rank +0** (Tamer line: +1).
- **Bond and evolution:** partners gain Bond; at Bond 5 they can evolve; at Bond 10 with a Name-Giving Ribbon they become **Named** (a full companion with a personality).
- **Monster Collector Codex:** tame one of every species for titles and relics (*Crown of the Beast God* Apex Relic).

## 7. Settlement Prosperity (Q30)
Every town/village has persistent stats:
| Stat | Raised by | Lowered by |
|---|---|---|
| **Wealth** | Quest rewards spent locally, trade routes, your investments | Raids, taxes, neglect |
| **Safety** | Clearing nearby dens, defending in Stampedes, hiring guards | Ignored threats, monster dens growing |
| **Faith/Morale** | Festivals, healing plagues, shrines restored | Disasters, cult activity |
| **Population** | Safety + Wealth over time; rescued refugees | Deaths, fleeing |

**Visual state stages** (the 3D town actually changes):
| Stage | What you see |
|---|---|
| 0 · Ruined | Burned houses, no NPCs |
| 1 · Struggling | Broken fences, boarded windows, beggars |
| 2 · Stable | Default |
| 3 · Thriving | Repaired roofs, new market stalls, children playing, music |
| 4 · Prosperous | New buildings, stone walls, a bigger guild branch, a festival plaza |
| 5 · Renowned | A statue of **you**, a new district, unique shops and rare traders |

Higher stages unlock better shops, quests, residents and trainers. Neglected towns can fall back.

## 8. Traversal Update (Q27, Q29)
- **Everyone:** sprint, climb ledges and rough walls (stamina), swim on the surface, ground mounts (horse, raptor, beast mounts).
- **Gliding:** races (Bird-kin, Fairy, Dragonkin), Wind magic (*Featherfall → Sky Glide*), or relic cloaks.
- **Flight:** abilities only. Dragonkin awakening, Wind Grand magic, Ki Sovereign, Seraph Wings relic, Transcendence. **No flying mounts.**
- **Teleport:** Waystones (network), Blink/Teleport spells, Space magic, Waystride (SSS).
- **Underwater:** stretch goal; designed so it can be added later as a region update.
