## 2. The region graph

**Nodes** are the 40 surface regions of `regions.json` (the other 9 rows are 8 underworld rows and Vesper). **Edges** come from three places, nothing is invented:

| Kind | Source | Distance (`km`) | Walkable (`foot`) |
|---|---|---|---|
| `road` | a `roads[]` entry of `physical_map.json`, joining the regions of its two end settlements (`via` lists regions it passes through) | the road's `lengthKm` | yes |
| `pass` | a road whose route crosses a named pass (`passId`, `passM`): roads 04, 05, 06 | the road's `lengthKm` | yes |
| `sea` | a sailed leg of a corridor (Port Goldmere - Naming Voyage harbor) | the leg's `lengthKm` | no (sailed; `TravelOptions.IncludeSea`) |
| `border` | two regions touch in the atlas region raster | straight line between the two anchors in `regions.json` (`kmBasis: anchor_straight_line`), `borderKm` shared | when the lowest crossing is at most the 2,000 m road limit and the shared border is at least 1 km |

River crossings are a property of a road edge (`riverCrossings`, `widestCrossingM`), not an edge: a river has no region of its own. `portals` is empty: Canon defines waystones only as respawn points, and the proposed fast travel between attuned waystones (`systems.md` §11.3) has no authored places yet (Canon §6 WF11).

**Rules** (`RegionGraph`): `Neighbours`, `Reachable`, `Orphans`, `Unreachable`, `Components`, and `ShortestPath` by km (Dijkstra, ties broken by region id so the answer is stable). `TravelOptions { IncludeSea, RoadsOnly }` choose what a journey may use. These are distances for planning and tests; travel *time* is `systems.md` §11.3 and not decided here.

**Totals today:** 99 edges (10 road, 3 pass, 1 sea, 85 border, of which 4 touch under 1 km and are not walkable).

The validator prints `graph findings 0`, **separate from the 28 map warnings**. A new orphan region is an error; a story-reached region that becomes reachable is a warning.

- **Scale (settled, Canon §6 WF9):** 1 map unit = 1 km, terrain tiles of 10.24 km (the tiles built today are 10 km and are rebuilt when streaming is built; `TileKm(built: true)`), Unity origin at the map's north-west corner (one value to rebase to Brindlewick later).
- **Tiles:** tile (tx, ty) starts at tx x tileKm km east and ty x tileKm km south of the north-west corner, whatever the origin; 15 x 10 tiles at either size. Points outside the map have no tile.

## Map view (the feed for the World page)

`WorldMapViews.Build(graph, knowledge, regions, dungeons, playerRegion)` (`Code/Ascend.Core/WorldMapView.cs`) returns plain lists a map UI draws without touching the graph, `MapKnowledge` or region data:
- **Nodes:** every graph land, so the sheet keeps its shape. Unknown lands have no name, rank band or dungeons; Heard lands show name and rank band; Visited lands also list their dungeons. `IsPlayerHere` marks the current land.
- **Edges:** a full line when both ends are known and one is Visited; a `Stub` when one end is Visited and the other Unknown. Ways between lands the player has not been to are left off.
- `KnownRouteKm` gives the walking km between two known lands over known ground only (null otherwise).
- Dungeons whose region is not a surface graph node (the Salvage Warren is in the Labyrinth) do not appear here; the Labyrinth registry feeds that page.
- Tests: `WorldMapViewTests`. Not built: the Unity drawing (`WorldMapWindow` still uses its own data) and wiring `PlayerMap` to call this.
