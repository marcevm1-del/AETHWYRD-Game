# Comparable RPG Research: More Ways to Make Aetheria Feel Distinct

**Research date:** 24 October 2026. **Status:** sourced observations plus clearly labeled Ascend design inferences. Official developer interviews and publisher materials are preferred; this is not a market-size or sales-performance report.

## Executive synthesis

Ascend already has scale, classes, a large bestiary, rank progression, companion bonds, a seven-day board, siege liberation, and an anime presentation target. The opportunity is to make those systems reinforce one another. The strongest added identity is an **adventurer who learns a living world and earns trust through visible action**: environmental cues teach where and when to act; hunt evidence changes tactics; companions add interpretations; local choices travel through believable channels; liberation changes the lived-in route.

The research does not justify copying another game's whole feature set. It supports targeted patterns: creature ecology and behavior, friction-light but optional guidance, multi-route quests, meaningful loadout tradeoffs, high-quality combat readability, and scope systems that let a small team author reusable variations.

## Source findings and design implications

### 1. Monster ecology should be observable and tactical

Capcom's *Monster Hunter Wilds* interviews describe large monster groups behaving according to environment-specific ecology, monsters changing with weather/locale conditions, and creatures interacting through herd behavior and predator encounters. The coverage also describes wounds as an earned tactical opportunity and tools that help players track targets through vertical terrain. ([PlayStation Blog, June 13 2024](https://blog.playstation.com/?p=393257); [PlayStation Blog, May 30 2024](https://blog.playstation.com/2024/05/30/monster-hunter-wilds-new-gameplay-details-from-todays-state-of-play/))

**Ascend inference:** give each monster family a handful of readable behaviors with consequences: feeding, sheltering, warning, guarding, fleeing, migrating, or exploiting weather. Let the player observe signs before a fight, and allow companions or tools to help interpret them. Avoid a continuous simulation of every creature. Use authored ecology states and encounter triggers. For the monster data library, distinguish reusable family behavior from species signature behavior and rank/region variants.

### 2. Navigation can be part of the fantasy, not a UI overlay

Sucker Punch's *Ghost of Tsushima* team described turning an established art-direction goal—the wind and moving landscape—into guidance that points toward objectives, while still asking the player to navigate the actual terrain. Their world-building article also emphasizes regional visual identity through controlled foliage variety and broad color choices. ([PlayStation Blog, May 29 2020](https://blog.playstation.com/2020/05/29/ghost-of-tsushima-your-questions-answered/); [July 9 2020](https://blog.playstation.com/2020/07/09/crafting-the-world-of-tsushima/))

**Ascend inference:** make the System's guidance optional and diegetic. A faint thread, drifting mana motes, shrine bells, birds, wind-bent grass, or a companion's fieldcraft can indicate a broad direction; the map route remains available as a player-selected aid. Different cultures may interpret the same phenomenon differently. The signal should never be required for accessibility: offer high-contrast markers, a clear route and audio/haptic options.

### 3. Quest freedom needs understandable outcomes, not endless branches

Obsidian's *Avowed* developers describe short-, medium- and long-horizon consequences; companion reactions and departures; quest approaches such as fighting, stealth and investigation; and the balance between leaving discovery for curious players and supplying enough hints to avoid opacity. ([Xbox Wire, Jan 24 2025](https://news.xbox.com/en-us/2025/01/24/avowed-choose-your-own-adventure-incredible-freedom/))

**Ascend inference:** author quests with a finite set of reusable solution verbs—combat, talk, stealth, track, craft, prepare, rescue, bargain—then choose two or three that fit each contract. Record consequence timing and receipt. An NPC's reaction, a board update or a changed patrol can carry a consequence without branching every line of every quest. Big choices need foreshadowing; small surprises can remain discoverable.

### 4. Equipment choice should alter rhythm and exposure

Obsidian's combat article on *Avowed* explains starting from weapon fundamentals and making heavier attacks trade stamina and recovery for impact, while lighter choices trade power for speed or control. It describes attributes as available across builds rather than hard class gates. ([Xbox Wire, Nov 21 2024](https://news.xbox.com/en-us/2024/11/21/player-choice-avowed-approach-combat/))

**Ascend inference:** make each weapon family communicate a distinct rhythm through reach, recovery, stagger, mobility, resource use and aerial control. Stats may improve expression, but avoid making one attribute a hidden mandatory gate for a weapon. Keep class identity in technique, passive synergy and tactical options. Give every weapon at least one safe baseline route so players who struggle with parries can complete the story.

### 5. Accessibility can preserve intensity while adding readable cues

Rebellion's *Atomfall* accessibility overview describes optional directional dialogue indicators, enemy visibility settings, distinctive attack sounds and configurable aiming cues that support players who reduce audio or need additional visual information. ([Xbox Wire, Apr 10 2025](https://news.xbox.com/en-us/2025/04/10/atomfall-accessibility/))

**Ascend inference:** present combat information redundantly: animation silhouette, timing sound, optional cue shape, controller vibration and subtitle label. Make shake, flashes, camera pull, lock-on strength, quick-time demands and auto-targeting adjustable. Difficulty should change enemy pressure, recovery windows and resource availability, not simply inflate health. Preserve a challenge mode for those who want stricter reaction tests.

### 6. Large worlds require composition and tooling

The *Ghost of Tsushima* world-building article describes using procedural tools to scale flexible environment construction and limiting foliage variety to keep each region visually memorable. The article also frames world readability and visual appeal as intentional composition. ([PlayStation Blog, July 9 2020](https://blog.playstation.com/2020/07/09/crafting-the-world-of-tsushima/))

**Ascend inference:** with 40 regions and a two-person core team, use a region kit and content budgets. Each region should have one unmistakable silhouette, a small palette of dominant flora/materials, one traversal grammar, a few family behaviors, a social rhythm, and a liberation delta. Reuse production grammar while protecting local identity. Do not interpret the number of regions as a promise to hand-build equivalent amounts of bespoke content in every one.

### 7. AI companions need useful autonomy and low-friction commands

Capcom's *Dragon's Dogma 2* materials describe Pawns choosing actions automatically, responding to the player's needs, using situational skills and accepting simple commands to regroup, spread out or prioritize support. The official overview frames their knowledge and conversation as part of travel as well as combat. ([PlayStation Blog, Sept 26 2023](https://blog.playstation.com/?p=384689); [Capcom Showcase recap](https://blog.playstation.com/2023/06/12/capcom-showcase-recap-pragmata-dragons-dogma-2-exoprimal-and-more/))

**Ascend inference:** companions should be capable by default, with commands that express intent rather than manually puppeteer each move. Give each partner a role, tactical priorities and personality reactions. Make useful behavior visible: a healer calls out recovery, a scout marks a flank, a guardian intercepts a strike. Commands should not interrupt the player's combo flow.

### 8. Support different explorer mindsets without splitting the world

A 2026 Xbox Wire interview about the upcoming *Fable* describes designing for players who follow the main story and those who explore and push systems. Its developers say optional life-sim systems should not be required for main-story progress, while the world still offers meaningful activities to curious players. ([Xbox Wire, Jan 22 2026](https://news.xbox.com/en-us/2026/01/22/fable-interview-overview-details-developer-direct-2026/))

**Ascend inference:** keep the central story clear while contracts, bonds, crafting, tournaments, restoration and mysteries deepen the same places. Optional systems should add context and expressive power, not become prerequisites for basic class growth, companion survival or story access.

### 9. Replay value should preserve learning, not excuse missing content

Silent Games' *Empyreal* interview describes New Game+ as a way to revisit distinct questline outcomes while retaining rewards and adding challenge. ([Xbox Wire, May 7 2025](https://news.xbox.com/en-us/2025/05/07/meet-the-cast-of-empyreal/))

**Ascend inference:** the first playthrough should feel complete. NG+ can provide optional alternate outcomes and challenge variants, but should not be the only way to see an essential companion scene or obtain a satisfying ending.
### 10. Population scale is a production system, not a headcount

Playground Games' June 2026 post about *Fable* gives an unusually candid production account of a 1,000-plus named population: every resident has a personality and routine, and the team found that procedural generation produced incoherent people, so it hand-designed the characters. The article also describes how schedules created environment requirements (beds, homes, workplaces), debugging problems, reputation interactions, persistent memories and a settlement-recovery system. ([Xbox Wire, June 10 2026](https://news.xbox.com/en-us/2026/06/10/fable-living-population-details-explained-xbox-games-showcase-2026/))

**Ascend inference:** retain the ambition for named residents but define levels of authored depth. Every named person can have a concise backstory, a desire, a social role and a few stable schedule anchors; anchor residents receive bespoke arcs; the core cast gets full writing and reactive scenes. Use procedural tools to propose combinations, then review and save fixed records. Do not imply every named resident is fully voiced, simulated and romanceable. The article's production details should be treated as warning about dependency chains, not a benchmark for a two-person team.

### 11. Culture and combat identity can be communicated before dialogue

Guerrilla's *Horizon Forbidden West* team describes using distinct social gestures and group behavior to make regional cultures legible, and designing machine roles, behaviors, weaknesses and audio cues so players can interpret threats. ([PlayStation Blog, authentic world](https://blog.playstation.com/?p=356989); [machine design](https://blog.playstation.com/?p=356418); [combat audio cues](https://blog.playstation.com/2021/12/06/horizon-forbidden-west-outsmart-your-enemies/))

**Ascend inference:** create a visual/sound grammar for each culture and monster family: silhouettes, materials, gestures, call sounds, idle behavior, alert state and signature attack. Reuse animation and rig families, but ensure each region or creature has a reason for its differences. Do not reduce a culture to one personality or costume; build internal variation and conflicting interests.
### 12. Party bonds can be felt through mechanics

Square Enix's *Final Fantasy VII Rebirth* interview says synergy moves were added so battles could express the relationships built between characters, alongside skill-tree unlocks and loadout choices. ([PlayStation Blog](https://blog.playstation.com/?p=384274))

**Ascend inference:** let companions contribute short, tactical assists that emerge from a player-created opening. Keep the protagonist under direct control at all times, as required by Ascend's single-character control rule. Start with one companion-specific assist, a simple cooldown and a readable trigger. Avoid a full party-combo matrix until one interaction demonstrates that it feels personal and does not interrupt the player's rhythm.
## New options to explore

These are proposals, not canon:

1. **Diegetic guidance dial:** choose System thread, local natural cues, companion navigation, conventional marker, or no guidance. The mode affects presentation, not rewards.
2. **Fieldcraft instead of mandatory tracking:** observation can reveal optional short-cuts or preparations; players can always proceed by following a broad lead or taking a direct encounter.
3. **Expedition loadout cards:** save weapon, skills, meal, tool and companion formations as one-click preparation sets; switching outside combat is free or low-friction.
4. **Two-weapon stance pairing:** optional later-game weapon pairing creates a deliberate swap combo, but each weapon still works alone. Prototype after basic weapon feel is proven.
5. **Monster response vocabulary:** families share clear verbs (charge, sweep, feint, guard-break, retreat, call help), then species add one signature rule. Helps players learn without making 680 species each need wholly unique AI.
6. **Rumor provenance:** show who told the player, how old the report is and whether another source agrees. A wrong clue should have a plausible source, not be random deception.
7. **Consequence receipts:** when actions alter safety, trust, guild standing or a companion bond, show a small in-world reason or later callback. Hide numerical meters unless the player chooses a detailed interface.
8. **Rest-stop conversation:** camps and inns offer one brief companion conversation, preparation action or recap, with no daily upkeep timer. This supports warm anime pacing without turning companions into a wage chore.
9. **Threat forecast, not raw difficulty prediction:** report rank band, observed attacks, missing facts and environmental hazards. Never promise safety from a calculated power number.
10. **Region sound signatures:** a small number of adaptive musical layers and environmental motifs change with safety, weather and time. Keep boss tracks authored and rare enough to remain special.
11. **Player-authored memory reel:** photo mode and selected moments can feed an optional end-of-arc montage; respect spoiler settings and never require screenshots to progress.
12. **Training encounters:** guild practice teaches one defense or movement concept at a time, then lets the player disable prompts and retry in a real encounter.

## Scope ladder

- **Ring 0 prototype:** one route, three monsters, three evidence motifs, one optional clue, two weapon rhythms, enemy telegraphs with adjustable cues, one NPC consequence, one recovery/rumor receipt.
- **Ring 1 production:** Brindlewick/Whisperwood only: introduce a board contract, family signature attacks, one companion observation, one siege state change and one liberated route/social difference.
- **Later rings:** expand weather states, migration, advanced weapon pairing and faction diplomacy only after telemetry/play observation shows the simpler loop is understood and enjoyable.
- **Never assume:** every species gets a unique skeleton; every NPC gets bespoke dialogue for every flag; every quest has all possible verbs; every region needs a bespoke major feature; the player must maintain companion finances to keep the party.

## What research cannot decide

Public developer interviews are selective descriptions, not complete design documents. They do not reveal Ascend's ideal encounter frequency, authoring cost, required region size, optimal difficulty curves or labor capacity. We must answer these with a playable Ring 0 route and observed sessions. Treat numerical targets as hypotheses and record actual friction, boredom, confusion and delight.
