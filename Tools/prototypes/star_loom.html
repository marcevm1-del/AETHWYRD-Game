The Star Loom

Click a star to light the shortest path to it (1 Spark per star; Sparks follow the rank bands, about 190 by the end). Click a lit star to refund it (1 refund point). Drag to pan, scroll or pinch to zoom.

One web, twelve doors

Every class starts at its own star on the inner ring, placed by its two main stats. Neighbours share stats, so a Swordsman can walk into Lancer or Guardian ground.

Wheels and crescents

Clusters of minor stars guard a named notable. The highways between them give +5 to the local stat, so the trip there still counts.

Keystones and Echo sockets

Each sector ends in a Keystone that bends a rule and costs something. Echo sockets hold an Echo Shard from a defeated monster.
