# The Hall of Thanes, Face by Face
*Brunhild Anvilsdottir, guide of the Hall of Thanes, who has never once been asked about the ceiling*

**Shelf:** Local history  
**Region:** Iron Hold  
**In-world date:** 996 SC  
**Status:** Draft. Player-facing. Not Canon until reviewed. Written as what a person in Aethwyrd could know.

Visitors ask me who the Thanes were. I tell them the faces will do it better.

## What the hall is

A long room cut into the heart of Karak Dûn, with a roof so high the lamps do not reach it. Down both walls, in niches, are the carved faces of every High Thane the hold has elected. They are life-size, unsmiling and very patient. They watch the living the way a good teacher watches a lesson: not to interrupt, only to be present.

## How to read a face

Look at the jaw. A Thane who carved their own face carved a firm jaw. A Thane whose face was carved by a successor has a jaw that is a little too firm, and eyes that are a little too kind. I can tell you which is which, and I will, if you buy me a cup of dark ale.

Look at the beard. The earlier Thanes wore it long and plain. The later ones braided it, and some of the braids are very fine indeed. There is a Thane near the middle of the right-hand wall whose beard is a map of the Ashen seams, and nobody has ever been able to prove it is not.

Look at the hands. Those are the true likenesses. A face may flatter; a hand does not. Every Thane's hands are the hands of a person who has worked, and every Thane's hands are carved on the base of the niche, where only the people who bow can see.

## The election

A High Thane is chosen from the clan heads, by a vote cast in stone: a pebble in a bowl. I have seen it three times. It takes a very long time. The dwarves of Iron Hold are not fast, but they are thorough, and a decision made after a week of argument is a decision that stays.

## A thing I tell the children

The faces are not there to be admired. They are there so that nobody forgets that the hold was built by people, one at a time, and that all of them are still looking.

Thorgrim is said to have made the first anvil. Every Thane since has made a promise on it, and the hall is where we keep the promises.
