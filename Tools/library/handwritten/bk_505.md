# The Big Things in the Ferns
*Wick Ashdown, a beetle-rider, for the young who wish to ride out and are not yet permitted*

**Shelf:** Field guide  
**Region:** Mossdeep  
**In-world date:** 1015 SC  
**Status:** Draft. Player-facing. Not Canon until reviewed. Written as what a person in Aethwyrd could know.

You will want to ride out. Everyone does. I did. This is what I wish somebody had told me, and what somebody did tell me and I did not listen to.

Everything in Mossdeep is too big, except us. That is the first law. Do not take it as a sorrow. Take it as information.

## Jackalope

Rank E, no element, weak to Fire, and it runs in packs. It looks, from a distance, like a very large rabbit with a branch on its head. From close, it looks like a very large rabbit with a branch on its head that is aimed at you. Do not meet a pack in the open. A tree between you and the herd is a good and generous friend.

## Firefly Swarm

E, Light, weak to Dark, an ambusher. It is beautiful, and it hides what is behind it. A swarm will fill your eyes with light, and while you are blinking, whatever is hunting you will close. Cover your eyes with your arm and back out slowly.

## Pixie

E, Wind, weak to Fire, passive. It is a very small creature and it is very loud, and it wants nothing from you but to be admired. Admire it. It will leave.

## Thornvine

E, Nature, weak to Fire, territorial. It is a plant, and it is not a plant you want to argue with. It does not move; it waits. Where there is one, there will be a gap in the other plants around it, a space the vine has made for itself. Do not step into the gap.

## Treant Sapling

Rank D, Nature, weak to Fire, territorial. It is a very young tree, and it is a very large young tree, and it does not like to be walked round. If you see one, keep a hundred paces and let it be. It will forget you.

## What I would tell my younger self

The creature is not the danger. The danger is the thing that you did not look at while you were looking at the creature. Bring a friend. Bring a beetle. Bring honey, for the beetle, and a little for yourself.

Sylvara, who keeps the beasts, is kind to those who ask first.
