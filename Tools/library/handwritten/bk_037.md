# What the Porter Carries
*Odo Brisk, porter, forty trips to Floor Ten and back*

**Shelf:** Field guide  
**Region:** Great Labyrinth (Floors 1-40)  
**In-world date:** 1006 SC  
**Status:** Draft. Player-facing. Not Canon until reviewed. Written as what a person in Aethwyrd could know.

A porter's job is to arrive. Heroes are for the middle of the story. I carry the pack, the water, the rope and the bad news, and I come back with the pack, the rope and the good news. The Guild books me by the day. The rest I learned on the stairs.

My rule: carry what keeps people alive, ignore what is only interesting, and run from what is neither.

## Lantern Leech

F, Water, weak to Lightning. It hangs from a lamp-hook and drains the light. It is a Guardian: it does not leave its post, so you do not need to fight it. Walk round it. Take a spare lamp. I have lost two flames to leeches and never a limb.

## Gremlin

Rank F, Lightning, weak to Earth, skittish. It steals small things. The small things are always the important ones: the key, the knife, the end of the rope. Pack the key inside your coat. A gremlin has never opened a coat in my hearing.

## Bubble Slime

E, Water, weak to Lightning. It is passive and drifts. The danger is that you will poke it. Do not poke it. It is more afraid of you than you are of it, and the popping is loud enough to bring something worse.

## Mimic

D, no Element, weak to Fire. An ambusher that wears the shape of a chest. If you find a chest in a place a chest would not be, it is not a chest. I leave unattended chests alone, and I have never lost a finger.

## Wall Stalker

E, Earth, weak to Wind. Aggressive and slow. You hear it before you see it, because stone scrapes against stone. Do not fight it in a narrow place. Back out to a wide room and let it follow.

## What goes in my pack

- Two lamps, one candle stub for each person, and a flint.
- Water for three days. Ten steps of stair are worth a swallow.
- A coil of rope that I have tested myself.
- Bread that does not crumble, and a tin of salt.
- Chalk. I always carry chalk.

## What I will not carry

Anyone's pride. It weighs more than the stone.
