# The Gate Desk: How We Work
*Marrow Penhallow, senior clerk, Guild desk at the Labyrinth gate*

**Shelf:** Guild manual  
**Region:** Great Labyrinth (Floors 1-40)  
**In-world date:** 1008 SC  
**Status:** Draft. Player-facing. Not Canon until reviewed. Written as what a person in Aethwyrd could know.

Welcome to the desk. You will be standing behind it for a long time, so please sit.

## What the desk is for

The Great Labyrinth runs for a hundred and twenty floors. The first forty are the Proving Halls, and they are our business. Our rank range is F to C: the floors run from kobolds and slimes to things that have taken a long time to get angry. We are not a gate that keeps anybody out. We are a gate that makes sure somebody knows who went in.

## The five forms

1. **The entry slip.** Name, rank, how many in the party, which floor, and when you expect to be back. Not when you *hope* to be back.
2. **The lamp count.** Two lamps per party or no stamp. That is the rule, and it is not the clerk's rule; it is Morrowyn's.
3. **The return slip.** Hand it in when you come out. If you do not, I will notice.
4. **The loss slip.** For what you left behind. The lost-and-found clerk will ask for it.
5. **The note to the next party.** Optional. Everyone fills it in anyway.

## Courtesy at the desk

- A rookie who is scared is not a rookie who is rude. Be kind.
- A veteran who is rude is a veteran who is tired. Be patient and give them water.
- When two parties want the same hall, ask which has been waiting longer. Nine times in ten they will agree without help.
- Never say "I told you so." Say "I'm glad you're back."

## What we do not say

The board does not say who is brave. Do not praise a rank. Do not say "a C-rank would have done that easily". A rank is a licence, and nothing more. I have watched people take a rank as an insult and a rank as a promise, and both are wrong.

## Hours

The desk opens at first light and closes when the last party is accounted for. On Solenday, Kaelsday and Vexday we stay open late, because those are the days the Labyrinth is busiest. On Morrowsday, keep a candle by the door.

## A last rule

If you cannot account for a party by the second night, send a runner to the Guild. Do not wait to be certain. It is better to be foolish than to be late.
