# A Cottage by the Moonwell
*Teague Ashleaf, wood elf, whose cottage has stood by the same pool since his grandfather planted the door*

**Shelf:** Household book  
**Region:** Sylvaran  
**In-world date:** 1018 SC  
**Status:** Draft. Player-facing. Not Canon until reviewed. Written as what a person in Aethwyrd could know.

My cottage is a round room with a fire in it. That is not modesty. It is architecture.

## Why round

A round room has no corner for a draught to sit in, no shadow to hide a damp and no wall that is further from the fire than the others. A round room says, to anyone who enters, that everyone is equally close. I think that is the courtesy my grandfather meant.

## The pool

The moonwell is thirty paces from the door. It glows faintly at night, a pale green that is almost the colour of a held breath. It is sacred, and I may not drink from it without permission, and I have never once asked for permission, because I do not need to drink from it to be glad that it is there.

We draw our washing water from the stream. We draw our drinking water from the well behind the house. We draw our peace from the pool, and it charges us nothing.

## The kitchen

There is no kitchen. There is a hearth, a pot and a shelf. What the pot holds depends on the season: nettle soup in spring, roasted roots in autumn, in winter whatever the larder has forgotten about. We eat at a low table, on the floor, with our knees up, and nobody has ever complained about the posture.

## The seed

When a child is named, we plant a seed in the Twin Grove, and the tree that grows is theirs for life. Mine is an oak, and it has been very patient with me. My son's is a birch, and it has not.

## The blight

It has come to the first hamlet. It has not yet come to mine. We keep a watch. We burn what we must. Every night, I walk to the pool and look at the green, and every night it is still green, and every night I give thanks to Sylvara for a thing so small that she may not notice.

I put that here so that whoever reads this knows what we were guarding.
