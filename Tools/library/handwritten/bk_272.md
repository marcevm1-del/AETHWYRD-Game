# The Mediation Desk: A Guide for Guild Clerks in Moraveil
*Pell Thistledown, clerk of the Guild's small desk by the Truce Bridge*

**Shelf:** Guild manual  
**Region:** Moraveil  
**In-world date:** 1016 SC  
**Status:** Draft. Player-facing. Not Canon until reviewed. Written as what a person in Aethwyrd could know.

Welcome. You have been appointed to the one Guild desk where the customers sometimes shout in two languages at once. It is a good desk. Bring a cushion.

## What we are for

The Guild registers adventurers and posts the board. In Moraveil, we do something more. We are, in practice, the only neutral party in the district, because nobody has ever thought the Guild was on a side.

## The desk

It sits on the bridge. That is not an accident. It stands at the midpoint, so that a goblin can approach from the village and a human from the town, and neither has to cross to the other's side first. The first rule is to keep the desk exactly in the middle.

## Languages

There are two, and you will need both. Learn to say *good morning*, *please*, *thank you* and *I am sorry* in each. If you can say them in the other person's tongue before they say them in yours, you will have a very good year.

## When there is a dispute

1. **Sit down.** Everyone sits. Nobody is taller than anybody else.
2. **Ask each to say what they want, and then what they need.** They are almost never the same.
3. **Repeat it back.** In both languages. Slowly.
4. **Send them to the council.** The joint council meets on the bridge, monthly. It has no army and no money, only the trust of both peoples. We are not the council. We are the door.

## What not to do

Do not take a side. Do not take a bribe. Do not tell a goblin what a human thinks, or a human what a goblin says, in a way that you have not checked with both. Do not, under any circumstances, make a joke about the other language.

## Courtesy

A weapon is left at the door of the Friendly Goblin, and also at the door of the market. We apply the same rule to ourselves.

## A last thing

Truce Day, the anniversary of the rescue that started it all, is the busiest day of the year. We close the desk and we eat. It is the only day when nobody asks the Guild for anything.
