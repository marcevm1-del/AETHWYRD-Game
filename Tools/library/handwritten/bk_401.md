# Hammer-Songs of the Third Hall
*Set down by Rurik Beard-of-Brass, who sings bass and has never been asked to stop*

**Shelf:** Ballad sheet  
**Region:** Iron Hold  
**In-world date:** 1011 SC  
**Status:** Draft. Player-facing. Not Canon until reviewed. Written as what a person in Aethwyrd could know.

Dwarves sing at work, and the work sets the rhythm. These are three songs of the Third Hall, with the strokes marked. An upright bar is a hammer-fall.

## Strike and Cool

*Steady. Four to a line.*

> | Heat it | beat it | cool it | true |
> | What was | iron | now is | you |
> | Strike it | shape it | quench and | stay |
> | What we | make will | see the | day |

## The Long Shift

*Slow. A walking song, for the corridors at the changing of the watch.*

> The lamp is lit, the pick is sound,
> the mountain breathes beneath the ground,
> I'll go down singing, come up whole,
> and say good night to the stone and the coal.

\* *Some sing "and the coal." Older versions have "and the soul." The mountain has not said which is correct.*

## The Dry One

*Fast. The joke is in the pause.*

> A dwarf, a human and a gnome walked in
> to a hall with a very good ale.
> The dwarf said, "Thank you." The human said, "Right."
> The gnome said nothing. (*pause*) She was already at the barrel.

\* *Dwarven humour is dry. The pause is the punchline. Do not rush it.*

## A note from the collector

I have sung these since before I had a beard. I have heard them sung by those who have never held a hammer, and they were not worse for it. A song is not the property of a smith. It is the property of whoever is willing to keep time.

If you cannot sing, tap on a table. We will not judge. Thorgrim, they say, made the first anvil ring, and everything after that was only echo.
