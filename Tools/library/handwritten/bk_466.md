# A Sherd in the Clover
*Nettle Brightwater, a Thimblefolk archivist, on a fragment brought to her by a big folk who did not know what it was*

**Shelf:** Valdrisian fragment  
**Region:** Mossdeep  
**In-world date:** 1002 SC  
**Status:** Draft. Player-facing. Not Canon until reviewed. Written as what a person in Aethwyrd could know.

A big folk came to my door with a piece of pottery the size of a house. I do not say this to be funny. It was the size of my house, and I had to read it from the roof.

## The sherd

It was brown, unglazed on one side and glazed green on the other. It had been broken from a larger vessel, and the break was old: the edge was rounded, the colour had darkened and a small moss had taken hold in the crack. It had been in the ground a long time.

On the glazed face there were marks. They were in rows, small and even, as a careful clerk would write. I do not read the old tongue. I read what I could, by the shapes.

## What I made of it

There is a figure that I take to be a tower. There is a figure that I take to be a road. There are three small circles in a row, which in our own writing would mean "three days." Beside them, a mark like a hand held up. I believe it says *stop*, or *wait*, or *peace*.

I have read in the Peat Library's catalogue that the old empire called Valdrisia wrote in what is called Old Weave-tongue, and that its scribes liked a tidy line. I will say this one was tidy. I will not say more.

## What the big folk said

He said he had found it in a field south of the valley mouth, under a plough. He said the ground there had always been poor, and nothing would grow in it. He said his father had never planted there, and his grandfather had said it was cursed.

I told him I did not think it was cursed. I told him I thought it was old.

## What I did

I copied the marks, as best I could. I sent the copy to the Mages' Academy. I gave the big folk a jar of honey and a note to say thank you. The sherd is on his mantelpiece. He tells me he looks at it every morning, and does not know why.

I think he is a person who has been told that he lives on the edge of something, and has not been told what.
