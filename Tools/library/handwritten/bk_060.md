# A Punt-Guide's Lunch
*Corvin Mere, punt-guide, who has never once been lost, only late*

**Shelf:** Cookery  
**Region:** Greymoor  
**In-world date:** 1002 SC  
**Status:** Draft. Player-facing. Not Canon until reviewed. Written as what a person in Aethwyrd could know.

On the fen you eat what keeps, what stays quiet and what you can hold in one hand while the other holds the pole. This is my list, and I have tested every item on someone I disliked first.

## The rules

1. **Nothing that crackles.** Sound dies in the fog, but it dies slowly, and a bag of crisp biscuits has ended more than one stalking.
2. **Nothing that spills.** A punt is a plank on a puddle.
3. **Never whistle, hum or sing while chewing.** We do not whistle on the water. I will not explain. You will be told by a person who is angry.

## The lunch

- **Black bread**, sliced the night before and wrapped in a damp cloth, so it does not dry. It is dense, a little sour, and it will keep you upright through an afternoon.
- **Smoked eel**, cut into strips. Chew slowly. It is the only food I know that tastes better when you are tired.
- **A twist of bog-berries**, dried in the sun on a reed mat, for when your mouth has gone sour from the fog.
- **A flask of cold herb tea.** Hot tea steams, steam rises and a punt with a cloud above it is a punt that can be found by things I would rather not meet.

## What to leave out

Do not carry meat that is not smoked. The fen smells it. Do not carry honey in a jar that clinks. Do not carry a hearth loaf that is still warm, because the smell of it travels farther than your voice.

## For the passenger

Eat when I say, not when you are hungry. You will not understand why until the day you do. Hold on when I say hold on, and do not turn round to look at what I am looking at.

If I say there is a light on the left, and it is not a lantern, we are going to the right. That is the whole of my art.

Sylvara has the beasts and the plants of this place. I have only the pole.
