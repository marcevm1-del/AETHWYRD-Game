# Seeds in Cups: How the Council Votes, for Guild Observers
*Brindle Thatch, honey-keeper and acting secretary of the council, writing for a visiting Guild examiner*

**Shelf:** Guild manual  
**Region:** Mossdeep  
**In-world date:** 1019 SC  
**Status:** Draft. Player-facing. Not Canon until reviewed. Written as what a person in Aethwyrd could know.

The Guild has asked how our council decides. It is a polite question and it deserves a plain answer.

## Who sits

Four guild-heads: the honey-keepers, the beetle-riders, the moss-weavers and the brewers. Each guild has one seat. They meet in a toadstool hall and they do not stand on ceremony, though they do stand on stools.

## The cups

There are two cups on the table, one blue and one yellow. Each member holds a seed. They drop it in the cup of their choice, one after the other, with their eyes closed. No one sees which cup. The secretary, who is I, counts the seeds out loud.

If the blue cup has more, the motion passes. If the yellow, it fails. If the cups are equal, we have tea.

## What is voted on

- Whether the big folk should be asked for help.
- Whether the Warm Springs may be used for laundry on Solenday.
- Whether the beetles may be kept in the hall.
- Whether the Guild examiner may sit on the good stool.

The last was settled last week. The result was a tie. We had tea.

## What the Guild should note

We are not a government in the sense that Aldmere is a government. We cannot make a law. We can make a custom, and a custom is stronger. It has no penalty and everyone obeys it.

## For the examiner

If the council asks you for your opinion, give it. Do not give it twice. They will think you are lobbying.

If the council offers you tea, drink it. If they offer you honey, taste it. If they offer you a seat, choose the smaller of the two. It is the sign that you understood.
