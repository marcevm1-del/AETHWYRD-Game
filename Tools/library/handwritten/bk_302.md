# The Lost Stone: A Keeper's Rite
*Brenna Coalwick, keeper of the stone-house, in the order that the rite is performed*

**Shelf:** Chapel notes  
**Region:** Iron Hold  
**In-world date:** 1015 SC  
**Status:** Draft. Player-facing. Not Canon until reviewed. Written as what a person in Aethwyrd could know.

I keep the stone-house, which is a small room with a very large shelf. On the shelf are the hearth-stones of every smith in the Third Hall. I know them by touch, in the dark, as a mother knows her children's coughs.

## The terrible omen

To lose a hearth-stone is a terrible omen. Everyone knows it. Nobody knows quite why. The stone is a pebble, a fist of granite, a thing you could pick up at a roadside. And yet a smith who has lost one walks differently. They carry themselves like a person who has misplaced a tooth.

## The rite

When a stone is lost, the smith comes to me. I do not ask how. I put the kettle on.

1. **The tea.** Strong, dark, with a little salt. It is not a sacrament. It is a kindness, and it has the same effect.
2. **The search.** We look together. We look in the pockets, the apron, the forge, the cellar and the bottom of the second drawer. Half the time, it is there.
3. **The waiting.** If the stone is not found by dusk, we light a small fire on the anvil and sit by it for the length of a candle. We do not speak.
4. **The new stone.** If the old one is gone for good, the smith picks a fresh one from the stone-house, from the heap of unclaimed stones, where it has been waiting.

## What the fresh stone is

It is not a replacement. I will say that clearly, because it is a mistake to think so. It is a new beginning. The old stone was a first forge. The new one is a second, with a whole life behind it.

## What I have learned

A smith who has lost a stone does not forget it. But I have never known one to be worse for the finding of another. They are quieter. They hold the new stone a little longer in the morning. They have learned what it is to be unlucky, and to be forgiven for it.

I think Thorgrim is not a god of omens. I think he is a god of second chances, and has been disguised as a god of iron for convenience.
