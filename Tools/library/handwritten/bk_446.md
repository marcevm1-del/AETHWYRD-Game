# The Dwarf Who Laughed Last
*A story for reading aloud in the Anvil's Rest, where it is told on slow evenings*

**Shelf:** Stories for reading aloud  
**Region:** Iron Hold  
**In-world date:** 1018 SC  
**Status:** Draft. Player-facing. Not Canon until reviewed. Written as what a person in Aethwyrd could know.

*(Read this one with a straight face. The humour is in the face. If the children laugh, you are doing it wrong.)*

In the hold of Karak Dûn there lived a dwarf named Borri who was known, throughout the clans, for never laughing.

"He is grim," said the Third Hall.

"He is serious," said the Fourth.

"He is dry," said the Fifth, which was the highest compliment they knew.

Borri did not mind. He went about his work, forged his nails and drank his ale, and every night, when he went to bed, he lay in the dark and thought, very carefully, about something funny that had happened that day.

Then, in the dark, in the quiet, where no one could see, he laughed.

---

One night, the walls were thin, and his neighbour heard him.

"Borri," said the neighbour in the morning, "you were laughing."

"I was not," said Borri.

"I heard you."

"You heard the mountain."

"The mountain does not say 'ha-ha-ha.'"

Borri considered this. "It might," he said. "On a good day."

The neighbour looked at him for a long time. Then, slowly, with great dignity, he began to laugh as well. Borri did not. He stood with his arms folded and a perfectly straight face.

And so it went, on and on, for the better part of a year: the whole hold laughing, and Borri, the only one who never did, standing in the middle of it like a rock in a river.

*(The reader says, in the driest voice available:)* **"He found it very funny."**
