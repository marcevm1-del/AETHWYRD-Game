# Mushrooms for the Market: A Cave-Farmer's Notes
*Grizzle Mossback, goblin cave-farmer of Moraveil, who has been told his handwriting is very large*

**Shelf:** Craft notes  
**Region:** Moraveil  
**In-world date:** 1007 SC  
**Status:** Draft. Player-facing. Not Canon until reviewed. Written as what a person in Aethwyrd could know.

My name is Grizzle, and I grow mushrooms. A human asked me to write down how, and offered me a pie. I accepted. This is the book.

## The cave

A good cave is cool, damp and dark, and it has a draught that you can feel on your wrist and not on your face. Too much draught and the caps dry out. Too little and they sweat. The right draught is one you can only find by standing very still, for a long time, and doing nothing.

## The bed

Mushrooms grow on straw and dung, which sounds worse than it is. We lay a bed in a long trough, a hand deep, and wet it with water from the underground stream. We leave it three days. We press the spawn in with our thumbs, a little apart, like seeds.

## The waiting

This is the whole of the craft. A mushroom does not hurry. It sits in the dark and thinks about it. After two weeks, you see a pale thread. After three, a small white button. After four, a cap you could eat.

You can do nothing to hurry it. I have tried. I once sang to a bed for a week. The mushrooms grew exactly as fast as they would have anyway, and I lost my voice.

## Picking

Take them in the morning, before the cave warms. Twist, do not pull. A twist leaves the root; a pull takes the bed. Lay them in a shallow basket, caps up. Never stack them, for a mushroom is a bruise waiting to be mentioned.

## Selling

At the Truce Bridge, the market town buys what we grow. A good basket buys a loaf, a pot of honey and a bit of ribbon for my daughter. The humans like our mushrooms; they say they taste of the cave. I say that is the point.

## A note

In the old days, a goblin grew mushrooms and a human did not eat them. Now a human buys them, and a goblin eats the human's bread, and nobody has been poisoned yet. I count that as a good year.
