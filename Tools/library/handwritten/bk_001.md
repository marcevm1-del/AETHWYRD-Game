# Neighbors Near Great Labyrinth (Floors 1-40)
*Hesk Tallow, ledger clerk at the Labyrinth gate*

**Shelf:** Field guide  
**Region:** Great Labyrinth (Floors 1-40)  
**In-world date:** 998 SC  
**Status:** Draft. Player-facing. Not Canon until reviewed. Written as what a person in Aethwyrd could know.

I count people in and I count people out. The difference between the two numbers is the only honest statistic in the Guild, and I am not allowed to publish it. So instead I write down what the people who come out say they met, and I add it up the way a clerk would. These are the neighbours I hear about most, in the order the gate-desk hears about them.

## Red Slime

Rank E, a Fire creature, weak to Water, and territorial. It sits at a corner and takes it for a home. The usual injury is a scorched sleeve. The usual cause is a rookie who walked through the middle of the passage instead of round the edge. Water solves it. A flask of cold water in the off hand solves it faster than any speech.

## Kobold Scavenger

F, skittish, and the most common thing in the first four floors. It will not stand and fight if it can take what you dropped and run. Most of what the Guild's lost-and-found shelf holds on a Thorsday came from a kobold, and was returned by one.

## Kobold Miner

Rank E, Earth, weak to Wind, an ambusher. It digs along the wall and waits. Listen for a tapping on the stone that is not in rhythm with your boots. When you hear it, move to the middle of the passage and let it come out into the open.

## Hollow Mourner

F, Dark, weak to Light. A quiet thing that drifts after you in the long halls and does not hurry. It ambushes from behind. The remedy is a lamp, and the rule is to look behind you at every turning. People who carry a candle stub tell me they never saw one. People who did not tell me they never stopped seeing one.

## Door Mimic

Rank F and a Guardian, weak to Fire. It looks like a door, and a door that is the wrong colour for the hall is not a door. Press nothing you do not need to press. Knock first. A door that knocks back is not a door either.

## How the desk uses this list

When someone asks me what is on Floor Two, I read them these five, and then I ask them what they carry. If they have a flask, a lamp and a friend, I wish them luck. If they have two of the three, I wish them more luck. If they have none, I give them a form to read while they think.

I do not count the ones who read the form and go home. They are my best statistic. Morrowyn keeps a lamp for the ones in the other column; I keep mine for those.
